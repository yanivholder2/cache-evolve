# H02

Growing v003's sampled probation victim from the 4 oldest entries to the 8 oldest will raise web_0 hit-rate at both sizes and lift the hit-rate summary above 0.5617.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In v003's `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change only `const VICTIM_SAMPLE: usize = 4;` to `const VICTIM_SAMPLE: usize = 8;` and update its doc comment.

Keep everything else in `sampled_main_victim()` the same:
- It still starts at `probation.head`, falling back to `protected.head`.
- It still walks `next` pointers within that one list, stopping early if the list is shorter than the sample.
- Ties still keep the earliest (most-LRU) node.

The contest in `evict_for_insert` stays strict `>`. The climbers, the sketch, and the counting stay untouched.

Optionally, update the `sampled_victim_picks_coldest_of_lru_sample` test comment. Its assertion still holds, because key 1 is still the coldest node in the larger sample.

Cost note for evaluation: each full-cache insert now does up to 8 sketch estimates instead of 4 (4 hash rows each). per-op-time should be checked against v003 and baseline to see whether the hit-rate gain is worth the extra time.

## Rationale

The cycle 0005 continuation asked for a sample-size sweep as its own step, kept separate from the sketch fix (H01). That separation keeps attribution clean. v003's gain came entirely from web_0, where a colder victim lowers the bar newcomers must clear and pushes out scan pollution faster. With a sample of 8, the walk reaches further toward the MRU end of probation. That tests whether the gain keeps growing or levels off. If hm_0 small drops, the cause is that recently admitted probation entries are now inside the sample.
