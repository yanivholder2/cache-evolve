# H02

Adding a floored tie-break toward the candidate (admit on `cand_freq == victim_freq` when `cand_freq >= 3`) to the conservative-update sketch recovers web_0 at both sizes and keeps that variant's hm_0 small gain, raising the hit-rate summary above cycle/0005/v003.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0007/v002, which is cycle/0005/v003 plus conservative update in `CountMinSketch::increment`, where only counters equal to the current row-minimum are incremented. Keep the conservative-update sketch, the double counting on miss-then-put, the coldest-of-4 `sampled_main_victim`, and both climbers exactly as they are.

Change only the admission test in `evict_for_insert` (in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`). Replace

```rust
if cand_freq > victim_freq {
```

with

```rust
if cand_freq > victim_freq || (cand_freq == victim_freq && cand_freq >= TIE_ADMIT_FLOOR) {
```

and add `const TIE_ADMIT_FLOOR: u32 = 3;` next to the other constants. With double counting, a key seen once arrives at estimate 2. The floor of 3 therefore means a tie admits the candidate only when it has been referenced at least twice. One-hit wonders still lose ties, which keeps the stickier-incumbent behaviour that helped hm_0 small. Warm repeat candidates win the exact low-end ties that conservative update creates, and those ties were what cost web_0.

Leave the other eviction branches unchanged, including the direct main eviction when `win.len < window_cap` and the capacity-1 fallback. Add a unit test where the candidate and victim tie at estimate 3 and the candidate is admitted, and another where they tie at estimate 2 and the candidate is rejected.

## Rationale

The v002 insight puts its web_0 loss down to conservative update squeezing small counts into exact ties, which strict `>` gives to the victim. In effect the admission bar went up. The cycle 0004 verdict against `>=` was reached under plain update, where ties were rarer and random. This hypothesis tests the cycle 0007 open question: treat sketch precision and the tie rule as one coupled admission policy.
