# H01

Loosening the late-hit tie cutoff from the last quarter to the last half of the candidate's window residency raises hit-rate by restoring web_0 small without giving back v001's large-cell recovery.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `LATE_HIT_DIVISOR` from `4` to `2`. `late_reuse()` keeps its current form,
`since_hit.saturating_mul(LATE_HIT_DIVISOR) <= resid`, so a window candidate now wins an estimate tie
(`cand_freq == victim_freq`) when its most recent window hit falls in the most recent half of its window residency,
measured on the insert clock (`tick`).

Leave everything else alone: strict `>` for non-late candidates, double counting in the sketch, the coldest-of-4 probation
victim sample, both climbers, and placement. Update the doc comments on `LATE_HIT_DIVISOR` and `evict_for_insert`
("most recent quarter") so they say "half". The inline unit tests should still pass. `early_window_hit_is_not_late` has
`since_hit == resid == 19`, and `19*2 <= 19` is false. Do not weaken any test.

## Rationale (optional)

c9 v001's insight places the useful signal between "any hit" (c8 v003, which drifted on large caches) and "last quarter"
(v001, too strict for short windows at 16384). This is a single-knob test at the midpoint. If web_0 small recovers and both
131072 cells stay at or above v001 (0.5877 / 0.7478), the cutoff is the lever. If the large cells fall, the large-window
drift starts below a half-residency cutoff, and the next step is a window-scaled cutoff instead.
