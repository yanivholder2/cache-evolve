# H03

Removing the alternating protected-split climber, so the protected share stays fixed at 80% and the window climber acts on every sample, raises hit-rate because the window adapts twice as fast and no longer reacts to hit-rate changes caused by the other knob.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

All edits go in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`.

1. In `climb()`, delete the protected turn and the `climb_turn` toggle. Every call runs the window-climber branch as it is
   now: reverse on a drop larger than `REVERSE_TOLERANCE`, decay the step by `STEP_DECAY` with a floor of 1, clamp the target
   to `[1, upper]`, then recompute `main_cap` and `protected_cap = main_cap * protected_pct / 100`.
2. `protected_pct` stays at `PROTECTED_PERCENT` (80) for the cache's lifetime. Remove the now-unused fields `prot_step`,
   `prot_prev_hit_rate` and `climb_turn`, and the constants `INITIAL_PROT_STEP`, or keep them only if removing them would
   break compilation elsewhere. Keep `MIN_PROTECTED_PERCENT` and `MAX_PROTECTED_PERCENT`, because `check_structure` in the
   inline tests uses them, and 80 lies within their bounds.
3. Do not change `sample_period`, `MIN_SAMPLE_PERIOD`, the step size, `REVERSE_TOLERANCE`, or the lazy rebalance logic. The
   only behavioural change is that the window climber gets every sample instead of every other one, and its
   `prev_hit_rate` now refers to the sample immediately before.
4. Keep the admission contest (strict `>` plus late-hit tie, `LATE_HIT_DIVISOR = 4`), the coldest-of-4 victim sample,
   double counting, and placement exactly as in v001. All existing inline tests must still pass
   (`adaptive_window_keeps_structure_sound` included).

## Rationale (optional)

c4 adopted the adaptive protected split as only a marginal winner. Its insight noted that alternating turns did not
separate the two knobs' effects, and that the protected step shrank to 1-point moves. Under alternation, each climber
compares its sample with a sample two periods earlier, and the other knob moved in between. The window climber therefore
reverses on noise and moves at half speed. That is worst at 131072, where c2/c3 found it already too slow. This variant does
not redesign the climber, which has failed 5 times. It removes one coupled knob and tests whether the protected climber
was carrying weight. If both large cells improve or hold while hm_0 is flat, the protected climber was noise. If hm_0 large
drops, the c4 gain on hm_0 large was real.
