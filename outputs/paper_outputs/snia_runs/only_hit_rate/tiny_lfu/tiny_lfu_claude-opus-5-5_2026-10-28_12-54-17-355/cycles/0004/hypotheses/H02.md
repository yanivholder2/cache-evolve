# H02

Breaking admission ties in favour of a previously seen candidate (admit when `cand_freq == victim_freq && cand_freq >= 2`) will raise hit-rate, mainly on the recency-driven hm_0, without letting web_0's one-hit scan keys into the main region.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Work in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs` of cycle/0003/v001. Change only the admission decision in `evict_for_insert()`.

- Current rule: `if cand_freq > victim_freq { admit candidate, evict victim } else { evict candidate }`.
- New rule: admit the candidate (evict `victim`, move `cand` to probation MRU) when `cand_freq > victim_freq`, **or** when `cand_freq == victim_freq && cand_freq >= TIE_ADMIT_MIN_FREQ`, where `TIE_ADMIT_MIN_FREQ: u32 = 2` is a new constant. Otherwise evict the candidate, as now.
- Why it is safe: on a tie, the candidate is the more recent of the two keys, since it just left the window and the victim is the probation LRU. Recency is the right tie-breaker for hm_0. The `>= 2` floor keeps one-hit keys (estimate 1, common on web_0's scans) from displacing an equally cold probation victim, which protects the scan resistance SLRU won on web_0.
- Do not change anything else: the hill climber, the 80/20 SLRU split, the lazy rebalance and the never-aged `u32` sketch all stay as they are. The early-return branches in `evict_for_insert` (window under cap, no main victim) are unchanged.
- Add an in-file unit test: capacity 2 (window 1, main 1). Bring the probation key and the window key to the same estimate of 2, then insert a newcomer, and assert that the old window key (the candidate) survives and the probation key is evicted. Add a second case with both estimates at 1 and assert that the candidate is evicted.

## Rationale

Ties are frequent in a sketch that is never aged, and cross-cycle insights flag strict `>` as resolving every tie in favour of the victim. Cycles 0001 and 0002 hit this through counter caps and halving. In v001 it still applies, because SLRU now picks probation victims that are often stale demoted entries with modest counts. This tests the open question "does the tie-break matter now that SLRU picks the victims?" with a one-line change. The hypothesis is refuted if hm_0 does not rise at either size, or if web_0 falls at either size.
