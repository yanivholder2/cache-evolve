# H02

Adding periodic halving of all sketch counters to v001, every 10×capacity increments and without any low counter cap, will raise hit-rate by letting stale hot keys in main lose admission contests to currently popular window candidates.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Work in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`. Keep v001's window, main list, and admission logic exactly as they are. Change only `CountMinSketch` and how it is constructed.

- Add `additions: u64` and `sample_size: u64` fields to `CountMinSketch`, and change `CountMinSketch::new(width)` to `new(width, sample_size)`. In `TinyLfuCache::try_new`, pass `sample_size = (10 * capacity).max(16) as u64`. The period is tied to capacity, as in Caffeine.
- Keep counters as `u32` and keep `MAX_COUNT = u32::MAX`. Do not add a 4-bit or 15 cap. Cycle 0001 found that a cap of 15 combined with strict `>` admission caused v003's regression on web_0 at the large size.
- In `increment`, after updating the rows, increment `additions` once per call (not once per row). When `additions >= sample_size`, call a new `reset()` method.
- `reset()` sets every counter in every row to `*c >>= 1` and sets `additions /= 2`, so the next reset comes after another `sample_size / 2` increments, matching the Caffeine/TinyLFU reset semantics.
- `estimate`, `position`, and `heap_bytes` stay unchanged, so the metadata size is identical to v001. Keep the strict `cand_freq > victim_freq` contest.
- The existing unit tests use capacity 2 or less, where `sample_size` is 16, and they perform fewer than 16 increments, so no reset fires and their behaviour does not change. Add one unit test checking that after `sample_size` increments of a single key, its estimate is roughly halved.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 showed that aging helps on its own, but its gain was hidden by the low cap. v001's counters never decay, so keys that were hot early on stay in main indefinitely. This hypothesis tests aging layered on the window, with the cap confound removed.
