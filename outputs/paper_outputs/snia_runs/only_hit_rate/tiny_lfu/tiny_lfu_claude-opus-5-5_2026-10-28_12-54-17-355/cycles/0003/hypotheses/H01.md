# H01

Splitting the adaptive-window cache's main region into a 20% probation and 80% protected SLRU, with the admission victim taken from probation LRU, will raise hit-rate, mostly on web_0 at both sizes.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's hill-climbing window (`climb`, sample counters, `window_cap`/`main_cap` retargeting, lazy rebalance) as it is. Split the main region into two lists:

- Extend `enum Region` to `Window, Probation, Protected`. Replace the single `main: List` field with `probation: List` and `protected: List`, and update `list_mut` to match. Add a field `protected_cap: usize`, computed as `main_cap * 80 / 100`. Recompute it whenever `main_cap` changes: in `try_new` and at the end of `climb`.
- Every node that enters main goes to probation MRU. This covers the not-full spill loop in `put`, the spill in `evict_for_insert`, and a contest winner (`push_back(cand, Region::Probation)`).
- `promote(idx)`: a Window node moves to window MRU, as now. A Protected node moves to protected MRU. A Probation node is unlinked and pushed to protected MRU. If that makes `protected.len > protected_cap`, demote the protected LRU (`protected.head`) to probation MRU. Repeat until it fits.
- In `evict_for_insert`, every place that currently uses `self.main.head` as the victim (the lazy-rebalance branch, the contest victim, and the defensive branch) should use `probation.head`, falling back to `protected.head` when probation is empty. Every check on `main.len` (`main.len < main_cap` in the spill conditions of `put` and `evict_for_insert`) should use `probation.len + protected.len`.
- Leave the sketch untouched: u32 counters, no aging, and the strict `>` contest.

Preserve these invariants: `win.len + probation.len + protected.len == map.len() <= capacity`, `window_cap + main_cap == capacity`, `window_cap >= 1`. Update the tests' `check_structure` helper (the one inside `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`) to sum all three lists. With capacity 1 or 2, `protected_cap` may be 0. In that case every probation hit demotes immediately, so the node stays effectively in probation. Make sure the demote loop terminates in that case: when `protected_cap == 0`, skip the move to protected.

## Rationale

The cycle-0002 SLRU (v001) helped web_0 at both sizes on top of the fixed window, and at web_0's large size it was one of only two levers that worked. The climber did nothing there. The two mechanisms target different failure modes (the window handles newcomer admission, SLRU handles victim choice), so the gains should add. A refutation would be web_0's large-size hit rate staying at or below v003's 0.5684.
