# H03

Making the hill climber's sample period independent of capacity (a fixed 4096 requests), with a step that restarts larger after each reversal, will let the adaptive window move at the 131072 size and raise hit-rate there.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Change only the climber in v003. Leave the lists, contest, and sketch as they are.

- In `try_new`, replace `sample_period = capacity.max(MIN_SAMPLE_PERIOD)` with a constant `SAMPLE_PERIOD: u64 = 4096`, used at every capacity. Keep `MIN_SAMPLE_PERIOD` only if it is still referenced. Otherwise remove it.
- Smooth the noisy per-sample hit rate before comparing samples. Add a field `ewma_hit_rate: f64`. In `climb`, compute `hr = sample_hits / sample_count`, then `ewma = 0.7 * ewma + 0.3 * hr` (seed it with `hr` on the first climb). Compare `ewma` with the previous `ewma` (stored in `prev_hit_rate`) instead of comparing raw `hr` values. Keep `REVERSE_TOLERANCE = 0.001`.
- Step schedule: keep `STEP_DECAY = 0.98` per climb. On a direction reversal, also halve the magnitude. Add a floor so the step never decays below `max(1, capacity / 1000)`. Once the step has fully decayed, the window can then still move by a meaningful amount at 131072 (about 131 entries per step), rather than 1.
- `INITIAL_STEP_PERCENT` (5%) and `MAX_WINDOW_PERCENT` (80%) stay the same. The lazy rebalance in `put`/`evict_for_insert` is unchanged.

Preserve `window_cap` in `[1, min(capacity-1, 80%·capacity)]` and `window_cap + main_cap == capacity`. The existing `adaptive_window_keeps_structure_sound` test must still pass. With capacities of 100 or less, 4096 requests per sample means few climbs in that test, which is fine.

## Rationale

The v003 insight attributes the dead result at web_0's large size (0.5684 against 0.5767 to 0.5815 for the other cycle-2 variants) to `sample_period = capacity`. That gives a 131072-entry cache about 8× fewer climbs per trace than the small one, and each climb is judged on two noisy samples. A fixed short period plus EWMA smoothing should give the large cache as many informed steps as the small one. A refutation would be the 131072 points not improving while the 16384 points hold or drop.
