## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 v002's own insight, and the cross-cycle notes, point to
fixed-fraction `len/4` probation as too short for web_0's late second touches
in 128-slot buckets. In the LRU lineage, deeper probation traded hm_0 away,
because the floor shrank the protected region, which there was the only
place reuse survived. GCLOCK+probation differs: main is frequency-ranked,
and main counters are decremented only on promotion, not on every miss. So a
64-slot main region may still hold hm_0's hot set. The large-bucket
configuration is untouched (256 = 1024/4), which isolates the experiment to
the small-size cells.

Refutation: if web_0 at 16384 does not beat v002's 0.4159, depth is not what
limits web_0 at the small size. If hm_0 at 16384 drops below 0.4485 by more
than web_0 gains, the protected-vs-probation trade-off also holds in the
GCLOCK lineage, and adaptation (ghost or dueling) is required instead.
