# H01

Inserting items demoted out of normal buckets at the MRU end (Back) of their stash bucket, instead of the LRU end (Front), will raise hit-rate on the MSR traces by keeping items that have already been reused from being evicted before brand-new stash arrivals.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

All edits are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.

Right now `promotion_get` passes one `insert_pos` (`InsertPosition::Front`) through `promote_to_target` / `promote_to_probing` into `demote_from_normal_bucket`, and it is used for every `core.put_at` in the cascade. So an item pushed out of its target bucket goes to the **Front** of its stash bucket. Under ClassicLRU that slot is index 0, the very next eviction victim. A brand-new item from `Segment::put` goes to the stash **Back**. The result is that an item which earned a place in a normal bucket gets evicted before items that were never hit at all.

Split the two roles:

1. Keep the promotion position as it is: `Front` for the stash→target insert in `promote_to_target` and for the target→probing insert in `promote_to_probing`. Items still have to climb before they can promote again.
2. Add a separate demotion position for the cascade. Change `demote_from_normal_bucket` so it no longer uses the caller's `insert_pos` for its stash inserts. Both `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, ...)` calls (the direct `true_target == source_idx` branch and the `cascade` branch) should use `InsertPosition::Back`. For the one-more-chance retry of a foreign probing resident into its true target (`core.put_at(Slot::Target, ...)`), keep the incoming `insert_pos` (Front). That keeps the change focused on the stash end.
   - The cleanest way is to add a parameter: `demote_from_normal_bucket(core, item, source_idx, retry_pos, stash_pos)`. `promote_to_target` and `promote_to_probing` pass `(insert_pos, InsertPosition::Back)`.
3. Leave `DashSegment::get` calling `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Front)`.

Invariants to keep: slot legality (stash inserts still go through `Slot::Stash`, which is derived from the key); no duplicates (every removed item is re-inserted exactly once or evicted at stash overflow); stash overflow stays the end of the line with no retry. The existing `unreachable!` checks after promotion still hold, because the promoted key is placed before any demotion happens.

The baseline unit tests `direct_to_stash_at_front_when_cold` and `cascade_to_stash_at_front_when_cold` encode the old Front-in-stash position. Update their expected indices in the same file's `#[cfg(test)]` module to match the new behaviour (demoted item at the stash Back).

## Rationale (optional)

This works like a second chance for items that have shown reuse. They get a full stash lifetime before they leave, so they are not sacrificed to one-hit-wonders. The change only adds a parameter, so per-op-time and metadata-size should stay the same.
