# H03

Routing a stash item that passes the promotion gate into a free slot of its probing bucket, whenever its target bucket is full, will raise the 128K hit-rate on both traces by avoiding the target-eviction cascade that a promotion into a full target triggers today.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

All edits go in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. Keep every v002 constant, the ghost, the rescue, the gate and the bypass check (which still looks only at the target's free slots) unchanged.

1. Add `const PROMOTE_INTO_FREE_PROBING: bool = true;`. Its doc comment: "a stash item that earned promotion while its target is full lands in a free slot of its probing bucket (MRU end) instead of evicting a target resident."
2. In the stash-hit promote branch of `promotion_get`, after `remove_at(item_idx)`, check these conditions:
   - `PROMOTE_INTO_FREE_PROBING` is set.
   - `core.buckets[target_idx]` is full.
   - `core.buckets[probing_idx].get_items().len() < get_max_size()`.

   If all hold, call `core.put_at(Slot::Probing, STASH_PROMOTION_TARGET_POS, data)`, add `debug_assert!(ret.is_none())`, and return `Some(&core.buckets[probing_idx].get_items()[pos])` after `get_position(key)`. Otherwise call `promote_to_target` exactly as today, followed by the existing target lookup.
3. The `unreachable!` message for the target lookup stays. Add an equivalent `unreachable!` for the probing lookup.
4. A key placed in probing this way follows the normal probing-tier rules from then on: a hit touches it in place, and a probing eviction retries it in its own target at `insert_pos`. Update the `promotion_get` doc comment and add a "New (cycle 0008)" line to the struct doc.

Tests:
- `gated_promotion_uses_free_probing_when_target_full`: fill target 0 with two direct puts and leave probing 1 empty. Put `k` (target 0) and hit it twice. Assert that `k` is in probing 1 at the MRU end, that both target residents are still present, and that `check_slot_legality()` holds.
- `gated_promotion_evicts_target_when_probing_full`: fill both buckets. The existing behaviour holds: `k` is in target 0 and the target LRU resident is in the stash.
- Existing tests such as `cascade_demotion_probing_to_full_target_to_stash` fill probing before promoting, so they stay valid. Check that `fill_target_via_stash` is unaffected: its target is never full at the moment of promotion.

Invariants: only free slots are used, so there is no new cascade. Slot legality holds, because a key may live in its own probing bucket. There are no duplicates and no metadata change. The cost is one extra length check on a promotion into a full target.

## Rationale (optional)

"Free-slot-only, no-cascade moves are safe" (c7 v002) is the lineage's most reliable recent rule, and rescue showed that free probing slots coexist with full targets at 128K. Today a proven stash item promoted into a full target evicts a target resident into the stash. That can overflow the stash and send a victim to the ghost, which may be the ghost-churn source behind the `web_0` 128K giveback. This lever acts on promotions, not on stash victims, so it does not overlap with the rescue. At 16K it should be neutral, because normal buckets are saturated there.
