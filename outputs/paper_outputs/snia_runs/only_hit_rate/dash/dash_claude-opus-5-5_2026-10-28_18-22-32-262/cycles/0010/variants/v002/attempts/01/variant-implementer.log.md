- **Credit decision in `put`:**
  - Near hits are credited exactly as before.
  - Far hits are credited only while `far_score >= 0`, or when the key is an exploration probe. Every credited far hit also gets `FAR_PROBE_FLAG` (bit 62).
  - Any other far hit enters with a count of 0, like a fresh key.
- **Carrying the flag:** promotion from stash to target keeps the flag next to the reference bit. The stash hit count masks off both high flags. The CLOCK test in `make_room_clock` masks the flag off, and clearing a reference bit leaves the flag in place.
- **Score updates:**
  - The score goes up by one when a flagged item is hit while resident: a target hit (hottest or not) or a probing hit. The flag is then cleared.
  - It goes down by one when a flagged item leaves its normal bucket through `demote_from_normal_bucket`. That covers CLOCK victims and `put_at` evictions, including the foreign-retry cascade victim.
  - Every existing counter reset clears the flag, so each key is scored at most once.

**Deviation from the hypothesis**

I didn't use `hash(key) & 7 == 0` to pick exploration probes. The segment is chosen by `hash % num_segments`, which is 8 or 64 here, so the low 3 hash bits are the same for every key in a segment.
