- **Level raised on hits.** The level goes up by one, capped at 3, on a stash hit (before the gate check), a target hit, and a probing hit. On a hit on the target's hottest item it is raised before the item moves, so it carries into probing.
- **New victim rule** in `make_room_clock`, which still does nothing unless the bucket is full:
  - it evicts the item with the lowest level;
  - on a tie it picks an item with reference bit 0, then the one nearest the LRU end;
