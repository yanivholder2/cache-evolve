# H02

Giving far-zone ghost hits gate credit only while a per-segment feedback score says such credit has been paying off should raise mean hit-rate: `web_0` gets its preferred short reach and `hm_0` 128K keeps its long tail, instead of one fixed factor-2 compromise.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

All edits go in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. Keep the ring length (`GHOST_FACTOR = 2`), consume-on-put, the rescue, the gate and bypass, CLOCK and the v001 ghost-carried bit unchanged. Near-zone ghost hits behave exactly as today.

1. **Ghost age.**
   - Change `GhostRing::remove` to return `Option<usize>`: the age of the matched slot, `(head + len - 1 - idx) % len`, where 0 means the newest entry.
   - Define `near = age < len / 2`, which is the most recent stash-capacity worth of evictions.
   - Keep a `#[cfg(test)] contains` helper.
2. **Feedback score.**
   - Add `far_score: i8` to `DashSegment`, starting at 0. Add `const FAR_SCORE_MIN: i8 = -8; const FAR_SCORE_MAX: i8 = 8;` and `const ADAPTIVE_FAR_CREDIT: bool = true;`.
   - Pass `&mut far_score` into `promotion_get` and the cascade helpers alongside `ghost`. The cleanest way is a small `struct GhostState { ring: GhostRing, far_score: i8 }` that replaces the `ghost: &mut GhostRing` parameter everywhere.
3. **Credit decision in `DashSegment::put`.**
   - On a ghost hit, the fingerprint is consumed as today.
   - If the hit is near, or `far_score >= 0`, credit it as v001 does: count `STASH_PROMOTION_HITS - 1` plus `GHOST_ORIGIN_FLAG`.
   - If it is far and `far_score >= 0`, also set a second marker `const FAR_PROBE_FLAG: usize = 1 << (usize::BITS - 2)`.
   - If it is far and `far_score < 0`, the put enters with count 0 and no flags, like a fresh key. As a 1-in-8 exploration probe, use `hash(key) & 7 == 0`: those keys get the credit and `FAR_PROBE_FLAG` anyway, so the score can recover.
4. **Carry `FAR_PROBE_FLAG` through promotion into the target.**
   - In the stash→target branch, preserve the bit: `lfu_counter = ref_bit | (flags & FAR_PROBE_FLAG)`.
   - Mask it off everywhere the stash count or the CLOCK bit is read. In normal buckets that means comparing `lfu_counter & 1`. This includes `make_room_clock`'s `== 0` test.
5. **Score updates.**
   - A resident hit (target non-hottest, target-hottest before promotion to probing, or a probing hit) on an item carrying `FAR_PROBE_FLAG`: `far_score += 1`, saturating, then clear the flag.
   - An item carrying `FAR_PROBE_FLAG` that leaves its normal bucket through `demote_from_normal_bucket`, or through `make_room_clock` or a `put_at` eviction: `far_score -= 1`, saturating.
   - The flag is cleared by every existing counter reset (stash entry, probing promotion, foreign retry), so each credited key is scored at most once.

Tests:
- `ghost_remove_reports_age`.
- `far_hit_uncredited_when_score_negative`: force `far_score = -1`, use a key whose hash does not match the exploration probe, and check it needs two stash hits.
- `far_probe_hit_raises_score`.
- `far_probe_demotion_lowers_score`.
- Existing ghost tests stay valid: they are near-zone, or run with score 0.

Invariants: no duplicates, slot legality, the `get` postcondition, and ghost recording on every out-of-cache eviction. Metadata grows by one `i8` per segment.

## Rationale (optional)

c7 showed that ghost value depends on reach and on the trace. `web_0` gains from a factor-1 ring and loses from stale far credit, while `hm_0` 128K keys return between one and two stash-capacities after eviction. Fixed reductions all lost on `hm_0` 128K: factor 1 in c7, and the occupancy-conditioned far credit in c8 v002. This changes the condition from occupancy to measured outcome. Far credit stays on wherever far-credited promotees actually get re-hit, and it shuts off where they get evicted unhit. The ring and its consume-on-put lifecycle are untouched.
