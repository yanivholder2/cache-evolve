# H03

Inserting gate-passing stash→target promotions at the target's MRU end (`Back`) instead of its LRU end (`Front`) will raise mean hit-rate above 0.395975, because an item that has proven two stash hits will no longer be the next victim evicted from its target.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

All edits go in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. Keep the two-hit gate, `STASH_DEMOTION_POS = Back`, the counter resets, and the target-hit, probing-hit and miss branches unchanged. `DashSegment::get` keeps passing `InsertPosition::Front` as `insert_pos`.

1. Add `const STASH_PROMOTION_TARGET_POS: InsertPosition = InsertPosition::Back;`, documented as "where a stash item that passed the gate lands in its target".
2. Give `promote_to_target` separate placement and retry positions: `fn promote_to_target(core, item, place_pos: InsertPosition, retry_pos: InsertPosition)`. It calls `core.put_at(Slot::Target, place_pos, item)`. On eviction it calls `demote_from_normal_bucket(core, evicted, target_idx, retry_pos, STASH_DEMOTION_POS)`, so foreign residents displaced by the cascade still retry at the cold `Front` position.
3. In the stash-hit promotion branch of `promotion_get`, call `promote_to_target(core, data, STASH_PROMOTION_TARGET_POS, insert_pos)`. Leave `promote_to_probing` and its `insert_pos` unchanged.
4. Do not change the `unreachable!` target lookup after the call. Placing at `Back` keeps the key resident in the target.

Effect: under ClassicLRU, a target hit on an item at the MRU end makes it the `promotion_candidate_index()`. A promoted item's next hit therefore moves it to probing, where before it had to climb first. Its target LRU neighbour, not the newcomer, is the one demoted on the next collision.

Tests: update `stash_hit_inserts_at_front_of_target` (rename it to `stash_hit_inserts_at_back_of_target`) to expect `items[0] == keys[0]` and `items[1] == keys[1]`. Re-check `target_hit_not_hottest_promotes_within_bucket`, `target_hit_hottest_promotes_to_probing`, `cascade_demotion_probing_to_full_target_to_stash` and `last_bucket_probing_wraps_to_zero`. The newly promoted key now sits at the MRU end, so one fewer `get` may be needed to reach probing. Adjust the `get` counts and comments so each test still exercises its named path. Update the call sites of `promote_to_target` in `direct_to_stash_position` to pass `(insert_pos, insert_pos)`, and keep their expected stash indices.

Invariants: only the slot position within the same target bucket changes. No-duplicates, slot legality and the get postcondition are preserved.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's insight in cycle 0002 found that `Front` promotion makes the newest promotee the target's LRU victim. Combined with the gate, this means an item that paid for two stash hits is evicted from the target first, which wastes the gate's filtering. This lever is about target placement, which no earlier cycle has varied. It is independent of H01 (who is gated) and H02 (when the gate applies). The risk is that hot residents are pushed out faster at 16K, so compare the 16K cells against the 128K cells.
