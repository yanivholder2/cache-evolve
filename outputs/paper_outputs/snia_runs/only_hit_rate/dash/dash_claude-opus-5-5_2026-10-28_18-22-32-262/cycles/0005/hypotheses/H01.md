# H01

Placing target→probing promotees at the probing bucket's MRU end (`Back`) instead of its LRU end (`Front`) will raise mean hit-rate above 0.39905, because an item that proved itself hottest in its target will stop being the next victim evicted from probing.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

All edits go in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. Keep `STASH_DEMOTION_POS = Back`, `STASH_PROMOTION_TARGET_POS = Back`, the two-hit gate with the `STASH_GATE_BYPASS_FREE_SLOTS = 2` bypass, the `lfu_counter` resets, and the stash, probing-hit and miss branches unchanged. `DashSegment::get` keeps passing `InsertPosition::Front` as `insert_pos`.

1. Add `const PROBING_PROMOTION_POS: InsertPosition = InsertPosition::Back;`, documented as "where an item promoted from its target into its probing bucket lands".
2. Change `promote_to_probing` to take separate positions, the same way cycle 0003 v003 split `promote_to_target`: `fn promote_to_probing(core, item, place_pos: InsertPosition, retry_pos: InsertPosition)`. It calls `core.put_at(Slot::Probing, place_pos, item)`. On eviction it calls `demote_from_normal_bucket(core, evicted, probing_idx, retry_pos, STASH_DEMOTION_POS)`, so a displaced probing resident still retries its own target at the cold `Front` position.
3. In the target-hit `is_hottest` branch of `promotion_get`, call `promote_to_probing(core, data, PROBING_PROMOTION_POS, insert_pos)`. Leave the `unreachable!` probing lookup as it is. Placing at `Back` keeps the key resident in probing.
4. Update the doc comment on `promotion_get` (the `insert_pos = Front` paragraph) and the struct doc to say that probing promotions now land at `PROBING_PROMOTION_POS`, and that only foreign-resident retries use `insert_pos`.

Effect: today, with 2-slot buckets and `Front` placement, a fresh probing arrival sits at the LRU end. The very next promotion into that probing bucket, from any of its neighbour's residents, pushes it back down. With `Back` placement the older, unrefreshed probing resident is the one displaced.

Tests: update the direct callers of `promote_to_probing` in `evicted_from_probing_cascades_to_target`, `target_retry_position` and `cascade_to_stash_position` to pass `(insert_pos, insert_pos)` (or `(InsertPosition::Back, InsertPosition::Back)` where they pass `Back` today). Keep their expected indices: the victim is still the probing LRU item that was already resident. Add `target_hottest_promotion_lands_at_probing_mru`: fill the probing bucket with one filler key, promote a target key into probing via `fill_target_via_stash` plus one `get`, and assert that the promoted key is at `items.len() - 1` in the probing bucket.

Invariants: only the slot position within the same probing bucket changes. No-duplicates, slot legality and the `get` postcondition are preserved.

## Rationale (optional)

Placement fixes have been the most reliable win in this run: MRU-end stash demotion (0001) and MRU-end stash→target promotion (0003). The probing tier is the one tier whose entry placement has never been varied, and it still uses the cold `Front` position. The cycle-0004 insight also suggests `web_0` 128K wants hot items to settle in probing more easily. This lever does that without loosening any admission rule, which keeps it safe at 16K.
