# H03

Halving an item's `lfu_counter` when it is demoted from a normal bucket into the stash will raise hit-rate on both traces, because stale target-tier frequency will stop shielding demoted items from the stash tail scan at the expense of fresh and ghost-credited arrivals.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle insights list "move stash ageing rather than remove it" as an open question. Removing the stash-hit halving (cycle 0005 v003) made items sticky and hurt every cell. Today an item that has fallen out of target carries its full target-tier count into the stash. That count was earned under different competition. It lets the demoted item beat a ghost-credited arrival (counter 1) in `lru_tail_lfu_victim`, even though target eviction just judged it cold. Halving it at the tier boundary (in the spirit of SLRU/TinyLFU) keeps the frequency ranking but lowers it, so ghost credits can compete. It also limits how high a counter an item carries back into target when it is re-promoted. This is the same stickiness mechanism the stash-hit halving controls, applied at a point where no hit is being penalised. This variant changes the ageing point, not the reach or the bonus, so it is independent of H01 and H02.
