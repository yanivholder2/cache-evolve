# H02

Adding a small per-segment ghost table of recently evicted stash keys to v002, so that a key re-inserted after eviction from the stash enters with `lfu_counter = 1`, will raise hit-rate on hm_0 by serving its long-gap reuse.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Start from v002's overlay. Keep the topology, the 8-slot tail scan, the cap of 15 and halve-on-cap ageing, the stash-hit counting and all landing positions unchanged. The change is to admission only.

1. **Ghost table.** Add a fixed-size ring of key fingerprints to the segment struct in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, e.g. `ghost: [u16; GHOST_SLOTS]` plus `ghost_head: u16`, with `const GHOST_SLOTS: usize = 64;`.
   - The fingerprint is 16 bits of the key's hash. Use bits that are not used for bucket or segment selection, e.g. the top 16 bits of the existing hash.
   - Use a reserved value, such as 0 remapped to 1, to mark an empty slot.
2. **Recording.** In the code path where a victim chosen by `eviction_candidate_index` is removed from a **stash** bucket and dropped from the cache (not demoted), write its fingerprint at `ghost[ghost_head]` and advance `ghost_head` modulo `GHOST_SLOTS`. Do not record victims evicted from normal buckets that are demoted to stash, because they are still resident.
3. **Admission.** In `Segment::put` (the stash insert path that stays at the existing position), compute the new key's fingerprint and scan `ghost` linearly.
   - On a match, set the new item's `lfu_counter = 1` before inserting it, and clear that ghost slot.
   - Otherwise the item starts at 0, as before.
   - Do not change where the item lands, and do not put it directly into target.
4. With counter 1, the returning key beats one-touch arrivals in the stash tail scan, so it gets a longer stash wait and a better chance of the re-reference that promotes it. That is the long-gap reuse hm_0 shows.
5. `Item` must not grow. The ghost table adds about 130 bytes per segment, so the evaluation should report metadata-size per entry against 40, as well as per-op-time, since `put` gains a 64-entry scan.
6. Add unit tests:
   - a key evicted from stash and then re-put starts with counter 1;
   - a fresh key starts with counter 0;
   - the ring overwrites the oldest fingerprint after `GHOST_SLOTS` evictions.

## Rationale (optional)

Every cycle has flagged hm_0's long-gap reuse as unserved. Recency-position tweaks (MRU landing, gated demotion) cannot reach it, and changing the normal/stash ratio starves promotion. A ghost history, as in ARC and 2Q's A1out, is the standard admission-side remedy. It gives frequency credit to a key whose reuse distance is just longer than the stash's residency, without enlarging the stash or changing the split. web_0 is scan-heavy, so few of its keys should hit the ghost, and its hit-rate should hold.
