# H01

Adding only MRU-end landing for stash→target promotions to v002, with v002's demotion position and probing trigger left as they are, will raise hit-rate at size 16384 on hm_0 without the web_0 regression that came with v001's bundled changes.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Start from v002's overlay. Keep its 24 normal / 8 stash topology, its frequency-aware LRU-tail victim, its stash-hit counting and its halve-on-cap ageing exactly as they are. Edit only `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.

1. Split the single `insert_pos` passed through `promotion_get` into two parameters, `promote_pos: InsertPosition` and `demote_pos: InsertPosition`.
2. Use `promote_pos` **only** for the `core.put_at(Slot::Target, ..)` call in `promote_to_target`, which is the stash→target move.
3. Keep the original single position (`InsertPosition::Front`) in these places:
   - the `core.put_at(Slot::Probing, ..)` call in `promote_to_probing`;
   - every `put_at` inside `demote_from_normal_bucket`.

   This leaves target→probing landing and demotion unchanged from v002.
4. In `DashSegment::get`, call `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back, InsertPosition::Front)`.
5. Do not change the target→probing trigger (`promotion_candidate_index`). Do not add a hit bit. Do not move demotion to mid-stash. Default `Segment::put` stays at (stash, Back).
6. Update the in-file unit tests that assert Front placement after a stash hit so they expect Back placement. Leave the probing and demotion tests as they are.

`Item` must not grow, so metadata-size stays at 40. Leave the trait signatures unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v001 in cycle 0002 bundled three changes, so its web_0 loss could not be attributed. The cycle insight asks whether MRU promotion alone still adds anything on top of the frequency filter. This variant applies MRU landing to the single stash→target hop, which is the tier entry that v002's filter does not protect. Under v002, a freshly promoted item lands at index 0 of target. It survives the tail scan only because it carries a non-zero counter. If MRU landing alone helps hm_0 at size 16384 and holds web_0 steady, then the earlier regression came from the trigger or demotion changes.
