# H01

Adding MRU-end promotion to v002's 24/8 topology, together with a stricter probing trigger and a mid-bucket stash demotion slot, will raise hit-rate at size 16384 without bringing back v001's web_0 regression at size 131072.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Start from v002's overlay. Keep its 24 normal / 8 stash bucket split exactly as it is.

1. **Promotion landing (taken from v001).** Replace the single `insert_pos` used on promotion and demotion with two separate positions:
   - a promote position that inserts at the MRU end (Back) of the destination bucket's ClassicLRU order, for stash→target and target→probing;
   - a demote position (see step 3).

   Every other caller of `insert_pos` (for example new-item insertion from `put`) keeps its current behaviour.
2. **Stricter probing trigger.** In v001, an item that had just come out of stash already sat at `promotion_candidate_index`, so its next target hit sent it to probing. Change the target→probing check in `promotion_get` (or wherever `promotion_candidate_index` is compared) so it fires only when both of these hold:
   - the item is at the hottest/MRU position;
   - the item has been hit at least once while resident in its current target bucket.

   Track this with a single bit or tiny counter that already exists on `Item`. The lowest bit of `lfu_counter` is fine, or a spare flag bit. Clear it on promotion into target and set it on the first target hit. Do not grow `Item`, because metadata-size must stay at 40.
3. **Softer demotion position.** When probing pressure demotes an item back to stash, insert it at the middle of the stash bucket's recency order (index = len/2) instead of the LRU end. Demoted items that are still useful then outlive fresh one-touch items, which enter at the stash's normal insert position.

Keep all trait signatures unchanged. Do not touch the gate-integrity files.

## Rationale (optional)

v001's MRU promotion gave the largest small-size gains, and v002's topology gave the largest large-size gains. v001's only regression came from the probing trigger firing too readily and from LRU-end demotion. This hypothesis stacks the two winners and removes both causes of that regression.
