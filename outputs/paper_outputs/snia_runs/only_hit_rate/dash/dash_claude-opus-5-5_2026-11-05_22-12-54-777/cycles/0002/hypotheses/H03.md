# H03

Pushing v002's normal/stash split from 24/8 to 28/4 buckets per segment, at the same total capacity, will raise hit-rate further at size 131072 by enlarging the protected tier while the 4-bucket stash still filters one-touch scan blocks.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Start from v002's overlay. Change only the per-segment bucket-count constants:

- normal buckets go from 24 to 28;
- stash buckets go from 8 to 4.

Keep the total of 32 buckets per segment, the slots per bucket and the total capacity the same.

Update every place that is derived from these counts so that indexing stays in range:
- stash-bucket selection, for example `hash % STASH_BUCKETS` or the equivalent;
- any probing-bucket offset (target+1 wraps modulo the normal count);
- any capacity arithmetic.

Do not change the admission, promotion, demotion or victim-selection logic. This is a topology-only sweep, so that the effect of the ratio can be measured on its own. Leave `Item` and the trait signatures unchanged, and do not touch the gate-integrity files.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 showed that the gain grows with normal-tier size at size 131072. Cycle 0001's open question asks whether that trend continues past 3/4 or whether a smaller stash stops filtering scans. A 7/8 split answers that directly. The expected result is a gain at size 131072, a neutral or slightly worse result at size 16384, and the most risk on web_0, where scan filtering matters most.
