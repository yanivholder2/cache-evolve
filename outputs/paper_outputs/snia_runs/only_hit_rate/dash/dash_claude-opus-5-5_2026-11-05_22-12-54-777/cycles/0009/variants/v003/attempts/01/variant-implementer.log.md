**How each H03 change was applied:**
1. **State:** each segment gets a `bypass_score: i8`, clamped to [-16, 15] and starting at 0. It also gets a `ghost_admitted` bitmap with one bit per normal-bucket slot (one `u64` per 64-slot bucket). The bit is not taken from `lfu_counter`, and the counter cap is unchanged. The added metadata is one `u64` per normal bucket plus one byte per segment.
2. **Marking:** every bypass that lands in target sets the bit on its landing slot. This covers the free-slot, cold-slot and positive-score cases.
3. **Reward:** a hit on a marked slot adds 1 to the score and clears the bit. This works whether the hit reorders the slot inside the bucket or promotes it to probing.
4. **Penalty:** when a marked slot is evicted, the score drops by 1 and the bit is cleared. This covers the bypass victim, stash→target and probing inserts, and the evictions in `demote_from_normal_bucket`'s retry.
   - Bits move with their items. For this, normal-bucket inserts now go through a new `normal_insert` helper, which evicts the same way `Bucket::put_at` does for a new key and shifts the bitmap to match.
   - When a hit reorders a bucket that has marked slots, the code finds where the item landed and moves its bit there.
   - Delete and overwrite never happen in this segment, so there was nothing to clear there.
5. **Gate:** `bypass_gate` now also lets a returner in when `score > 0`, evicting the slot the existing victim scan picks (lowest counter in the tail window). Otherwise v002's stash fallback runs unchanged.
6. **Everything else** is unchanged from v002.

**Unit tests:** v002's segment tests are kept, adjusted for the new state. New ones cover:
- marking a slot on bypass, then rewarding once on re-hit;
- bypassing into an all-warm target when the score is positive;
- the penalty when an unhit marked slot is evicted;
- the gate outcomes for each score sign;
- score saturation;
- the bitmap tracking item moves (bucket sizes 4, 64 and 70).

The main risk is a performance cost. When a bucket has marked slots, every hit in it scans the bucket to find where the item moved; buckets with no marked slots skip this. A positive score means more marked slots, so watch per-op-time on the 131072 cells.
