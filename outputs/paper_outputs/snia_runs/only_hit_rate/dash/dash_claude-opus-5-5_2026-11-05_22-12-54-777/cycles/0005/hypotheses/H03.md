# H03

Stopping a stash hit from triggering bucket-wide halving (the hit item leaves the stash at once) will raise hit-rate on hm_0, most at size 16384, by keeping co-resident stash items' counters intact, ghost-credited admissions included.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0004/v002's overlay. Keep the ghost ring (64 slots, bonus 1), topology, the 8-slot tail window, cap 15, normal-bucket halve-on-cap ageing and all landing positions unchanged. The only change is how the stash hit is counted in the stash branch of `promotion_get` in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.

1. **New helper.** In `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`, add `pub fn record_hit_saturating<K, V>(item: &mut Item<K, V>)`. It does `item.lfu_counter = (item.lfu_counter + 1).min(LFU_COUNTER_CAP)` and never touches any other item. Leave `record_hit` unchanged: normal buckets keep using it through `ClassicLRU::get_from_position` and the hottest-in-target promotion.
2. **Stash branch.** In `promotion_get`, replace `record_hit(core.stash_buckets[stash_idx].get_items_mut(), item_idx);` with `record_hit_saturating(&mut core.stash_buckets[stash_idx].get_items_mut()[item_idx]);`. The hit is still counted, so the promoted item still carries a non-zero counter into target. But a hit item that arrives near the cap (for example a demoted item with counter 15) no longer halves every other stash resident on its way out.
3. **Not changed.** `promote_to_target`, the Front landing, and normal-bucket ageing all stay the same. If the item lands in target at 15, any later halving happens in target, where it belongs.
4. **Tests.**
   - Add a test: a stash bucket holds item A with `lfu_counter = 15` and item B with `lfu_counter = 1`. After a `get` on A, B's counter is still 1, and A is in target with counter 15.
   - Keep `stash_hit_counts_and_carries_counter` passing: counter 1 after the first stash hit.
   - Add a policy-level test for `record_hit_saturating`: it clamps at the cap and leaves neighbours untouched.
5. **Reporting.** Report per-op-time: the stash-hit path loses a possible bucket-wide loop, so it should be equal or slightly lower. Metadata-size is unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Halving exists to age a bucket's residents relative to each other. But a stash hit always removes the hit item from the stash right away, so halving the stash on its behalf ages the items that stay behind for an increment that never stays in that bucket. Demoted items arrive in the stash carrying high counters from the protected tier. One re-hit then wipes out every co-resident ghost credit (1→0) and every partial history. That is the fragility v002's insight flagged, and the reason cycle 0004's fast ageing hurt small-size hm_0 most. This is a different lever from H01 (ghost reach) and H02 (bonus size): it removes the drain itself. It is not a faster- or slower-ageing sweep, because normal-bucket ageing is untouched.
