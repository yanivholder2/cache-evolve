# H02

Exempting keys that re-entered small through the weak-ghost-returnee path from the correlated-reference cap raises hit-rate, because a quick reuse right after a ghost return is per-key proof of reuse and not insertion-burst noise.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, add a `returnee: bool` field to `Entry` (default
`false` in `Entry::new`). Extend `push_small_new(key, value, freq)` with a
`returnee: bool` argument and store it on the entry. In `put()`, the weak/old
ghost-returnee arm (`Some(Loc::Ghost { .. })` falling through to
`push_small_new(..., GHOST_RETURN_SMALL_FREQ)`) passes `true`. The first-admission
arm (`None`) passes `false`.

In `get()`, apply the burst cap only when the entry is not a returnee: the
condition becomes `in_small && !entry.returnee && clock - entry.small_tick < crp`.
A returnee's small hit always does `freq = (freq + 1).min(MAX_FREQ)`, so a
weak returnee (which enters with freq 1) graduates on its first reuse in small,
even a quick one, when main is full. That restores the cycle-0004/0005
behaviour for returnees only. Keep `CRP_DIVISOR = 8` and leave every other path
unchanged: strong ghost→main admission, the ghost-age window, the
graduation thresholds, the freq reset on graduation, and main eviction.

Mirror the change in the test `Reference` model by adding the same flag to its
`push_small` and its `get`. Update the test
`weak_ghost_returnee_correlated_get_stays_at_freq_one`, whose expectation flips:
after the `get` the freq is 2, and on the next `put` the key graduates into
main instead of being re-ghosted. Rename the test to match. Keep the invariant
checks as they are (`index.len() == resident + ghost_live`, resident ≤ capacity).

## Rationale (optional)

0009-v003's cap is meant to catch first-time keys whose hits all sit in one
insertion burst. A weak returnee has already shown a spaced re-reference: it was
evicted, aged past a full small turnover as a ghost, and came back. Capping its
next quick hit makes it pay a second ghost round trip before reaching main.
On web_0 that is the cost named in the 0009-v003 insight. This hypothesis
leaves the window alone (unlike H01) and uses per-key history to decide where
the cap applies, which is the one lever type that has repeatedly won. Expected
outcome: web_0 @ 16384 recovers, and hm_0 keeps most of its gain because
hm_0's burst-only polluters are mostly first-time keys.
