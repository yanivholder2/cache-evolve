# H03

Gating insertion with a TinyLFU frequency-sketch admission filter, so a new key replaces the LRU victim only when its estimated frequency is higher, will raise hit-rate on the MSR traces at the larger cache sizes in particular.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Keep the baseline's HashMap plus arena doubly-linked LRU list and its recency movement (`unlink` / `push_back`) unchanged, and add an admission step to `put` in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`:

- Add a `FrequencySketch` struct in a new file `src/lru_cache/sketch.rs` (declared with `mod sketch;` in `mod.rs`). It is a count-min sketch with 4-bit counters packed into a `Vec<u64>`: width = next power of two ≥ `capacity` words (16 counters per word), depth 4, and row indices from a 64-bit mix of the key hash (e.g. splitmix64 of `std::hash::DefaultHasher` output) with a per-row seed. API: `increment(&mut self, h: u64)` caps each counter at 15; `frequency(&self, h: u64) -> u8` returns the min over rows. Ageing: count increments, and when the count reaches `10 * capacity`, halve every counter (`(w >> 1) & 0x7777_7777_7777_7777`) and halve the count.
- Add a `sketch: FrequencySketch` field to `LruCache`.
- `get`: hash the key once and call `sketch.increment(h)` on **every** call, hit or miss. The JNI caller always calls `get` before `put`, so misses get counted too. Promotion on a hit stays as in the baseline.
- `put` on an existing key: same as the baseline.
- `put` on a new key when the arena has room: insert as in the baseline (no filtering during warm-up).
- `put` on a new key when full: read `victim = head` and compare `sketch.frequency(hash(new_key))` against `sketch.frequency(hash(nodes[victim].key))`. If the candidate's count is strictly greater, evict the victim and reuse its slot as in the baseline. Otherwise **reject**: return without changing the map or list, so the victim stays resident. Optional tie-break to avoid adversarial lock-out: when the candidate's count is ≥ 6 and the victim's count is ≥ the candidate's, admit with probability 1/128 using a cheap xorshift state stored in `LruCache`.
- Invariants still hold: rejected keys are simply not resident (P1 and P3 are unaffected; P2 holds because admitted values are written unchanged).
- Update or adjust any unit test in `mod.rs` that assumes every `put` gets inserted (e.g. `eviction_chain`, `eviction_removes_lru`, `capacity_one`) to account for admission; do not touch `tests/` or `src/test_utils/`.

## Rationale (optional)

H01 and H02 change how resident entries move around. This hypothesis leaves movement alone and asks whether a new block should enter at all. MSR web_0 and hm_0 have skewed frequencies with a long tail of rarely reused blocks. A frequency-gated admission filter stops that tail from evicting the hot set, and TinyLFU-style filters are known to lift LRU hit rates well on such traces. The cost is one hash plus a few sketch reads and writes per op, so expect a modest increase in per-op-time.
