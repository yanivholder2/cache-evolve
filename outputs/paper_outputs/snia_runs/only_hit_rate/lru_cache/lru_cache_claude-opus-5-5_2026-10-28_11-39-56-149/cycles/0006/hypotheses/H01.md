# H01

Raising the hill climber's step cap from 256 to 1024, with the sample period left at its 50_000 cap, will let the window grow further on small-size web_0 and raise the overall hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

Change exactly one lever in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, in `LruCache::try_new`: the initial step magnitude.

- Replace `let step_init = std::cmp::min(std::cmp::max(1, capacity / 64), 256);`
  with `let step_init = (capacity / 16).clamp(1, 1024);`.
  For both evaluated sizes (16_384 and 131_072) this gives `step_init = 1024`, up from 256. That
  is 4x the step, and 6.25% of capacity per round at the small size.
- Do not change anything else. Keep `sample_period = clamp(10 * capacity, 2_000, 50_000)`, the
  2% per-round decay, the reset to `step_init` when the magnitude reaches 1, flip-on-drop,
  `min_win = max(1, capacity/100)`, `max_win = 75%`, the sketch (4x width, doorkeeper, dedup,
  reset at 16x capacity), the tie-admitting duel and the duel during window growth.
- Update the stale assertions in the in-file unit test `climber_keeps_window_in_bounds`: for
  capacity 1000, `step_init` becomes `62`, not `15`. Update the header comment that describes
  the step formula. Keep the other invariants as they are: `win_cap` stays within
  `[min_win, max_win]`, `prot_len <= prot_cap`, and `map.len() <= capacity`.
- Optional diagnostic (does not change behaviour): at each `climb()`, emit a `log::debug!` with
  `win_cap` so the settled window per cell can be read.

## Rationale

Cycle 0005 found that the ceiling never binds and that a shorter period hurts. The step cap is
the remaining untested climber knob, and the cycle-0005 insight lists it as the next one-lever
test. Both sizes currently hit the same 256 cap. At 16_384, the window can therefore move only
about 1.5% of capacity per round, and the step decays toward 1 before the window reaches a
web_0-friendly share. A larger step, measured over the same long period, should reach a larger
window in fewer rounds, without the half-applied resize problem that shorter periods caused.
The risk is extra climber noise on small-size hm_0. The window floor stays in place to limit it.
