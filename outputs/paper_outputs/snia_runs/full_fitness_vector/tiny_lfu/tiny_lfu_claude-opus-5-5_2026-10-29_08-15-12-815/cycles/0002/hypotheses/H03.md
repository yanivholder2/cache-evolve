# H03

Replace v001's fixed 1% window with a hill-climbing adaptive window size, so the window grows on recency-heavy phases of the MSR traces and raises hit-rate, especially at the 16384-entry size.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Start from v001's W-TinyLFU source. Leave the sketch and hashing as they are.

Turn `window_cap` from a constant into a tunable that is adjusted at runtime. Recompute `main_cap = cap - window_cap` and `protected_cap = 0.8 * main_cap` each time it changes. Add a Caffeine-style hill climber to `TinyLfuCache`:

- Fields: `sample_hits: u32`, `sample_misses: u32`, `prev_hit_rate: f32`, `step: i32` and `sample_period = 10 × cap`. Increment `sample_hits` in the `get` hit path and `sample_misses` in the miss path.
- When `sample_hits + sample_misses >= sample_period`, compute `hr = hits / (hits + misses)`.
  - If `hr` dropped compared with `prev_hit_rate`, reverse the direction of `step`.
  - Shrink the step's magnitude by a decay factor of 0.98 per period. Keep a floor of 1 entry.
  - The initial step is `±(cap × 0.0625)`.
  - Apply `window_cap = clamp(window_cap + step, 1, cap × 0.8)`.
  - Store `prev_hit_rate = hr` and reset both counters.
- To apply a resize lazily, never evict on resize:
  - If the window is now over its cap, move window LRU entries to probation MRU.
  - If it is under its cap, the next admission fills it naturally.
  - If protected is over its new `protected_cap`, demote protected LRU entries to probation.
  - Do at most a small bounded number of moves (e.g. 8) per `put` to keep the tail flat, and carry any remaining moves over to later operations.
- Initial `window_cap` stays at 1% of `cap`.

Region bookkeeping, `on_hit` semantics and the frequency contest between the window evictee and the probation victim are unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v001's hit-rate gain is concentrated at the small cache size. MSR block traces mix scan-like and recency-heavy phases, where a 1% window is likely too small. Adaptive window sizing is the remaining big hit-rate lever in W-TinyLFU. It does not overlap with H01's merge or H02's sketch-layout work.
