# H01

Port v001's 1% LRU window plus 80/20 probation/protected SLRU onto v003's single-fast-hash cache, with the region tag stored in spare link bits, to raise hit-rate to v001's level or above while keeping v003's per-op-time and the 48-byte Node.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

Start from v003's source. Keep `FastHasher` / `BuildHasherDefault<FastHasher>` for the map, and keep the single-hash sketch API (`increment_hashed`, `estimate_hashed`, row indices sliced from one 64-bit hash) exactly as it is.

Bring over v001's residency structure:
- Use three `RegionList` head/tail/len records: window, probation and protected.
- Size them with `window_cap = max(1, cap/100)`, `main_cap = cap - window_cap` and `protected_cap = 0.8 * main_cap`.
- Every newcomer goes into the window MRU.
- When the window is over capacity, its LRU entry becomes the candidate. The candidate is compared against the probation LRU victim, or the protected LRU victim if probation is empty, using `estimate_hashed`. The winner stays in or enters probation and the loser is evicted. Reuse the evictee's slot for the newcomer, as v001 does.
- `on_hit` works as follows. A window hit moves the entry to the window MRU. A probation hit promotes the entry to protected. If protected is then over its cap, its LRU entry is demoted to probation MRU. A protected hit moves the entry to the protected MRU.
- Compute each key's hash once per `get` and once per `put`, and pass the u64 to both the map lookup and the sketch call. Hash the victim at most once per contest.

Remove v001's `region: u8` field from `Node`. Instead, pack the 2-bit region into the top 2 bits of one of the node's existing link fields (prev or next index/pointer). Mask the tag off on every link read with `link & !REGION_MASK`, and add `region()` / `set_region()` helpers. Assert with a `const` check or a unit-free `size_of` comment that `Node<u64,u64>` stays 48 bytes. Capacities are far below 2^62, so the high bits are free.

Keep the strict `>` admission comparison and the existing sketch width and depth. Do not add aging in this variant, so its effect can be attributed separately.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001 found that the hit-rate and latency fixes are on separate axes, and recommended merging them as the first step. Packing the region tag removes v001's only metadata regression.
