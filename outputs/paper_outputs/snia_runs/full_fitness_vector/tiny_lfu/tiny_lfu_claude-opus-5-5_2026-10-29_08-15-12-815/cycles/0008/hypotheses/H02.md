# H02

Letting admission ties go to the window candidate when its estimate is at least 2 restores large-size web_0 hit-rate on top of cycle 0007 v003's miss-dedup.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

There is one behavioural edit, in `evict_one()` in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`. Leave everything else in v003 unchanged, including its dedup logic, its `additions` accounting (skipped increments still do **not** advance the clock), and its `get`/`put` structure. That keeps this a single-variable test of the tie-break.

Replace the admission test `if cand_freq > victim_freq` with
`if cand_freq > victim_freq || (cand_freq == victim_freq && cand_freq >= 2)`.

- A candidate seen only once (estimate ≤ 1 under v003's dedup) still loses ties. One-hit wonders stay out, which keeps v003's small-size gain.
- A candidate seen again (estimate ≥ 2) now wins a tie against the probation head. This acts as an explicit, bounded recency bonus, the role the parent's double count used to play implicitly.
- The win branch does not change: unlink the victim, move the candidate to probation, and return the victim's slot. The lose branch also stays the same.

Write the condition so it compiles to compares and a single branch, with no new loads: both estimates are already in registers. Update any v003 test that asserts a tie outcome. Add a unit test: at capacity 2, when the candidate and the victim both have estimate 2, the candidate is admitted. When both have estimate 1, the victim stays.

## Rationale

v003's insight could not tell whether its web_0 @ 131072 loss (−1.7 pt) came from the slower aging clock or from losing the newcomer edge against the strict `>` test. H01 brings back the clock alone on the incumbent. This variant brings back the tie edge alone on v003. Together they separate the two effects. The `>= 2` guard stops the bonus from also handing ties to cold keys.
