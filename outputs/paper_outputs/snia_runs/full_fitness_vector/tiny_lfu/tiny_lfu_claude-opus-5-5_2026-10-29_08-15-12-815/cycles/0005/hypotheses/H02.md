# H02

Sizing the admission window as an absolute floor, `max(1% of capacity, min(5% of capacity, 820 entries))`, on top of conservative update will lift hit-rate above 0.56 by keeping the 5%-window gain at 16384 and conservative update's gain at 131072.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Edit only `TinyLfuCache::try_new` in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`. Replace

```rust
let window_cap = (capacity / 100).max(1);
```

with

```rust
const WINDOW_FLOOR: usize = 820;
let pct1 = capacity / 100;
let pct5 = capacity / 20;
let window_cap = pct1.max(pct5.min(WINDOW_FLOOR)).max(1);
```

Define `WINDOW_FLOOR` next to the other constants at the top of the file. At 16384 the window becomes 819 entries, the same as cycle 0004's v003 5% window. At 131072 it stays at 1% (1310 entries), so the main region keeps every slot it has in the parent. For small capacities, the `pct5` cap keeps the window from swallowing the cache (capacity 2 still gets a window of 1). Leave `main_cap = capacity - window_cap` and `protected_cap = 80% of main_cap` unchanged, and update the `log::info!` line to match.

Do not touch `get`, `put`, `evict_one`, `on_hit` or the sketch. The branch-free conservative update from the parent stays as it is, so every window exit still goes through the `cand_freq > victim_freq` contest. Check that `eviction_prefers_hot_items`, `cold_window_candidate_is_evicted` and `capacity_one_works` still pass, since their window sizes at capacity 1 and 2 are unchanged. Add a test that asserts `window_cap` is 819 at capacity 16384, 1310 at 131072 and 1 at 2.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 showed the two hit-rate levers work at opposite sizes. Conservative update (v001) gained only at 131072. The 5% window (v003) gained only at 16384 and slipped slightly at 131072, because there it took slots from the main region. A size-aware floor should add the two gains together. This is a pure hit-rate test: the hot path is byte-identical to the parent, so any per-op-time change should be read as noise.
