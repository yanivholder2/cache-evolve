# H03

When a guarded stash→target promotion targets a full bucket, swap the promoted key with the target's LRU entry in one step instead of evicting and cascading, to cut put/get p99 (per-op-time) on the stash→target path.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In cycle/0005/v001's Dash segment, change only the stash-hit branch that promotes a stash item into its target bucket **when that target `is_full()`** and the full-target guard has passed (the third stash hit at large sizes, or the second at small sizes via `full_target_guard`). Today this branch makes room in target by evicting/demoting the target's LRU entry. That entry may be retried in the neighbour/probing bucket and may finally land in stash, where it can force a stash eviction. Replace this with a **direct swap**:

- Remove the promoted key from its stash slot. This frees exactly one stash slot.
- Remove the target bucket's LRU-position entry. Insert the promoted key into target at the normal promote position, and reset its `lfu_counter` to 0 as the existing code does when a key lands in target, so the probing guard starts clean.
- Write the demoted target entry into the stash slot that was just freed, with `lfu_counter = 0`. It then has to earn re-entry through the normal stash gate.
- No neighbour/probing retry and no extra stash eviction. Exactly one target bucket and one stash slot are touched, and the item count stays the same.

Leave promotion into a non-full target, the guard thresholds, the size gate, the full-probing guard and its arm, put, the per-segment tick, and the Item layout unchanged.

## Rationale

The cycle 0005 insight says the full-probing arm fires rarely, and that the remaining get/put tails probably come from stash→target moves and put eviction. Cycle 0005 v002 showed that turning a cascade into a two-bucket swap removes tail cost with no hit-rate change at the probing tier. This tests the same "remove the move, don't defer it" lesson one tier down, on a path that fires much more often. Hit-rate should hold or rise slightly: the demoted target entry stays cached (in stash) instead of possibly pushing a reused neighbour resident out, and no fresh stash entry is evicted. Confirmation: get p99 < 148 ns or put p99 < 100 ns, with hit-rate ≥ 0.3948. Refutation: hit-rate falls in the large cells, which would mean the neighbour retry was keeping useful target residents cached.
