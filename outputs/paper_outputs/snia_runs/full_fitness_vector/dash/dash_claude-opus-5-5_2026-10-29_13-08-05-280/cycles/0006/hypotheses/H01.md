# H01

Fold cycle 0005 v002's direct two-bucket swap for guarded full-probing promotion into cycle 0005 v001 and remove the redundant stash-side size gate, to lower get p99 (per-op-time) while keeping metadata-size at 24 and hit-rate at ≥ 0.3948.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

Start from cycle/0005/v001: 24-byte Item (`u32` tick from a per-segment counter, `u8` lfu_counter), full-target stash guard with the `full_target_guard` size gate, and the full-probing guard that needs two hottest-position hits.

1. **Direct swap (port from cycle/0005/v002).** In the target-hit path of the Dash segment, change only the `is_hottest` arm where the destination probing bucket `is_full()` and the guard has passed (second hottest-position hit). Replace the existing cascade (probing-LRU eviction → foreign/neighbour retry → target-LRU eviction → stash insert) with a single swap:
   - Remove the hot key from its target slot.
   - Remove the probing bucket's LRU-position entry. Insert the hot key into probing at the normal `promote_pos`.
   - Write the demoted probing entry into the target slot that was just freed, at the target's LRU end (`Back`). Reset its `lfu_counter` to 0.
   - No foreign retry, no target eviction, no stash insert. Exactly two buckets are touched and the item count stays the same.
   Keep the guard's increment/threshold/reset logic exactly as it is in v001. Promotion into a non-full probing bucket stays unchanged.
2. **Drop the size gate.** Remove the `full_target_guard` field and the `GUARD_MIN_CAPACITY` constant from `DashSegment`. In the stash→target branch, always require the extra (third) stash hit when the target is full, as in cycle/0004/v003. This removes a branch and a field read from the stash-hit path. The cycle 0005 insights show the gate is worth only about 0.0001 hit-rate in small-cache cells now that the probing guard exists.

Leave the Item layout, the per-segment tick, put, and eviction unchanged.

## Rationale

The cycle 0005 Continuation asks for exactly this merge. v001 and v002 touch separate paths (Item layout/tick vs. the full-probing arm), so their gains should add up: v001's put and get-tail improvement, plus v002's removal of the deferred cascade. Removing the gate tests whether it is truly redundant. Confirmation: metadata-size 24, hit-rate within ±0.0002 of 0.39485, and get p99 ≤ 148 ns with p50 ≤ 76. If the small-cache cells drop by more than about 0.0002, the gate still earns its place.
