# H02

Halving `PROBE_WINDOW` on the eviction lineage will lower get p50 and get p99 (per-op-time) and leave hit-rate within ±0.0005 of the parent.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Parent behaviour: on a hottest-position hit in a full probing bucket, the arm checks whether the hit is recent enough, using a tick-age test against `PROBE_WINDOW` and the per-segment `u32` tick. If it is, the arm promotes the item and evicts the probing LRU resident outright. The parent no longer uses the swap or the cascade.

The change:
1. Set `PROBE_WINDOW` to half its current value, rounded down, with a minimum of 1. Change nothing else on that arm.
2. Make sure the window bound is a `const`, or is computed once per segment or cache, and is never computed per get. Cycle 0008 v002 flagged a per-get bound computation as a possible cause of its median regression. If the parent computes the bound on every get, hoist it into a `const` or into a field set at construction time, without increasing the size of `Dash<u64,u64,DashSegment>` (currently 24 bytes). If neither option keeps that size, use a `const`.
3. Keep the following unchanged:
   - the full-target three-hit door guard;
   - the two-hit stash gate;
   - the two-hottest-hit full-probing guard;
   - the single outright eviction;
   - the 24-byte Item;
   - the common target-hit path, with no new reads or writes.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0008 v002 showed that `PROBE_WINDOW` is a latency throttle, not a hit-rate lever. Doubling it gained nothing in hit-rate and raised get p50 and p99. The direction this hypothesis tests has not been tried yet: narrowing the window on the cheaper eviction arm, which cycle 0008 listed as an open question.

On that arm the promotion frees a slot instead of saturating buckets. A narrower window should therefore cost little or no hit-rate.

How to read the result: compare get p50 and p99 against H01, the same-session incumbent copy, and not against the cross-session numbers. The hypothesis is refuted if:
- hit-rate drops by more than 0.0005, which would mean the arm's promotions carry real hits; or
- get latency does not fall relative to H01.
