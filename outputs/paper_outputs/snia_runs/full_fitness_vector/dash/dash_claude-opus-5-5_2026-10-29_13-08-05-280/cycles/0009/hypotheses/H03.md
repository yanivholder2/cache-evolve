# H03

Stash victim selection with age-bounded protection will raise hit-rate in the large-cache cells (web_0-large and hm_0-large) without the small-cache losses that cycle 0008 v003's unbounded protection caused. The rule spares a reused stash entry only if its last hit is recent and only within the two LRU-end slots.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Change the stash victim routine: the code path that picks which stash entry leaves when an insert needs a slot in a full stash bucket. The parent evicts the entry at the LRU position. Cycle 0008 v003 changed this same routine. Replace that choice with a bounded, aged check:

1. Look only at the two LRU-most stash slots. Call them `lru0`, the current victim, and `lru1`, the next one toward MRU. Do not scan the whole bucket. Cycle 0008 v003's put cost appeared on exactly this stash-full put path.
2. Spare `lru0` and evict `lru1` instead only when all of these hold:
   - `lru0.lfu_counter >= 1`, meaning it has been hit while in stash;
   - `segment_tick.wrapping_sub(lru0.tick) < STASH_PROTECT_AGE`, meaning the hit was recent, using the per-segment `u32` tick already stored in Item;
   - `lru1.lfu_counter == 0`.
   In every other case, evict `lru0` exactly as the parent does.
3. Add a new `const STASH_PROTECT_AGE: u32`. Set it to the stash bucket capacity times 4 ticks, or to the equivalent of about one stash lifetime given how the segment tick advances on inserts. Name the choice in a comment.
4. When `lru0` is spared, it keeps its position. Do not move it to MRU and do not reset its counter. That way it gets one reprieve and ages out on the next pressure event after its window passes. This is the aging bound that cycle 0008 lacked.
5. Constraints:
   - Do not touch the common target-hit path.
   - Do not add fields to Item. It must stay 24 bytes.
   - Keep the two-hit stash gate, the full-target door guard, the probing guard, `PROBE_WINDOW`, and the single outright eviction exactly as they are in the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0008 v003 protected never-hit-preferring stash entries with no aging. That filled the stash with stale one-hit keys and starved fresh arrivals, and hit-rate fell in every cell. Small caches were hurt most.

This variant addresses that failure in three ways:
- A spare is allowed only for a *recent* hit.
- Only one adjacent slot is considered.
- A spared entry is never refreshed.

As a result, fresh arrivals are displaced at most one position earlier, and stale hits exit normally.

How to read the result:
- **Confirmed:** large-cache web_0 or hm_0 rises by 0.001 or more, the small-cache cells stay within −0.0005, and put p99 stays near the same-session H01 control.
- **Refuted:** a hit-rate loss in the small cells. The stash-exit lever is then dead, and cycles should stop proposing it.
