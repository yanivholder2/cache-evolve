## Rationale (optional)

The standing measurement rule from cycle 0009 is that every cohort includes an identical-copy control of its parent, and latency is scored only against that control. Cycle 0009's control was a copy of the swap lineage (0007 v003), not of the eviction lineage that H02 and H03 build on. So there is still no same-session latency reading for v003 itself.

How to read the result:
- **Confirmed:** hit-rate matches 0009 v003 to the last digit in every cell.
- **Refuted:** any hit-rate difference, which would mean the simulator is nondeterministic, or that the copy is not faithful.

Latency is not judged against the cross-session parent numbers. It is the reference that the other two variants are judged against.
