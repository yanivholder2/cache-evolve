# H02

Halving `PROBE_WINDOW` on top of 0009 v003's bounded stash protection will lower get p50 and get p99 against the same-session H01 control and keep hit-rate within ±0.0005 of v003 in every cell.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

Port cycle 0009 v002's window change onto v003, and change nothing else.

1. On the full-probing hottest-hit arm, set `PROBE_WINDOW` to half its value in the parent, rounded down, with a minimum of 1. This is the tick-age test that decides whether a hottest-position hit in a full probing bucket promotes the item and evicts the probing LRU resident outright. 0009 v002 applied the same halving to 0008 v001, so use the value that v002 ended up with.
2. Make sure the bound is a `const`, not something computed on each get. If the parent computes it per get, hoist it into a `const`. Do not add fields that would change `size_of::<Dash<u64,u64,DashSegment>>()`, which is currently 24 bytes.
3. Keep the following exactly as they are in the parent:
   - the bounded stash-victim protection: `STASH_PROTECT_AGE`, the `lru0`/`lru1` check, the single reprieve, and no MRU refresh;
   - the two-hit stash gate;
   - the full-target door guard;
   - the two-hottest-hit probing guard and its single outright eviction;
   - the 24-byte Item;
   - the common target-hit path, with no new reads or writes.

## Rationale (optional)

The two levers act on different paths. The window throttles the probing arm on the get path. The protection acts on stash exit, on the stash-full put path. That makes this the stacking test that cycle 0009 asked for. Earlier stacking failures (0002, 0007) came from levers on the *same* arm.

How to read the result:
- **Confirmed:** hit-rate is within ±0.0005 of v003 in every cell, with hm_0 keeping v003's gain, and get p50 and get p99 are both below H01's same-session values.
- **Refuted, hit-rate side:** hm_0 loses v003's gain. That would mean the narrower window interacts with stash protection, perhaps because spared stash keys climb through probing.
- **Refuted, latency side:** get latency is not lower than H01. That would mean the window is not a meaningful latency knob on the eviction arm, and it should be left alone.
