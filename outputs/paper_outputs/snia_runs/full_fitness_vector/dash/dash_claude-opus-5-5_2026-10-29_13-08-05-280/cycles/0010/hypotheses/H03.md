# H03

A small per-segment ghost filter of fingerprints from stash entries that were evicted before their first hit, used to pre-credit those keys with one hit on re-arrival, will raise hit-rate in web_0-large by at least 0.001 without losing more than 0.0005 in any other cell.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

This is an admission change at the stash gate. It touches only the put/insert path and the stash-victim path. It must not touch the get path.

1. **Ghost storage.** Add a fixed-size ghost table to the per-segment struct (the struct that already holds the per-segment `u32` tick):
   - `ghost: [u16; GHOST_SETS * GHOST_WAYS]`, with `const GHOST_SETS: usize = 16` and `const GHOST_WAYS: usize = 4`, for 128 bytes per segment;
   - a `ghost_next: [u8; GHOST_SETS]` round-robin cursor, or a single shared `u8` cursor if that is simpler.

   A fingerprint is 16 bits of the key hash that are *not* used for bucket or segment selection, with 0 reserved for "empty" (map 0 to 1). The set index is 4 further hash bits, independent of both the bucket selection and the fingerprint.

   Neither Item nor `Dash<u64,u64,DashSegment>` may grow: Item stays at 24 bytes and `size_of::<Dash<..>>()` stays at 24. The segment struct is heap-allocated behind Dash, so growing it is allowed. If it turns out to be stored inline in Dash, halve the table to 8×4 and put it behind the existing segment allocation instead. Do not grow Dash.
2. **Recording.** In the stash-victim routine, after v003's bounded-protection decision picks the final victim, write the victim's fingerprint into its set if `victim.lfu_counter == 0`, meaning it was never hit while in stash. Use the round-robin slot for that set. Do not record victims that were hit at least once, because those have already shown reuse and the gate treated them fairly.
3. **Consulting.** On the miss/insert path, where a new key is placed into stash with `lfu_counter = 0`, scan the 4 ways of its ghost set. On a match:
   - insert the key with `lfu_counter = 1`, so that its next stash hit satisfies the two-hit gate;
   - clear that ghost way to 0, so each ghost entry gives at most one credit.

   On no match, behave exactly as the parent does.
4. **Unchanged:**
   - the target-hit and probing-hit get paths, with no ghost reads there;
   - `STASH_PROTECT_AGE` and the `lru0`/`lru1` protection logic;
   - the full-target door guard and its threshold;
   - `PROBE_WINDOW` and the single outright eviction;
   - the per-segment tick semantics.

   The credit only advances a key within the stash gate. It must not let the key skip the full-target door guard.

## Rationale (optional)

web_0-large has stayed flat under every stash-exit, probing-arm and placement lever. Cycle 0009 points to admission as the remaining lever. My reading is that web_0's reuse distances exceed one stash lifetime: a key arrives, is evicted from stash before its second touch, and then comes back, only to be judged as brand new again. The ghost filter remembers exactly those "came back after one stash lifetime" keys, at no Item cost.

This differs from cycle 0003's failed re-admission credit in two ways:
- That credit applied to *demoted target residents*, and it sped up swaps among keys that were already reused. This one applies only to keys that were never hit in stash.
- The credit here is one hit, can be used once, and stops at the gate.

How to read the result:
- **Confirmed:** web_0-large rises by 0.001 or more and no cell falls by more than 0.0005. Put p99 should stay near H01's same-session value, since the change adds only a 4-way u16 scan on misses.
- **Refuted, no gain:** web_0-large stays flat. The trace is then capacity-bound and not admission-bound, and stash-gate admission should be dropped as the lever for web_0-large.
- **Refuted, small-cache loss:** small cells lose. Credited keys would then be crowding out fresh arrivals, the same failure mode as cycle 0008's unbounded protection.
