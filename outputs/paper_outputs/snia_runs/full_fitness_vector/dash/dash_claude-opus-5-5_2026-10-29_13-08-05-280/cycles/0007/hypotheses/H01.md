# H01

Remove the redundant stash-side size gate from cycle 0006 v002, keeping its full-probing cascade, to raise small-cache hit-rate the way cycle 0006 v001 did without paying for the swap's get/put tail.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

cycle/0006/v002 was built on cycle/0005/v001. It still has the `full_target_guard` size gate (field on `DashSegment` plus the `GUARD_MIN_CAPACITY` constant) and the original full-probing cascade. It also adds the lazy `PROBE_WINDOW` tick-age check on the `is_hottest` + full-probing arm.

Make exactly one change:

- Delete the `full_target_guard` field, its initialisation in the segment constructor, and the `GUARD_MIN_CAPACITY` constant.
- In the stash-hit branch that promotes a stash item into a **full** target bucket, always require the extra (third) stash hit. This is the cycle/0004/v003 / cycle/0006/v001 behaviour at every cache size. Remove the conditional that lowered the requirement to two hits when the gate was off.

Do not change any of these: the `PROBE_WINDOW` arm and its timestamp handling, the full-probing cascade (do NOT port the swap), promotion into non-full buckets, put, eviction, the per-segment tick, or the 24-byte Item layout.

## Rationale

Cycle 0006 v001 bundled two changes, the swap and dropping the gate. It gained in both small-cache cells (web_0 0.3613→0.3618, hm_0 0.3511→0.3521). The size gate only acts at small sizes, so those gains most likely come from dropping the gate, not from the swap. The swap is the change that cost get p99 (184 ns) and put p99 (132 ns). Applying only the gate removal to v002 should stack the small-cache gains on top of v002's large-cache window gains, giving hit-rate ≈ 0.3956. It also removes a field read and a branch from the stash-hit path, so per-op-time should hold at ≤ 76. Refutation: the small cells stay at v002's values. That would mean v001's small-cache gain came from the swap.
