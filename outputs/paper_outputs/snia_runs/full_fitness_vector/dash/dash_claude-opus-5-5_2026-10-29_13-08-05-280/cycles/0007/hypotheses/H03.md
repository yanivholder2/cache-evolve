# H03

Port cycle 0006 v002's lazy `PROBE_WINDOW` tick-age check onto cycle 0006 v001's guarded full-probing swap, to test whether their large-cache hit-rate gains add up, raising hit-rate above 0.3956.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

cycle/0006/v001 = cycle/0005/v001, plus the direct two-bucket swap on the guarded full-probing promotion, minus the `full_target_guard` size gate. Its full-probing guard still uses the plain "two hottest hits" rule, with the count in `lfu_counter`.

In the target-hit path, change only the `is_hottest` arm where the destination probing bucket `is_full()`:

- **`lfu_counter == 0` (first hottest hit):** set `lfu_counter = 1` and do not promote, as today. Also store the segment's current tick in `item.timestamp`.
- **`lfu_counter >= 1`:** compute `age = seg_tick.wrapping_sub(item.timestamp)`.
  - If `age <= PROBE_WINDOW`, run v001's existing swap unchanged: the hot key goes into probing at `promote_pos`, and the probing LRU entry goes into the freed target slot at `Back` with `lfu_counter = 0`.
  - Otherwise, treat the hit as a fresh first hit: keep `lfu_counter = 1`, refresh `item.timestamp` to the current tick, and do not swap.
- Define `PROBE_WINDOW` exactly as cycle/0006/v002 does: a per-segment `u32` equal to the segment's total slot count (all buckets plus stash). Compute it once at construction, or use a constant if the slot count is compile-time.

The non-hottest target-hit arm must not read or write `lfu_counter` or `timestamp`. Do not change the swap mechanics, the stash→target guard, put, eviction, the per-segment tick (still bumped only on insert), or the 24-byte Item layout.

## Rationale

The two cycle 0006 Winners improved hit-rate through different mechanisms on different arms of the cascade/swap decision. v001's swap keeps demoted probing residents cached and drops the gate, gaining in all cells except hm_0-large. v002's recency filter stops slow-recurring keys from taking guarded probing slots, and it gained most in the large cells, including hm_0-large (0.4440), which is v001's weak cell. If the gains are independent, the merge should reach about 0.3958 with metadata-size 24. The window also makes the swap fire less often, which may partly offset v001's get p99 of 184 ns. Confirmation: hit-rate > 0.39532 and hm_0-large ≥ 0.4440. Refutation: hit-rate at or below v001's. That would mean the swap already keeps the keys the window was filtering for, so the two levers overlap.
