# H02

On the guarded full-probing promotion in cycle 0006 v002, replace the multi-bucket cascade with a single eviction of the probing bucket's LRU entry. This frees a target slot instead of keeping buckets saturated, to cut get/put p99 (per-op-time) with no hit-rate loss.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In cycle/0006/v002's Dash segment target-hit path, change only the `is_hottest` arm where the destination probing bucket `is_full()` **and** the guard has passed (second hottest hit within `PROBE_WINDOW`, the branch that currently promotes). Today that branch runs the cascade: probing-LRU eviction → foreign/neighbour retry → target-LRU eviction → stash insert. Replace it with:

1. Remove the probing bucket's LRU-position entry and **drop it from the cache entirely**. Do not reinsert it anywhere: no neighbour retry, no stash insert. Decrement the item count / segment occupancy the same way the normal eviction path does.
2. Remove the hot key from its target slot. Insert it into the probing bucket at the normal `promote_pos`, with the same counter/timestamp reset the existing promote path applies.
3. Leave the freed target slot **empty**.

Exactly two buckets are touched, and the segment ends with one free slot in the target bucket. Later puts and stash→target promotions for that bucket take the cheap non-full path.

Leave everything else as it is: the first-hit/stale-hit (non-promoting) branches of the arm, `PROBE_WINDOW`, the stash→target guard and size gate, promotion into non-full probing buckets, put, put-eviction, the per-segment tick, and the 24-byte Item.

## Rationale

The cross-cycle lesson is that removing work beats deferring it, and that swaps fail as a tail fix because they never free a slot (cycle 0006). The cascade frees a slot, but it touches up to four buckets. The swap touches two but frees nothing. Evicting the probing LRU entry outright is the only option that is both cheap and makes room. The dropped entry is the least-recent resident of a full probing bucket. The cascade would most likely have pushed it to stash, where it is evicted soon anyway, so the hit-rate loss should be negligible. Confirmation: get p99 < 156 ns or put p99 < 99 ns, with hit-rate ≥ 0.3950 and metadata-size 24. Refutation: large-cache cells drop by more than about 0.0003. That would mean the cascade's re-homing of probing evictees was holding on to useful keys.
