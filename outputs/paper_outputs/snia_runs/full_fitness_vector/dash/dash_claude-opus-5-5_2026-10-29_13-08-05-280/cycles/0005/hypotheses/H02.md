# H02

When v003's guarded promotion into a full probing bucket fires, swap the promoted key with the probing LRU victim in one step instead of running the probing-LRU → foreign-retry → target-LRU → stash cascade, so that get p99 (per-op-time) falls without giving up hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Change only the `is_hottest` promotion arm of the target-hit path in the Dash segment, and only the case where the destination probing bucket `is_full()` and v003's guard has passed (the key's second hottest-position hit). That arm currently removes the key from target, evicts the probing bucket's LRU entry, retries that entry in the foreign/neighbour bucket, and lets any overflow cascade through target-LRU into stash. Replace it with a **direct swap**:

- Remove the hot key from its target slot. This frees exactly one slot in that target.
- Remove the probing bucket's LRU-position entry, insert the hot key into probing at the normal `promote_pos`, and write the demoted probing entry into the target slot that was just freed. Place it at the target's LRU end (`Back`), so it is the next target victim unless it gets hit again.
- No foreign-bucket retry, no target-LRU eviction, no stash insert. The move touches exactly two buckets and the number of items stays the same.

Keep v003's guard (the `lfu_counter` check on the hottest hit of a full probing bucket) and its counter reset on promotion exactly as they are. Reset the demoted entry's `lfu_counter` to 0 so it has to earn its way back. Promotion into a *non-full* probing bucket, and all other paths (stash→target guard, put, eviction), stay unchanged.

## Rationale

The v003 insight says the get-tail cost comes from the deferred cascade, which is still the slowest path. A swap is O(1) in buckets touched and removes moves instead of deferring them, which is the pattern that improved latency in c1/c3. It should also help hit-rate: the cascade currently pushes reused residents out of a *neighbouring* target and into stash. The swap displaces only the coldest probing entry, and that entry stays cached in target. If get p99 falls toward v002's 150 ns while hit-rate stays ≥ 0.3948, the claim is confirmed. A drop in hit-rate would mean the cascade's foreign-retry was keeping useful probing residents.
