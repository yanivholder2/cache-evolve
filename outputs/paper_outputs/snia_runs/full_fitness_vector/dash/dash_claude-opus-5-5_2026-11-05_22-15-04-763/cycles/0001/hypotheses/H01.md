# H01

Placing items demoted out of the normal tier at the MRU end of their stash bucket, instead of its LRU end, raises hit rate on the MSR traces by giving proven-hot items a full second residency in stash.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

All changes are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.

Today `promotion_get` threads one `insert_pos` (`InsertPosition::Front`) through
every placement: stash→target promotion, target→probing promotion, and every
demotion in `demote_from_normal_bucket`. So an item evicted from its target
lands in stash at `Front`, which under ClassicLRU is index 0, the next eviction
victim. An item that earned a place in the normal tier gets almost no time in
the stash, the tier that holds 75% of segment capacity (24 of 32 buckets).

Split the single position into two:

- `promote_pos` — used by `promote_to_target` and `promote_to_probing` for the
  item being promoted. Keep it `InsertPosition::Front`, as in the baseline
  (items still have to climb to MRU before they promote again).
- `demote_pos` — used for every insert made inside `demote_from_normal_bucket`.
  That covers the direct `true_target == source_idx` → `Slot::Stash` insert, the
  foreign-resident retry `core.put_at(Slot::Target, ..)`, and the cascade
  `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, ..)`. Set it to `InsertPosition::Back` (MRU).

Concretely: change the `promote_to_target` / `promote_to_probing` signatures
to take both positions, or hard-code `InsertPosition::Back` inside
`demote_from_normal_bucket` and drop its `insert_pos` parameter. Keep
`DashSegment::get` calling `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key,
InsertPosition::Front)` for the promote position. Keep the `put` default
(new items go to stash `Back`) unchanged, and keep the `unreachable!` checks
after promotion, since the promoted item itself still lands in target/probing.

Update the in-file unit tests whose position expectations depend on the
demotion end: `target_retry_*`, `direct_to_stash_*`, `cascade_to_stash_*`.
Demoted items should now be at the MRU end whatever the promote position is.
Do not touch `tests/`.

## Rationale (optional)

With Front demotion, the stash acts as a FIFO for new arrivals and drops
recently hot items almost at once, which is a known weak spot on block-I/O
traces that have medium reuse distances (web_0, hm_0). Demoting to MRU makes
the stash an LRU over "new + recently demoted" items, so demoted items get a
real second chance. Per-op time should not change: the cost is the same
`Vec::push` vs `insert(0)`, and push is slightly cheaper.
