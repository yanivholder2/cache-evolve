# H03

Putting items demoted out of the normal tier at the stash MRU end (instead of the LRU end) on top of v002's two-hit gate raises hit rate by giving proven items a full stash residency in which to earn re-promotion.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

All changes are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.

Today the single `insert_pos` (`InsertPosition::Front`) from `DashSegment::get`
is passed through `promote_to_target` / `promote_to_probing` into
`demote_from_normal_bucket`. So an item evicted from the normal tier enters
stash at index 0 (the LRU end, the next victim), and the gate also cleared its
mark. Under v002 such an item now needs two hits before it is evicted almost at
once, so recently hot items are effectively lost.

Change `demote_from_normal_bucket` to drop its `insert_pos` parameter and use
fixed positions inside:

- Direct branch (`true_target == source_idx`): clear `lfu_counter = 0` as v002
  does, then `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, InsertPosition::Back, item)` (MRU).
- Foreign-resident retry into true target: keep it as
  `core.put_at(Slot::Target, InsertPosition::Front, item)`, which matches the
  current Front behaviour.
- Cascade from that retry: clear `lfu_counter = 0`, then
  `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, InsertPosition::Back, cascade)`.

Update the call sites in `promote_to_target` and `promote_to_probing` to the new
signature. The promoted item itself still uses the caller's `insert_pos`
(Front). Do not change the `promotion_get` branches, the first-hit marking, or
`put`.

Tests: `direct_to_stash_position` and `cascade_to_stash_position` now expect
the demoted item at the stash MRU end (`len - 1`) for both promote positions.
Collapse each `_at_front_when_cold` / `_at_back_when_hot` pair to assert MRU,
and keep the `lfu_counter == 0` assertions. `target_retry_*` stays Front-based:
change both to expect index 0, or keep only the Front case. Do not touch
`tests/`.

## Rationale (optional)

This tests cycle 0001's untested H01 idea where it should matter most: under the
gate, an LRU-end demotion together with a cleared mark makes it very hard for a
normal-tier victim to come back. MRU demotion turns stash into an LRU over new
and recently demoted items, close to an SLRU where probationary entries age
normally. `Vec::push` at Back is cheaper than `insert(0)`, so per-op time should
not regress and may improve slightly.
