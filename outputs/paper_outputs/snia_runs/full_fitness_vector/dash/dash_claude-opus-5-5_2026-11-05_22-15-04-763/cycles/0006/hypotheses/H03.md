# H03

Fusing the stash-hit promotion chain into a single in-place exchange (one rotate per bucket) will cut `get` p99 on cycle 0005 v002's forced third-hit promotion without changing hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Replace the three-step sequence used when a stash hit promotes into a **full** target (or probing) bucket. Today the sequence is `remove_at` on the stash bucket, then `promote_to_target`, then `demote_from_normal_bucket`, which re-inserts the victim at stash MRU. Make it a single fused helper, e.g. `swap_promote(stash_bucket, stash_idx, normal_bucket)`:

1. Take the promoted item out of `stash_bucket[stash_idx]` and take the LRU victim out of `normal_bucket`.
2. Normal bucket: rotate the occupied slots by one so the LRU slot is dropped and the MRU slot is free (one `copy_within`/`rotate_left` over the slots), then write the promoted item at MRU.
3. Stash bucket: rotate slots `[stash_idx..len)` by one toward the vacated index so the MRU slot is free, then write the victim there. This gives the same final order as `remove_at` followed by MRU insert, with one shift instead of two and no length decrement/increment.
4. Reset or set `lfu_counter` on both items exactly as the existing chain does: the promoted item's normal-tier state, and the demoted victim's fresh stash state.

Use the helper wherever this chain runs with a full destination bucket:
- the third-hit forced promotion of a deferred item;
- the non-deferred second-hit promotion.

Keep the probing-room overflow and the non-full destination case on the existing code. Final slot contents and order must match the parent exactly, so hit rate should be bit-identical; any difference indicates a bug. The bucket counts in each bucket do not change. Metadata stays at 40 B.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycles 4 and 5 showed that skipping or deferring the cascade only moves its cost elsewhere. The open question is whether the chain itself can be made cheaper. This is a pure cost change with identical policy semantics: it removes one shift and the separate length bookkeeping from the stash bucket. That makes it a clean test of whether the remaining `get` p99 (189 ns in the parent) comes from the cost of moving data within buckets.
