# H02

Skipping the MRU reorder on a normal-tier hit when the item already sits in the newest two slots of its bucket will lower `get` p50 without measurable hit-rate loss.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Edit the normal-tier hit branch of `get`. This applies to hits in the target or probing bucket only, not stash hits. Today it moves the hit item to the MRU end of the bucket by shifting the slots between its index and the end.

- Before the move, compare the hit index `i` with the bucket length `len`. If `i + 2 >= len` (the item is already at MRU or one slot from it), return the value without shifting or rewriting any slot.
- Otherwise, run the existing MRU move unchanged.

Leave the stash-hit path (first-hit `lfu_counter` gate, promotion, probing-room overflow, MRU demotion) unchanged, along with all `put`/eviction logic. Do not add new per-item state. Metadata stays at 40 B.

If the bucket's MRU end is index 0 instead of `len - 1`, mirror the condition (`i <= 1`). The rule is "within 2 slots of MRU, skip the reorder".

## Rationale (optional)

The `get` p50 gap to baseline (93–95 ns vs 75 ns) is now attributed to the common hit path. Cycles 3 and 5 ruled out scan order and scan direction, which leaves the per-hit MRU shift and the extra stash probe as suspects. This change isolates the MRU-shift suspect cleanly. It removes stores and the memmove on the hottest hits (skewed traces re-hit recently touched items), and the LRU order changes by at most one position. A p50 drop with flat hit rate confirms the shift as a cost source. A flat p50 rules it out and points to the stash probe. Check hm_0 at 131072 first for retention loss.
