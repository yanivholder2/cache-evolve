# H02

Bounding live ghosts by total cache capacity instead of `main_size` will raise hit rate on web_0 at the 131072 size without lowering hm_0.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

This variant tests one variable: the ghost bound. Everything else in cycle/0003/v002 (`src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`) stays as it is, including the freq reset on graduation, the `freq > 1` threshold, the `capacity / 4` small share, and the hashing.

- Add a field `ghost_cap: usize` to `S3Fifo`. In `try_new`, set it to `capacity`, which is `small_size + main_size`. Today the bound is implicitly `main_size`, about 0.75 × capacity.
- In `push_ghost()`, change the live-bound check from `self.ghost_index.len() >= self.main_size` to `self.ghost_index.len() >= self.ghost_cap`. Change the stale-tuple compaction trigger from `self.ghost.len() > 2 * self.main_size` to `self.ghost.len() > 2 * self.ghost_cap`.
- In `try_new`, size the preallocations to the new bound: `ghost: VecDeque::with_capacity(2 * ghost_cap + 1)` and `ghost_index: HashMap::with_capacity(ghost_cap)`.
- Update `ghost_live_bound_and_disjoint_from_resident` to assert against `cache.ghost_cap` and `2 * cache.ghost_cap`.

Ghost-hit handling, eviction order, and residency rules stay the same. Only the length of the remembered history changes.

## Rationale

Cycles 1 and 3 point to the ghost bound as a hidden variable that is coupled to main size. Freeing ghost budget (cycle-3 v001) gave a free gain, and the remaining web_0 loss is at the large size. The original S3-FIFO paper sizes its ghost at roughly the cache's entry count, not main's. The test is to compare web_0@131072 against v002's 0.5723 while hm_0 holds at 0.455 and 0.7331. If web_0 regresses, longer ghost history isn't what web_0 is missing, and the next probe should be the small-queue share.
