# H01

Raising the small-queue share from 1/4 to 1/3 of capacity lengthens probation and raises hit-rate on web_0 at 131072 without losing the summary.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Make one change only, in `S3Fifo::try_new`. Replace the small-queue sizing
`(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` with
`(capacity / 3).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Keep the `capacity == 1` special
case. `main_size` stays `capacity - small_size`, so main shrinks by about 8% of
capacity. The ghost bound stays tied to `main_size` (both the live
`ghost_index.len() >= self.main_size` check and the `2 * main_size` compaction
trigger), and the preallocation sizes in `try_new` follow the new
`small_size`/`main_size`. Leave everything else unchanged: `MAX_FREQ = 7`, the
`freq > 1` graduation threshold, the reuse-gated main-victim ghost, and the
freq/reused reset at graduation. Update the header comment to name this variant
(cycle 6 H01). Existing inline tests should still pass, because capacities 4, 5
and 8 give the same small sizes or still satisfy their assertions. Check
`main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance` (capacity 5 → small 1, main 4,
same as before).

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 5 v001 cut the small share to 10% and regressed web_0 at both sizes,
which means web_0 needs a longer probation window to collect the second hit
that graduation requires. This hypothesis tests the opposite direction as a
single variable. hm_0 at 16384 may lose slightly because main gets smaller. The
open question is whether web_0-large gains more than that.
