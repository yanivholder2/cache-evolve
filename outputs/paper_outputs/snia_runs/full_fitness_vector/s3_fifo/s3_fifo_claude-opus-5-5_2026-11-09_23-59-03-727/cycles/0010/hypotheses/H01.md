# H01

Narrowing the small-queue share from 1/5 to 1/6 for capacities at or below 32768 raises hit rate at 16384 without touching the 131072 cells.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

This is a one-constant change in `S3Fifo::try_new` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. In the `capacity <= SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD` branch, replace `(capacity / 5).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` with `(capacity / 6).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Leave `SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD` (32768), the `capacity == 1` case, and the `> SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD` branch (`capacity / 4`) exactly as they are. `main_size` is still `capacity - small_size`, and the ghost bound is still `main_size`. The ghost deque/index pre-sizing in `try_new` still derives from `main_size`.

Update the inline unit tests that depend on the split:
- `small_share_is_size_aware`: 16384 → small_size 2730, main_size 16384 − 2730; the edge case becomes `SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD / 6`.
- `ghost_live_bound_and_disjoint_from_resident`: capacity 8 → small = 8/6 = 1, main = 7 (unchanged values, but update the comment).

Do not change anything else: eviction, ghosting, the freq cap, and graduation all stay the same. Update the header comment to describe this as the cycle-10 share probe.

## Rationale (optional)

Global 1/10 hurt web_0 (cycle 5), and 1/4 → 1/5 at small sizes helped both hm_0 and web_0 slightly (cycle 9). 1/6 is the single fine-tuning probe that cycle 9 recommended. It tells us whether the 16384 optimum sits below 1/5 or whether 1/5 is the peak. The 131072 cells are bit-identical by construction, so any loss is confined to 16384 and can be read directly. If this probe loses, hit rate should be treated as converged.
