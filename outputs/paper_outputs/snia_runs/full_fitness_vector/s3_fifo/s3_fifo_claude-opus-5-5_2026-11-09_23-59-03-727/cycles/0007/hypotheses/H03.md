# H03

Replacing the ghost's growable `VecDeque` with periodic `retain` compaction by a fixed `main_size`-slot ring buffer cuts `put` p50/p99, lowering per-op-time while leaving hit rate within noise.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

This is a structural change in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` only. Do not change any policy rule: keep the reuse-gated main-victim ghost, the reset on graduation, `freq > 1`, small share 1/4, MAX_FREQ 7, and no source tag.

1. Replace `ghost: VecDeque<(K, u64)>` with a fixed ring:
   - `ghost_ring: Vec<Option<(K, u64)>>`, allocated once in `try_new` with length `main_size.max(1)` and filled with `None`.
   - `ghost_head: usize`, the next slot to overwrite.
   - Keep `ghost_index: HashMap<K, u64, FxBuild>` (key → seq) and `ghost_seq`.
2. Rewrite `push_ghost(key)`:
   - Take the old record out of `ghost_ring[ghost_head]`. If it is `Some((old_k, old_s))` and `ghost_index.get(&old_k) == Some(&old_s)`, remove `old_k` from `ghost_index`. This is the only eviction of a live ghost.
   - Write `Some((key.clone(), ghost_seq))` into the slot, insert `key → ghost_seq` into `ghost_index`, increment `ghost_seq`, and advance `ghost_head = (ghost_head + 1) % len` (or use a compare-and-reset instead of `%`).
   - Remove the `pop_back` scan loop and the `ghost.len() > 2 * main_size` `retain` compaction entirely. `push_ghost` now does O(1) work with no loop, and the ring never reallocates.
3. A ghost hit in `put` still does `ghost_index.remove(&key)`. The ring slot becomes stale and is cleaned up when the ring wraps over it. Liveness stays "`ghost_index[key] == seq`".
4. Semantics note: the window is now the last `main_size` ghost insertions, not the last `main_size` live ghosts, so stale slots shorten the effective history slightly. The live count stays `≤ main_size`, so the existing bound invariant still holds.
5. Update the unit tests in `mod.rs`. In `ghost_live_bound_and_disjoint_from_resident`, replace the `ghost.len() <= 2 * main_size` check with `ghost_ring.len() == main_size.max(1)` and keep the live-bound and disjointness asserts. Update `in_ghost` if needed. Keep `entry_fits_in_padding` at 24 bytes. The `S3Fifo` struct size may change slightly, which is fine.

## Rationale (optional)

Cross-cycle findings say footprint and locality drive the `put` tail, and the only layout direction left is shrinking ghost structures. In the current ghost, every `pop_back` scan and every O(2·main_size) `retain` happens on a miss, which is a latency spike, and the deque holds up to 2·main_size tuples. A fixed ring halves the ghost's tuple storage, never reallocates or compacts, and makes ghost maintenance strictly O(1) per miss. Of the timing directions still open, this is the only one aimed at the tail itself and not at probe count or load factor, both of which have already failed. Any change in hit rate should be tiny and should be read as a side effect, not the target.
