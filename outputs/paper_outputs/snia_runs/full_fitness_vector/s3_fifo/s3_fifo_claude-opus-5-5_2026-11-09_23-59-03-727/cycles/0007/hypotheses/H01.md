# H01

Re-admitting small-queue demotees that hit in the ghost into the small queue with `freq = 1`, instead of straight into main, raises hit rate on web_0 at 131072 without costing hm_0.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Make one change to the ghost-hit branch of `S3Fifo::put` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Everything else stays as in the parent: Fx slab+hash, reuse-gated main-victim ghost, freq/reused reset on graduation, `freq > 1` graduation, small share 1/4, MAX_FREQ 7.

1. The ghost needs to know where each record came from. Encode the source in the low bit of the ghost tag, as cycle-6 v003 did: `tag = (ghost_seq << 1) | from_main as u64`. Change `push_ghost(&mut self, key: K)` to `push_ghost(&mut self, key: K, from_main: bool)`. `evict_main` passes `true` and `evict_small` passes `false`. Liveness checks (`ghost_index.get(&k) == Some(&s)`) work unchanged because they compare the whole tag.
2. In `put`, after `self.ghost_index.remove(&key)` returns `Some(tag)`:
   - **Main-victim ghost hit** (`tag & 1 == 1`): keep the parent's behaviour exactly. Evict from main if it is full, `alloc`, `main.push_front(i)`, and leave freq at 0. Do NOT copy v003's `freq = 1` credit.
   - **Small-demotee ghost hit** (`tag & 1 == 0`): if `small.len() >= small_size`, call `evict_small()`. Then `alloc` and `small.push_front(i)`, and set `slab[i].freq = 1` with `reused = false`. The ghost hit counts as one reuse, so one more hit during the next probation pass reaches `freq >= 2` and graduates the key through the normal `evict_small` path. A key that gets no hit is demoted to the ghost again as before.
3. Eviction work stays bounded. `evict_small` still pops exactly one entry per call (no multi-pop loop), and `evict_main` is unchanged.
4. Update the unit tests inside `mod.rs` (not the locked `tests/` directory). `ghost_hit_promotes_back_to_main` should become a test that a small-demotee ghost hit lands in small with freq 1. Add a test that such a key graduates to main after one further `get` followed by a small eviction. `main_victim_is_ghosted` should still assert that a main-victim ghost hit goes to main with freq 0. Keep `entry_fits_in_padding` at 24 bytes and keep the ghost-bound and disjointness tests.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 6 concluded that web_0's large-size gap lies in how the ghost hands keys to main. Loosening that handoff (v003's credit, `freq >= 1` graduation) always cost web_0, and stricter admission has never been tested. Right now a single ghost hit sends a one-shot returner straight into main, where it can push out a live resident. Requiring one confirming hit inside small filters out those returners at the cost of one probation pass. Small-demotee hits are the common ghost hits, so this rule fires often, unlike the main-victim path that v003 found too rare.
