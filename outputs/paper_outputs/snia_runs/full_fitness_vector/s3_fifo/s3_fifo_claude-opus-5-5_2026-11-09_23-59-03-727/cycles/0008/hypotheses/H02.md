# H02

Setting a key's freq to 1 instead of 0 when it graduates from small to main gives it one free second chance in main and raises hit rate on the small-cache traces.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, inside `evict_small()`, change only the graduation branch (`freq > 1`). Replace `entry.freq = 0;` with `entry.freq = 1;`. Keep `entry.reused = false;`, so the main-victim ghost gate still counts only reuse inside main. Leave all other paths as they are:
- Ghost-hit admission in `put()` still inserts with `Entry::new` (freq 0).
- The `get()` increment and cap (`MAX_FREQ = 7`) stay the same.
- The decrement-by-one sweep in `evict_main()` stays the same.
- The ghost bound and small share stay the same.

A newly graduated key now survives its first `evict_main` sweep without a hit. The sweep drops it to 0 and reinserts it at the front, so it needs no hit in main to get past one full main rotation. A freshly graduated key can no longer be the immediate victim. Each `evict_main` call still evicts at most one entry. Update the in-file test `graduation_resets_freq_and_reused` to assert `freq == 1` and `!reused` after graduation, and rename it to `graduation_sets_one_credit_and_clears_reused`. `Entry` stays 24 bytes. Update the header comment to say "Cycle 8 H02: graduation grants freq = 1".

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 3 showed that a full carry-over of small-queue freq was worse than resetting to 0. Graduation is still the strongest reuse signal the policy has: two hits inside probation. Granting a single credit sits between those two cases, and nobody has tested it. This lever acts on every graduated key, not only on ghost hits. That matters because ghost-path levers have been too rare to move hm_0 at 16384. The ghost gate is untouched, so graduated keys that are never reused in main still leave without taking a ghost slot.
