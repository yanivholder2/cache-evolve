# H01

Shrinking the small queue from 25% to 10% of capacity raises hit rate on the MSR traces by giving reused keys more main-queue residency while still filtering one-hit wonders.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `S3Fifo::try_new` (src/s3_fifo/mod.rs), replace the small-queue sizing
`(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` with `(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`,
keeping the `capacity == 1 => small_size = 1` special case. `main_size` stays
`capacity - small_size`, so the main queue grows to ~90% of capacity, and the
ghost queue (sized as `main_size`) grows with it. Pre-allocate the `VecDeque`s
with the new sizes.

Do not change any other movement rule: graduation threshold (`tail.freq > 1`
in `evict_small`), ghost re-admission to main, and the second-chance loop in
`evict_main` all stay as-is. Invariants to preserve: `small_size >= 1`,
`small_size + main_size == capacity`, and `small.len() + main.len() <= capacity`
after every `put`.

## Rationale (optional)

The S3-FIFO paper reports 10% as the robust small-queue fraction across block
and web traces; the baseline's 25% spends a quarter of the cache on probationary
entries. At sizes 16384/131072 the 10% small queue is still large enough
(1.6K/13K entries) to observe a second reference before demotion, and the
ghost queue still rescues keys whose reuse distance exceeds the small queue.
This isolates the partition ratio as the single variable.
