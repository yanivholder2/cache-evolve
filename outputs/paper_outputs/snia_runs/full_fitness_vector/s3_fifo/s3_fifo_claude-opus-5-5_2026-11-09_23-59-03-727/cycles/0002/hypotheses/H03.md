# H03

Replacing v002's linear scans over small, main, and ghost with a slab plus hash index cuts per-op-time by turning `get`, the resident check in `put`, and the ghost-membership check from O(capacity) into O(1), while making exactly the same eviction decisions as v002.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

All edits are in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, starting from v002's overlay. The policy stays the same; only the data structures change.

1. **Bounds.** Change the inherent impl to `impl<K: Eq + std::hash::Hash + Clone, V> S3Fifo<K, V>` and add the same bounds to the struct if needed. The `framework::Cache` impl already requires `Hash + Eq`.
2. **Slab storage.** Keep `Entry<K, V> { key, value, freq: u8 }` exactly as it is so `size_of::<Entry<u64,u64>>()` stays 24. Store entries in `slab: Vec<Entry<K, V>>` and recycle freed slots through `free: Vec<usize>`. Change `small` and `main` to `VecDeque<u32>` (or `usize`) holding slab indices.
3. **Resident index.** Add `index: HashMap<K, u32>` mapping each resident key to its slab slot. Small and main can share one map because the code never asks which queue a key is in on a hit.
   - `get`: look up the key in `index`, then bump `slab[i].freq` (saturating, capped at `MAX_FREQ`).
   - `put`: if the key is resident, overwrite the value in place.
   - When a key is evicted to the ghost or dropped, remove it from `index`, push its slot onto `free`, and move the key out of the slab with `std::mem::replace` or `Option`-free swapping, so no `V: Default` bound is needed.
   - Moves between small and main, and second-chance rotations in `evict_main`, only move the `u32` index between deques. The slab slot and the `index` entry stay the same.
4. **Ghost index with lazy deletion.** Change `ghost` to `VecDeque<(K, u64)>` and add `ghost_index: HashMap<K, u64>` plus a counter `ghost_seq: u64`.
   - `push_ghost(key)`: while `ghost_index.len() >= main_size`, pop from the back. If the popped `(k, s)` still matches the live sequence number `ghost_index[k] == s`, remove `k` from `ghost_index` and stop. Otherwise the entry is stale, so discard it and keep popping. Then push `(key, ghost_seq)` to the front, insert it into `ghost_index`, and increment `ghost_seq`.
   - Ghost hit in `put`: `ghost_index.remove(&key).is_some()` replaces the `iter().any` and `retain` scans, and leaves a stale tuple in the deque.
   - To keep stale tuples from accumulating, compact the deque with `retain` against `ghost_index` once `ghost.len() > 2 * main_size`.
5. **Pre-allocation.** In `try_new`, pre-allocate `slab`, `index`, and `ghost_index` with capacity `capacity` / `main_size`.
6. **Tests.** Update the in-file tests that poke at `cache.main` and `cache.small` directly (`main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance`, `main_victim_is_ghosted`, `small_eviction_promotes_to_main_after_multiple_hits`) to go through a small `#[cfg(test)]` helper that inserts an entry into a given queue with a given freq, and to check membership by key. Do not touch anything under `tests/`.

Invariants:
- The sequence of eviction, promotion, and ghost decisions must match v002 exactly. That means the same order and the same live-ghost bound of `main_size`, counted as `ghost_index.len()`. Hit rate should therefore match v002 bit for bit.
- `index.len() == small.len() + main.len() <= capacity`.
- A key appears in at most one of `index` and `ghost_index`.

## Rationale (optional)

All three policies in cycle 1 do a linear `find` over small and main on every `get` and `put`, plus a linear `any` and `retain` over a ghost deque of up to `main_size` keys on every miss. At capacity 131072 this dominates per-op cost. Cycle-1 timing shifts were ruled environmental, so a structural complexity reduction is the cleanest way to win on per-op-time for real. Because decisions are unchanged, the experiment isolates per-op-time: hit rate should equal v002's 0.541575, `Entry` stays 24 bytes, and only the fixed per-cache size of `S3Fifo` grows (two `HashMap`s and a `Vec`).
