# H01

Shrinking the small queue to 1/5 of capacity only when capacity is at most 32768, and keeping 1/4 above that, will raise summary hit rate by lifting hm_0 at 16384 while leaving the saturated 131072 cells unchanged.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Make one change to `S3Fifo::try_new` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`: the small/main split now depends on cache size. Add a constant `SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD: usize = 32768`. When `capacity <= SMALL_CACHE_THRESHOLD`, compute `small_size = (capacity / 5).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Otherwise keep the existing `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Keep the `capacity == 1` special case, and keep `main_size = capacity - small_size`. All reservations that derive from these sizes (`small`, `main`, `ghost` with `2 * main_size + 1`, and `ghost_index` with `main_size`) follow automatically and need no edits.

Leave every other part of the policy exactly as in the parent: `MAX_FREQ = 7`, decrement-by-one aging in `evict_main`, the `freq > 1` graduation threshold, resetting freq and `reused` at graduation, reuse-gated ghosting of main victims, and the ghost live bound of `main_size`. At 16384 the ghost bound grows a little because `main_size` grows. That is part of the experiment and should not be compensated for.

Update the in-file tests to match. Add a unit test checking that `S3Fifo::<u64,u64>::new(16384)` has `small_size == 3276` and that `new(131072)` has `small_size == 32768`. Existing tests use capacities 4, 5 and 8, so their sizes change (for example, 8/5 = 1 instead of 2). Check that each test's assertions still hold, and adjust the setup only where a test depended on the exact small size, without weakening what it asserts.

## Rationale

Cycle 5 v001 used 10% globally. It helped hm_0 at 16384 but hurt web_0 at both sizes. Cycle 6 grew the small share and that also failed. At 131072 every policy change has left hit rate flat, so a global change can only lose there. Applying a milder shrink only at the small size tests the last untested hit-rate lever that cycles 5 and 8 pointed to. If web_0 at 16384 loses more than hm_0 at 16384 gains, treat hit rate in this policy family as converged.
