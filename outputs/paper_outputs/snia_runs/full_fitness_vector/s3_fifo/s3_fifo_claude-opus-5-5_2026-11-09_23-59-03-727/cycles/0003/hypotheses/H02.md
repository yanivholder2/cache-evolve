# H02

Resetting an entry's frequency to zero when it graduates from small to main, with no other change, will show whether the freq reset alone drives the hit-rate change cycle-2 v002 saw on web_0, and is expected to raise the hit-rate summary on hm_0.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Change exactly one line of behaviour in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, in `evict_small()`.

- In the promotion branch (`self.slab[i as usize].freq > 1`), set `self.slab[i as usize].freq = 0` before `self.main.push_front(i)`. Do it after the `evict_main()` call so the victim scan doesn't see the reset entry. The entry is not at main's tail yet in any case, but keep this order.
- Keep the graduation threshold at `freq > 1`. Do not change it to `freq >= 1`; that is what cycle-2 v002 bundled in, and this variant exists to separate the two effects.
- Leave everything else unchanged: main-victim ghosting, the ghost bound, ghost-hit admission to main at `freq = 0`, `MAX_FREQ = 3`, and the slab/hash structure.
- Add an in-module unit test: put key 1 into a capacity-4 cache, `get` it twice, insert enough keys to force a small eviction, then assert that key 1 is in main with `freq == 0`.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle-2 v002 combined "graduate after one hit" with "reset freq on promotion". It won on hm_0 and lost on web_0, and the two causes couldn't be told apart. Today, a graduated entry carries the hits it collected in small (up to 3) into main and survives up to three extra main sweeps for them. The reset makes main residency depend only on reuse *inside* main. If web_0 falls relative to v003 while the threshold stays fixed, the reset is the web_0 culprit. If web_0 holds or improves, the looser threshold was the cause.
