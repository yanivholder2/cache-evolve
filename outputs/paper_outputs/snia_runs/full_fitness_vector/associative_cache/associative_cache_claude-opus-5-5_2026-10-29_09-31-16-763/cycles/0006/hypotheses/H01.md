# H01

Removing the global atomic logical clock from v002's tag-filtered bucket, and skipping timestamp stores entirely on the ClassicLRU path, will lower get and put per-op-time while leaving hit rate and the 24-byte `Item` unchanged.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0005/v002's overlay (the byte-tag pre-filter in
`src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`). Port the clock
removal from cycle/0005/v001 into it:

1. `src/shared/item.rs`: replace v002's `Item::new` with v001's version.
   Delete the process-wide `static LOGICAL_CLOCK` / `AtomicU32::fetch_add`
   and any `tick()` helper. `Item::new` sets `timestamp: 0` and
   `lfu_counter: 0`. Keep the field order `key, value, lfu_counter: u32,
   timestamp: u32` so `Item<u64,u64>` stays 24 bytes, and keep the
   `item_u64_u64_is_24_bytes` test.
2. Eviction policy sources (the ClassicLRU/CLOCK/LFU/etc. implementations
   under `src/shared/`): remove every call to the global tick in
   `get_from_position` or elsewhere. Policies must not write `timestamp`.
3. `AssociativeCacheBucket`: add `clock: u32` and `next_tick()` exactly as
   in v001. **Only when `!self.lip`**, stamp the timestamp
   (`items[index].timestamp = self.next_tick()` in `get_from_position`, and
   `item.timestamp = self.next_tick()` before the push in the non-lip
   `put` branch). The lip/ClassicLRU branches do **no** timestamp writes
   and do not touch `clock`, since ClassicLRU orders by position only.
   Before relying on this, check that no ClassicLRU code reads
   `timestamp`. If one does, stamp from `clock` on that path the way v001
   does, rather than skipping the store.
4. Keep the tag scan (`get_position_tagged`), the tag mirroring on every
   `items` mutation, BIP period 32 and quarter-depth insertion
   byte-for-byte. Keep the existing `tag_tests`.

Invariants: all four hit-rate cells match the parent exactly, the
metadata-size summary stays at 24, and `tags.len() == items.len()` holds
after every public operation.

## Rationale (optional)

v001 and v002 removed separate costs (the shared-line RMW on put, and
full-key compares on the miss scan). This variant stacks the two and
measures the combination against a same-session parent. It also tests
the open question of whether timestamp stores can be skipped when no
active policy reads them.
