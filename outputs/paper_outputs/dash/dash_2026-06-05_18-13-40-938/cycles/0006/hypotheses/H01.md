# H01

Expand stash bucket capacity from 4 to 6 buckets (conserving total cache capacity) to extend the filtering window for tier advancement decisions and improve hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/geometry.rs` in the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` method to increase the stash bucket allocation while reducing normal buckets proportionally to maintain total capacity budget.

**Specific implementation:**
1. Change `num_of_stash_buckets` from 4 to 6 in the DashGeometry returned by `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()`
2. Change `num_of_normal_buckets` from 28 to 26 in the same geometry (conserves capacity: 6 stash + 26 normal = 32 buckets instead of 4 + 28 = 32)
3. All other geometry parameters (bucket_size, num_of_segments) remain unchanged

This keeps the total slot budget constant (32 buckets × 16 slots/bucket = 512 slots) while rebalancing the stash:normal ratio from 4:28 to 6:26.

## Rationale

Cycle 0005's robust 0.684 ceiling suggests the current tier-capacity distribution may constrain filtering effectiveness. The stash is the first line of filtering: items must survive stash occupancy to qualify for tier advancement. With only 4 stash buckets (64 slots), items experience rapid eviction pressure before their access patterns can be accurately characterized. Expanding to 6 stash buckets (96 slots) gives 50% more filtering time while slightly reducing target/probing capacity (from 448 to 416 slots). This tests whether the current cache is bottlenecked by insufficient stash buffering—a purely architectural constraint that insertion-position mechanics and occupancy thresholds cannot address. If the 0.684 ceiling is due to premature tier advancement of under-proven items, expanded stash filtering should improve hit rate.
