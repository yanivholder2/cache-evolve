# H02

Improve hit rate by expanding the stash tier from 4 to 8 buckets to increase the filtering window for characterizing hot items before promotion into the hot tiers.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the geometry in `src/shared/geometry.rs` in the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` method to rebalance bucket allocation:

1. Change `num_of_stash_buckets` from 4 to 8, expanding the stash's capacity for characterizing item access patterns.

2. Reduce `num_of_normal_buckets` from 28 to 24 to maintain the same total capacity (4 stash + 24 normal = same as original 4 + 28, just reallocated). The 24 normal buckets remain split as 12 Target + 12 Probing (matching the original proportion of 14+14 from 28 normal buckets, scaled down).

3. Preserve all other mechanisms from the baseline intact:
   - Three-tier hierarchy (Stash, Target, Probing) with front insertion for promotion
   - Demotion cascade structure (probing → target → stash)
   - LRU eviction within buckets
   - No occupancy-aware routing or alternative promotion paths

This tests whether the 0.684 ceiling is defended by insufficient stash filtering: if items spend too little time in the stash tier to be confidently characterized as hot before promotion, a wider stash window might improve filtering signal. The expanded stash provides more residence time for items to prove their access pattern before entering the competitive hot tiers, potentially reducing false-positive promotions and improving hit rate through better-qualified candidates entering target.
