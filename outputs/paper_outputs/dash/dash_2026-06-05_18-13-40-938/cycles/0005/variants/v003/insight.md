# Variant v003 Insight

## Hurt

- **hit-rate**: Occupancy-based promotion gating delays tier advancement, preventing timely identification and elevation of hot items accessed during high-occupancy periods.

## Takeaway

Gating stash-to-target promotion on occupancy (70% threshold) reduces hit rate below baseline, suggesting that immediate tier progression on stash hit—regardless of current occupancy—is more effective at capturing hot items. The hypothesis predicted that enforcing a filtering discipline through delayed promotion would improve tier decisions, but the data shows that deferring promotion during high occupancy instead suppresses the signal needed to identify genuinely hot items early. The filtering problem may require a different lever than occupancy-based gating.
