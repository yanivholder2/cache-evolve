# H03

Improve hit rate by refining gated tier-skip selectivity to require access_count ≥ 3 (instead of ≥ 2) before permitting stash-to-probing promotion, reducing false-positive hot-tier insertion and improving filtering discipline.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v003

## Changes

Modify the gated tier-skip implementation from v003 to increase selectivity of which stash items qualify for acceleration:

1. In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, locate the tier-skip gating logic in `promotion_get()` where it currently checks `access_count ≥ 2`.

2. Change the gating threshold from `access_count ≥ 2` to `access_count ≥ 3`, raising the bar for which stash items earn direct promotion to the probing tier. This makes the skip mechanism more selective: only items that have been accessed 3+ times in stash (demonstrating sustained interest) are permitted to skip ahead.

3. Preserve all other aspects of v003's design intact:
   - The `skip_history_flag` mechanism for marking skipped items
   - The recovery enforcement where skipped items evicted from probing demote to target (not directly to stash), giving them a second-chance filtering step in target before final stash demotion
   - The demotion-path back insertion (MRU) for demoted items (cycle 0003's proven mechanism)

4. Additionally, ensure demotion-path back insertion is consistently applied to items demoted from both target and probing, reinforcing the proven benefit that demoted (tier-advanced) items deserve MRU ranking for rapid re-promotion eligibility.

This tests whether tighter filtering on tier-skip (requiring access_count ≥ 3 instead of ≥ 2) improves hit rate by reducing the flood of "false positive" stash hits that haven't proven sustained value, while maintaining the architectural insight that recovery-enforced tier-skip can coexist with demotion-back-insertion discipline.
