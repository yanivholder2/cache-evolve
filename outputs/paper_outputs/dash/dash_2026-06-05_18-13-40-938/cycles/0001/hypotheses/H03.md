# H03

Promote stash hits directly to the probing bucket (target+1) instead of routing through target to raise hit rate by fast-tracking proven items to the hot tier.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, modify the `promotion_get` function to skip the target tier for stash hits. Replace the `promote_to_target(core, data, insert_pos)` call (line 157) with `promote_to_probing(core, data, insert_pos)` when a stash hit is found. This bypasses the target tier entirely for stash-resident items, giving them immediate placement in the hot tier. Items subsequently evicted from the probing bucket via this fast-track path will demote to their true target bucket (one-more-chance) by the existing `demote_from_normal_bucket` logic, which recognizes foreign residents and routes them accordingly. The initial stash insertion on cache misses and all other promotion paths (target and probing hits) remain unchanged.
