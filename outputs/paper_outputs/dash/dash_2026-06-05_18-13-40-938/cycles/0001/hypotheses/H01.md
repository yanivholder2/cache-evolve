# H01

Insert stash-promoted items at the back (MRU) position in the target bucket instead of front to raise hit rate by giving proven items immediate eligibility for further promotion.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, modify the `promotion_get` function to call `promote_to_target(core, data, InsertPosition::Back)` instead of `InsertPosition::Front` when handling stash hits (line 157). Stash hits are rare proof of interest; inserting them at the back (MRU position) gives them immediate eligibility to promote to probing on the next access, rather than forcing them to climb the entire target bucket first. Items demoted from probing or target to stash remain inserted at the original position parameter passed down the cascade. The one-more-chance demotion logic and all invariants remain unchanged.
