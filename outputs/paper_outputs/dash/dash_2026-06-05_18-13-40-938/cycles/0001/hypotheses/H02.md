# H02

Use LFU (Least Frequently Used) eviction policy instead of ClassicLRU to raise hit rate on workloads with stable bimodal popularity by protecting high-frequency items.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the cache initialization to use `EvictionPolicyKind::Lfu` instead of `EvictionPolicyKind::ClassicLRU` for all segments. The LFU policy is already implemented in `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` and tracks access frequency via `item.lfu_counter`, incrementing each time `get_from_position` is called. This causes `eviction_candidate_index()` to identify the lowest-counter item for eviction and `promotion_candidate_index()` to identify the highest-counter item for probing eligibility. Items with higher frequencies are protected from eviction at each tier (stash, target, probing). This should improve hit rate on LIRS-style workloads where item popularity is bimodal and stable, making frequency a better predictor of future reuse than recency alone.
