# H02

Test architectural simplification by collapsing the two-tier hot-side (target and probing) into a single unified "hot" tier, eliminating promotion gatekeeping overhead and testing if simpler hierarchy improves hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the tier architecture in `src/shared/geometry.rs`, `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, and `src/dash/segments/segment_core.rs` to implement a two-tier hierarchy (stash + hot) instead of three-tier (stash + target + probing):

1. In `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()`, keep 4 stash buckets but allocate all 28 normal buckets to a single "hot" tier instead of splitting 14 to target and 14 to probing
2. In `Slot` enum definition, remove or merge the `Target` and `Probing` variants into a single `Hot` tier
3. Modify `promotion_get()` in DashSegment to insert hot items directly into the unified hot tier rather than the two-stage target→probing advancement. Items in stash that qualify as hot (strongest candidates) go directly to hot tier, eliminating the intermediate target filtering step
4. Preserve the demotion-back-insertion strategy (from cycle 0003) by demoting items from hot tier to stash with back insertion

This tests whether the two-tier hot-side complexity (requiring items to pass target filtering before probing access) is the architectural bottleneck. If the workload's access pattern can tolerate a simpler structure, removing the promotion gatekeeping may improve throughput and hit rate. The test validates whether tier count is the constraint or whether the current three-tier hierarchy is well-matched to the workload.
