# H01

Use Back-insert promotion to keep hot items at MRU position, raising hit-rate by reducing unnecessary demotion.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, modify the `promotion_get()` function (lines 141–189) to use `InsertPosition::Back` instead of `InsertPosition::Front` when calling `promote_to_target()` and `promote_to_probing()`.

Specifically:
- Line 157: change `promote_to_target(core, data, InsertPosition::Front)` to `promote_to_target(core, data, InsertPosition::Back)`
- Line 170: change `promote_to_probing(core, data, InsertPosition::Front)` to `promote_to_probing(core, data, InsertPosition::Back)`

This makes promoted items land at the back (MRU end) of their destination buckets instead of the front (LRU end). Items no longer need to "earn" their way up the LRU stack before promoting again; they stay closer to MRU and face less eviction pressure while climbing tiers. Keep all demotion logic and cascade behaviour unchanged.

## Rationale

The baseline's Front-insert strategy forces promoted items to re-establish MRU position before they can promote again, adding climb time. Back-insert lets them skip this climb and stay hot longer. On Zipfian traces where most hits are on a small set of keys, this should reduce demotion cascades and keep hot items resident longer.
