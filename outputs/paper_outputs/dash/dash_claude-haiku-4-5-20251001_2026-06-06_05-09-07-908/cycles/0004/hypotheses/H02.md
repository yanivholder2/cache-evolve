# H02

Push bucket allocation to 85/15 geometry to test the asymptote of hash-indexed capacity concentration.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, modify the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` function to allocate buckets even more aggressively toward the normal tier:

- Change the normal bucket allocation to `num_of_normal_buckets = (total_buckets * 17) / 20` (17/20 = 85%)
- Change stash allocation to `num_of_stash_buckets = total_buckets - num_of_normal_buckets`
- Clamp `num_of_normal_buckets` to at least 2 (required for probing) using `.max(2)` to ensure structural validity on small bucket counts

For baseline's total of 32 buckets, this yields 27 normal + 5 stash (85%/15%), pushing further from v003's 75/25 and v002's 62.5/37.5.

Keep `bucket_size` and `segment_size` unchanged. The total capacity remains constant; capacity is reallocated from the stash to the hash-indexed normal tier.

## Rationale

The progression from baseline (25/75) → cycle 0002 v002 (62.5/37.5) → cycle 0003 v003 (75/25) shows sustained improvement across both hit-rate and latency with no saturation observed. Cycle 0002's speculation that 62.5/37.5 might be approaching asymptote was disproven; 75/25 delivered cleaner wins without regression. Pushing further to 85/15 tests whether the asymptote remains open or if diminishing returns finally appear. Simple geometry-only variants consistently outperform complex compositions (e.g., v003 beat gating-based v001 and v002 in cycle 0003), making this pure-geometry extension the most likely to succeed. On Zipfian traces, further reduction of the small stash (from 8 to 5 buckets) should trade acceptable demotions against more reliable cold-miss capture in the larger primary tier.
