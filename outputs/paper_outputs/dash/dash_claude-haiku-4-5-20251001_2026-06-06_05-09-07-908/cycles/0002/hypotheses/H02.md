# H02

Pushing normal bucket allocation further to 20/12 ratio raises hit-rate by maximizing hash-indexed capacity.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, modify the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` function to allocate buckets more aggressively toward the normal tier:

- Change `num_of_normal_buckets = (total_buckets + 1) / 2` to `num_of_normal_buckets = (total_buckets * 5) / 8` (rounding down)
- Change `num_of_stash_buckets = total_buckets / 2` to `num_of_stash_buckets = total_buckets - num_of_normal_buckets`

For baseline's total of 32 buckets, this yields 20 normal + 12 stash (62.5% / 37.5%), up from v002's 50/50 split.

Keep `bucket_size` and `segment_size` unchanged. The total capacity remains constant; capacity is reallocated from the unindexed stash to the hash-indexed normal tier.

## Rationale

v002 proved that wider normal buckets improve hit-rate (from 0.663 to 0.6965) by reducing cold misses that fall through to the stash. The cycle insight asks: is 16/16 the optimal sweet spot, or is there further geometry tuning to explore? This hypothesis tests whether pushing even further—to a 62.5% normal allocation—continues to yield improvements. The tradeoff is a smaller stash (more demotions may fully evict instead of landing in stash), but Zipfian workloads are dominated by hot keys that stay in higher tiers, so stash size may matter less than catching cold misses in the primary tier.
