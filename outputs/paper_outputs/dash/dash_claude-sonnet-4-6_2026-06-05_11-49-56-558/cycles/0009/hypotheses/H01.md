# H01

Pushing the normal/stash split from 7/8 to 15/16 (30 normal + 2 stash buckets) raises hit rate by expanding hot-tier capacity to capture a larger fraction of the Zipfian working set, or reveals the inflection point where stash shallowness begins to cost more than it gains.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, change the normal-bucket computation in `layout()` from:

```rust
let normal = (total_buckets * 7 / 8).max(2);
```

to:

```rust
let normal = (total_buckets * 15 / 16).max(2);
```

Keep the existing clamping guards unchanged: `normal = (...).max(2)` (required for probing) and `stash = (total_buckets - normal).max(1)` (at least one stash slot). For the production configuration (32 total buckets), this yields 30 normal + 2 stash, compared to v001's 28 + 4.

Keep all other overlay files from cycle/0008/v001 unchanged: `ClassicLRUFreqProtect` eviction policy in `eviction_policy.rs`, the `new_bucket` factory override in `dash_bucket.rs`, and the second-chance recovery at `InsertPosition::Front` with `lfu_counter` reset on demotion in `dash_segment.rs`.

## Rationale

Cycle gains from geometry expansion have been positive and monotone (16→24→28 normal buckets), but the absolute lift is shrinking with each step. Two competing forces now dominate: more normal capacity captures a larger slice of the hot working set, but a shallower stash (2 slots) reduces buffering headroom for burst cold arrivals. This test pins down whether the slope is still positive at 30 normal buckets or whether the inflection point falls between 28 and 30.
