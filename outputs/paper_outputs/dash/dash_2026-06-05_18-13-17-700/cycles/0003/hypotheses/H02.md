# H02

Switching the promotion insert position from Front to Back so stash→target and target→probing arrivals land at the MRU end lets hot keys climb the promotion ladder faster and raises hit-rate on the skewed LIRS trace, on top of v001's winning split.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In the overlaid `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, change
`DashSegment::get` to call `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back)`
instead of `InsertPosition::Front`. Per the existing doc on `promotion_get`,
`Back` makes promoted items land at the MRU position of the destination
bucket, so a single subsequent access promotes them to the next tier
(stash → target → probing) rather than forcing them to re-earn MRU from the
LRU end first. Leave the promotion/demotion routing (`promote_to_target`,
`promote_to_probing`, `demote_from_normal_bucket`, the cascade-to-stash path)
and the `is_hottest` promotion gate unchanged — only the insert-position
argument flips. Keep the `put` default (cold inserts still go to
`Slot::Stash`, `InsertPosition::Back`) untouched so initial placement is
unaffected. This builds on v001's inherited 24/8 geometry overlay, isolating
the promotion-position lever as the only behavioural change.

## Rationale (optional)

Promotion position is an untried protected-tier lever the cycle-0002 insight
flagged as open. Faster promotion should get genuinely hot keys into the
recency-protected probing tier sooner on a high-skew trace, while the
front-insert "earn your way up" delay may be costing hits.
