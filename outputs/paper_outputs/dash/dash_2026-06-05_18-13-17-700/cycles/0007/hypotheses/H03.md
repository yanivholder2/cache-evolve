# H03

Extending v002's single relocation hop into a bounded multi-hop cuckoo cascade raises hit-rate by finding room deeper in the stash when the immediate alternate bucket is full.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

v002 gives a stash overflow victim exactly one relocation attempt and drops it
if its alternate bucket is also full — the failure mode the cycle-0006 insight
named. Deepen `stash_insert` in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs` into a
bounded cuckoo cascade:

- After the incoming item lands in its primary bucket and evicts a victim,
  attempt to place the victim into its alternate home. If that bucket is full,
  evict *its* LRU resident and recurse: that newly-displaced resident is in
  turn relocated to its own alternate home, and so on.
- Bound the chain with a fixed depth budget `const RELOCATION_MAX_HOPS: usize
  = 3`. Decrement per hop; when the budget is exhausted, drop the final
  homeless item (baseline drop behaviour). This guarantees termination even
  when keys ping-pong between two homes.
- Each hop relocates a key only into one of its own two legal homes
  (`get_index` or `stash_alt_index`), so `check_slot_legality` continues to
  hold. Exactly one item is carried as "homeless" through the chain and at
  most one is dropped at the end, so total stash occupancy is conserved (no
  capacity inflation).
- When a hop finds an alternate bucket with spare room, place the item there at
  `InsertPosition::Back` and stop early. Keep the `stash_size < 2` fallback to
  baseline single-bucket behaviour.

Preserve all promotion/demotion logic, the get-path two-home lookup, and
`contains_key` from v002 unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The relocation gain was marginal because one hop rescues only a sliver of the
dwell-time headroom: when both a key's homes are full it dies immediately. A
short cascade lets the rescue chase free space a few buckets deep, converting
more would-be drops into surviving cold keys, while the depth cap keeps the
per-op cost bounded and prevents thrash.
