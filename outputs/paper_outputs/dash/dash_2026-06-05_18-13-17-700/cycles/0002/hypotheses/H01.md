# H01

Pushing the normal/stash bucket split past v002's even point toward a normal-tier-dominant 3:1 ratio further raises hit-rate on the skewed LIRS trace.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, modify `BucketGeometry::layout` for
`BaselineGeometry`. v002 currently conserves the total bucket budget and
splits it evenly (`num_of_normal_buckets = total_buckets / 2`). Replace
that even split with a normal-dominant split that gives roughly three
quarters of the bucket budget to the promotion-protected normal tier:

```
let total_buckets = self.topology.num_of_normal_buckets
    + self.topology.num_of_stash_buckets;
let num_of_normal_buckets = (total_buckets * 3 / 4).clamp(2, total_buckets - 1);
let num_of_stash_buckets = total_buckets - num_of_normal_buckets;
```

Keep `num_of_segments` and `bucket_size` exactly as seeded so the budget
identity `segments * (normal + stash) * bucket_size == total_capacity`
still holds and `P6 capacity_conservation` passes. Preserve the existing
clamps (`num_of_normal_buckets >= 2` for probing, `num_of_stash_buckets >= 1`)
so the minimal proptest topology (2 normal / 1 stash) still resolves to a
valid layout — with `total_buckets == 3`, `3*3/4 == 2`, clamped to 2, which
keeps stash at 1. This is the natural continuation of the cycle-0001 sweep:
v002 established that shrinking the over-provisioned stash helps; this
variant probes whether the optimum lies further along the same axis or
whether too small a stash starves admission and regresses.

## Rationale (optional)

The seeded harness split was 8 normal / 24 stash and v002's move to 16/16
gained ~0.018 hit-rate. The cycle insight flags v002's ratio as a single
unswept point; 3:1 toward normal tests the next point on that curve.
