# H03

Re-inserting a probing-evicted key at the MRU end of its target bucket (hot demotion) raises hit-rate by protecting oscillating hot keys that cycle in and out of the probing tier from immediate re-eviction.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the baseline, `demote_from_normal_bucket`
(`src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`) carries the caller's `insert_pos`
through the one-more-chance retry, and on the baseline hit path that value is
`InsertPosition::Front`. So a key that was hot enough to reach probing but then
got evicted re-enters its true target at the *cold* (LRU/eviction) end and is
the very next victim — recently-hot keys that oscillate around the
target↔probing boundary are discarded almost immediately.

Change only the demotion landing: in `demote_from_normal_bucket`, force the
target retry insert to `InsertPosition::Back` (MRU) so a key demoted out of a
normal bucket re-enters its target at the freshest end and gets maximum
protection from immediate re-eviction. Apply the same `Back` landing to the
cascade-to-stash insert in that function. Leave the *promotion* paths
(`promote_to_target`, `promote_to_probing`) and the `get` entry point at the
baseline `InsertPosition::Front`, so this isolates a pure *demotion-recency*
lever and is distinct from v002 (which flipped the whole hit path to `Back`).

Preserve all routing and invariants: the `true_target == source_idx` →
direct-to-stash branch and the foreign-resident retry-into-target branch keep
their current structure, slot legality holds, and capacity is conserved
(geometry untouched). Update the in-file unit tests that pin demotion landing
position (`target_retry_lands_at_front_when_cold`,
`direct_to_stash_at_front_when_cold`, `cascade_to_stash_at_front_when_cold`) to
the new `Back` landing.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003 confirmed the *hit*-path insert position is near-exhausted, but the
*demotion*-path insert position has never been varied independently; on a LIRS
trace the keys that thrash across the probing boundary are exactly the live
working set, so keeping them warm on demotion is a cheap, untested eviction-dynamics lever.
