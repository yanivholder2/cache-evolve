# H02

Admitting new keys directly into their target bucket while using the stash only as a demotion sink raises hit-rate by removing the stash-probation delay that hot keys must otherwise survive.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

The baseline `Segment::put` (default in `src/dash/segments/segment.rs`, used
unchanged by `DashSegment`) routes every freshly inserted key to
`Slot::Stash` at `InsertPosition::Back`, so a hot key must first earn a stash
hit before it is ever promoted into its target bucket. On the high-skew trace,
cross-cycle insight already showed the cold stash was over-provisioned;
this hypothesis tests the extreme of that lever — eliminating stash *admission*
entirely while keeping the stash as the *demotion* overflow area.

Override `put` on `DashSegment` (`src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`) so a new
key is inserted via `core.put_at(Slot::Target, InsertPosition::Front, item)`
instead of into the stash. Keep the cold (`Front`) landing so a one-hit-wonder
still sits at the eviction end of its target and is cheap to evict. Any item
evicted by that target insert must continue to cascade through
`demote_from_normal_bucket` exactly as today, and the stash remains the
destination for demoted items, so the stash still exists and stays populated —
only its role narrows from "admission probation" to "demotion sink."

Preserve invariants: keep the uniqueness `debug_assert!(!core.contains_key(...))`
from the default `put`, keep slot legality (target index derived from the key),
and conserve total capacity (geometry untouched). Update or replace the in-file
unit test `put_goes_to_stash` to assert the new direct-to-target routing.

## Rationale (optional)

Every prior win protected the warm set *after* admission; none questioned
whether the stash-probation step itself costs hits on a trace whose hot keys
recur fast enough that probation just delays their protection. This isolates
the admission-routing lever the continuation flagged as untried.
