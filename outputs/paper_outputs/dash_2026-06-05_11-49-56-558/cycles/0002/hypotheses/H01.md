# H01

Stacking MRU re-entry on top of v003's 50/50 topology will produce an additive hit-rate gain over v003 alone on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, in `DashSegment`'s `Segment::get` implementation, change the single call to `promotion_get` from `InsertPosition::Front` to `InsertPosition::Back`:

```rust
fn get(&mut self, key: &K) -> Option<&Item<K, V>> {
    promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back)
}
```

This is the exact change v001 applied against the baseline; here it is layered onto v003's source instead. No other file needs modification — `geometry.rs` is inherited from v003 (16 normal + 16 stash buckets) and all other logic stays unchanged.

Effect: items promoted from stash into their target bucket land at the MRU end (`Back`) of the bucket's Vec rather than the LRU end (`Front`). A reactivated cold item immediately qualifies for probing-tier promotion on its very next access, collapsing the re-entry penalty from O(bucket-size) accesses to one. Combined with v003's expanded hot-tier, more hot-tier slots are available and the MRU re-entry ensures those slots are filled faster — the cycle-1 insight identified these as orthogonal axes with no known negative interaction.

## Rationale

Cycle-1 insight explicitly flagged combining MRU re-entry (v001) with the 50/50 topology split (v003) as "a natural low-risk compound to test." Neither variant touched the other's axis. The expected additive gain is v001's +1.57 pp on top of v003's 0.6965 baseline.
