# H03

Increasing the lfu_counter credit awarded on stash promotion from 1 to 2 will increase hit rate by giving promoted items enough of a head start in primary to survive the next cold-burst insertion cycle rather than being immediately re-evicted.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`, change every stash-promotion site where `stash_item.lfu_counter += 1` appears to `stash_item.lfu_counter += 2`. There are two such sites:

1. In `put()`, the Case 2 branch (key found in stash):
   ```rust
   // before
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 1;
   // after
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;
   ```

2. In `get()`, the stash-hit promotion branch:
   ```rust
   // before
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 1;
   // after
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;
   ```

No other changes. The `stash_push()`, `evict_to_stash()`, and primary eviction logic are preserved exactly.

## Rationale

A stash item that arrives in primary with `lfu_counter = original + 1` competes directly against items with `lfu_counter ≥ 1` that were never evicted. On the next insertion that requires an eviction, the promoted item — if its counter is only 1 — may be selected as the LFU victim again, sending it back to the stash immediately. This round-trip consumes a stash slot without delivering a hit. A credit of +2 gives the promoted item one extra eviction-cycle of protection, increasing the probability it accumulates a further primary hit before its next eviction. The parent already showed that the stash mechanism is productive; this hypothesis tests whether the credit size is the binding constraint on how long promoted items persist in primary.
