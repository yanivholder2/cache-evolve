# H02

Persistent per-node access frequency counters that survive demotion (halved rather than reset) and a scan of the LRU quarter of the protected segment will improve hit-rate over cycle/0002/v003 by demoting genuinely infrequent protected entries rather than strictly the oldest one.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

cycle/0002/v003's frequency-aware demotion attempt (cycle/0002/v002) failed for two reasons: a 4-node scan window covering a negligible fraction of the protected segment, and counters reset to zero on every demotion. This variant fixes both in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`:

1. **Add `freq: u32` to `Node<K, V>`** — initialized to `0` whenever a slot is allocated or reused in `put()`.

2. **Increment `freq` on every cache hit** — in `get()`, after resolving `idx` from the map, add `self.nodes[idx].freq = self.nodes[idx].freq.saturating_add(1)` before the segment-specific movement code. Apply this to both protected and probationary hits.

3. **Replace `demote_prot_head()` with a minimum-frequency scan of the LRU quarter**:
   - Compute `scan_len = (self.prot_count / 4).max(1)`.
   - Walk forward from `prot_head` for `scan_len` nodes, collecting `(arena_index, freq)` pairs.
   - Select the node with the lowest `freq` among the scanned nodes (tie-break: take the first encountered, i.e. the LRU among ties).
   - Unlink that node from the protected list and demote it to probationary MRU; decrement `prot_count`, increment `prob_count`, update `segment` to `Segment::Probationary`.
   - After demotion, **halve** the evicted node's counter: `self.nodes[victim_idx].freq /= 2`. This preserves relative ordering of accumulated signal across demotion cycles rather than wiping it.

4. **Do not change any other logic** — ghost admission, the 80/20 split, probationary eviction into the ghost set, and all other paths remain identical to cycle/0002/v003.

The scan is bounded to `prot_count / 4` nodes at most, so the worst-case demotion cost is O(capacity/4) rather than O(1). This is acceptable for a hit-rate evaluation; throughput can be measured separately if this variant wins.

## Rationale

cycle/0002/v002's failure was diagnosed as a signal-design flaw, not evidence that frequency is uninformative inside the protected segment. A scan window of 4 nodes over a 1638-slot protected segment (at capacity=2048) observes less than 0.25% of candidates — effectively the same victim as strict LRU. The LRU quarter (about 410 nodes) is large enough to find genuine low-frequency entries without scanning the entire segment. Halving counters on demotion keeps durable signal alive: a node that accumulated freq=20 over two residency periods carries freq=10 into its next evaluation, clearly distinguishable from a newly promoted node with freq=0.
