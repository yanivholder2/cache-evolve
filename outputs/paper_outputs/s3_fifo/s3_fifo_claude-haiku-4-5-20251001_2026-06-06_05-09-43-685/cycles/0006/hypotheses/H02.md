# H02

Implement multi-level ghost-queue frequency decay to preserve re-access candidates while avoiding inflated admission signals.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, enhance ghost-queue management with frequency tracking and stricter decay semantics:

1. Change the `ghost` field type from `VecDeque<K>` to `VecDeque<(K, i8)>` to track frequency values (including negative decay states).

2. In `evict_main()`, when moving the evicted tail item to ghost:
   - Decrement the item's frequency: `ghost_freq = tail.freq.saturating_sub(1)` (preserves frequency-1)
   - Only push to ghost if `ghost_freq >= -2` (allowing decay down to -2 before final discard)
   - Items at freq=0 enter ghost with freq=-1, items at freq=1 enter with freq=0, etc.
   - This creates a three-level decay: original freq → -1 → -2 → discard

3. In `put()`, when a ghost hit occurs:
   - Extract both key and preserved frequency from the ghost entry
   - Re-admit to main queue with `max(0, ghost_freq + 3)` (boost by +3 to account for decay levels traversed)
   - Clamp to `[0, MAX_FREQ]`
   - This reflects the observation that an item with ghost_freq=-2 was once freq=2, and should recover that signal

4. Keep main-queue eviction logic as-is (second-chance pass with frequency decrement).

5. Use fixed promotion threshold `freq > 2` in `evict_small()` (no occupancy-responsive adaptation).

This tests the explicitly flagged open question from cycle 0005: whether stricter decay semantics (to -2 or -3) can preserve re-access opportunities without corrupting admission signals. The boost-on-re-admission decays alongside the ghost retention time, giving items a fair chance to be re-accessed before expiration.

## Rationale

Cycle 0005 v001 and v003 failed because preserving frequency in ghost without decay discipline leads to inflated hotness signals that corrupt main-queue decisions. The insight is that decay should be tighter (to -2 instead of discarding immediately at 0) while re-admission boost should reflect the decay trajectory. By allowing items to decay through -1 to -2 levels, we preserve warm-but-not-hot items that have one or two accesses, while the boost formula `ghost_freq + 3` correctly reconstructs their expected hotness. Expected outcome: improved hit-rate (0.720+) by retaining re-accessible candidates, with manageable latency overhead since ghost queue frequency tracking is read-only post-eviction.
