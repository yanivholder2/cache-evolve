# H02

Increase v001's small-queue capacity from 1/4 to 3/10 of total capacity to provide longer frequency-accumulation window while maintaining strict freq > 2 promotion threshold, recovering hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, modify the `try_new()` function to expand the small-queue size. Change the small queue sizing calculation from `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `((capacity * 3) / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Keep `evict_small()` with freq > 2 threshold and all other logic unchanged from v001.

## Rationale

v001 achieves best-in-class latency (138ns) with conservative 1/4 queue split. v003 demonstrates that larger small queue (1/3) helps hit-rate reach 0.7112 but costs latency (177ns). A middle ground at 3/10 (30% vs v001's 25% and v003's 33%) balances the benefits: items get longer to accumulate frequency under v001's strict threshold before small-queue eviction, improving hit-rate on items with sparse-but-genuine access patterns. The latency impact should be less than v003 since we retain v001's stricter admission filter.
