# H02

Route ghost hits through small queue with strict freq > 2 promotion threshold to combine proven tight admission filtering with ghost re-routing heuristic.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, implement a compound variation:

1. Change the promotion threshold in `evict_small()` from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 2` (the strict admission filter proven effective in cycle/0001/v001 and cycle/0002/v001).

2. Modify the ghost-hit path in `put()`: instead of directly promoting ghost keys to main queue, route them back through the small queue. When `in_ghost` is true:
   - Remove the key from ghost queue
   - Check if small queue is full and call `evict_small()` if needed
   - Push the entry to small queue with `freq = 0` (as if re-discovered)
   This forces ghost hits to re-establish hotness in small queue before graduating to main.

## Rationale

Cycle 0001 tested ghost re-routing (v002) with baseline loose threshold (freq > 1), achieving only modest results. Simultaneously, cycle/0002/v001 proved that freq > 2 threshold delivers superior latency and hit-rate stability. This hypothesis tests the compound variation: whether tight admission threshold (freq > 2) combined with ghost re-routing recovers hit-rate without abandoning the latency gains. The tight threshold prevents marginal re-admitted items from flooding main queue, while the small-queue re-route gives warm ghosts a second chance to prove hotness. Expected outcome: better hit-rate than baseline's loose filter while maintaining latency performance of v001-class variants.
