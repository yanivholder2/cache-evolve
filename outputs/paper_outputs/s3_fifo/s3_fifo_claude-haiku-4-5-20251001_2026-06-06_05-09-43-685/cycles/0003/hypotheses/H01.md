# H01

Separate warm-ghost tracking to preserve frequency signals and recover hit-rate on items evicted from main queue with moderate hotness.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, implement a two-tier ghost queue:

1. Add a new field `warm_ghost: VecDeque<Entry<K, V>>` to the `S3Fifo` struct to track items evicted from main queue with `freq >= 1` (along with their frequency value), separate from the existing cold-ghost queue (which tracks only keys from small-queue evictions).

2. Modify `evict_main()` to push items with `freq > 0` to `warm_ghost` instead of discarding them (currently items with freq > 0 are re-inserted to front with decremented frequency; instead, move them to warm_ghost with preserved frequency).

3. Modify `put()` to check `warm_ghost` first before cold `ghost`. If key is in warm_ghost, remove it and restore directly to main queue with its preserved frequency (no eviction needed if space), bypassing the small-queue route.

4. Maintain warm_ghost size at `main_size` (same as cold ghost). When warm_ghost overflows, evict oldest entries to cold ghost.

## Rationale

v003 achieves latency through lazy eviction while maintaining hit-rate. The current design loses frequency information when items evict from main: they drop into the undifferentiated ghost queue and lose signal. By tracking warm-ghost items (evicted with freq > 1) separately with preserved frequency, re-access to these items skips the small-queue validation step and restores hot items directly. This implements the "ghost tracking" heuristic from cross-cycle insights while preserving v003's lazy-eviction latency benefit (no new eviction calls). Hypothesis: warm ghosts recover hit-rate on items that were legitimately hot but temporarily displaced by access spikes, without impacting tail latency.
