# H01

Shrinking the small queue from ~7% (capacity/14) to ~5% (capacity/20) continues the monotone transfer of resident slots to main, probing whether the one-time-access filter remains effective at this tighter allocation and whether the sizing floor extends below the level confirmed in cycle 0008.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the small-queue sizing formula from `capacity / 14` to `capacity / 20`:

```rust
let small_size = if capacity == 1 {
    1
} else {
    (capacity / 20).max(1).min(capacity - 1)
};
```

All other logic stays identical to cycle/0008/v002 — eager promotion at freq >= 2, `freq > 1` eviction threshold in `evict_main()`, ghost cap at `main_size / 2`, MAX_FREQ = 5, and unconditional `push_front` for new small-queue entries. Update the comment block at the top of the file to reflect the new small-queue divisor.

At capacity 2048 this reduces small from ~146 slots to ~102 slots, transferring ~44 additional resident slots to main. The filter must still screen enough one-time-access traffic through this narrower window; if it becomes too coarse and cold items begin polluting main directly, hit rate will plateau or regress, locating the floor experimentally.
