# H01

Reducing the ghost cap from `main_size/2` to `main_size/4` on top of v003's configuration sharpens re-access signal precision further and raises hit rate by discarding the cold outer half of the ghost window that v003 still tracks.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, inside `try_new()`, change the ghost size calculation from:

```rust
let ghost_size = (main_size / 2).max(1);
```

to:

```rust
let ghost_size = (main_size / 4).max(1);
```

For a 2048-slot cache, v003's main_size is ~1844, so ghost_size drops from ~922 keys to ~461 keys. The `ghost_size` field already exists in the struct from v003's overlay and is already used in `evict_small()` as the ghost overflow guard — no other edits are needed. All other v003 settings are preserved exactly: `small_size = capacity / 10`, the ghost overflow guard `if self.ghost.len() >= self.ghost_size`, promotion threshold `freq > 1`, and `MAX_FREQ = 3`.

## Rationale

Cycle 0002's insight identified ghost truncation to `main_size/4` as "the most pressing next probe." v002 and v003 both confirmed that halving the ghost cap improves hit rate by eliminating eviction history too old to carry re-use signal. With v003's enlarged main queue (~1844 slots), items linger longer before eviction — meaning newly evicted keys are colder on average than in the baseline, and the outer half of the current ghost window (positions 461–922) tracks items with even lower re-visit probability. Cutting to main_size/4 tests whether the re-use distance for workload-a fits within 461 evictions, concentrating ghost membership only on the most recently displaced keys.
