# H01

Reducing the small queue from 10% to ~8% of capacity (`capacity / 12`) further enlarges the main queue's resident footprint and raises hit rate beyond cycle/0001/v001.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

In `try_new()` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change the `small_size` calculation from

```rust
(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)
```

to

```rust
(capacity / 12).max(1).min(capacity - 1)
```

For a 2048-slot cache this gives `small_size = 170` (vs 204 in v001), adding 34 more resident slots to main (1878 vs 1844). The `main_size` calculation (`capacity - small_size`) is unchanged, so the ghost queue capacity grows correspondingly. All other logic — the promotion threshold (`freq > 1`), `MAX_FREQ`, `evict_main`, `evict_small`, and the ghost-hit re-admission path — remains identical to v001.

## Rationale

The cycle-0001 insight explicitly left open whether 10% is the floor or whether 8% continues the upward trajectory. This hypothesis takes one calibrated step downward from v001's winning ratio, keeping the small queue large enough to absorb cold bursts (170 slots at capacity=2048) while reclaiming 34 more slots for the main queue's second-chance retention pool. If the hit-rate improvement from 25%→10% was proportional to the main-queue capacity gained, a further 34-slot gain should yield a proportional — if smaller — improvement.
