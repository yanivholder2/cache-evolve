# H02

Combine v001's structural selectivity (1/6 small-queue admission) with frequency-depth increase (MAX_FREQ 3→4) to test whether the two orthogonal improvements compound synergistically.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Modify two constants in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. First, the `try_new()` method already computes `small_size` as `(capacity / 6)...` from v001 — keep this unchanged. Second, change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` at the top of the file to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 4;`. Cycle 0001 v001 discovered that increasing MAX_FREQ from 3 to 4 improves hit-rate (0.7177), and Cycle 0003 v003 confirmed the same gain holds with 1/5 small-queue sizing. This hypothesis tests whether v001's tighter 1/6 structural selectivity and MAX_FREQ=4 frequency depth represent genuinely complementary axes that produce larger combined improvement than either alone. Items in the main queue gain one additional eviction round before permanent discard, potentially better protecting moderately-reused items that the tighter admission gate allowed through.
