# H01

Increase the frequency depth from 4 to 5 to test whether deeper frequency levels continue improving hit rate on sustained reuse patterns, exploring the point of diminishing returns.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant at the top of `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 4;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 5;`. This extends the maximum frequency counter by one level, allowing items to survive up to 5 eviction rounds in the main queue before permanent discarding. Since v001 demonstrated that increasing MAX_FREQ from 3 to 4 improves hit rate, this change tests whether the frequency-depth dimension continues to yield benefits. Items in the main queue with freq > 0 are decremented and re-inserted; with MAX_FREQ=5, they persist longer, potentially capturing more value from sustained access patterns. Track whether this improvement continues linearly or hits diminishing returns.
