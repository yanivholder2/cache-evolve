## Changes

Modify both constants in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`: change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 7;` and adjust the small-queue sizing computation from `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 12).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. This pairs cycle/0006/v003's proven 1/12 structural selectivity with an aggressive frequency depth (MAX_FREQ=7) that is more than double the baseline. All other eviction mechanics unchanged: frequency increment capped at MAX_FREQ=7 on access, freq > 1 promotion threshold, main-queue second-chance eviction with decrement.

## Rationale

Cycles 0002-0008 have revealed a discontinuous optimization surface: early cycles showed structural selectivity (1/4→1/12) as the dominant axis, but Cycles 0007-0008 showed the inflection point where frequency-depth becomes competitive at the saturation boundary (1/12-1/14). The current winner (v003: 1/14+MAX_FREQ=5) pursues maximum structural aggressiveness. However, the open question asks whether a peak exists or compounding scales monotonically. By testing a less-extreme structural ratio (1/12, which was the proven sweet spot in Cycles 0006-0007) paired with much deeper frequency protection (MAX_FREQ=7 versus the baseline 3), this hypothesis explores whether the optimization peak might lie at a different point—potentially one where items have longer tenure to earn protection rather than needing to pass an ultra-tight admission gate. This tests whether the structure-frequency trade-off surface is unimodal or contains multiple local optima.
