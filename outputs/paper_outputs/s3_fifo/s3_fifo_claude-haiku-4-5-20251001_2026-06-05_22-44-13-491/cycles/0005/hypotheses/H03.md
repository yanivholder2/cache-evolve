# H03

Combine v003's aggressive small-queue reduction (1/8) with frequency-depth increase (MAX_FREQ 3→5) to test whether deeper frequency protection compounds synergistically at the tighter admission boundary.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Modify two constants in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. First, the `try_new()` method already computes `small_size` as `(capacity / 8)...` from v003 — keep this unchanged. Second, change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` at the top of the file to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 5;`. Cycle 0001 discovered that MAX_FREQ=4 improves hit-rate over the baseline 3; Cycle 0002 showed marginal gains from 4→5. However, Cycle 0004 v002 tested MAX_FREQ=4 with only 1/6 small-queue sizing and underperformed v003's pure 1/8 structural selectivity. This hypothesis tests whether deeper frequency protection (MAX_FREQ=5) provides larger compounding gains when paired with v003's tighter 1/8 admission gate. Items in the main queue gain one additional eviction opportunity before permanent discard, potentially better protecting moderately-reused items that the tighter admission gate filtered through. This orthogonal test clarifies whether frequency-depth becomes more valuable as the small-queue selectivity increases.
