# H01

Reduce small-queue capacity from 1/8 to 1/9 to incrementally map the saturation boundary and test whether hit-rate gains persist beyond v003's aggressive reduction.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small-queue admission size. Change the line `(capacity / 8).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 9).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This narrows the small queue from 1/8 to 1/9 of total capacity. Cycle 0004 v003 demonstrated that 1/8 continues the monotonic trend of hit-rate improvement; this incremental step tests whether the saturation boundary lies beyond 1/8 or approaches at 1/9. The hypothesis provides fine-grained saturation discovery by exploring the next step in the reduction axis without the aggressive jump that 1/10 would represent.
