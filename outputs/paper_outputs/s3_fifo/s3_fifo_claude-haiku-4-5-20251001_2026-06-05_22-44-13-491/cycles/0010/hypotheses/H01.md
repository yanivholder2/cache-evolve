# H01

Push extreme structural selectivity to 1/16 while maintaining the frequency protection at MAX_FREQ=5 to determine whether the structural axis extends beyond v001's 1/15 frontier or has reached the saturation cliff.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce small-queue capacity from the current 1/15 ratio to 1/16. Change the line `(capacity / 15).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 16).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. Keep `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 5;` unchanged. All other eviction logic remains identical: frequency increment on access, freq > 1 threshold for small-to-main promotion, main-queue second-chance eviction with frequency decrement.

## Rationale

Cycle 0009's v001 demonstrated that pushing structural selectivity to 1/15 still yields measurable gains (+0.0007 over cycle/0008/v003), yet the magnitude is the smallest across the last three cycles, signaling diminishing returns. The cycle insight directly asks: "Has structural selectivity finally hit its ceiling, or does 1/16–1/20 remain viable?" This hypothesis aggressively tests the structural frontier one step further while preserving the proven MAX_FREQ=5 frequency protection. If 1/16 continues the pattern, it indicates the saturation cliff lies even tighter; if performance degrades, it pinpoints the boundary and confirms 1/15 as the true structural optimum.
