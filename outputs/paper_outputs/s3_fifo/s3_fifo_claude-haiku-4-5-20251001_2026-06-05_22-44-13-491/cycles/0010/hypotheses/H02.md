Deepen frequency protection to MAX_FREQ=7 while preserving the proven 1/14 structural selectivity at the saturation boundary to test whether extended main-queue retention compounds beyond v002's MAX_FREQ=6 gains or plateaus.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 6;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 7;`. Keep the small-queue sizing at `(capacity / 14).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` unchanged, maintaining the proven 1/14 structural selectivity. All other logic identical: frequency increments on access up to the new MAX_FREQ=7 ceiling, freq > 1 threshold for small-to-main promotion, main-queue eviction with frequency decrement.

## Rationale

Cycle 0009's v002 extended MAX_FREQ from 5 to 6 at the proven 1/14 structural selectivity and achieved a +0.0003 gain—the smallest improvement magnitude in the cycle, suggesting frequency-depth leverage is plateauing. The cycle insight notes: "frequency-depth leverage plateaus at diminishing return magnitudes... items passing the extreme 1/14 gate are so carefully curated that further extending their tenure yields marginal benefit. The axis remains productive but at a steeply declining slope." This hypothesis tests whether the frequency dimension has truly hit saturation or if one additional level (MAX_FREQ=7, allowing items one more eviction round) can still unlock value. If it yields measurable gain, frequency remains productive; if marginal or negative, it confirms the frequency saturation boundary lies between MAX_FREQ=6 and MAX_FREQ=7.
