## Takeaway

The dynamic promotion threshold adjustment had no measurable impact on hit rate. The change made small-queue-to-main promotion more selective when main queue exceeded 80% capacity (requiring frequency ≥ 3 instead of ≥ 2), but this filtering strategy neither improved nor degraded performance on workload-a. This suggests that either the workload's access patterns rarely trigger sustained main queue congestion at the 80% threshold, or the increased selectivity filters out some useful items whose benefit cancels the gains from removing cold items.
