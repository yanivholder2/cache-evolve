This skips incremental steps beyond 1/12 to quickly test whether utilization collapse occurs at even tighter selectivity or whether the workload continues to reward extreme admission selectivity.

## Rationale

Seven consecutive cycles (baseline through cycle 0006) have shown consistent improvements from 1/4 down to 1/12 with zero degradation, an exceptionally long monotonic trend. The cycle 0006 insight raises the critical question: "Where is the true saturation boundary?" and notes that "the absence of degradation at 1/12 is consistent with prior cycles and warrants further aggressive probing." This hypothesis takes the aggressive path from baseline by jumping two steps beyond v003's 1/12 to 1/14, rapidly determining whether saturation exists between 1/12 and 1/14 or whether the boundary remains even tighter. If 1/14 still improves or maintains performance, the boundary persists further out and future cycles should continue aggressive reduction; if it degrades, saturation is constrained and fine-grained boundary mapping should resume.
