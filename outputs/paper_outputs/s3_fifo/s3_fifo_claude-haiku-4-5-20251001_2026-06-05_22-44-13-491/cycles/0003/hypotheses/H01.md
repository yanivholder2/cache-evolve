# H01

Reduce small-queue capacity from 1/5 to 1/6 of total capacity to further tighten the admission selectivity gate and locate the saturation point for hit-rate gains.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small-queue admission size. Change the line `(capacity / 5).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 6).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This narrows the small queue from 1/5 to 1/6 of total capacity, forcing only items with the highest early-access frequencies to earn promotion to the main queue. The tighter admission gate continues v003's winning structural-selectivity strategy and tests whether the hit-rate gains persist below 1/5 or approach a utilization cliff where the queue becomes too constrained to absorb working-set diversity.
