# H03

Increase MAX_FREQ from 3 to 4 while retaining the 1/5 small-queue sizing from v003 to test whether frequency-depth and structural-selectivity axes are complementary, combining gains from cycles 0001 and 0002.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant at the top of `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` in the already-tuned v003 variant. Change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 4;`. This restores the frequency-depth benefit that cycle 0001 v001 found (3→4 delivered a 0.7177 hit-rate) while keeping v003's successful 1/5 small-queue sizing. The hypothesis tests whether the structural selectivity (1/5 admission gate) and frequency depth (MAX_FREQ=4) represent orthogonal improvements that compound when combined fresh, rather than being substitutable axes. Items in the main queue gain one additional eviction round before permanent discard, potentially better protecting moderately-reused items that the tighter admission gate allowed through.
