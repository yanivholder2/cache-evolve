## Changes

Modify the `evict_small()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to tighten the frequency-selectivity requirement for items graduating from the small queue to the main queue. Change the condition `if tail.freq > 1 {` to `if tail.freq > 2 {` on line 113 (or the equivalent line in the evict_small implementation). This requires items to accumulate at least frequency 3 (three accesses while in small queue) before promotion to main, rather than the current threshold of frequency 2. The stricter selectivity at the promotion gate exploits the fact that v003's already-tight 1/5 small-queue constraint may allow us to be more selective about which items graduate, forcing only those with strong within-small-queue reuse evidence to reach the main queue, potentially improving the overall frequency balance.
