# H01

Setting the ghost queue to half of main on the best incumbent banks most of v003's put-latency win while recovering the near-reuse hits lost at the 25% setting, improving per-op-time without regressing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

In `evict_small()`, change the ghost-trim threshold from the current
`if self.ghost.len() >= self.main_size` to
`if self.ghost.len() >= (self.main_size / 2).max(1)`. This caps the ghost
queue at 50% of main (vs. 100% in v001 and 25% in cycle/0003/v003),
halving the linear `self.ghost.iter().any(...)` membership scan that
dominates `put` latency while keeping a longer reuse-distance window than
v003's quarter-length ghost. Leave the `with_capacity(main_size)`
allocation, the promotion logic, and `MAX_FREQ` untouched. The goal is to
land on the latency/hit-rate frontier the cycle-0003 ghost-shrink exposed:
this directly tests whether the near-reuse hits dropped at 25% live in the
25–50% band of recent evictions.

## Rationale (optional)

v001 holds the best hit-rate (0.7284) but the slowest put path (p50
494ns); v003 cut put p50 to 241ns at 25% ghost but lost hit-rate to
0.7257. The 50% point probes whether the frontier has a knee that keeps
v001's hit-rate while banking part of v003's latency win.
