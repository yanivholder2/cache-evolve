# H02

Stacking the raised frequency-saturation ceiling onto the narrowest proven split combines the two confirmed-orthogonal hit-rate levers at their individual best settings, lifting hit-rate above either alone.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Raise the saturation ceiling from `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3` to
`const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 7`, keeping v001's `capacity / 32` split and
full-length ghost intact. v001 established the narrowest paying split
(`capacity/32`) at `MAX_FREQ=3`, while cycle/0003/v002 raised the ceiling
to 7 but only on the wider `capacity/16` split. The combination
(`capacity/32` + `MAX_FREQ=7`) has never been measured. Because the split
(how-many-fit) and the freq ceiling (how-long-a-proven-key-survives) are
confirmed orthogonal axes, deeper durability for hot keys should compound
with the extra main capacity. Preserve the `get()` saturation clamp
(`.min(MAX_FREQ)`) and the `evict_main` second-chance decrement exactly as
they are; only the constant changes.

## Rationale (optional)

Both levers are deep in diminishing returns individually, but they have
never been combined at their respective best points. This is the last
untested cell in the split×freq-ceiling grid and isolates whether the
remaining marginal gains are additive or already saturated.
