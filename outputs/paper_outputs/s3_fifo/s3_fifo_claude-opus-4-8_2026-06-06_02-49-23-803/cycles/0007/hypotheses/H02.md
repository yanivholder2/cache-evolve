# H02

Cluster proven-hot keys at the front of `main` by moving an entry to the head on freq saturation, shortening the median scan-to-hit and lowering get-p50 (per-op-time).

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

v002 already scans `main` before `small` in `get`, so get-p50 is the median
distance from the head of `main` to the hit. On a Zipfian head the median get
lands on a small set of hot keys, but FIFO ordering leaves those keys scattered
through `main`, so the scan is longer than it needs to be. Add an in-place,
no-extra-container reorder that front-loads hot keys:

- In `get` (and only there), after bumping `freq`, detect the saturation
  transition: when an entry's `freq` reaches `MAX_FREQ` *and the entry lives in
  `main`*, relocate that entry to the front of `main` (find its current index,
  `main.remove(idx)`, `main.push_front(entry)`). Do nothing for entries already
  at/near the front or living in `small`.
- Trigger only on the transition into `MAX_FREQ` (i.e. when the pre-increment
  freq was `MAX_FREQ - 1`), not on every hit, so the O(position) move is rare
  and amortised — repeated hits on an already-saturated, already-front key cost
  nothing extra.
- Leave `small`, `ghost`, `evict_small`, `evict_main`, capacities, and the
  main-before-small scan order exactly as in v002. Eviction still removes from
  the back of `main`; clustering hot keys at the front simply means the
  second-chance loop reaches them later, which matches the keys S3-FIFO already
  protects, so hit-rate should stay ~0.7312.

Preserve `Entry` at 24 bytes (no field added) and the `small.len() +
main.len() <= capacity` invariant.

## Rationale (optional)

Distinct from H01's hash index: this keeps the linear scan but makes the median
hit shallow by physically migrating the Zipfian-hot keys to the head, paying an
O(n) move only at the rare freq-saturation transition. No new container, so
metadata-size is untouched and only get-p50/hit-rate are in play.
