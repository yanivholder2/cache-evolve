# H03

Widen the ghost cap from 1/2 to 5/8 of main to accommodate v003's doubled (dual-source) ghost inflow and recover near-reuse hits, lifting hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, the ghost cap is computed twice as
`(self.main_size / 2).max(1)` — once in `evict_main` and once in
`evict_small`. The 50%-of-main length was established as the knee in cycle
0004, but that sweep was done on a ghost fed by a **single** source
(small-queue demotions only). Variant v003 then added a **second** inflow —
freq=0 main victims via `evict_main` — roughly doubling the rate at which
keys enter the ghost. At a fixed 50% length, the two sources now compete for
the same slots, so each key's residence time in the ghost is shorter than
when the knee was measured, potentially evicting small-demotion keys before
their near-reuse window elapses. Test a modestly longer window matched to
the higher inflow:

- Change both occurrences of `(self.main_size / 2).max(1)` to
  `((self.main_size * 5) / 8).max(1)`.

**Invariants to preserve:** keep both ghost sources (small demotions and
main victims), freq=0 re-admission, the capacity/32 split, and MAX_FREQ=3
unchanged — this hypothesis isolates the ghost *length* knee under the new
dual-source content regime. Do not add struct or `Entry` state; metadata-size
must stay 24. Expect a possible small put-latency cost from the longer
membership scan; the test is whether the recovered near-reuse hits outweigh it.

## Rationale (optional)

The 50% knee predates dual-source ghost content. Re-finding the length knee
for the v003 inflow regime is the one untested cell on the ghost-content
frontier the cross-cycle notes flagged as still live; 5/8 is a single
conservative step past 1/2, short of the 100% window already shown to over-scan.
