# H01

Promote small-queue entries to main on their first reuse (freq >= 1) instead of requiring two reuses, raising hit-rate by retaining proven items.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `evict_small()` (src/s3_fifo/mod.rs), change the promotion test from
`if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 0`. This matches the canonical
S3-FIFO rule from the paper: a small-queue entry that was touched at
least once while resident graduates into the main queue rather than
being demoted to the ghost queue. Leave the ghost-insertion branch
(including the `ghost.len() >= main_size` cap and `pop_back`) unchanged,
and leave `evict_main()` untouched. The capacity bound is preserved
because the `self.main.len() >= self.main_size` eviction check before
`push_front` already runs in this branch. The hypothesis is that the
baseline's stricter `freq > 1` threshold discards too many genuinely
reused entries on workload-a; loosening it to first-reuse should lift
hit-rate without changing per-op cost or metadata size.
