# H01

Narrowing the small/main split one step further, from `capacity / 16` to `capacity / 32`, raises hit-rate on workload-a by moving still more capacity into the reuse-proven main queue.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `S3Fifo::try_new`, change the small-queue sizing expression from
`(capacity / 16).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to
`(capacity / 32).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Leave `main_size = capacity - small_size`
and every other field untouched. This is a pure integer-knob change to
`small_size`/`main_size`; do not add struct fields (keep metadata-size at 24)
and do not alter the `evict_small` promotion test (`freq > 1`) or the ghost
re-admission path. The point is to probe whether the monotone split sweep has
passed its knee: at `capacity / 32` the small admission filter holds ~64 slots
at capacity 2048, and the test is whether it still screens one-hit-wonders or
finally under-screens them and regresses hit-rate.

## Rationale (optional)

v001 (`capacity / 16`) is the current best and the cross-cycle narrative flags
the split knee as the key open question — do not assume monotonicity. This is
the cheapest, most direct probe of where the filter stops paying off.
