# H01

Replace the linear ghost membership scan on `put` with an O(1) ghost hash-set plus lazy deletion to lower put p50/p99 (per-op-time).

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

The v001 incumbent already made `get` O(1) via the `entries` hash index, but
`put` still pays the last linear scan on the hot path: in `put()`,
`self.ghost.iter().any(|ghost_key| ghost_key == &key)` (membership test) and
the subsequent `self.ghost.retain(|ghost_key| ghost_key != &key)` (removal) are
both O(ghost_len) over a queue sized at ~main_size/2. This is the put-p50 floor
(put p50 = 297ns vs get p50 = 23ns) that the cross-cycle notes attribute to "the
ghost membership scan on put."

Add a `ghost_set: HashSet<K, FastBuildHasher>` field to `S3Fifo` alongside the
existing `ghost: VecDeque<K>` (which is retained purely for FIFO eviction order).
Construct it in `try_new` with `HashSet::with_capacity_and_hasher(main_size,
FastBuildHasher::default())`, mirroring the existing `entries` construction.

Rewrite the ghost handling so membership and removal are O(1):
- In `put()`, replace the `ghost.iter().any(...)` test with
  `self.ghost_set.contains(&key)`.
- On a ghost hit, instead of `ghost.retain(...)`, do `self.ghost_set.remove(&key)`
  (O(1)) and use **lazy deletion** for the order queue: leave the key in the
  `ghost` VecDeque as a tombstone and skip it later.
- When evicting (in `evict_main`/`evict_small`), where a victim key is pushed into
  the ghost queue, also `self.ghost_set.insert(tail_key.clone())`. When trimming
  the ghost queue (`self.ghost.pop_back()`), pop until you find a key still present
  in `ghost_set` (skip tombstones), and `ghost_set.remove` whatever you discard.

Invariants to preserve exactly: the ghost capacity bound `(main_size/2).max(1)`
must be enforced against *live* ghost entries (count tracked by `ghost_set.len()`,
not the tombstoned VecDeque length); ghost re-admission must still promote into
`main` from freq=0; and no admission/retention decision may change. The scored
`Entry` stays 24 bytes; `ghost_set` lives only in the unscored `S3Fifo` container.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the direct analog of v001's winning move — eliminate the scan rather than
reorder it — applied to the one linear walk that remains on the put path. It
targets put latency specifically while holding hit-rate bit-identical.
