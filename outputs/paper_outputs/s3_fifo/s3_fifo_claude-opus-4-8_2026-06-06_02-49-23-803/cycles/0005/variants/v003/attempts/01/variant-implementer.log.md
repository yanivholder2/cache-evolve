Verification of faithfulness and invariant safety:
- **No new struct field** — reuses the existing `ghost: VecDeque<K>`, so `Entry`/`S3Fifo` layout and metadata-size (24) are unchanged.
- **Ghost cap unchanged** at 50% of main; main-victim keys now compete in the same fixed window as small-demotion keys.
- **No duplicate-key risk**: the victim is popped out of `main` (now non-resident), and ghost holds only non-resident keys, so the resident/ghost sets stay disjoint.
- Admission filter, split, `MAX_FREQ`, and graduation/demotion thresholds are all untouched.
