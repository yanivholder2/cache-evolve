# H01

Give the adaptive partition a momentum step and a wider clamp so it tracks larger phase swings, raising hit-rate beyond the tight ±1 orbit.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Build on v001's source-steered adaptive split. Two coordinated edits, both in
`src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`:

1. Widen the clamp window. Change `small_lower()` from `capacity / 48` to
   `capacity / 96` (still `.max(1)`) and `small_upper()` from `capacity / 12`
   to `capacity / 8` (still `.max(1)`). This lets the split travel further from
   the proven `capacity/24` center when the workload sustains a directional
   imbalance.

2. Replace the fixed ±1 nudge in `put()`'s in-ghost branch with an
   acceleration/momentum controller. Add two `usize`/`i8` fields to `S3Fifo`:
   `step` (current move magnitude, init 1) and `last_dir` (-1, 0, or +1, init
   0). In the in-ghost branch, compute the desired direction (`+1` to grow
   small for a weak `from_main == false` hit, `-1` to shrink small for a strong
   `from_main == true` hit). If the desired direction equals `last_dir`, double
   `step` (saturating, capped at 4); otherwise reset `step` to 1. Apply the
   move by `step` in the chosen direction, then clamp `small_size` to
   `[small_lower() .. small_upper()]` (use `.clamp()` or saturating arithmetic —
   never underflow `usize`), set `last_dir` to the desired direction, and
   recompute `main_size = capacity - small_size`.

Preserve every other invariant: `small_size + main_size == capacity` at all
times, ghost capacity continues to track `main_size`, no resident entries are
relocated when the bound shifts, and the in-ghost key is still re-admitted to
the front of main after the (possible) `evict_main()`. Keep `get`, `evict_main`,
`evict_small`, and `evict_ghost_one` unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v001's gain was explicitly bounded by a tight clamp and a single-step move that
the cycle-0008 insight flagged as possibly under-reaching. Momentum lets the
controller converge fast on sustained shifts while still collapsing back to
single steps when the signal reverses, directly probing the "widen the orbit /
change the step size" open question without touching the falsified static-sizing
axis.
