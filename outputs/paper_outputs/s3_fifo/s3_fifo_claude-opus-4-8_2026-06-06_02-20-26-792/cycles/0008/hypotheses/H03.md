# H03

Make the small admission queue recency-aware by moving entries to the front on a hit, raising hit-rate by keeping short-term-reused keys resident in the thin small queue instead of churning them out FIFO.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

All ordering work to date targeted the *main* queue (the move-to-front win in
cycle 0004, then the exhausted refinements in cycle 0005). The *small*
admission queue has never had its ordering touched — it remains strict
position-blind FIFO. Test that untested surface.

In `get()`, step 1 (the small-queue hit branch) currently bumps `freq` in place
without reordering. Change it to mirror main's discipline: on a small-queue hit,
`remove` the entry at its position and `push_front` it, after applying the same
`freq = freq.saturating_add(1).min(MAX_FREQ)` bump. Return a reference to the
just-pushed front entry's value.

Preserve everything else: the `freq` clamp, the `freq > 1` promotion test in
`evict_small`, the partition sizes, main move-to-front, the `evict_main`
rotation, and all ghost handling. Only the intra-`small` ordering on a hit
changes.

## Rationale (optional)

In the thin ~4% small queue, a key reused within a short window is currently at
risk of FIFO eviction before its second hit lands. Move-to-front keeps such keys
resident so the short-term reuse becomes a small-queue hit rather than a miss.
The trade this probe measures: protecting short-term reuse in small versus
delaying tail-time promotion of hot keys into main.
