# H02

Deepening the count-min sketch from 4 to 6 rows lowers the min-estimate overcount and improves hit-rate, an axis orthogonal to sketch width.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `SKETCH_DEPTH` from `4` to
`6`, leaving `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR = 4` and the rest of v001 unchanged.
`CountMinSketch::new` already allocates `depth` rows
(`vec![vec![0u8; width]; depth]`) and `increment`/`estimate` already loop
`0..self.depth`, so the only edit is the constant. The `position_for`
double-hash stepping `(h1 + row * (h2 | 1)) & (width - 1)` must keep
producing distinct columns across the two added rows — it does, because
`h2` is forced odd and `row` ranges over the new depth. Preserve the
`min()` reduction in `estimate` and the per-row saturating increment.
This probes a different signal-quality axis than H01: more independent
hash rows reduce the count-min *overestimate* (the min of more samples is
tighter), where width reduces per-row collision load. Expect a small
hit-rate lift; per-op-time will rise modestly because `increment` and
`estimate` now touch 6 rows per op instead of 4, and metadata-size (the
tracked `Node` bytes) stays at 24 since node layout is untouched.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle open question asks whether counter/sketch precision keeps
buying hit-rate; depth is the untested precision knob (every prior win
moved width or layout), so this isolates whether row count is a live lever
or already saturated at 4.
