# H02

Widen the count-min sketch (SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR 4 -> 16) on v003 to reduce counter collisions and sharpen frequency estimates, raising hit-rate above 0.7369.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Target the admission signal quality rather than its policy. v003 sizes the
sketch in `try_new` as
`width = (capacity * SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR).max(MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH).next_power_of_two()`
with `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR = 4`. At capacity 2048 this gives a width-8192,
depth-4 u8 table; hashed collisions in such a table cause the count-min
estimator to systematically overestimate cold keys, blurring the
`incoming_freq > victim_freq` comparison and the sampled-coldest victim choice.

- Raise `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` from `4` to `16` (one isolated constant change),
  quadrupling sketch columns and roughly quartering the collision rate so
  estimates approach true frequencies.
- Leave depth, the u8 counter width, the FastHasher, double-hashing, sampled
  eviction, node layout, and the admission test exactly as v003 has them.

Note the metadata-size fitness summary reports the per-entry `Node<u64,u64>`
byte size (48), not the sketch heap, so this change does not move the reported
metadata-size axis — it spends only real sketch heap and a small per-op cost
from the larger table. The hypothesis is that sharper frequency estimates make
both the admission filter and the sampled-coldest victim selection more
accurate, netting a hit-rate gain. If 16 over-spends per-op time, the same
lever is testable at 8.
