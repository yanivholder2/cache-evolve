# H03

Replacing v003's per-row SipHash with v002's single FastHasher double-hashing will slash its per-op-time while retaining the hit-rate lift from sampled eviction.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

v003 holds the cohort's best hit-rate (0.7381) thanks to its
sampled-coldest victim walk (`SAMPLE_VICTIMS = 5`) and u8 counters, but
its per-op-time is the worst (109ns) because `CountMinSketch::position`
runs a fresh `DefaultHasher` (SipHash) for every row on every increment
and estimate — and the sample walk multiplies those estimate calls. Cut
that cost by importing v002's hashing path while keeping v003's
residency logic untouched:

- Add the `FastHasher` struct (multiply-xorshift fold with the
  `0x9e37...` seed and `0x517c...` multiplier, xorshift `finish`) exactly
  as in v002.
- Replace `CountMinSketch::position(key, seed)` with v002's
  `base_hash<K>(&self, key) -> u64` (one `FastHasher` pass) plus
  `position_for(&self, h: u64, row: usize) -> usize` (double hashing:
  `(h1 + row*h2 | 1) & (width-1)`).
- Rewrite `increment` and `estimate` to compute `let h =
  self.base_hash(key);` once and derive each row's column via
  `position_for(h, row)`, instead of re-hashing per row.
- Keep `MAX_COUNT: u8`, the `Vec<Vec<u8>>` counters, the
  `SAMPLE_VICTIMS` sampled-coldest walk in `put`, and the
  estimate-`as u32` min logic exactly as v003 has them.

The hypothesis: hit-rate stays at v003's ~0.738 (the sampled eviction
and counter precision are unchanged), while per-op-time falls toward
v002's range because each op now pays one cheap fold instead of
`depth` SipHash passes — making the hit-rate win affordable.
