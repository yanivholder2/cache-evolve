# H02

Tune admission threshold to incoming_freq >= victim_freq - 1 to test the gradient between strict and permissive policies, raising hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs around line 102 in the `put` method, change the admission condition from `if incoming_freq > victim_freq {` to `if incoming_freq >= victim_freq.saturating_sub(1) {`.

This admits items with frequencies within 1 unit of the victim's frequency, occupying a middle ground between the strict `>` (baseline) and permissive `>=` (cycle 0002's failed v001). The `saturating_sub(1)` ensures no underflow for small frequencies and preserves the selectivity while being marginally more permissive than strict inequality.

Keep all other logic (sketch geometry, LRU ordering, eviction) unchanged to isolate the admission threshold as the only variable.

## Rationale

Cycle 0002's cycle-level insight explicitly called out this direction: "Does the admission threshold have a gradient between > and >=? Is there an intermediate condition (e.g., incoming_freq >= victim_freq - 1) that admits slightly more while preserving hit-rate?" Cycle 0002's v001 showed that >= is too permissive (hit-rate 0.7377, worse than cycle 0001's v001 at 0.7409, despite v001's superior sketch width). Testing a -1 offset provides a precise intermediate point to understand the threshold gradient and whether marginal relaxation can improve hit-rate without the cliff observed in cycle 0002/v001.
