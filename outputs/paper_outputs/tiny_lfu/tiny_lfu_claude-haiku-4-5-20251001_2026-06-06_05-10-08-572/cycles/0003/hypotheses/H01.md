# H01

Increase sketch width to 8 while maintaining depth-3 to combine frequency estimation accuracy with latency efficiency and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

In src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs line 9, change `const SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR: usize = 4;` to `const SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR: usize = 8;`.

Maintain `SKETCH_DEPTH = 3` (inherited from parent v002) and the admission condition `if incoming_freq > victim_freq` (strict, not relaxed).

This hypothesis directly tests the open question from cycle 0002's insight: "Can we recover v002's hit-rate loss by pairing depth 3 with increased width?" v002 achieved excellent per-op-time (90 ns) but sacrificed 0.5% hit-rate (0.7362 → 0.7334) due to reduced frequency estimation accuracy. Cycle 0001's v001 demonstrated that SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR=8 improves hit-rate to 0.7409 while maintaining baseline latency (110 ns). This hypothesis tests whether the two orthogonal levers—depth reduction for latency and width expansion for accuracy—synergize on a proven latency-optimized baseline, potentially delivering both wins simultaneously.

## Rationale

v002 proved depth-3 is stable and fast; v001 (cycle 0001) proved width-8 improves hit-rate. Both maintained strict admission (>). The combination is the natural next step to recover the hit-rate lost to depth reduction while keeping its latency benefit.
