## Changes

In src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs line 102, change the admission condition from `if incoming_freq > victim_freq`
to `if incoming_freq >= victim_freq`. This relaxes the admission barrier to match v003's policy
while keeping v001's superior frequency estimation (SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR=8, SKETCH_DEPTH=4).

The hypothesis tests whether these two orthogonal levers—estimation accuracy and admission
threshold—synergize to find a balanced sweet spot. v001 improved both hit-rate and latency via
wider width; v003 traded hit-rate for throughput via relaxed admission. Combining them may admit
more qualifying items without degrading frequency quality, since v001's wider sketch reduces
false frequency estimates. Keep all LRU and eviction logic intact.
