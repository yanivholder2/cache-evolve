# H03

Increase sketch depth to 6 independent hash rows to improve frequency estimation accuracy and hit-rate on high-cardinality Zipfian workloads.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs line 8, change SKETCH_DEPTH from 4 to 6. This increases the number of
independent hash functions in the count-min sketch, reducing collision probability and improving
the minimum (consensus) frequency estimate returned by the estimate() method.

The cycle-0001 insight shows that frequency estimation accuracy dominates the fitness landscape
on Zipfian traces. v001 improved hit-rate by increasing width (reducing collisions within rows);
this hypothesis tests whether adding rows (more independent functions) yields similar or additive
gains. Deeper sketches increase per-op latency cost but may justify it via hit-rate improvement,
especially on traces where collisions remain the bottleneck despite four independent hashes.
Keep SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR=4 and admission condition (>) baseline-aligned.
