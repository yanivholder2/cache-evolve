## Rationale

Cycle 0002 established that depth reduction from 4 to 3 achieved 25% per-operation latency improvement for <0.3% hit-rate loss. Depth has been stable at 3 since cycle 0002 across all subsequent cycles (0003-0007), suggesting 3 is robust. However, no prior cycle has directly tested depth 3 → 2, making this an unexplored boundary. The cross-cycle-insights document asks: "Can sketch geometry shrink further than depth-3, width-3?" This hypothesis tests the orthogonal depth dimension while holding eviction policy (N=2) and width (4) constant. If depth-2 preserves hit-rate, a 33% reduction in sketch query cost could yield material per-op-time improvements. If it degrades hit-rate sharply, it confirms depth-3 is the practical minimum for this workload's Zipfian distribution.
