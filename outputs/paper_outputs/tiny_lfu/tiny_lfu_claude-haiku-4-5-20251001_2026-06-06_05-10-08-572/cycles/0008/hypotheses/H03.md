## Rationale

Cycle 0007 v003 proved that width-3 improves latency by ~17% without hit-rate loss. Cycle 0002 established that depth reduction (4→3) yields 25% latency improvement. The cross-cycle-insights explicitly ask: "Can sketch geometry shrink further than depth-3, width-3?" This hypothesis tests a combined minimal geometry (2×3) that has not been directly evaluated. It differs from H02 by starting from cycle 0007 v003's proven width-3 baseline and LRU-only eviction, rather than the incumbent's N=2 frequency-aware policy. The hypothesis explores whether the synergy of two reduction dimensions yields superlinear latency gains or whether they interact with the eviction strategy (LRU vs. frequency-aware) in ways that affect hit-rate stability. If depth-2 + width-3 with LRU maintains hit-rate within 0.3% while improving latency, it establishes a new minimal-footprint frontier; if it regresses hit-rate, it clarifies the interaction between sketch geometry and eviction policy.
