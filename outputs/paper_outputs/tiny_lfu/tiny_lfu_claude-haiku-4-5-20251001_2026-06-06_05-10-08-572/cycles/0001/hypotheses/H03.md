Relax admission policy from strict inequality to equality to admit more items when frequencies tie, improving hit rate via increased diversity.

## Changes

In src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs line 102, change the admission condition from `if incoming_freq > victim_freq`
to `if incoming_freq >= victim_freq`. This allows incoming items with equal estimated frequency
to the victim to be admitted, rather than rejected. On traces with many tied-frequency items or
when sketch hash collisions cause frequency estimates to align, this change will admit more
candidates, increasing the resident set diversity. It may slightly alter the eviction order when
frequencies collide, but preserves the core LRU recency and sketch-based frequency semantics.
Expect hit-rate improvement on workloads where incoming and victim frequencies are close.
