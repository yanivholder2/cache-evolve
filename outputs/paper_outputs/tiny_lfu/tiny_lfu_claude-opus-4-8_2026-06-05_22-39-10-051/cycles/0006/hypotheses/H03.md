# H03

Requiring a probationary node to have a sketch estimate of at least 2 before it may enter the protected tier reserves protection for keys with proven reuse and improves scan-resistance hit-rate on the LIRS trace.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

In the winner v002, a probationary node is promoted into the protected tier on
its first hit whenever the protected tier has spare room (the
`self.protected_len < self.protected_cap` branch of `promote()`). On the LIRS
workload-a trace, scan-like single-reference keys can therefore occupy protected
slots that genuinely-hot keys could use. This change adds a frequency floor for
protected entry — it gates *promotion within the cache*, NOT *admission into the
cache*, so warming keys still reside in probation and remain hittable.

Concretely:
- Add a module constant `const PROMOTE_FLOOR: u32 = 2;`.
- At the top of `promote()`, after the early-return for already-protected nodes,
  compute `let promotee_freq = self.sketch.estimate(&self.nodes[idx].key);`.
- Guard BOTH promotion arms with the floor: a probationary node may only enter
  the protected tier when `promotee_freq >= PROMOTE_FLOOR`. When the estimate is
  below the floor, `unlink(idx)` then `push_back_prob(idx)` — i.e. refresh its
  recency at the probationary tail and leave it probationary (do not touch
  `protected_len`).
- When `promotee_freq >= PROMOTE_FLOOR`, fall through to v002's existing logic
  unchanged: unconditional promotion if `protected_len < protected_cap`,
  otherwise the frequency contest against `prot_head`.

Preserve invariants: `protected_len` accounting stays exact, the `put()`
admission gate (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`) is untouched, and the eviction
victim remains the probationary head.

## Rationale (optional)

The refuted admission doorkeeper blocked *cache entry* and delayed warming keys;
this floor only delays *protected entry*, keeping single-reference scan keys
evictable in probation while reserving the protected tier for demonstrated reuse —
a distinct mechanism not previously tested.
