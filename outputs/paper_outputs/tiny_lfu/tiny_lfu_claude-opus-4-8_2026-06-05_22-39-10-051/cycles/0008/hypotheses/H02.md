# H02

Enlarging the protected SLRU fraction from 0.8 to 0.9 of capacity raises hit-rate by shielding more proven-hot keys from probationary eviction, pushing the protected size past the largest fraction ever tested.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

The SLRU protected fraction has only ever been measured at two points: the
default 0.8 (`protected_cap = (capacity * 4) / 5`) and a shrunken 2/3 (cycle
0005 v003), which regressed. Cycle 0005's conclusion was explicit — "the SLRU
favours a *larger* protected tier, not a smaller one" — yet no variant has
tested a fraction *above* 0.8. The optimum may lie higher: a larger protected
tier keeps more frequency-proven keys resident and out of reach of admission
churn, and the only cost is a smaller probationary region, which still retains
at least one slot (the always-present victim).

Concretely, in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs` `try_new()` (lines 70–71):

1. Change `let protected_cap = (capacity * 4) / 5;` to
   `let protected_cap = (capacity * 9) / 10;` (0.9 of capacity).
2. Keep the existing clamp `let protected_cap = protected_cap.min(capacity.saturating_sub(1));`
   exactly as-is so there is always at least one probationary slot and a valid
   eviction victim when the cache is full.

Change nothing else: the promotion floor, the frequency-gated promotion
contest, `sample_prot_demotee`, and the admission gate all stay identical. At
capacity 2048 this moves `protected_cap` from 1638 to 1843, shrinking
probation from 410 to 205 slots — a meaningful but bounded shift in the favored
direction. Preserve the invariant that `protected_len` only ever counts nodes
actually linked into the protected chain.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the one SLRU sizing direction the search has never explored: every
prior fraction probe went smaller and lost. If hit-rate is genuinely capped the
result ties; if the protected tier was under-sized at 0.8, this is the cheapest
remaining structural lift.
