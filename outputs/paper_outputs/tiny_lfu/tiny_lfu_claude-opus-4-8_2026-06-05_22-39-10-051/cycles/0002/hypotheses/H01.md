# H01

Enlarging v003's frequency-ranked victim sample from 5 to 16 candidates evicts genuinely cold residents more often and further raises hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In the v003 overlay (`src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`), the eviction path in `put()`
walks up to `SAMPLE` (currently 5) resident nodes forward from the LRU head
and picks the one with the lowest `sketch.estimate`. v003's insight states the
gain is slim at SAMPLE=5 only because the very front of the LRU list rarely
hides a much-colder item, and explicitly flags a larger sample as the way to
test whether the effect grows.

Make exactly one change: raise `const SAMPLE: usize` from `5` to `16`. Keep the
sampling loop semantics identical — forward scan head→tail, strictly-lower
`estimate` replaces the current victim, ties favour the node nearer the head,
and the `incoming_freq > victim_freq` admission gate is unchanged. Do not
modify the sketch, the LRU linkage, or the in-place slot overwrite that v003
relies on to keep capacity and value handling untouched.

Preserve invariants: at most one slot is overwritten per admission, the loop
terminates early when the list is shorter than SAMPLE (the `cursor == None`
break), and a never-resident key is never sampled. This isolates "victim
sample breadth" as the single independent variable, so a hit-rate delta versus
v003's 0.7381 attributes cleanly to sample size — and tells us whether the
frequency-ranked eviction gain scales or plateaus.
