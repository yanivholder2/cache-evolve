# H03

Require the incoming key to beat the eviction victim by a fixed frequency margin rather than by a strict tie-break, protecting churn-surviving probationary keys to raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

The eviction contest in `put` admits whenever `incoming_freq > victim_freq`
(margin of zero — a single extra observed hit is enough to swap a resident
for a newcomer). On a churny trace this lets a marginally-hotter arrival
that will not be reused evict a probationary key that *would* have been hit
again. Strict guards have repeatedly won; this widens that guard with a
hysteresis margin.

Add a module-level constant `const ADMISSION_MARGIN: u32 = 1;`. In the
cache-full branch of `put`, change the admission test from
`if incoming_freq > victim_freq` to
`if incoming_freq > victim_freq.saturating_add(ADMISSION_MARGIN)`. Keep the
victim as `prob_head`, the `incoming_freq`/`victim_freq` derivation, and all
SLRU/promotion logic exactly as-is.

Effect: a newcomer must be at least 2 observations hotter than the
probationary victim to displace it, so warm-but-not-hottest residents
survive transient bursts. This is distinct from H02 (which removes the
incoming key's self-count) — here the incoming estimate is unchanged but the
bar it must clear is raised. The `saturating_add` keeps the comparison safe
at the `u32::MAX` ceiling. The always-a-victim invariant is unaffected since
a failed contest simply drops the newcomer, as today.
