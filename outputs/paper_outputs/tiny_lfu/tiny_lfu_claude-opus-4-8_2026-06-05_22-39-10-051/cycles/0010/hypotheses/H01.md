# H01

Stacking the pre-increment admission score with a one-step hysteresis margin tightens the eviction gate further and lifts hit-rate above either winner alone.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

v002 already scores the incoming key at the admission gate by its
*pre-increment* frequency: in `put()`, `let incoming_freq = prior;` and the
gate admits when `incoming_freq > victim_freq`. Layer v003's hysteresis margin
on top of that self-consistent score, rather than choosing one or the other.

Concretely:

1. Add a module-level constant `const ADMISSION_MARGIN: u32 = 1;` next to the
   existing `PROMOTE_FLOOR` constant.
2. In `put()`, keep `incoming_freq = prior` (do NOT revert to the
   post-increment `self.sketch.estimate(&key)` that v003 used). Change only the
   admission test from `if incoming_freq > victim_freq` to
   `if incoming_freq > victim_freq.saturating_add(ADMISSION_MARGIN)`.

Leave the promotion path, `PROMOTE_FLOOR` gate, `sample_prot_demotee`, and all
SLRU/sketch structure untouched. The intent is the conjunction: a newcomer must
be scored by its prior (excluding-current) frequency AND clear the resident by a
full margin before it can evict. This directly tests the cycle-0009 open
question of whether the two tightenings stack or merely consume the same handful
of near-tie contests (in which case hit-rate matches v002/v003 at 0.7443 and the
combination is redundant).
