# H01

Reducing PROMO_THRESHOLD from 6 to 5 will capture an additional thin Zipfian frequency slice and raise hit rate by admitting items whose true access frequency lies at 5.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant declaration:

```
const PROMO_THRESHOLD: u32 = 6;
```

to:

```
const PROMO_THRESHOLD: u32 = 5;
```

No other changes. The `was_protected` bypass (`if freq >= PROMO_THRESHOLD || self.nodes[idx].was_protected`) remains intact; it is essential for preventing capacity-overflow demotees from being incorrectly penalised by the stricter gate on re-access. Update the struct-level doc comment to reflect the new threshold value and the rationale (admitting items with true frequency 5, which the 8× accurate sketch now distinguishes from frequency 4).

The hypothesis is that the frequency band at 5 — items requested often enough to be genuinely warm but just below the current gate — is large enough on the workload-a Zipfian distribution to produce a measurable hit-rate increment, following the same pattern as the 8→6 reduction in cycle 0009. The risk is that the frequency-5 cohort is too thin to offset the increased protected-segment churn caused by admitting lower-frequency items; the `was_protected` bypass limits the churn by recycling overflow demotees without re-clearing the gate.
