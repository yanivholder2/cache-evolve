# H01

Scanning k=4 candidates from the LRU end of the protected segment and demoting the minimum-frequency one, rather than always the LRU head, reduces protected-segment pollution by cold overflow survivors and improves hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

Modify `maybe_demote_from_protected()` to use a k=4 frequency scan before demoting, mirroring the probationary victim scan that was added in cycle 0004.

Current behaviour: the method unconditionally demotes `prot_head` (the single LRU-tail item of protected).

New behaviour: walk up to `EVICTION_SCAN_K` (4) items starting from `prot_head`, calling `self.sketch.estimate(&self.nodes[idx].key)` on each. Track the index with the minimum estimated frequency (`demotion_idx`), breaking ties by taking the item closest to `prot_head` (i.e., the LRU-oldest in the window). After the scan, demote `demotion_idx` — not necessarily `prot_head` — by calling `prot_unlink(demotion_idx)`, decrementing `protected_len`, setting `self.nodes[demotion_idx].was_protected = true`, calling `prob_push_back(demotion_idx)`, and incrementing `probationary_len`.

The outer `while self.protected_len > self.protected_capacity` loop is unchanged; only the single-demotion inner body changes.

No other functions, constants, or struct fields require modification. `EVICTION_SCAN_K` is already defined as 4 and should be reused rather than introducing a new constant.

## Rationale

Five cycles have refined the probationary side to near-optimum, but the protected segment has always used pure LRU ordering internally. When `was_protected` overflow-demotees re-enter protected via the bypass, they land at the MRU tail regardless of their sketch frequency. Over time some cold-ish items accumulate near the LRU head of protected not because they are truly hot but because they have not been accessed recently. A k=4 scan finds the coldest among these without requiring a full-segment traversal, the same trade-off that proved worthwhile for probationary eviction.
