# H01

Replacing unconditional LRU demotion from the protected segment with a k-candidate minimum-frequency scan will increase hit rate above cycle/0002/v001 by keeping the highest-frequency items resident in protected longer on a stable Zipfian workload where frequency is more predictive than recency.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `get()`, locate the block that handles protected-segment overflow (the `if self.protected_len > self.protected_capacity` branch, lines ~89–99 of v001's overlay). Currently it unconditionally demotes `self.prot_head` (the LRU item). Replace this with a bounded minimum-frequency scan:

1. Walk up to 8 nodes from the LRU end of the protected list (starting at `self.prot_head`, following `.next` links).
2. For each candidate node, call `self.sketch.estimate(&self.nodes[candidate].key)`.
3. Demote the candidate with the minimum estimate (break ties toward LRU order, i.e. the head, to preserve the current default when all frequencies are equal).

The scan is bounded at 8 steps so its cost is O(8) — effectively O(1) — while covering the coldest tail of the protected segment. All other logic (updating `protected_len`, `probationary_len`, `in_protected` flag, `prob_push_back`) remains identical to v001. No changes to `put()`, `CountMinSketch`, or any other function.

## Rationale

The protected segment currently uses pure LRU ordering. On workload-a's stable Zipfian distribution the sketch's accumulated counts are reliable frequency signal. Demoting by minimum frequency rather than by minimum recency should reduce cases where a recently-promoted but lower-frequency item displaces an older but highly-accessed item that was sitting quietly at the LRU end of protected.
