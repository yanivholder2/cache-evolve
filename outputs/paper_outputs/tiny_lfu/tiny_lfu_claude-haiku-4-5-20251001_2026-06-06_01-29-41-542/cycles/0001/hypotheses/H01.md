# H01

Increase sketch width to reduce hash collisions and improve frequency precision on hot workloads.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` constant in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs` (currently 4) to 8. 

The sketch is initialized as `width = (capacity * SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR).max(MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH).next_power_of_two()`.

With the current factor of 4, high-cardinality workloads may experience significant hash collisions in the Count-Min sketch, causing popular items to share counter slots with unpopular ones. This inflates frequency estimates for cold items and suppresses estimates for hot items. Doubling the width factor to 8 will reduce collisions, improving the precision of frequency estimates in the admission contest. Keep all other sketch parameters unchanged (depth remains 4, counter saturation at u32::MAX).

## Rationale

The baseline achieves 0.7362 hit rate. Hash collision errors in frequency estimation directly impact which items are admitted vs evicted. A wider sketch trades space (negligible at 8x width) for better signal separation when deciding between the incoming key and the LRU victim.
