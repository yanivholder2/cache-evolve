# H02

Replace pure LRU eviction with a frequency-recency hybrid that biases victims toward low-frequency items and deprioritizes recently-touched hot items to improve hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the eviction victim selection in the cache eviction path to replace strict LRU ordering with a hybrid metric. When selecting a victim, compute a score for each candidate equal to (current_time - last_access_time) / (frequency_estimate + 1), preferring candidates with the highest score (oldest and coldest). This way, recently-accessed hot items are protected even if they are among the oldest, while cold items become victims regardless of recency. Implement this scoring by iterating through eviction candidates and tracking the maximum score seen, returning that candidate as the victim. Preserve the existing admission gate and sketch logic from the baseline unchanged.
