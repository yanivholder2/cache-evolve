## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the `put()` method's admission gate check (currently line 118: `if incoming_freq > victim_freq`), change the comparison operator from `>` to `>=`. This allows items with equal frequency estimates to the victim to be admitted. Cycle 0001 v003 tested this relaxation and regressed; however, the incumbent (v001) now uses the refined hybrid victim-selection policy (`age / (frequency + 2.0)`) that intelligently prioritizes cold, infrequent items. With this improved eviction logic, a more permissive gate may no longer hurt hit-rate, since the eviction layer can now better identify and remove items that shouldn't have been admitted. Test whether the gate's strict selectivity was an artifact of the simpler eviction policy, or whether it remains optimal.
