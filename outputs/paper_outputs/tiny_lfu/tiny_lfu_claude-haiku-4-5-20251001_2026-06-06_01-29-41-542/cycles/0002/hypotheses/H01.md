# H01

Push sketch width factor to 16x to further reduce hash collisions and improve frequency estimation fidelity.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Increase SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR from 8 to 16 in src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs. This doubles the sketch width relative to cache capacity, reducing hash collision probability in the Count-Min sketch from approximately 1/(2^8) to 1/(2^16) for uniform hash distributions. The wider sketch will further improve frequency estimates for both hot and cold items, enabling the admission gate to make more precise decisions when comparing incoming candidates to LRU victims. Keep all other logic unchanged, including the admission threshold (strict >), the increment behavior, and the LRU recency ordering.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001's v001 proved that width is a practical bottleneck—doubling from 4x to 8x raised hit rate by 0.0047 over baseline. This hypothesis extends that winning trajectory to test whether diminishing returns have set in or if further width improvements continue to yield gains.
