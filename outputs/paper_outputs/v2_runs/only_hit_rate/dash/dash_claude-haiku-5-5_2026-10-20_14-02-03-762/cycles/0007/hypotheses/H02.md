# H02

Halving the reuse counter on normal-tier demotion instead of resetting it to zero will keep partial reuse credit for keys that return soon after demotion, raising hit rate on LIRS workload-a at size 2048.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

Start from the v001 overlay (2-bit saturating reuse counter with stash promotion gated on counter >= 2). Find the site in the v001 overlay where an entry's reuse counter is cleared when the entry is demoted from the stash or the probing tier into the normal tier. Replace the clear-to-zero with a right shift by one (counter = counter >> 1), so a counter of 2 or 3 becomes 1 and a counter of 1 becomes 0. Leave the eviction-path reset as it is in v001, so only demotion changes. Keep the promotion threshold (counter >= 2), the saturation at 3, and the 0.75 occupancy branch unchanged. No new metadata bits are needed. Preserve the invariant that the counter never exceeds 3 and that eviction still chooses victims by the v001 rule.

Note for the implementer: the v001 insight flags the demotion reset as unverified. Confirm the current reset semantics in the v001 overlay before editing, and state in the insight which reset site was changed.
