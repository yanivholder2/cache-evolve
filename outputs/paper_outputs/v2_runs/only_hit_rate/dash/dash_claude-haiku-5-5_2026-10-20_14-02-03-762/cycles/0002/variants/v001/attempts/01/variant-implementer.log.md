**What the overlay does**

- A stash hit now chooses its landing slot in `promotion_get`, via a new `stash_landing_position`:
  - **Back (MRU):** the target bucket has a free slot, or the target is empty, or its LRU-end resident is stash-origin.
  - **Front (LRU end):** the target is full and its LRU-end resident is a normal-tier item.
- `LRU-end` is taken from the bucket's `eviction_candidate_index()`, so it follows whichever eviction policy the bucket uses.
- `Item` is unchanged. Stash-origin is tracked by a `stash_origin: HashSet<K>` on `DashSegment`, which is added on each stash→target promotion. Once the set outgrows the segment's total capacity, it is pruned to keys still resident in a normal bucket.
- The probing-tier path, the demotion cascade and eviction are untouched. `get` still passes `InsertPosition::Back`; the stash branch ignores that argument and chooses its own landing.
- The `get` and struct docstrings and the `promotion_get` comment now describe the conditional default instead of calling LRU-end promotion the baseline.

**Tests**

- The existing segment tests are kept. `stash_hit_inserts_at_back_of_target` still holds because the target has a free slot there.
- Two new tests cover the two conditional branches: `stash_hit_lands_front_when_full_of_normal_tier` and `stash_hit_lands_back_when_lru_resident_is_stash_origin`.

**Known imprecision**

The stash-origin flag is per key, not per residency. A key promoted from the stash and later demoted back into a normal bucket from probing still counts as stash-origin until it is pruned. This affects only the Back/Front choice, not slot legality or capacity. The hypothesis does not specify how origin should be tracked, so I chose this approach rather than adding a field to `Item`.
