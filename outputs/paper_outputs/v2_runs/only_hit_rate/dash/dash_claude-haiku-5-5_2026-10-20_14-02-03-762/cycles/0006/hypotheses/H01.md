# H01

Replacing the one-bit seen-in-stash marker with a 2-bit saturating reuse counter will raise hit-rate over cycle/0004/v001 on LIRS workload-a at size 2048 by filtering out stash items that are re-hit only once.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Keep v001's MRU landing for re-referenced stash items and its occupancy-conditional promotion gate unchanged. Replace the single seen-in-stash bit with a 2-bit saturating counter stored in the same entry metadata slot.

- On each stash hit, increment the counter, saturating at 3.
- In the promotion branch that v001 currently gates on the marker, promote a stash item only when its counter is at least 2. Below the occupancy threshold (0.75), keep v001's first-hit promotion behaviour unchanged.
- When an item is demoted or evicted from the stash, reset its counter to 0 so it cannot carry reuse credit into a new residency.
- Do not change the probing-tier logic, the foreign-eviction target retry, or victim selection in `evict_one()`.
- Preserve the existing stash, probing, and normal tier invariants. If the 2-bit field does not fit in the current spare tag bit, pack it into the smallest free field of the entry and note the layout in the insight.

## Rationale (optional)

The second-hit gate was the most consistent hit-rate lever across cycles 2 and 3. A one-bit marker can only express "re-hit at least once." A counter tests whether an item keeps being re-hit, which may separate persistent reuse from a single revisit. Recording metadata-size here also tests whether the extra bit is affordable.
