# H03

Drop the one-more-chance retry in `demote_from_normal_bucket` so foreign probing evictions go straight to stash, cutting per-op time on put and get paths at a small hit-rate cost.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Edit `demote_from_normal_bucket()` in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`.
The current `else` branch (taken when the evicted item is a foreign resident
of `source_idx`) re-inserts the item into its true target via
`core.put_at(Slot::Target, insert_pos, item)` and, if that evicts again,
forwards the cascade victim into `Slot::Stash`.

Replace that `else` branch so the foreign item is placed directly into the
stash:

- `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, insert_pos, item);`
- Keep the `debug!` log, updated to say the foreign resident goes straight to
  stash.
- Remove the target-retry and cascade-to-stash code path from this function.

Keep the `true_target == source_idx` branch exactly as it is (item goes to
stash), keep the function signature, keep `promote_to_target` and
`promote_to_probing` unchanged, and keep `insert_pos` honoured on the stash
insert.

Update the in-file tests that encode the retry behaviour:
`evicted_from_probing_cascades_to_target`, `target_retry_lands_at_front_when_cold`,
`target_retry_lands_at_back_when_hot`, and `cascade_to_stash_at_front_when_cold` /
`cascade_to_stash_at_back_when_hot`. Rewrite them to assert the new placement
(evicted foreign items land in stash, honouring `insert_pos`). Do not touch
`EvictionPolicy` implementations or the `Bucket` trait.
