# H01

Moving a further 128 slots from the stash to the probing tier at fixed total capacity will raise hit-rate above 0.6906 on LIRS workload-a at size 2048, because probing residency is the binding resource.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

Start from v003's probing-tier and stash-tier sizing. Locate the constants (or the derived size expressions) that set the probing-tier slot count and the stash slot count, and change them so that the probing tier gains a total of 256 slots relative to the baseline split (v003 gained 128), with the stash shrinking by the same amount. Total capacity must stay exactly equal to the size-2048 budget used by v003. Recompute any derived sizes (bucket counts, stash-full retry thresholds, occupancy bounds) from the new split so they remain consistent with the new tier sizes; do not hard-code the old values into those derivations.

Keep the re-reference promotion gate and the MRU landing for stash hits exactly as v003 has them. Do not alter eviction victim selection, the reuse counter, or the occupancy logic.

Record hit-rate for the single trace at size 2048. If the gain over v003 is flat or negative, that is the answer: the probing optimum lies between 128 and 256 extra slots. Metadata-size and per-op-time should also be recorded if the harness exposes them.
