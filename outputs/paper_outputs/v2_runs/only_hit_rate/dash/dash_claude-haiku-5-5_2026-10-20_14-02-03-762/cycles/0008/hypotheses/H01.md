# H01

Requiring a saturated reuse counter for probing-tier-origin stash promotions should raise hit-rate on LIRS workload-a at size 2048 by admitting only items with persistent reuse.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

Start from the cycle/0007/v002 overlay and keep its halve-on-demotion reset of the 2-bit reuse counter unchanged. In the stash-to-normal promotion path, split the promotion gate by where the entry entered the stash from:

- For entries that arrived in the stash from the probing tier, promote only when the 2-bit reuse counter is at its saturated value (2) at the moment of the hit. Otherwise leave the entry in the stash and apply the normal landing rules.
- For entries that arrived in the stash by any other route, keep the existing second-hit gate exactly as the parent implements it.

Do not change the MRU landing of promoted entries, the increment site of the reuse counter, the occupancy branch, or the foreign-eviction retry. Do not move entries out of the probing tier or change any eviction victim choice. The origin tag needed for the split may come from the existing per-entry metadata; if no spare bit exists, the implementer should report that rather than widen entries.

## Rationale (optional)

The saturating counter is the only reuse-tracking lead with any hit-rate signal (cycle 6), and the admission and promotion gates are the only levers that have moved hit-rate. Gating only the probing-origin path tests whether persistent reuse matters more for items that have not yet proven themselves in the stash, while leaving the proven second-hit path alone.
