# H01

Gate stash promotion on a second hit only while stash occupancy is high, so hit-rate rises under stash pressure without suppressing early promotions when the stash has room.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Keep v001's MRU landing (`InsertPosition::Back`) for stash-promoted items unchanged. In the stash-hit branch of `DashSegment::get`, make the second-hit gate conditional on stash occupancy: when the stash has free slots (occupancy below a threshold, start with 0.75 of stash capacity), promote a stash item on its first hit as the parent did before v001; when occupancy is at or above the threshold, require the seen-in-stash marker to be set (second hit) before promotion, as v001 does. Compute occupancy from the existing stash slot counts; do not add new counters to the entry layout. Do not change `evict_one()`, the probing-tier path, or the normal-tier foreign-eviction retry. Preserve the one-bit marker semantics exactly as in v001.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 showed that skipping the foreign-eviction retry hurts most under stash pressure, and v001's unconditional second-hit gate suppresses promotions even when the stash is not full and those promotions would be free. Gating on pressure tests whether the gain comes from pressure-specific admission filtering. Measure hit-rate on the LIRS workload-a trace at size 2048 and at least one additional capacity.
