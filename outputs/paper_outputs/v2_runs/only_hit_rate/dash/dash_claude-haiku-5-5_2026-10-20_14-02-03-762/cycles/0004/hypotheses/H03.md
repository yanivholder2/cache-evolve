# H03

Make `evict_one()` prefer the least recently landed stash entry in the target bucket over the current victim choice, to raise hit-rate by keeping recently re-referenced stash entries resident.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `evict_one()`, when the victim must come from the stash tier of the target bucket, choose the stash entry that landed earliest (the entry at the front of the bucket's stash order, since MRU landing via `InsertPosition::Back` places new arrivals at the back) instead of the current selection. Implement the ordering with the existing slot order; do not add timestamps or counters to the entry layout. Leave the probing and normal-tier victim paths unchanged, and leave `DashSegment::get` and its MRU landing untouched. Do not use the "seen in stash" marker in this scan; cycle 0003 showed marker-based victim preference hurts. Before editing, the implementer must read the baseline's current victim selection in `evict_one()` and state in the insight what it was, so the diff against baseline is attributable.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001 found that landing position matters more than movement, and that MRU landing for stash hits is the one consistent positive signal. This hypothesis tests the complementary eviction side from a clean baseline, independent of the second-hit gate, so the cohort learns whether the eviction-order lever works on its own. Per-op-time should also be recorded, since no prior variant has measured it; the baseline value is 183.
