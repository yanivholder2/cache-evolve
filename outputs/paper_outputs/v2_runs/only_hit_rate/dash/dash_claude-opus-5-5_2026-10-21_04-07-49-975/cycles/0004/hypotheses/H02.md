# H02

Replacing the fixed stash→target promotion with a TinyLFU-style frequency duel against the target bucket's eviction victim will raise hit-rate by keeping frequently hit normal-tier residents from being displaced by keys that only just reached two stash hits.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

All edits are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. Today a stash item that reaches `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD` is always moved into its target bucket. That unconditionally evicts the target's LRU resident, whatever that resident's hit history. The resident's hit history is not tracked at all, because `lfu_counter` is reset to 0 on promotion and never incremented in the normal tier.

1. Track normal-tier frequency: in `promotion_get`, use `saturating_add(1)` on the item's `lfu_counter` in the target-bucket branches (both the `is_hottest` and the intra-promote paths) and in the probing-bucket branch, before the item is moved or touched. Cap it at `NORMAL_FREQ_CAP = 15`. Keep `data.lfu_counter = 0` when an item is promoted from stash, so frequency in the normal tier starts fresh. Normal-tier promotion decisions (`is_hottest`) stay position-based and must not read this counter.
2. Duel at the stash→target edge: in `promotion_get`, when `hits >= PROMOTE_THRESHOLD` and `core.buckets[target_idx].is_full()`, read the target's `eviction_candidate_index()` victim and its `lfu_counter` (`victim_freq`). Treat the candidate's score as `hits`.
   - If `hits > victim_freq`, promote exactly as today.
   - Otherwise, do not promote. Halve the victim's counter (`victim_freq / 2`, for aging, so a stale resident cannot block forever). Set the candidate's stash counter to `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1`, so its next hit duels again. Serve it in place from stash with `get_from_position`, which also refreshes its stash LRU position.
   - If the target bucket has a free slot, always promote (there is no victim).
3. Demotion: in `demote_from_normal_bucket`, keep overwriting `lfu_counter` with `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1` before the stash insert, so normal-tier frequency never leaks into the stash threshold or into v002's second-chance scan.
4. Keep `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD = 2`, `DEMOTE_TO_STASH_POS = Back`, Front landing in target, the second-chance scan and the cascade path unchanged.
5. Tests: the existing tests promote into empty or non-full buckets and should still pass. Add one test where a full target holds a victim with `lfu_counter = 5`. The stash candidate must stay in stash after two hits, and the victim's counter must drop to 2. Add another test where a victim with counter 0 loses the duel and is demoted as before.

## Rationale (optional)

The stash→target edge is the only lever in the lineage that has paid off. Every earlier change at that edge used a fixed bar that looks only at the candidate. A comparative bar weighs the candidate against the specific resident it would evict, which is the TinyLFU admission idea. It reuses the existing `lfu_counter` field and adds no metadata. Unlike the threshold-3 variant (0003 v001, flat), it does not filter fresh keys more strictly in general. It filters only when the resident it would replace has proven more valuable.
