# H03

A key returning on a very recent ghost hit (age ≤ 1 epoch) should be admitted straight into its normal-tier target bucket, skipping the stash probation round trip; this raises hit-rate by freeing stash slots for keys that are still unproven.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0009/v002. Today a ghost hit with age ≤ 4 epochs inserts the key at stash MRU. To reach its target bucket it must then earn stash hits up to the stash→target threshold.

- On the insert path, after the ghost lookup, split the gated ghost-hit branch by age:
  - **Age ≤ 1 epoch (very recent):** insert the key directly into its normal-tier target bucket, using the same placement routine and landing position that a stash→target promotion uses today (Front landing, the existing victim choice, and the existing demotion of the displaced resident into the stash). Initialise its counter to the value a freshly promoted key gets. Clear or invalidate the ghost entry, as the existing ghost-hit path already does.
  - **Age 2..4 epochs:** keep today's behaviour and insert at stash MRU.
  - **Age > 4 or ghost miss:** keep today's behaviour and insert at stash LRU.
- If the target bucket's placement would need a probing/displacement step that the promotion path can't do from an insert context, fall back to the stash MRU insert. Do not add a new cascade path.
- Keep the epoch length, the 4-epoch cutoff, the ghost table size, the second-chance scan, demotion MRU re-entry, and the stash→target threshold unchanged.
- Keep `len`/size accounting correct: the key is inserted exactly once, into either the normal tier or the stash.

## Rationale (optional)

The 0004 insight asked whether a ghost hit should admit "straight to target", and that has never been tested. Every variant since 0005 has used the ghost only to choose a position inside the stash. A key evicted and requested again within one epoch has a reuse distance well inside the cache, which is the strongest evidence the design has. Routing these keys through stash MRU spends a protected stash slot and then waits for a promotion hit they will very likely earn anyway. Sending them to target changes who reaches the stash at all, which is the lever the 0008 and 0009 continuations named. The displaced normal resident is demoted to stash MRU exactly as on a normal promotion, so no protected capacity is lost. If hit-rate drops, the stash's role as a holding area is worth more than the faster promotion, and the stash-mediated path is confirmed as the better design.
