# H02

Replace v003's fixed BIP fresh-key insertion with set-dueling DIP, which picks between BIP (LRU-end) and MRU stash insertion at runtime from leader-bucket misses, to raise hit-rate without overfitting to loop-heavy phases.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

v003 hard-codes bimodal insertion for fresh stash keys in `DashSegment::put`: 15/16 go to `InsertPosition::Front` (LRU end) and 1/16 to the MRU end. Make that choice adaptive per segment, as in Qureshi et al.'s Dynamic Insertion Policy:

1. Add a per-segment saturating counter `psel: u16` to the segment struct. Use a 10-bit range of 0..=1023, initialised to 512. This is the only metadata added: 2 bytes per segment.
2. Classify stash buckets by index with a cheap fixed rule. Bucket indices where `idx % 32 == 0` are **BIP leaders** and always use v003's BIP insertion. Indices where `idx % 32 == 1` are **MRU leaders** and always insert fresh keys at the MRU end with counter 0, which is the pre-v003 cycle-0003 v002 behaviour. All other buckets are **followers**.
3. On a full miss (key absent from normal and stash tiers) whose key hashes to a leader stash bucket, update `psel`. A miss in a BIP leader increments it (saturating at 1023). A miss in an MRU leader decrements it (saturating at 0). Put the update in the same miss/insert path in `DashSegment::put` where the BIP decision is made now.
4. Followers use MRU insertion when `psel > 512` (BIP leaders are missing more) and use v003's BIP insertion otherwise. The existing BIP 1-in-16 counter keeps driving the bimodal choice inside BIP mode.
5. Demoted ex-residents keep entering at MRU with `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1` pre-credit in every mode. The second-chance scan in `stash_put`, normal-tier landing and cascade logic are unchanged.

Invariants: keep the behaviour deterministic (no RNG; leader choice comes from the bucket index). If `psel` stays ≤ 512 the variant must reduce exactly to v003 on all followers.

## Rationale (optional)

Cross-cycle insights flag the risk that BIP's scan resistance overfits LIRS loop patterns. Keys with moderate reuse distance lose under BIP but win under MRU insertion. Set dueling lets the trace's own phases choose. It costs 2 bytes per segment and one branch on miss, which also tests whether the 0004 gain comes from loop phases only or holds throughout the trace.
