# H03

Make normal-tier evictions cascade-free by sending a displaced foreign resident straight to its own stash, instead of demoting it into a neighbour bucket's target, to lower per-op-time while holding hit-rate at v003's level.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

This reapplies the cascade-free routing idea from cycle 0003 v003 on top of the current winner. That idea was hit-rate neutral at the time and was never measured on per-op-time. The ghost table, the ghost-hit MRU override, BIP at v003's period, the second-chance scan, `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD` and normal-tier landing are unchanged.

1. In the normal-tier insert/promotion path of `DashSegment`, find the step that frees a slot in a full target bucket by moving a foreign resident (one whose home bucket is the neighbour) into the neighbour's target or probing bucket. That move can itself evict, and so cascade. Replace the step: insert the displaced resident directly into the stash through the same routine used for demoted ex-residents. That routine keeps its MRU re-entry and pre-credit, so it stays a cheaply recoverable ex-resident.
2. Remove any loop or recursion that existed only to resolve the cascade, so each normal-tier insert touches at most the target bucket, the probing bucket and one stash bucket.
3. If the stash insert evicts a stash victim, write its fingerprint to the ghost table exactly as v003 does now.

Invariants: a key must never be held twice, and lookups must still find displaced keys in the stash. No allocation.

## Rationale (optional)

Insights from cycles 0003–0006 say the cascade no longer matters for hit-rate, because the stash recovers demoted items cheaply. Per-op-time has gone unmeasured for six cycles and is the axis most likely to reward this change. Bounding the work per insert removes the worst-case cascade path. Expected result: hit-rate within noise of 0.722 and lower per-op-time. A hit-rate drop would mean neighbour-target demotion still matters now that BIP and the ghost are in place.
