# H01

Landing stash→target promotions at the MRU end of the target bucket while keeping demotions at the LRU end will raise hit-rate by protecting freshly re-referenced items from immediate re-eviction.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

All edits are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs` (segment extension point).

Today `DashSegment::get` calls `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Front)`,
and that single `insert_pos` is used for both *promotions* (stash→target,
target→probing) and *demotions* (`demote_from_normal_bucket` into target/stash).
As a result a stash hit lands at index 0 (LRU end) of its target bucket and is
the very next victim when another stash hit lands in that target. With 64-slot
buckets and ClassicLRU (evicts index 0), a just-proven item can be pushed back
to stash by the next promotion into that target.

Split the single position into two:

1. Change the signatures of `promote_to_target`, `promote_to_probing` and
   `promotion_get` to take two parameters: `promote_pos: InsertPosition` (where
   the promoted item itself lands) and `demote_pos: InsertPosition` (where items
   pushed down the cascade land). `promote_to_target` / `promote_to_probing`
   use `promote_pos` for their own `core.put_at(...)` call and pass
   `demote_pos` to `demote_from_normal_bucket` (whose signature stays as is;
   its `insert_pos` argument becomes `demote_pos`).
2. In `DashSegment::get`, call
   `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back, InsertPosition::Front)`:
   promotions land at MRU (`Back`), demoted items land at LRU (`Front`) of the
   bucket that receives them, so they stay first in line for eviction.
3. Leave the "is hottest" check for target→probing unchanged
   (`promotion_candidate_index()` = last slot under ClassicLRU). Note that with
   `Back` placement a stash-promoted item is immediately the hottest in its
   target, so its *next* hit promotes it to probing. That is intended:
   two hits after insertion move an item into the protected probing tier.
4. Keep the post-insert lookups and `unreachable!` guards (the item is still
   resident in target / probing after its own insert, because an insert never
   evicts the item it just placed).
5. Update the in-file unit tests that hard-code the old behaviour:
   `stash_hit_inserts_at_front_of_target` should now expect the newly promoted
   key at the back (`items[1] == keys[1]`), and the `target_hit_*` tests need
   their expected orderings/hit counts adjusted to the new MRU landing. The
   `*_position(InsertPosition, idx)` helper tests call `promote_to_*` directly;
   pass the same position for both new parameters there so they keep testing
   the same thing.

Invariants preserved: slot legality (only Target/Probing/Stash slots derived from
the key are used), capacity bound (all inserts go through `put_at`), uniqueness
(every move is `remove_at` followed by one insert).

## Rationale

The baseline is effectively an SLRU whose protected segment (target) admits new
members at its eviction end, so the protection is nearly nil. Separating
"promoted" placement from "demoted" placement is the classical SLRU fix and the
smallest movement change that should move hit-rate (0.6634) noticeably. Per-op
time should not change: the same number of bucket operations is performed.
