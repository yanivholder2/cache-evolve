# H01

Removing BIP's blind MRU path from the recency-gated ghost variant raises hit-rate, because the gate and the BIP removal filter different admission routes and their gains should add up.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0007/v002 (recency-gated ghost, age ≤ 4 epochs, still running the 4×-rarer BIP from cycle/0006/v003) and port cycle/0007/v001's BIP removal onto it:

- In the stash insertion path for a fresh key, i.e. the branch taken when there is no ghost hit **or** the ghost hit fails the recency gate, delete the bimodal choice. Always insert the key at the stash LRU (probation) end. Drop the periodic "1 in `BIP_PERIOD` goes to MRU" branch completely.
- Remove the BIP state: the `BIP_PERIOD` constant and the per-segment (or global) BIP insertion counter, plus any increment of it on the insert path. Delete the struct field rather than leaving it unused.
- Leave everything else exactly as in v002:
  - Ghost hits that pass the recency gate (age ≤ 4 epochs) still insert at stash MRU.
  - The epoch counter and the per-ghost-entry epoch tag are unchanged.
  - Demoted ex-residents still re-enter the stash at MRU with their pre-credit.
  - The stash→target promotion threshold, the second-chance stash victim scan and Front landing in normal buckets are unchanged.

Invariant: after this change, a key can reach stash MRU in only two ways, a recent ghost hit or a demotion from the normal tier.

## Rationale (optional)

Both v001 (no BIP, +0.0018 over the shared parent) and v002 (gated ghost, +0.005) gained on cycle/0006/v003. They change different branches: v001 the ghost-miss branch, v002 the ghost-hit branch. If they stack, the expected result is about 0.729 or higher. If the result stays at about 0.727, the gate already took back the capacity that BIP was wasting. Either outcome answers the cycle-0007 open question.
