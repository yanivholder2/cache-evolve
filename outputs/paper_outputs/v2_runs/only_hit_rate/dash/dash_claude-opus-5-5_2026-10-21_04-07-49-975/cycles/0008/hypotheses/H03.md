# H03

Grading where a ghost hit lands in the stash by its age raises hit-rate: very recent ghost hits go to MRU, moderately old ones to the middle of the stash bucket, and stale ones to probation. This keeps some of the gated-out reuse without taking the full protected MRU slot.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

In cycle/0007/v002, the ghost-hit branch of stash insertion currently makes a binary choice: age ≤ 4 epochs goes to stash MRU, and anything older is treated as a fresh key at the LRU/probation end. Replace this with three tiers, computed from `age = current_epoch - ghost_entry.epoch` (wrapping):

- **age ≤ 2:** insert at the stash MRU end, as today.
- **2 < age ≤ 4:** insert at the middle position of the stash bucket's recency order. Use index `stash_len / 2` in the same ordering the existing MRU/LRU insertion uses. Shift entries the same way the MRU insert does, so the order stays a valid permutation.
- **age > 4, or a ghost miss:** use the existing fresh-key path unchanged (BIP state included).

Implement the middle insertion as one helper next to the existing MRU and LRU stash insert helpers, for example `insert_stash_at(pos)`. The MRU and LRU calls can stay as they are or route through the same helper.

Do not change:

- epoch advancement and ghost table size
- the counter and pre-credit given to ghost-admitted keys (they get the same counter as today; only position changes)
- the eviction victim choice and the second-chance scan
- demotion MRU re-entry
- the promotion threshold

## Rationale (optional)

Across cycles, placement has been the lever and counters have not. Recent evidence beats old evidence, but the 0007 gate was a hard cutoff. A mid-position landing gives moderately recent returners a shorter protected lifetime. They survive if they reuse soon, and they age out before they displace ex-residents at MRU, so they take less of the zero-sum protected capacity than a full MRU admission. This tests whether the best evidence → placement mapping is graded rather than binary, which is a different lever from H02's cutoff sweep and H01's BIP removal.
