# H01

Raise the stash→target admission threshold from 2 to 3 on top of v003's MRU stash re-entry for demoted items to increase hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Start from cycle/0002/v003's source and port cycle/0002/v002's admission change into it:

- Set `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD` to `3` (v003 has `2`).
- On both demotion paths, the direct `true_target == source_idx` path and the cascade path, the pre-credit for an item demoted from the normal tier must stay `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1`, written in terms of the constant and not as a literal. Ex-residents then still return to their target after one stash hit, and only fresh keys need three. Check every site that seeds or compares the stash hit counter, and replace any hard-coded `1` or `2` that meant "threshold minus one" or "threshold" with the constant.
- Keep `DEMOTE_TO_STASH_POS = Back` (MRU re-entry for demoted items) unchanged. Fresh `put` keys still enter the stash at the default position. The target one-more-chance retry still uses the incoming `insert_pos`.
- Leave the target→probing edge and Front landing unchanged.

Invariants: the hit counter must not overflow its field width at a value of 3 (check the counter's type and bit width). No new metadata fields.

## Rationale (optional)

Both cycle-2 gains came from the "high bar for fresh keys, low bar plus protection for ex-residents" split, through different mechanisms: v002 on admission, v003 on retention. This variant tests the open question of whether they stack, or whether MRU-protected ex-residents crowd out fresh keys that are waiting for their third hit.
