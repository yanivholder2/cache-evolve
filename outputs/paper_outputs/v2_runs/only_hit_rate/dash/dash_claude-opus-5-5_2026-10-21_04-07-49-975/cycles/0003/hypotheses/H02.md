# H02

Make stash victim selection pick the first item with no stash hits nearest the LRU end, instead of the plain LRU item, to increase hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In v003's stash eviction path (where a full stash bucket picks a victim under ClassicLRU, today the item at the LRU end, index 0), replace the fixed victim choice with a bounded scan:

- Walk the stash bucket's slots from the LRU end toward the MRU end. Choose the first slot whose stash hit counter is `0`. That is a fresh key that has not been referenced again since it entered the stash, and it is not a pre-credited ex-resident.
- If no slot in the bucket has a counter of `0`, fall back to the current behaviour and evict the LRU-end item.
- Second-chance aging: every slot the scan skips (counter > 0) has its counter decremented by 1, but never below `0`. Protected items therefore cannot stay forever without new hits, and the scan finishes in at most one pass of the bucket.
- Do not change the slot ordering apart from removing the victim. Remove the victim with the bucket's existing remove/shift helper so the LRU order of the remaining items is kept.

Keep `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD = 2`, `DEMOTE_TO_STASH_POS = Back`, the `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1` pre-credit on both demotion paths, and all normal-tier logic exactly as in v003. No new metadata fields; reuse the existing per-slot stash hit counter.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 showed that the stash acts as a victim cache and that retention is where the current gains are. MRU re-entry protects a demoted item only by position, so a burst of fresh one-touch `put` keys can still push it out. A CLOCK-like, frequency-aware victim choice protects keys that have shown reuse (pre-credited ex-residents and keys partway to promotion) regardless of their position. It filters scan-like one-touch traffic in the stash itself. That is a different control from the admission threshold (H01) and from normal-tier routing (H03).
