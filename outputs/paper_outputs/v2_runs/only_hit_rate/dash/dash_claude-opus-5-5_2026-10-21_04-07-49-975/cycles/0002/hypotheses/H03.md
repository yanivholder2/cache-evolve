# H03

Inserting items demoted from the normal tier at the MRU end of their stash bucket, instead of the LRU end, will raise hit-rate by making the stash act as a victim cache that keeps pre-credited ex-residents long enough to win back their slot.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

All edits are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. This changes where demoted items land *in the stash* only. Admission thresholds and normal-tier placement stay as they are.

Right now `DashSegment::get` passes `InsertPosition::Front` to `promotion_get`. That position is passed down to `demote_from_normal_bucket`, which uses it for its `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, insert_pos, ...)` calls. An item that had earned normal-tier residency, and that v003 pre-credits with `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1` stash hits, therefore lands at index 0 of a full stash bucket. ClassicLRU evicts index 0, so it is the next item thrown out of the cache. The pre-credit is wasted unless the key is re-referenced right away.

1. In `demote_from_normal_bucket`, use `InsertPosition::Back` for the two `Slot::Stash` inserts (the direct `true_target == source_idx` branch and the `cascade` branch). Keep the `Slot::Target` one-more-chance retry on the incoming `insert_pos`, so normal-tier ordering does not change. Add a doc comment explaining that stash re-entries from demotion are placed at MRU.
2. Leave `promote_to_target`, `promote_to_probing`, `promotion_get`, the PROMOTE_THRESHOLD logic and the pre-credits unchanged. Leave the stash insert position used by `put` for new keys unchanged, so fresh keys still enter stash at the default position.
3. Update the tests: `direct_to_stash_position` and `cascade_to_stash_position` currently expect the stash index to follow `insert_pos`. Change the Front-variant tests (`direct_to_stash_at_front_when_cold`, `cascade_to_stash_at_front_when_cold`) to expect the demoted item at the MRU index (1, after the pre-existing `stash_pre`), and rename them to describe the new behaviour. The Back variants stay as they are.

Invariants: the item and slot are the same as before; only its index inside the stash bucket changes. All inserts still go through `put_at`, so capacity and uniqueness hold.

## Rationale

H01 and H02 tighten admission. This hypothesis works on retention: whether the stash holds on to the keys that are most likely to come back. Items demoted from the normal tier have shown reuse at least twice, which makes them better stash residents than one-touch keys. Placing them at stash MRU is a cheap way to make them live longer than one-hit arrivals, in the spirit of SLRU/2Q, and it adds no extra normal-tier moves. Cycle 0001 showed that extra normal-tier moves cause demotion cascades.
