# H02

Raising v003's stash→target admission threshold from two stash hits to three, while still letting demoted ex-residents return after one hit, will raise hit-rate by filtering medium-reuse keys out of the normal tier as well.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

All edits are in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. This is a parametric probe of the v003 mechanism. Nothing else in the logic changes.

1. Change `const PROMOTE_THRESHOLD: usize = 2;` to `const PROMOTE_THRESHOLD: usize = 3;`.
2. Keep the demotion pre-credit expression `PROMOTE_THRESHOLD - 1` on both stash inserts in `demote_from_normal_bucket` (direct and `cascade`). It now evaluates to 2, so an item that already earned normal-tier residency still needs only **one** stash hit to return. Only fresh keys face the stricter bar. Add a short comment noting this.
3. Keep the `lfu_counter = 0` reset on promotion in `promotion_get`'s stash branch. Leave the target and probing branches unchanged.
4. Update the in-file tests that drive a fresh key into the target through `put` + gets (`first_stash_hit_stays_in_stash`, `stash_hit_inserts_at_front_of_target`, `target_hit_*`, `cascade_demotion_probing_to_full_target_to_stash`, `last_bucket_probing_wraps_to_zero`). Each needs three stash gets instead of two. For `first_stash_hit_stays_in_stash`, assert the key is still in stash after the second get and in the target after the third. Add a test that an item demoted to stash returns to its target after a single get.

Invariants: no Item layout change and no new slots. A sub-threshold hit only reorders items within one stash bucket.

## Rationale

The cycle-0001 insight asks whether two is the best threshold. The stash (24×64) is three times the size of the normal tier (8×64), so it has room to hold keys through a third reference. If hit-rate rises again, the admission edge has not reached its optimum yet. If it falls, two is near the optimum, and future cycles should move to the other edges (H01) and to the stash's own retention (H03). The two outcomes clearly separate the explanations, and the test also probes per-op-time, since fewer remove/insert cascades on gets are expected.
