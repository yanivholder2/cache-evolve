# H02

Letting a key seen twice be admitted directly at the MRU slot while first-time admitted keys go to the LRU end of the bucket will raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

In the incumbent's insert path (after the second-sighting filter passes), keep admission as-is but add a one-bit "recently re-admitted" notion: if the key passed the filter AND the filter bit was set by an earlier eviction-free sighting, insert at MRU position; otherwise (e.g. when the bucket is not full and the key passes) insert at the position next to MRU rather than at MRU, so that hits promote to MRU (existing behaviour) and unproven entries are first to be victimised. Do not change victim selection, probing, or stash logic. Preserve all invariants of bucket slot ordering.

## Rationale (optional)

Protect entries with proven hits over just-admitted ones; small change on the proven path.
