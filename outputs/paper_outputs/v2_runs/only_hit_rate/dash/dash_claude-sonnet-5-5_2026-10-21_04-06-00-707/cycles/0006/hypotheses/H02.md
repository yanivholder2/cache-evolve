# H02

Insert each admitted key into the less-loaded of two hash-derived candidate buckets (power-of-two-choices) to raise hit-rate by cutting forced evictions in hot buckets.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the overlay's insert path, after the second-sighting filter admits a key, compute a second bucket index from an independent hash (for example, rehash the key with a different odd multiplier). Count the occupied slots in the primary and the alternate bucket. Insert into the one with fewer occupied slots, and break ties toward the primary. Lookup must probe both candidate buckets (primary first, then alternate) before the stash, and promotion and hit handling must work on whichever bucket holds the entry. Preserve existing capacity, the filter, and the MRU-on-hit behaviour. Keep the segment/bucket locking and concurrency invariants of the baseline. If the second probe breaks them, hold both bucket locks in index order.

## Rationale (optional)

Bucket and probing structure is the main untried lever. Placement-order tweaks are saturated, but load balance across buckets is not.
