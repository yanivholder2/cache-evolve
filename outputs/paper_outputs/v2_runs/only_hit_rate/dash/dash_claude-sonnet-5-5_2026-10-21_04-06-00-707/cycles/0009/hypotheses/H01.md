# H01

Make the ghost-table counter restoration conditional on the returning key's saved counter being at least 2 to raise hit-rate while cutting wasted ghost writes.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In the eviction path of the overlay in `src/dash/` that writes the ghost entry for an evicted victim, only record the ghost entry when the victim's two-bit counter was >= 2 at eviction time (skip cold victims). On admitted misses, keep the ghost lookup, but restore the counter to min(saved, 2) instead of a flat "at least 1". Keep the second-sighting admission filter unconditional and unchanged. Preserve all eviction scan and aging logic.

## Rationale (optional)

Ghost entries for cold victims likely add noise by pinning one-hit keys at counter 1. Filtering by saved heat should keep the gain and reduce ghost churn.
