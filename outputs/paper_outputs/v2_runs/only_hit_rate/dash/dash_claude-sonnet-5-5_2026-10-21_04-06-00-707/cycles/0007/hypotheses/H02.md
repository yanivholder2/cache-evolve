# H02

Make the reuse-bit victim scan prefer unreferenced slots in the stash before the home bucket to raise hit-rate by retaining reused main-bucket entries.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

In the eviction function of the v001 overlay, change the scan order. Currently the first unreferenced slot found is evicted. New behaviour: pass 1 scans stash slots only and evicts the first unreferenced one; pass 2 scans the target bucket for the first unreferenced slot; pass 3 (all referenced) clears the referenced flags of the target bucket slots (second chance) and evicts the slot at the LRU end of the bucket (the position-based victim used by the baseline). Do not touch the admission filter, promotion, or flag set/clear sites. Invariant: the number of resident entries and lookup semantics are unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Currently all-referenced buckets fall back to an unspecified path; the explicit second-chance clearing avoids permanent pinning of stale referenced entries.
