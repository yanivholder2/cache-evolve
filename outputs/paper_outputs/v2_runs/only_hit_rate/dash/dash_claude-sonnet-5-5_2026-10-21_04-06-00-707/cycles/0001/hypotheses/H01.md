# H01

Insert stash-hit items at the MRU end (InsertPosition::Back) of the target bucket instead of the LRU end to raise hit rate on workload-a.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, change `DashSegment::get` to call `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back)` instead of `InsertPosition::Front`. This makes stash→target promotions, probing→target retries and cascades to stash all land at MRU, so recently-hit items are not immediately the next victim. Leave `promotion_get`, `promote_to_target`, `promote_to_probing`, `demote_from_normal_bucket` otherwise untouched. Note that the in-file unit tests assert Front behaviour via `get`; update `stash_hit_inserts_at_front_of_target` and related tests that go through `get` as needed so `cargo test` still passes (the helper tests that pass `insert_pos` explicitly are unaffected).

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline's cold-insert design penalises items that just proved themselves with a hit; on recency-skewed LIRS traces MRU insertion should retain them longer.
