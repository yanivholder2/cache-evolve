# H01

Make the reuse counter saturate higher (3 bits, max 7) with a decrement-by-one aging in the evictor scan to raise hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

In the eviction path (the per-bucket victim scan that decrements the aged reuse counter), widen the per-slot counter from 2 bits to 3 bits (saturating at 7). A hit increments by 1 (saturating). During the evictor scan, keep local aging (decrement each scanned non-victim slot by 1) and choose the slot with the lowest counter as victim. Keep the second-sighting admission filter and the v002 all-hot-bucket drop rule, but define "hot" as counter >= 2 so the drop rule still triggers. Preserve all other behaviour and the public API.

## Rationale (optional)

Counter width is the one reuse-state parameter not yet varied; distinguishes frequently from occasionally reused keys.
