# H02

Remove the all-hot-bucket drop rule from v002 and instead break victim ties by evicting the oldest-inserted slot, targeting hit-rate with a simpler eviction path.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Delete the branch that drops an incoming key when every slot in its bucket has a nonzero counter; always evict a victim. Among slots tied for the lowest counter, pick the one inserted earliest (use slot index order as a proxy for insertion age, since MRU-on-promotion keeps hot entries toward the front: choose the last slot among ties). Leave the admission filter and counter aging unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's gain over its parent was noise-level, so reverting tests whether the drop rule contributes anything and whether tie-breaking by position helps.
