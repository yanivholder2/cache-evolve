# H03

Demote displaced victims to the LRU (front) end of the stash/probing bucket from baseline to cut demotion-cascade evictions of useful items and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In baseline `DashSegment`, find the cascade/demotion path where an item displaced from the target bucket is moved into the probing bucket or stash. Make the displaced item land at the cold end (`InsertPosition::Front`) of its destination so that it is the first victim there, rather than evicting an existing warmer entry. Leave `get`/`promotion_get` as in baseline. No new metadata, no change to bucket sizes.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001 found movement-induced cascades hurt; making demoted items cold limits their collateral damage. Tests an independent lever from v001 on the baseline.
