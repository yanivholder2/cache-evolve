# H01

Restoring a ghost-remembered reuse counter on readmission of recently evicted keys will raise hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In the overlay of the dash cache (the file holding the eviction/admission logic from cycle 7 v001), add a small fixed-size ghost table (e.g. 4096 entries per cache, direct-mapped by key hash, storing a 16-bit key fingerprint plus the victim's 2-bit counter at eviction time). On eviction of a victim, write its fingerprint and counter into the ghost slot. On a miss that passes the existing one-bit second-sighting admission filter, look up the ghost table: if the fingerprint matches, insert the new entry with its counter initialised to max(1, saved counter) and clear the ghost slot; otherwise insert with counter 0 as today. Keep the admission filter unconditional and unchanged. Keep the aging-on-scan logic unchanged. Invariants: no change to the public API, no change to the hit path; the ghost table must be allocated once at construction and be thread-safe in the same way as the existing filter (relaxed atomics are fine).

## Rationale (optional)

Evicted-then-returning keys are the ones the counter cannot see. Remembering their history is a structurally different lever from refining the live counter.
