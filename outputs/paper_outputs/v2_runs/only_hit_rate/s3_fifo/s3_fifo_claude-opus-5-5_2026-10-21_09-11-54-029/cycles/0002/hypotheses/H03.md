# H03

Shrinking the small queue further, from 10% to 5% of capacity (102/1946 at capacity 2048), will raise hit-rate on workload-a by giving main and the ghost queue even more room.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, in `try_new`, change the small-queue sizing from `(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 20).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`. Keep the `capacity == 1` special case. `main_size = capacity - small_size` is unchanged in form, and the ghost stays sized to `main_size` as in v001 (so it grows to 1946).

Do not touch the graduation threshold (`freq > 1`), ghost re-admission, `evict_main`, `MAX_FREQ`, or the data structures.

Update the inline test `small_queue_is_ten_percent_of_capacity`: rename it to `small_queue_is_five_percent_of_capacity` and assert `small_size == 102` and `main_size == 1946` for capacity 2048. All other tests (including the capacity-4 and capacity-5 ones, where `capacity / 20` clamps to 1) must still pass. Check that `ghost_hit_promotes_back_to_main` and `small_eviction_promotes_to_main_after_multiple_hits` still hold with small_size = 1 at capacity 4.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001 showed that moving from a 25% to a 10% small queue helped, and that the ghost queue cushions a shorter probation window. This variant extends the partition sweep in the same direction to see whether hit-rate is still rising or has peaked near 10%. If it regresses, the probation window has become too short for keys that need two hits to graduate.
