# H02

Graduating small-queue entries to the main queue after any reuse (freq ≥ 1) instead of two reuses, and resetting their frequency to 0 when they arrive in main, raises hit rate on workload-a by keeping more reused keys resident.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change two things in `S3Fifo::evict_small` (src/s3_fifo/mod.rs):

1. **Graduation threshold.** Change the condition `if tail.freq > 1` to
   `if tail.freq >= 1`. A small-queue entry that got even one `get` hit during
   its small-queue stay now graduates to the main queue. Only entries with
   `freq == 0` are demoted to the ghost queue.
2. **Frequency reset on graduation.** Set `tail.freq = 0` before
   `self.main.push_front(tail)`. A graduated entry then starts in main with no
   leftover second-chance credit and has to be re-referenced while in main to
   survive `evict_main`. This stops one burst of small-queue hits from buying up
   to 3 extra passes through main.

Keep the order of operations the same: call `evict_main()` first if
`self.main.len() >= self.main_size`, then push the graduated entry to the
front of main. Leave the ghost re-admission path in `put`, `evict_main`, the
partition sizes in `try_new`, and `MAX_FREQ` unchanged. Capacity invariants
(`small.len() <= small_size`, `main.len() <= main_size`) do not change.

Update `small_eviction_promotes_to_main_after_multiple_hits` and
`main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance` only if they assert on the old
threshold or on freq values after graduation. The policy change above is the
spec.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline needs two hits inside a small queue of 512 entries. On loop-heavy
LIRS-style traces, many keys are reused only once within that window, so they
get demoted to the ghost queue and have to come back through a ghost hit, which
is a miss. A threshold of 1 matches the common libCacheSim configuration. The
freq reset keeps the main queue from filling with entries whose credit was
earned only during their burst in the small queue. This idea is separate from
H01's partition resize.
