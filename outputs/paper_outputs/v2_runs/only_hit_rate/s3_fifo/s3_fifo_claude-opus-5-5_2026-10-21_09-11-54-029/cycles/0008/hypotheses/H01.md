## Rationale

Cycle 7 noted that the cap and decay verdicts (cap 7 best, halving slightly negative) were measured under FIFO reinsertion. Under SIEVE, each credit costs a full hand lap of residency, so a cap-7 key can stay up to 7 laps after its last hit. Clear-on-pass is the far end of the retention schedule. If hit-rate drops beyond ±0.001, multi-lap credit still matters under SIEVE and the cap/decrement rule is confirmed. If it rises, the SIEVE gain came from evicting stale survivors sooner, and lower caps (2–3) should be probed next.
