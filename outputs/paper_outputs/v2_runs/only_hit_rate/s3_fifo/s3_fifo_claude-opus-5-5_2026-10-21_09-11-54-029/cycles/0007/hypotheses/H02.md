# H02

Replacing main's FIFO-reinsertion rotation with SIEVE-style in-place retention will raise hit-rate on workload-a, because survivors keep their queue position instead of jumping ahead of newer entries.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Change only how the main queue picks an eviction victim. The small queue, ghost ring, graduation rule (`freq > 1`), ghost re-admission (insert at main head with freq 0), main victims recorded in the ghost, the 5% split and `MAX_FREQ = 7` all stay unchanged.

1. Replace `main: VecDeque<usize>` with an intrusive doubly linked list over slab slots. Add `prev: u32` and `next: u32` to `Entry`, using `u32::MAX` as the null sentinel. Add `main_head`, `main_tail`, `main_len: usize` and `hand: u32` to `S3Fifo`. Insertions (the ghost-hit path in `put` and graduation in `evict_small`) link the slot at `main_head`. The insertion helper is `main_push_head(idx)`.
2. Rewrite `evict_main()` as a SIEVE sweep. Start at `hand`, or at `main_tail` if `hand` is null. Look at the entry there:
   - If `freq > 0`, decrement `freq` by 1, leave the entry where it is, and move `hand` to `prev` (toward the head). If `prev` is null, wrap to `main_tail`.
   - If `freq == 0`, this is the victim. Set `hand` to its `prev` (null is allowed, meaning restart at the tail), unlink it, `release` it, `ghost_insert` its key, and stop.
   Keep the linear decrement (do not clear freq to 0) so the cap-7 retention semantics carry over.
3. Update every user of `main.len()` to use `main_len`. Update the `#[cfg(test)]` helpers (`test_push_main_back`/`front`, `queue_keys`/`main_keys`) to walk the linked list from head to tail. Keep their signatures.
4. The ring-reference test `matches_ring_reference_on_pseudo_random_streams` no longer applies to main ordering. Change its reference `RefS3Fifo::evict_main` to the same SIEVE semantics: a `VecDeque` plus a hand index, where survivors stay in place. Keep the small, ghost and residency assertions. `max_freq_key_survives_seven_main_passes_then_evicted_on_eighth` and `main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance` may need their expected victims updated to SIEVE order. Document each change in a comment.

Invariants to keep: `index.len() == resident_len()`, resident count ≤ capacity, and the ghost bound tests.

## Rationale (optional)

Every partition, credit, graduation and re-admission lever is flat or negative. The order in which main evicts is the one axis never touched. SIEVE (Zhang et al., NSDI '24) differs from FIFO reinsertion in one way: retained objects do not move ahead of new arrivals. New and ghost-readmitted freq-0 keys therefore sit at the head, behind the hand. Old survivors near the hand get re-examined sooner once their credit drains, so cold re-entries are not evicted ahead of them as early. A delta beyond ±0.001 in either direction is informative.
