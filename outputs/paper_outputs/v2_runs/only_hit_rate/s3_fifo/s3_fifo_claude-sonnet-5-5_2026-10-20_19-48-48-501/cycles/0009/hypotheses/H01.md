# H01

Add hit-path promotion from the small queue to main when freq reaches 2, on top of the incumbent's higher freq cap, to raise hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In the cache's `get` hit branch, after incrementing the entry's freq (saturating at the parent's raised cap), check whether the entry lives in the small queue and its freq is >= 2. If so, remove it from small and insert it at the tail of main (the same position eviction-time promotion uses). Keep the small and ghost sizing (~5% small queue, shortened ghost), the eviction-time promotion logic and the main second-chance reinsertion scan unchanged. Update the size and occupancy counters when moving the entry. This isolates hit-path promotion from sizing, since the parent already has the sizing.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 of cycle 0008 gained but was confounded with bundled sizing. The incumbent already has the sizing, so any gain here comes from the promotion.
