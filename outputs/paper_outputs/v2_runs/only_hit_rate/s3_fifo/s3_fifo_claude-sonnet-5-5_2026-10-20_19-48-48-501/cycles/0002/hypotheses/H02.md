# H02

Doubling ghost capacity to 2×main_size on top of v002 will raise hit-rate by remembering evicted keys longer, catching reuse distances beyond the cache size.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In src/s3_fifo/mod.rs introduce `ghost_cap = main_size * 2` (a field or computed value in `try_new`, also used for `VecDeque::with_capacity` of ghost). Replace the `self.main_size` comparisons that bound the ghost in both `evict_main` (the `while self.ghost.len() >= self.main_size` loop) and `evict_small` (`if self.ghost.len() >= self.main_size`) with `ghost_cap`. Everything else (small_size = capacity/4, promotion gate `freq > 1`, ghost-hit insertion into main with freq 0) stays as in v002. Note metadata-size will grow; the claim targets hit-rate.
