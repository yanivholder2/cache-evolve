Shrinking the small queue from capacity/4 to capacity/10 on top of v002 will raise hit-rate on the LIRS workload by leaving more room for proven-reuse items in main.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `S3Fifo::try_new` (src/s3_fifo/mod.rs), change `small_size` from `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` (keep capacity==1 special case). `main_size = capacity - small_size` as before. Keep v002's main-victim ghosting, the `freq > 1` promotion gate, ghost-hit-to-main behaviour and ghost cap (`main_size`) untouched. Capacity bound invariant must hold.
