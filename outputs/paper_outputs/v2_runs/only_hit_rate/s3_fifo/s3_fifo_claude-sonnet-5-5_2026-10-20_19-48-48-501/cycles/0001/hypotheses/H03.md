# H03

Admitting ghost-hit keys into main with an initial frequency of 1 will raise hit-rate by giving proven-reused keys a second chance against main eviction.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, `put()` ghost-hit branch: after constructing `Entry::new(key, value)` and before `self.main.push_front(...)`, set `entry.freq = 1`. Leave the rest of `put`, `evict_small`, `evict_main`, and `get` unchanged. Capacity bounds and ghost removal (`retain`) remain as-is.

## Rationale (optional)

A ghost hit is evidence of reuse distance longer than the small queue; with freq 0 the entry is the first to be evicted from main's tail on the next pressure, wasting the signal.
