# H03

Capping the freq counter at 2 instead of 3 in the baseline will speed up demotion of stale items in main and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the baseline S3-FIFO source, find where the per-entry freq counter is incremented on a hit (probably `min(freq + 1, 3)`). Lower the cap to 2. Leave the small and main queue sizes, the promotion gate, the ghost handling and the linear decrement in main eviction unchanged. Entries that reach freq 2 still get promoted or reinserted as before, but they can never accumulate more than 2 reinsertion chances.

## Rationale (optional)

Faster halving of freq hurt in cycle 2. This tests a different aging mechanism, a lower cap, from the baseline. The result can later be combined with the smaller small queue.
