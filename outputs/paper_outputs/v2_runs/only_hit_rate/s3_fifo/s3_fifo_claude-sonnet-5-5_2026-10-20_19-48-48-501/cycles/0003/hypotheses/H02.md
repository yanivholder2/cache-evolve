# H02

Shortening the ghost queue to half its length on top of the smaller small queue will tighten admission filtering and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In the overlay's S3-FIFO source, locate the ghost queue capacity (the limit used when pushing evicted keys into the ghost FIFO, including the main-victim ghosting added in cycle 1). Halve it, for example from `capacity` to `capacity / 2`, with a minimum of 1. Keep the small queue at capacity/10 and leave the promotion gate and the main freq decrement unchanged. When the ghost is full, evict the oldest ghost entry exactly as before.

## Rationale (optional)

A longer ghost hurt in cycle 2 because it loosened admission. This tests the opposite direction.
