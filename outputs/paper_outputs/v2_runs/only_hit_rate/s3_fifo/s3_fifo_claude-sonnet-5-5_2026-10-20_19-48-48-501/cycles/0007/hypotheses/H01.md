# H01

Requiring freq>=2 (instead of >=1) for small-to-main promotion on top of the 0006/v003 sizing will raise hit-rate by keeping weakly-reused keys out of main.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

In the small-queue eviction routine (the function that pops the small FIFO tail and decides between promoting to main and ghosting), change the promotion test from `freq > 0` to `freq >= 2`. Items with freq < 2 go to the ghost as before. Keep the small queue at ~5% of capacity, the ghost at ~0.5x main, main-evictee ghosting, and ghost-hit admission (to main, freq=0) unchanged. Do not touch the main-queue eviction scan or the freq cap. Confirm the behaviour change: the promotion branch must differ from v003 (earlier "strictness" tests may have been no-ops), so ensure the small-queue freq counter can actually reach 2 while an item is in small (increment on each get hit, saturating at the existing cap).

## Rationale (optional)

Loosening admission lost in prior cycles; strictness has never been verified as a behaviour change.
