Combining cycle 0003 v001's smaller small queue with a ghost queue halved to capacity/2 will raise hit-rate through stricter admission.

## Parent

## Changes

In the S3-FIFO source, keep the small queue sizing at max(1, capacity/20). Change the ghost queue capacity to max(1, capacity/2) (cycle 0003 v002 did this on the earlier line and tied at 0.728). Leave the promotion gate, the ghost-hit path, and main eviction (freq decrement and cap) unchanged. Preserve the invariant that ghost entries are evicted FIFO and that the ghost never holds keys currently resident.
