# H02

Cap the number of reinsertions per main eviction call and evict the tail afterwards, to bound per-op time while keeping hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

In the main-queue eviction loop, add a counter of reinsertions performed in the current eviction call. After 8 reinsertions (tail freq>0 → decrement and move to head), stop looping and evict the current tail entry regardless of its freq (ghosting it as usual). Leave small-queue sizing, ghost length, promotion threshold, and v002's insertion logic untouched. Invariants: eviction always terminates in at most 9 pops; capacity is respected. This tests whether the unbounded-scan worst case matters for hit-rate at all (expected: neutral hit-rate, lower worst-case work).
