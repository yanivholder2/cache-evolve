# H03

Promote small-queue victims to main only when freq>=1 AND they were seen in the ghost, otherwise admit on freq>=2, to raise hit-rate via recency-aware admission instead of ghost bonuses.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from baseline S3-FIFO, apply three simple changes: (1) on main-queue eviction, insert the evicted key into the ghost (as in cycle 1's win); (2) shrink the small queue to roughly 5% of capacity and shorten the ghost to about 0.5x main size (the saturated gains from cycles 2-4 in one step, without extra state); (3) on a ghost hit insert directly into main with freq=0 (no boost, no grace). Do not add any per-key ghost flags. This checks whether the incumbents' gains reproduce from baseline in a simpler, smaller diff, so a cleaner parent can be used later.
