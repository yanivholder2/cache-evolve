# H02

Making the small-queue eviction promote on freq>=1 but tie-break by moving small-queue victims with freq>=2 straight to main's head will raise hit-rate by retaining hot keys.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the small-queue eviction routine, split the promotion branch on the entry's freq. Entries with freq>=2 are inserted at the front (next to be evicted last, i.e. the most protected end) of main, instead of the tail, while freq==1 entries are inserted as in the parent. Implement by giving main a queue that supports push at the protected end (for example a VecDeque push_front relative to the eviction end). Do not change the promotion threshold, ghost length, small ratio or freq cap. Verify the diff changes behaviour: main insertion position must differ for freq>=2 entries.
