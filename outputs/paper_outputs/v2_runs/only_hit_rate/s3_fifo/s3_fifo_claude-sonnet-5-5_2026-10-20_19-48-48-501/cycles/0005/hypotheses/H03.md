# H03

Replacing the baseline's ghost-hit-to-main admission with a ghost that remembers a per-key hit count (ghost-hit keys re-enter main with freq=2 if they had been hit while in small) will raise hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Start from the baseline S3-FIFO overlay. In the small-queue eviction path, when an entry with freq==0 is evicted to the ghost, record its last-seen freq (0 or 1 bits) in the ghost entry, and also ghost main-queue victims (as the cycle 1 win did) recording freq=2-flag. On a ghost hit during insert, admit directly to main with initial freq = 2 if the ghost entry's flag is set, else freq = 0. Keep ghost length at capacity/2 and the small queue at the reduced ratio used in cycle 0004/v001 (about 1/20 to 1/10 of capacity, copy the parent's value). Ghost storage becomes a map key -> flag; preserve the FIFO eviction order of the ghost.
