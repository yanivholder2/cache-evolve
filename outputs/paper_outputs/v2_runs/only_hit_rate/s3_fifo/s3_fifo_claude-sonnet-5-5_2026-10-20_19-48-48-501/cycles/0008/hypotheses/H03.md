# H03

Promoting an item from the small queue to main immediately on its second hit (instead of at small-eviction time) will raise hit rate by protecting reused items from small-queue churn.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline source, first apply the sizing-only configuration (small queue ~5% of capacity, shortened ghost). Then in the `get`/hit path: when a hit lands on an entry residing in the small queue and its freq (after increment) reaches 2, move that entry directly to the main queue's insertion end (remove it from small, adjust the small/main size counters, reset freq to 0). Use lazy removal or the existing deletion method for the small queue so the queue stays consistent. The small-eviction logic is unchanged for items that never reach freq 2. Preserve the capacity invariant and ghost behaviour.

## Rationale

Prior promotion-gate tweaks tied, possibly because they are no-ops at eviction time; acting on the hit path changes behaviour verifiably and tests a structurally new lever.
