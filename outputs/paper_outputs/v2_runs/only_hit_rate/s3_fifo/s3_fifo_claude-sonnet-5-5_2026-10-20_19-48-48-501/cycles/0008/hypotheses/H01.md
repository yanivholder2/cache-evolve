# H01

Adaptively resizing the small queue from ghost-hit feedback will raise hit rate over the fixed ~5% small queue.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In the cache's insert/ghost-hit path, add two counters: `ghost_hits_window` (admissions served via a ghost hit) and `small_evict_to_main_window` (small evictees promoted to main), reset every `capacity` requests. Make the small-queue target size a variable `small_target` initialised to the current ~5% of capacity. At each window end: if ghost hits exceed 2% of window requests, grow `small_target` by 1% of capacity (cap 20%); if zero ghost hits and promotions are below 0.5% of requests, shrink by 1% (floor 2%). Use `small_target` wherever the fixed small-size threshold is used for deciding when to evict from small. Keep ghost length, promotion gate and main eviction untouched. Invariants: total resident items never exceed capacity; ghost length remains the existing short value.

## Rationale

Sizing is the only lever that has paid off; a fixed ratio may be a local optimum, and feedback could find a better one without hand tuning.
