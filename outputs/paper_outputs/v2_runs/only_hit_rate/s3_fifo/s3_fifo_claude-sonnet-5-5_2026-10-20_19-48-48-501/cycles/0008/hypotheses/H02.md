# H02

Raising the frequency counter cap (e.g. to 7) in the sizing-only configuration will raise hit rate by preserving more reuse history in main.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Locate the per-entry frequency saturation constant (the cap used when incrementing freq on a hit) and raise it from its current value to 7 (3 bits). Keep the promotion gate, the decrement-by-one on main reinsertion, small size and ghost length unchanged. Make sure the freq field type can hold 7 (widen the integer field if it is a 2-bit packed type). Past cycles showed lowering the cap lost, so this tests the opposite direction; main-eviction second-chance loops then give hot items more passes before eviction.

## Rationale

The workload rewards keeping reuse history; a larger cap is the untested direction.
