# H02

Shrinking the small queue to about 2% of capacity on the 0009/v001 sizing will raise hit-rate if the optimum lies below 3%.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In the overlay's constructor, change the small-queue capacity to `max(1, capacity * 2 / 100)`. Main gets the remainder. Keep the ghost length as in the parent, scaled to the new main size only if it is already derived from capacity. Keep the promotion rules and main eviction unchanged. Do not add any other change.

## Rationale (optional)

Insight says smaller small queues beat baseline. This tests whether the trend continues past 3%.
