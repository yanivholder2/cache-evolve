# H01

Raising the small-queue share to about 5% of capacity on the 0008/v002 sizing, with its shortened ghost unchanged, will raise hit-rate by locating the sizing optimum between 3% and larger.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In the overlay's S3-FIFO constructor (where the small queue capacity is computed from total capacity), change the small-queue capacity to `max(1, capacity * 5 / 100)`. Leave the main capacity as the remainder. Leave the ghost length, promotion rule (freq threshold) and main-eviction second-chance scan exactly as in the parent. Do not touch any other logic.

## Rationale (optional)

No head-to-head comparison of 3%, 5% and larger exists on a shared parent. This cohort probes the 5% point.
