# H02

Replacing the linear ghost `retain` and `iter().any` scans with an indexed ghost structure will cut per-op-time on the miss path while leaving the hit-rate of cycle/0009/v002 unchanged at 0.717.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Keep the zero-frequency push in `evict_main` from cycle/0009/v002 unchanged. Change only the ghost queue representation. Replace the `Vec`-backed ghost queue, which is scanned with `retain` on re-admission and `iter().any` on lookup, with a `VecDeque` of `(key, sequence)` entries plus a `HashMap<key, sequence>` index that records the latest sequence for each resident ghost key. On ghost push, assign the next sequence number, insert or overwrite the index entry, and push to the deque. On ghost lookup, check the index in O(1). On ghost hit, remove the index entry, so the stale deque entry is skipped lazily. When the ghost capacity is exceeded, pop from the front of the deque and remove the index entry only if its sequence matches (so a re-pushed key is not dropped early). Eviction ordering must match v002 exactly: a key is a ghost member if and only if it is in the index, and ghost capacity is counted on live entries only. Do not change the small or main queue logic, the promotion bar, or the frequency handling. Keep the existing ghost-window size constant.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle 0009 insight flags that v002 adds ghost entries without changing the cost of ghost lookups, and that the linear ghost scan is the likely dominant miss-path cost. This hypothesis holds hit-rate fixed and tests whether removing the scan frees per-op-time. A rerun must report per-op-time and metadata-size alongside hit-rate, and confirm the overlay edit is present in the built code.
