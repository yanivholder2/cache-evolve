# H03

Feeding main-queue evictions into the ghost queue only when their frequency is at least 1 will raise hit-rate on lirs workload-a at capacity 2048, because the gain in cycle/0009/v002 comes from warm keys rather than cold ones.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline, add a ghost push in `evict_main`, the complement of cycle/0009/v002. Where v002 pushes a victim only when its frequency is zero, this variant pushes a victim only when its frequency is greater than or equal to 1, and does nothing for zero-frequency victims. The pushed key goes into the existing ghost queue through the same push helper the small-queue eviction path already uses, so ghost capacity and the ghost lookup logic are unchanged. Keep the existing ghost-hit path, which re-admits a ghost hit through the small queue as in the baseline. Do not change the small-queue promotion bar, the frequency decay or cap, the reinsert position, or the ghost window size. Keep all other eviction behaviour identical to the baseline.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 showed that feeding zero-frequency main victims into the ghost queue gives a small hit-rate gain. Those keys were cold, so the gain may not come from re-reference of warm keys. Feeding warm victims is the opposite choice and was not tested. A flat or negative result would show the v002 gain is specific to cold main-evicted keys. A positive result would indicate the ghost feed helps warm keys more.
