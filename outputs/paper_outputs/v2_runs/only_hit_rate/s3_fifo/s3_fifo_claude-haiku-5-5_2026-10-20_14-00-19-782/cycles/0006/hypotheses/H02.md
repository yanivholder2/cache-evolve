# H02

Giving main-queue victims with nonzero frequency a second pass at the tail, instead of demoting them to the ghost queue, will raise hit-rate on Zipfian traces by keeping warm main-queue residents longer.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the main-queue eviction path (the reclaim loop that runs when the main queue is full), change the handling of a candidate whose frequency is greater than zero. Currently such a candidate is decremented and reinserted at the head, and a zero-frequency candidate is evicted. Replace the decrement-and-head-reinsert with reinsert at the tail, and decrement its frequency by one. Only zero-frequency candidates are evicted, as before. Cap the number of reinsert passes per eviction at the main-queue length so the loop terminates. Leave the small-queue path, the promotion bar (two hits), the ghost queue, and the admission logic unchanged. Keep the frequency cap value as in baseline. Report hit-rate, metadata-size, and per-op-time.
