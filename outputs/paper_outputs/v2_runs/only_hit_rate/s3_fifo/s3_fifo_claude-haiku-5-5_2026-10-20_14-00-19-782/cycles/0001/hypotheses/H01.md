# H01

Lower the small-queue promotion threshold from two hits to one so that any reused small-queue entry graduates to the main queue, raising hit-rate on the lirs workload-a trace at size 2048.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/s3_fifo.rs`, change the promotion predicate in `evict_small()` from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 0`. Keep everything else in `evict_small()` intact: when the predicate holds, still call `evict_main()` first if `self.main.len() >= self.main_size`, then `push_front` the tail onto `main`; otherwise still push the key into `ghost` with the existing ghost-cap check. Do not change `get()`, `put()`, `evict_main()`, `MAX_FREQ`, or `try_new()`. Preserve the invariant `small.len() + main.len() <= capacity`, and do not add fields to `S3Fifo` or `Entry`, so metadata-size stays at 24 bytes for `Entry` and 112 bytes for `S3Fifo`.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline's `evict_small()` demands two `get` hits (freq saturates at 3 and starts at 0) before an admission-queue entry is promoted, so a key hit exactly once while in small is treated as cold and its key goes to ghost. Dropping the bar to one hit is the most direct probe of whether the small queue is discarding useful reuse. The expected trade-off is more main-queue pressure, which may cost some hit-rate on scan-heavy traces, so the experiment should compare hit-rate against the baseline value of 0.7164.
