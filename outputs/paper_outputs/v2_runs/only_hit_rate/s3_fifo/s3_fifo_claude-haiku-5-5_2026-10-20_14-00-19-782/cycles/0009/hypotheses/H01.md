# H01

Storing a 64-bit key fingerprint instead of the full key in the ghost queue will reduce metadata-size on the ghost path while leaving hit-rate unchanged on lirs workload-a at capacity 2048.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change the `ghost` field from `VecDeque<K>` to `VecDeque<u64>` and add a helper `fn fingerprint<K: Hash>(key: &K) -> u64` that hashes the key with a fresh `MulHasher` and passes `finish()` through a 64-bit avalanche mix (for example the splitmix64 finalizer) so that similar keys do not collide in the low bits. Apply it at the three ghost touch points:

- `put`, ghost membership test: replace `self.ghost.iter().any(|ghost_key| ghost_key == &key)` with a scan comparing `u64` fingerprints against `fingerprint(&key)`.
- `put`, ghost re-admission: replace `self.ghost.retain(|ghost_key| ghost_key != &key)` with a `retain` on the fingerprint.
- `evict_small`, ghost push: push `fingerprint(&key)` instead of `key` into `ghost`. The bounded `pop_back` at `main_size` stays as is.

Keep `small`, `main`, `resident`, `freq`, `MAX_FREQ`, and `try_new` sizing unchanged. Keep `Entry<K, V>` in place because the metadata-size measurement binary names it. Queue order and eviction decisions must be identical to the parent except for fingerprint collisions, so the expected hit-rate delta is zero on this trace.

## Rationale (optional)

v001 (cycle 0008) left the ghost queue holding full keys, so every ghost entry costs `sizeof(K)` plus a clone on each eviction. Shrinking each ghost entry to 8 bytes targets metadata-size directly and also makes the ghost scan compare integers instead of `K`, which may cut per-op-time on the miss path. Ghost-admission rules are not changed, so this does not reopen the ghost-admission axis that earlier cycles found flat or negative.
