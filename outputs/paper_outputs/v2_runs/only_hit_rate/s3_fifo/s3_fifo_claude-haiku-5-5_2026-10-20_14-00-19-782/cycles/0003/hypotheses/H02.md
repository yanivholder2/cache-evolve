# H02

Requiring three hits instead of two before a small-queue entry is promoted to main should raise hit-rate by keeping one-hit and two-hit returners out of the main queue, targeting hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Keep everything in cycle/0001/v002 as it is, including ghost re-admission through `small`, the `/4` small-queue sizing, and `MAX_FREQ = 3`.

In `evict_small()`, change the promotion test from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 2`. Entries that reach `freq == 3` (three `get()` hits, since `MAX_FREQ` saturates at 3) are promoted to `main`. Entries with `freq <= 2` move to `ghost` as before.

Leave `evict_main()`, the ghost path in `put()`, and the ghost queue size unchanged.

Update the in-file unit test `small_eviction_promotes_to_main_after_multiple_hits` so it calls `get(&1)` three times before the eviction, because the bar is now three hits. Leave `main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance` as it is, since it sets `freq` directly.

The cycle 0001 result showed that relaxing the bar to one hit costs hit-rate. This hypothesis tests the opposite direction on the same parent. Report hit-rate, metadata-size, and per-op-time. Hit-rate should be at least 0.7164 to count as a non-regression, and above 0.7164 to count as a gain.
