# H02

Promoting a small-queue entry to main at the moment its frequency reaches 2 on a hit, instead of waiting for it to reach the small-queue tail, raises hit-rate on lirs workload-a at capacity 2048 because proven-warm keys stop competing for the small queue's slots.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change only the hit path and the small-queue bookkeeping in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Leave `evict_main` and the ghost branch of `put` unchanged.

- In `get`, after the frequency bump on a hit, check whether the entry is in `small` and its `freq()` is now `>= 2`. If so, find its index in `small` (`self.small.iter().position(...)`), remove it with `self.small.remove(i)`, and move it to the front of `main`. Before the push, call `evict_main()` when `main.len() >= main_size`, as `evict_small` already does on its promotion branch.
- Do the move at most once per entry. Once an entry is in `main`, the existing `get` bump path applies unchanged.
- Leave `evict_small` as in baseline. Its `freq > 1` promotion branch becomes rarely reached, but keep it so entries that reach freq 2 without a hit are still handled.
- Do not change `Entry::new`, `MAX_FREQ`, the promotion threshold in `evict_small`, the ghost queue sizing, or the main-queue second-chance decrement.
- Invariants to preserve: capacity bound `small.len() + main.len() <= capacity`; a key is never in both `small`/`main` and `ghost`; `get` still clamps frequency at `MAX_FREQ`.
- Add one unit test: put a key into small, call `get` twice, and assert the key is now in `main` and not in `small`.

Report hit-rate, metadata-size, and per-op-time. Expected: hit-rate rises or stays flat. Per-op-time may rise on hits because of the linear position scan over `small`, which is bounded by `small_size`.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycles 0001 to 0007 varied when a small-queue entry is promoted only at eviction time (the promotion bar in `evict_small`). None of them moved promotion to access time. Eager promotion changes how long warm keys occupy the small queue, so it is a different lever from the threshold tests that already came back flat or negative.
