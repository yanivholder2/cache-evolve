# H03

Admitting ghost-hit re-insertions into the main queue with frequency 1 instead of 0 raises hit-rate on lirs workload-a at capacity 2048, because a re-requested recently evicted key gets one extra main-queue pass.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change only the ghost-hit admission path of `S3Fifo::put` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Leave the ghost window size, the small-queue routing, the promotion bar, and the main-queue sweep unchanged.

- In `put`, in the `if in_ghost { ... }` branch, after `self.ghost.retain(...)` and the `evict_main()` call, construct the entry as `let mut entry = Entry::new(key, value); entry.freq = 1;` and push it with `self.main.push_front(entry);` in place of the current `Entry::new(key, value)` push.
- Keep the ordering of the branch as in baseline: remove from ghost, evict from main when `main.len() >= main_size`, then push to the front of main.
- Do not change `Entry::new`, which still sets `freq: 0` for fresh small-queue admissions. Do not change `evict_small`, `evict_main`, `MAX_FREQ`, or `get`.
- Invariants to preserve: capacity bound `small.len() + main.len() <= capacity`; the ghost queue contents are unchanged on every path except the removal of the re-admitted key; `get` clamps frequency at `MAX_FREQ` as before.
- Add a unit test next to `ghost_hit_promotes_back_to_main` that asserts the re-admitted entry has `freq == 1` after `put`.

Report hit-rate, metadata-size, and per-op-time. Expected: hit-rate rises or stays flat; cost is essentially unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Prior cycles tested ghost window size, ghost routing through the small queue, and adaptive small-queue sizing, and all of them lost hits or stayed flat. None of them changed the frequency a ghost re-admission carries into main. The open question from cycle 0003 asks whether ghost re-admissions behave differently when they are admitted by a different path. This tests that directly while keeping the ghost mechanics intact.
