# H01

Rotating a victim that survives an admission contest to probation MRU turns the frozen 8-entry victim window into a CLOCK-style sweep and raises hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, `TinyLfuCache::put`, edit the full-cache path after `let admit = ...`. Today, when `admit` is false, nothing happens. The victim and the other 7 sampled probation entries stay at the probation head. So the same small window of oldest entries is the comparator for every following newcomer until one of them finally loses. A single moderately-counted head entry can block many newcomers in a row, while lower-count probation entries just behind the window are never offered as victims.

Add an `else` branch for the rejected case: `self.unlink(victim_idx); self.push_back(victim_idx);`. This moves the surviving victim to the MRU end of its own segment list. Because `unlink` and `push_back` dispatch on `segment`, this also works when the victim came from the protected fallback (probation empty). Do not touch `prot_len`, the sketch, the ghost ring or the map. Only list order changes.

Keep these unchanged: strict `>` admission plus the ghost-tie rule, `VICTIM_SAMPLE = 8`, the dedup logic, the promotion gate in `on_hit`, and `select_demotion`. Add one unit test. Fill a 4-entry cache, make the probation head's estimate higher than a cold newcomer's, `put` the newcomer, and assert that it is rejected, that the former head is now `prob_tail`, and that `check_structure` passes. Existing tests that assert on `prob_head` after a rejection may need their expectations updated. Only change the asserted position, not the admission outcome.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycles 0003 and 0009 varied the sampling *depth* and found it inert, because depth trades young-key eviction against a higher victim estimate. This change leaves depth alone and changes *which* entries the window covers over time. Each rejection gives the defender a second chance, as in CLOCK, and exposes the next-oldest probation entries. It is a single-lever change to victim rotation, which no earlier cycle has tested.
