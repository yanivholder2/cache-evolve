# H02

Admitting warm candidates that lose the contest with a small probability (Caffeine-style jitter, 1/128 when the candidate's estimate is above 5) will raise hit-rate on workload-a, because it breaks the lock-in of residents with stale-high lifetime counts without adding aging.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs` of the parent (the SLRU with plain LRU demotion and probation-only 8-way min-estimate victim sampling), change only the admission decision in `put`.

- Add a field `rng: u64` to `TinyLfuCache` and seed it in `try_new` with a fixed constant (e.g. `0x9E37_79B9_7F4A_7C15`) so that runs are deterministic. Add a helper `fn next_rand(&mut self) -> u64` that advances `rng` by the golden-ratio increment and returns `splitmix64(rng)`. Reuse the existing `splitmix64`.
- Replace `if incoming_freq > victim_freq { ...admit... }` with: admit if `incoming_freq > victim_freq`. Otherwise, if `incoming_freq >= JITTER_MIN_FREQ` (const `6`) and `self.next_rand() & 127 == 0`, admit anyway. Otherwise reject as before. Only call the RNG when the first test fails and the candidate is warm, so cold misses never use RNG state. Note that this is not tie-admission: one-hit and low-count newcomers (estimate ≤ 5) still lose every tie and every lower comparison.
- The admit path (unlink the victim, fix `prot_len`, reuse the slot, `push_back` into probation, update the map) is unchanged. Do not touch the sketch width, dedup, `select_victim`, or the SLRU promotion and demotion logic.
- Add a unit test showing that a candidate with estimate ≤ 5 that ties the victim is never admitted over many trials, while a candidate with estimate ≥ 6 that loses is eventually admitted (within a few thousand trials).

## Rationale

The cycle history says lifetime counts are the right signal and aging always hurt. The cost of lifetime counts is that a warm key repeatedly loses to residents whose counts reflect old popularity. Rare random admission of keys that are demonstrably warm adds just enough churn to let such keys in, without the wholesale freshness that aging imposes. This lever acts on the contest, not on the sketch, the window or SLRU internals, so it is distinct from every earlier lineage.
