# H02

Shrinking the probation victim sample from 8 to 3 entries will raise hit-rate on workload-a by evicting closer to probation-LRU order, so recently admitted keys are no longer chosen just for having the lowest lifetime count.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change `const VICTIM_SAMPLE: usize = 8;` to `const VICTIM_SAMPLE: usize = 3;`. Change nothing else:

- `select_victim` keeps the same walk from `prob_head` with the strict `<` tie-break toward the head, and still falls back to `prot_head` when probation is empty.
- Leave the admission predicate in `put` (strict `>`, plus the ghost exact-tie path with `GHOST_TIE_MIN_FREQ = 2`), the ghost ring, `select_demotion` (`DEMOTE_SAMPLE = 4`), the 80/20 SLRU split, dedup and the 16× sketch unchanged.
- Update the doc comment on `VICTIM_SAMPLE`. Existing tests must still pass. `victim_is_min_frequency_among_oldest_not_lru_head` evicts key 2 (the head, which is the minimum among the first 3), so its assertions still hold. Add a test where the minimum-estimate probation entry is 4th from the head and assert that it is *not* chosen: the minimum among the 3 oldest is chosen instead.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003 showed that *widening* the sample hurt. Without aging, the lowest estimate tends to mark the youngest resident, so deeper scans evict recently admitted keys that are still useful. The opposite direction has never been tested. Cycle 0002's jump from pure LRU to an 8-way sample gave only a marginal gain, so the best depth may lie between 1 and 8. A 3-way sample still lets frequency skip an obviously hot head entry, but limits the scan to the oldest probation entries. Newly admitted keys then get a full probation pass to collect a hit before they can be chosen. This tests only sampling depth, not the contest, the sketch or the segment structure. That makes it independent of H01's sketch-cap change and H03's promotion gate.
