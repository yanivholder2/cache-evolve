# H02

Let a ghost-hit newcomer win the admission contest by a margin (admit when `incoming_freq + 2 >= victim_freq` and `incoming_freq >= 3`) instead of only on exact ties, to raise hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Keep the parent's ghost ring (a FIFO of `capacity` evicted-key hashes plus `ghost_set`) and everything else unchanged. Change only the admit predicate in `put()`:

```rust
let admit = incoming_freq > victim_freq
    || (is_ghost
        && incoming_freq >= GHOST_MIN_FREQ
        && incoming_freq + GHOST_MARGIN >= victim_freq);
```

- Replace `GHOST_TIE_MIN_FREQ = 2` with `GHOST_MIN_FREQ: u32 = 3` and add `GHOST_MARGIN: u32 = 2`. Use `saturating_add` for the addition.
- The ghost-hit path still removes the key's hash from `ghost_set` when it is admitted. The victim is still recorded into the ghost ring. A ghost that loses the contest stays in the ring (unchanged).
- Never-seen keys (not in `ghost_set`) keep strict `>` with keep-victim-on-tie.
- Do not change `select_victim`, `select_demotion`, the sketch, dedup, or the SLRU ratios.
- Update the unit test `ghost_tie_admits_recently_evicted_but_not_never_seen`. A ghost key at estimate 3 must now be admitted against a victim at estimate 5. A ghost key at estimate 2 must be rejected against a victim at 2 (it is below the minimum). A never-seen key at estimate 4 must be rejected against a victim at 5.

## Rationale (optional)

v001's insight showed that the exact-tie rule almost never fires, because unaged estimates rarely match. The margin is the other path that cycle 0007 named. A key that was evicted within the last `capacity` evictions, has come back, and has been seen at least 3 times shows real reuse evidence that lifetime counts underweight. The minimum of 3 keeps this away from the cold one/two-hit churn that sank tie-admission in cycle 3. This tests whether recency can help through the contest without decaying counts. That makes it a different test from H01's aging.
