# H01

Narrowing the frequency sketch's counters from u32 to u16 halves the sketch's fixed metadata footprint and lifts metadata-size while keeping hit-rate within noise of 0.7362.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `CountMinSketch` (src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs), change the field `counts: Vec<Vec<u32>>` to `Vec<Vec<u16>>`, and change `MAX_COUNT` from `u32::MAX` to `u16::MAX`. `CountMinSketch::new` builds `vec![vec![0u16; width]; depth]`. `increment` keeps its saturating guard (`if *counter < MAX_COUNT { *counter += 1 }`) with the new type. `estimate` widens each row read with `as u32` before taking the minimum, so its return type and the admission comparison `incoming_freq > victim_freq` in `put` stay unchanged.

Update `heap_bytes` to `self.depth * self.width * std::mem::size_of::<u16>()`. Update the doc comments on `heap_bytes` and `sketch_heap_bytes` so they name u16 rather than u32.

Keep exactly as in the parent: `SKETCH_DEPTH`, `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR`, `MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH`, the seeded `position` hashing, the sketch sizing in `try_new`, the intrusive LRU list, and the v002 u32 index types and capacity guard. Do not touch tests/, tiny_lfu_jni.rs, Cargo.toml, or src/lib.rs.

## Rationale (optional)

The sketch table is the only fixed metadata term that `sketch_heap_bytes` reports, so halving its counter width is a direct metadata-size lever. The estimates only differ from the parent for keys counted above 65,535 times, where saturation can create ties under the strict `>` test. Those ties could freeze admission for very hot keys, so hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 is the risk to check. If it drops, the sketch width is the likely cause.
