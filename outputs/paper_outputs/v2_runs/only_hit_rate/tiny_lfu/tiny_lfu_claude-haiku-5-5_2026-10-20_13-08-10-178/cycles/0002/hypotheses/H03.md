# H03

Flattening the frequency sketch's `Vec<Vec<u32>>` into one contiguous `Vec<u32>` should lower per-op-time while leaving hit-rate bit-identical to 0.7362.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `CountMinSketch` (src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs), replace `counts: Vec<Vec<u32>>` with a single `counts: Vec<u32>` of length `depth * width`, allocated in `new` as `vec![0; width * depth]`. The counter for row `r` and position `p` lives at index `r * self.width + p`.

In `increment`, keep the loop over rows and the call `self.position(key, row as u64)`. Index the flat table at `row * self.width + pos`, keeping the saturating guard against `MAX_COUNT`.

In `estimate`, replace the `enumerate`/double-index body with a fold over rows that reads `self.counts[row * self.width + self.position(key, row as u64)]` and takes the minimum, starting from `u32::MAX` or using `min()` over a mapped iterator. The return value is unchanged.

Do not change `position`, the seeding, `SKETCH_DEPTH`, the width formula in `try_new`, the `heap_bytes` formula, or any LRU or admission logic. Because each (row, position) pair maps to the same logical counter as before, every estimate and every admission decision is identical, so the hit-rate should match the parent exactly.

Keep the v002 u32 index types and capacity guard as in the parent. Do not touch tests/, tiny_lfu_jni.rs, Cargo.toml, or src/lib.rs.

## Rationale (optional)

Each access currently follows four separate heap allocations and pointer chases through `Vec<Vec<u32>>`. Flattening to one allocation keeps the row bounds check simple and improves cache locality on the hot increment and estimate paths. The change is a layout-only edit that leaves the sketch's behaviour alone, so it is a clean per-op-time test: any hit-rate movement would indicate a bug rather than a tradeoff. The insight from cycle 0001 that the v002 fitness vector omitted per-op-time means the speedup must be read from the per-op-time dimension directly.
