# H01

Scoping the saturation response to the saturated row should raise hit-rate over the table-wide halving rejected in cycle 0009, because cold keys keep their admission history in the other rows.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In `CountMinSketch::increment()` (src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs), add a `const SAT_THRESHOLD: u32` set to `u32::MAX / 2`. When a counter that `increment` just bumped reaches `SAT_THRESHOLD`, halve only the counters in that same row (`self.counts[row]`), leaving every other row untouched. Do not halve on a fixed access count and do not touch other rows. Keep `estimate()`, `position()`, and `select_victim()` exactly as they are, and keep `MAX_COUNT` saturation as the hard cap. The probation victim scan with `TAIL_SCAN = 3` stays as in v001.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0009 v003 halved every row when one counter saturated and lost hit-rate because one hot key erased history for all cold keys. The cross-cycle notes say aging must be scoped per key or per row. This is the per-row variant. It is a single-row change, so its effect on the admission comparison is bounded to one row of the min-across-rows estimate.
