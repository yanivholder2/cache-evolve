**Concern for the cohort:** `u32::MAX / 2` is about 2.1 billion. Counters are `u32` and reach that only after billions of increments to the same sketch cell, which a single lirs workload-a trace at size 2048 is unlikely to produce. If so, the halving never fires and this variant behaves like its parent, so any measured difference would be noise. I implemented the threshold as the hypothesis specifies rather than substituting a smaller one. If the cycle wants the mechanism to actually exercise, the threshold needs to be lowered.
