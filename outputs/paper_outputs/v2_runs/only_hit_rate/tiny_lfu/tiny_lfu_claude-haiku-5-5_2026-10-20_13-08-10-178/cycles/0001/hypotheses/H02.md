# H02

Narrowing the intrusive LRU link fields and the map's stored index from `usize` to `u32` reduces the per-entry `Node<u64,u64>` footprint from 48 bytes to 32 bytes, targeting the metadata-size dimension without changing eviction behaviour.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the index type used for the intrusive list and the map from `usize` to `u32`:

- `Node<K, V>`: `prev` and `next` become `Option<u32>`.
- `TinyLfuCache`: `head` and `tail` become `Option<u32>`; `map` becomes `HashMap<K, u32>`.
- `try_new(capacity)`: add a guard that returns `None` (with a `log::error!`) when `capacity > u32::MAX as usize`, so every stored index fits in `u32`.

Update `get`, `put`, `unlink`, and `push_back` to convert at the boundary: index into `self.nodes` with `idx as usize` and store indices with `as u32`. Keep the `Vec<Node<K,V>>` arena, the head-is-LRU and tail-is-MRU orientation, and the admission rule unchanged. `Node` must still derive `Debug`. Expected result: `size_of::<Node<u64,u64>>()` drops from 48 to 32 bytes (key 8 + value 8 + two `Option<u32>` at 8 each). Leave `CountMinSketch` and `sketch_heap_bytes()` untouched. Existing tests in the `tests` module must pass without modification.
