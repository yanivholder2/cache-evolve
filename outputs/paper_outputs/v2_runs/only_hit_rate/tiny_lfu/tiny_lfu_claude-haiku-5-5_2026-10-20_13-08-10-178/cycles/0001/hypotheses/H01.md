# H01

Periodically halving the count-min sketch counters after every 10×capacity accesses raises hit rate on workload-a at size 2048 by letting stale frequency history decay so that newly hot keys can win admission against the LRU victim.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Add two fields to `TinyLfuCache` in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`: `ops_since_halving: usize` (initialised to 0) and `sample_size: usize` (initialised to `capacity * 10`). In `get()` and in `put()`, increment `ops_since_halving` once per call, right after the existing `self.sketch.increment(...)` line. When `ops_since_halving >= sample_size`, call a new `CountMinSketch::halve(&mut self)` method and reset `ops_since_halving` to 0. `halve()` iterates every row and every counter and right-shifts each `u32` by 1 (`*c >>= 1`). Leave `CountMinSketch::increment`, `estimate`, `position`, and the admission comparison `incoming_freq > victim_freq` in `put()` unchanged. Do not change `try_new` sizing, the sketch width, or the arena/LRU linked-list logic. Halving must not alter the cache's resident set on its own; it only affects future admission decisions. Keep `sketch_heap_bytes()` behaviour identical so the metadata-size dimension is unaffected.
