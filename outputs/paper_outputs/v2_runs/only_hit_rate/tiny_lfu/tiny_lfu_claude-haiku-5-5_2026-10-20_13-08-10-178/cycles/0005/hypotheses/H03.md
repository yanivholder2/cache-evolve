# H03

Halving all sketch counters only once every 10 capacity-worth of accesses, instead of on a short schedule, should raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by letting stale frequency history age out without discarding the signal admission still needs.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Edit `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`. The baseline has no aging, so add it with a sample window:

1. Add two fields to `TinyLfuCache`: `samples: u64` (initialised to `0` in `try_new`) and `sample_limit: u64` (initialised to `10 * capacity as u64`).
2. Add a private method `fn record(&mut self, key: &K)` on `TinyLfuCache` that does `self.sketch.increment(key);`, then `self.samples += 1;`, and when `self.samples >= self.sample_limit` calls `self.sketch.halve()` and resets `self.samples = 0`.
3. In `get` and `put`, replace the existing `self.sketch.increment(...)` calls with `self.record(...)`. Keep all other logic in both functions unchanged.
4. Add `fn halve(&mut self)` to `CountMinSketch` that right-shifts every counter in every row by 1 (`*c >>= 1`).

Do not change `estimate`, `position`, `heap_bytes`, or the admission comparison `incoming_freq > victim_freq` in `put`. Halving is the only aging trigger; do not add any halving on hits or on eviction. `sketch_heap_bytes()` is unchanged because no counter table is resized.

Distinction from the earlier fixed-schedule halving that lost hit-rate: this uses a long sample window of 10x capacity, so it only ages history on traces much longer than the cache. If hit-rate falls, the result would say the sketch needs its full history at this capacity and aging should be dropped for good.

Report hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 and the number of halvings that occurred in the run.
