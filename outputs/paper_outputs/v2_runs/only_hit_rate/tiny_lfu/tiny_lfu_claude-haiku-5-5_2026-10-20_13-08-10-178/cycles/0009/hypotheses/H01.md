# H01

Widening the probation victim scan from two LRU-tail entries to three raises hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by evicting the coldest of the three oldest probation entries.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In `TinyLfuCache::select_victim()` (src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs), replace the two-entry comparison with a bounded scan over the first `TAIL_SCAN = 3` entries of the probation list, starting at `self.probation.head` and following `self.nodes[idx].next`:

- Add `const TAIL_SCAN: usize = 3;` next to the existing protected constants.
- Walk at most `TAIL_SCAN` entries. For each, read `self.sketch.estimate(&self.nodes[idx].key)` and keep the index with the strictly lowest estimate.
- On an exact tie, keep the entry that was reached first (the older one, closer to the head), matching the current v002 tie rule.
- If the probation list has fewer than `TAIL_SCAN` entries, scan what exists. If probation is empty, keep the existing fallback to `self.protected_seg.head`.

Preserve everything else: `put()` still compares `incoming_freq > victim_freq` and rejects the newcomer otherwise, `promote()` and `demote` behaviour are unchanged, and the sketch is untouched. Do not change `SKETCH_DEPTH`, `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR`, or the protected share.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle-0008 insight lists "does a wider tail scan beat a two-entry comparison?" as the open question for the victim rule. Scanning three candidates lets the cache skip past a hot-but-old probation entry when the second entry is also hot, so the single-tail-pair comparison is no longer the only chance to protect a re-referenced resident.
