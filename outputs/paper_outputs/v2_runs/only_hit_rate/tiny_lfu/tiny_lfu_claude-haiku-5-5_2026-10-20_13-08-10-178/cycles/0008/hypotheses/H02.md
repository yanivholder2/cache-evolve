# H02

Choosing the probation victim as the colder of the two LRU-tail entries should raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by evicting low-frequency residents instead of whichever entry happens to be oldest.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Where the eviction path selects the probation-segment victim (the LRU tail of probation, reached from `put()`), examine the two entries at the LRU end of probation instead of one. Look up each candidate's sketch estimate with `estimate()`, and evict the one with the lower estimate. On an exact tie, evict the older (LRU-tail) entry so the behaviour matches the incumbent when frequencies are equal. Do not touch the protected segment, demotion placement at the back of probation, the promote-on-every-hit rule, or the sketch update path (`CountMinSketch::increment`, depth, width). If probation holds only one entry, fall back to the incumbent single-victim choice. Keep the admission comparison itself unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle insights say changes to how admission sees keys have lost hit-rate, but they have not touched which resident is evicted once admission has already decided. This variant leaves the admission decision intact and changes only the victim among probation entries, so a loss would point at the victim rule rather than at frequency signal. Cycle 0007 v001 showed probation at a single slot is harmful, so the two-candidate scan should only be used when probation has at least two entries.
