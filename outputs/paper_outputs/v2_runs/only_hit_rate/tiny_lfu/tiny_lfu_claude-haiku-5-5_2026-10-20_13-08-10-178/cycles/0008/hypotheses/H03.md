# H03

Remembering recently evicted key hashes in a bounded ghost history and granting re-referenced keys a one-count admission bonus should raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by recovering frequency signal that eviction currently discards.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Add a bounded ghost structure to the cache: a fixed-capacity FIFO ring of 64-bit key hashes plus a membership set, sized equal to the main-region capacity. When `put()` evicts an entry, push that entry's key hash into the ghost structure, dropping the oldest ghost when full. When `put()` receives an incoming key whose hash is present in the ghost structure, remove it from the ghost structure and add 1 to that key's estimate before the admission comparison against the victim. Do not decrement or halve any sketch counter, do not withhold any sketch increment, and do not change the segment split or promotion rule. Keep the ghost bookkeeping out of `CountMinSketch` so the sketch's update and estimate logic stays as it is in the baseline.

## Rationale (optional)

Earlier cycles that changed when admission sees a key removed or withheld frequency and lost hit-rate. This hypothesis does the opposite: it adds frequency evidence for keys that were recently resident and were evicted, which the sketch alone may under-count after eviction. The ghost bonus is a small additive term rather than a gate, so it should not suppress the sketch history the insights say must stay intact. It will add per-cache metadata, so expect a metadata-size cost; the hypothesis is judged on hit-rate.
