## Changes

In `put()`, the admission comparison between the incoming key's sketch estimate and the eviction victim's estimate currently requires the incoming estimate to be strictly greater. Change it so that when the victim lives in the probation segment, equality also admits (`>=` instead of `>`). When the victim is in the protected segment, keep the strict comparison, so resident protected entries still win ties. Leave `CountMinSketch::increment` and `estimate`, the width and depth constants, the protected share (about 9/10), and the promote-on-every-hit rule untouched. Confirm the exact current operator before editing; if the existing rule already admits on ties, invert the change and instead reject ties against probation victims.

## Rationale (optional)

After conservative update, many cold keys share the same low estimate, so ties are frequent and the tie rule decides a large share of admissions. Admitting ties against probation victims only affects keys that are equally cold, so it should not disturb protected residents, which the cross-cycle insights identify as the segment that matters.
