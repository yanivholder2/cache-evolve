# H02

Enlarging the protected segment of the main region beyond the incumbent ratio raises hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by keeping more re-referenced keys out of probation.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Start from cycle/0004/v002 and keep its sketch unchanged. In the main-region code that sets the protected segment's capacity, increase the protected share of the main region by about 10 percentage points over the incumbent setting (from roughly 4/5 to roughly 9/10 of the main region). Keep the existing promotion path, where a probation hit moves an entry into protected, and keep the existing demotion placement, where demoted protected entries go to the back of probation. Do not change `put()`'s admission comparison `incoming_freq > victim_freq`, the sketch, or `evict_one()` logic beyond what the new protected capacity implies. Ensure the probation segment keeps at least one slot so eviction still has a candidate.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 showed that narrowing the protected segment (halved cap) lost hit-rate, which suggests the cap may be a binding constraint and that widening it is the untested direction. This variant moves only the segment ratio, so any change in hit-rate can be attributed to the split.
