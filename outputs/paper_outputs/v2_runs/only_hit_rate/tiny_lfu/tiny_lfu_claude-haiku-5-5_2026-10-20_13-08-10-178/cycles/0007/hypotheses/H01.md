# H01

Raising the protected share of the main region further, from roughly 9/10 to roughly 19/20, will lift hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by keeping more re-referenced entries out of probation.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Keep v002's protected/probation split mechanism unchanged and change only the protected capacity constant that sets the protected share of the main region, moving it from roughly 9/10 to roughly 19/20 of main-region capacity. Leave the probation capacity at no fewer than one slot, so probation never becomes zero-sized and new entries still have a landing place. Do not change promotion on hit, demotion placement (keep demoted entries at the back of probation), the sketch, `CountMinSketch::increment`, `estimate()`, or the admission path in `put()`. The sketch width and conservative-update changes already in the parent stay as they are.

## Rationale

v002 showed the protected share is a positive lever, and the cycle 0006 insight asks for the gain to be tested along the same axis before building on it. Cycle 0004 found that halving the protected cap hurt, so the direction of the trend is the open question. This hypothesis tests whether the gain keeps growing past 9/10 or peaks and then falls as probation room shrinks.
