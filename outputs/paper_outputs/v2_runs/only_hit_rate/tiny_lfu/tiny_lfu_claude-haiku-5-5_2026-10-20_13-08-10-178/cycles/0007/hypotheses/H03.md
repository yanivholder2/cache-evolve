# H03

Promoting a probation entry to protected on a hit only when its sketch estimate is at least that of the protected tail will raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by keeping the protected segment for entries that are actually hot.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Keep v002's protected share and sizing as they are. Change only the promotion rule on a hit in the main region: when a key in probation is hit, compute its estimate with `estimate()` and compare it to the estimate of the least-recently-used entry at the protected tail. If the probation key's estimate is greater than or equal to the protected tail's, promote it to protected as now, demoting the protected tail to the back of probation. If it is lower, leave the probation key in probation and move it to the most-recently-used position of probation only. Do not change demotion placement, the sketch's update rule, `CountMinSketch::increment`, or `put()`'s admission comparison against the LRU victim.

Keep the promotion comparison cheap: reuse the estimate already computed for the access where possible, and make no extra sketch increments.

## Rationale

The protected segment has been the source of the two main-region gains so far, but promotion is still unconditional on a second hit. Using frequency to gate promotion targets the case where a one-off re-reference displaces an entry that is genuinely more frequent. This is a different mechanism from the sizing changes in H01 and from the aging and admission families the notes already rule out, since it changes segment membership and not the estimator or admission timing. It is a new use of the sketch signal, not a rewrite of it.
