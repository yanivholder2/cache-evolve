# H01

Shrinking the protected segment from 4/5 to 1/2 of capacity raises hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by giving probation enough room to catch one-touch-then-return keys before they are evicted.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

Change the protected-segment cap in `TinyLfuCache::try_new` from `(capacity * 4 / 5).max(1)` to `(capacity / 2).max(1)`. Update the doc comment on `PROTECTED`/`segments` only if it becomes inaccurate. Leave everything else untouched: `touch()` (hit moves to protected MRU, overflow demotes protected LRU to probation MRU), `eviction_candidate()` (probation LRU first, protected LRU only when probation is empty), the admission comparison in `put()` (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`), and the sketch. The sketch, the segment list mechanics, and the `len` bookkeeping must stay exactly as in v002. This is a single-constant change so that the effect of the split ratio is isolated from the recency logic the incumbent already established.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 showed that the probation/protected split is the only lever so far to move hit-rate upward, but its 80/20 split was chosen without testing. Cycle 0003's open question asks whether the gain depends on the split. Varying the ratio is the smallest experiment that answers this and does not touch sketch or admission behaviour, which earlier cycles showed to be the costly side.
