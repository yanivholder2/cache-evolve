# H02

Doubling the sketch width per cache slot from 4x to 8x reduces counter collisions enough to sharpen admission estimates and raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048, at a measurable metadata cost.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` constant from `4` to `8`. This feeds `TinyLfuCache::try_new`, which computes `width = (capacity * SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR).max(MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH).next_power_of_two()`, so at capacity 2048 the width doubles from 8192 to 16384 counters per row. Keep the sketch depth, the `position()` hash and seeds, the counter type, `MAX_COUNT`, and the `increment`/`estimate` logic unchanged, so the only difference is the number of counters per row. `sketch_heap_bytes()` already reports `depth * width * size_of` and will reflect the larger table with no other edit. Keep the admission comparison in `put()` and the LRU list in `unlink`/`push_back` untouched.

## Rationale (optional)

Earlier cycles changed the sketch's logical behaviour (decay, key-to-counter mapping) or its layout, and never changed how many counters it has. Fewer collisions give each key's `estimate()` less over-count from other keys, which is the signal `put()` uses to decide admission. This is a different lever from the decay and mapping experiments already ruled out. The change is cheap to implement and its cost is directly visible on the metadata-size dimension.
