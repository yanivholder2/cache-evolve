# H03

Inserting protected-overflow demotions at the LRU end of probation, rather than the MRU end, raises hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by letting entries that were hit once but have gone cold be evicted before fresher probation entries.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

In `TinyLfuCache::touch`, the demotion branch currently calls `self.push_back(demoted, PROBATION)`. Add a helper `push_front(&mut self, idx: usize, seg: usize)` that sets `nodes[idx].segment = seg`, sets `nodes[idx].next = self.segments[seg].head`, sets `nodes[idx].prev = None`, links the old head's `prev` back to `idx` (or sets `segments[seg].tail = Some(idx)` if the list was empty), sets `segments[seg].head = Some(idx)`, and increments `segments[seg].len`. Use `push_front` in `touch()` for the demotion so a demoted entry becomes the next probation victim candidate. Leave `eviction_candidate()`, `put()`, the admission comparison, the sketch, and `push_back` as they are in v002. Confirm that `unlink` still correctly handles a node at the head of probation.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's `touch()` demotes the protected LRU entry to the MRU end of probation, which gives it another full pass through probation before eviction. Cycle 0003's open question asks how the main-region recency order should treat demoted entries. This hypothesis tests the opposite placement while keeping the segment sizes, the sketch, and the admission rule fixed, so any change in hit-rate comes from demotion placement alone.
