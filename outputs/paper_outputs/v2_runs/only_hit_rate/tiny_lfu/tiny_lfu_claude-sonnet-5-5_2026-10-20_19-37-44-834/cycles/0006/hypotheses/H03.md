# H03

Replace pointer-width linked-list links in the segment lists with u32 slot indices into a preallocated entry slab to reduce metadata-size.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the cache's entry/list storage, replace per-entry `usize`/pointer prev/next links (and any boxed nodes) with `u32` indices into a single `Vec` slab of entries allocated at capacity, with a free-list for reuse. Store the segment tag (window/probation/protected) as a `u8` in the entry. Keep lookup map, sketch, window/probation/protected capacities and admission logic exactly as in baseline so behaviour (hit-rate 0.736) is identical. Goal: lower reported metadata-size and improve locality for per-op-time. Do not touch src/tiny_lfu/tiny_lfu_jni.rs.
