# H02

Add a frequency-weighted protected-segment demotion (demote the lowest-sketch-estimate of the two oldest protected entries) to raise hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

In the code path that handles protected-segment overflow (when a probation hit promotes an entry into protected and protected exceeds its capacity), instead of demoting the LRU tail unconditionally, inspect the two least-recently-used protected entries and demote the one with the lower sketch `estimate()`; ties demote the older. The demoted entry goes to the probation MRU as before. Do not change sketch contents, admission/tie-admit rule, or segment capacities. This keeps all frequency information (no aging, no width change) and only uses it more at the demotion point.
