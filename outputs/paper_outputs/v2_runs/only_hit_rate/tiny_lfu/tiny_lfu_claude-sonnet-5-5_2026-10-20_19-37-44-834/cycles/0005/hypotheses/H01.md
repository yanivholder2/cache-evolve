# H01

Loosening admission further (candidate wins when its estimate is within 1 of the victim's, only if the candidate was just hit in the window) will raise hit-rate slightly over the tie-admit duel.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

In the admission duel function (where the window victim "candidate" is compared with the main-segment probation victim), keep the existing tie-admit rule (candidate admitted when est(candidate) >= est(victim)). Add one extra case: also admit when est(candidate) + 1 == est(victim) AND the candidate had at least one hit while resident in the window (track with a per-entry `window_hits: u8` flag/counter set in the window-hit path, reset on insert). Do not touch the sketch, its sizing, increment or hashing. Keep all other behaviour (segment sizes, eviction order) unchanged. Invariant: capacity bounds and sketch frequencies are untouched; only the admit predicate changes.

## Rationale (optional)

Only admission loosening that preserves frequency information has helped so far. Using in-window recency evidence is a new signal beyond the sketch.
