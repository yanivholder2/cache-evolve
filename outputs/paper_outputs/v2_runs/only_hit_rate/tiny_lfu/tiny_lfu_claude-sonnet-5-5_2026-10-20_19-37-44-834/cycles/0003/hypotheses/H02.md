# H02

Make the window-to-probation admission duel frequency-weighted with tie-admit and a protected-segment-aware victim choice to raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In the window-to-probation duel in `put` (the place that compares sketch estimates of the window candidate and the probation victim): (1) change the admission test from strict `cand > victim` to `cand >= victim` when the candidate has been seen before (estimate >= 2), keeping strict `>` for estimate <= 1 so one-hit scans are still rejected; (2) when probation is empty, use the LRU of the protected segment as the duel victim instead of evicting the candidate unconditionally. Do not add aging or a doorkeeper (both lost hit-rate in cycle 2). Sketch, segment sizes and promotion rules stay unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 2 indicates frequency information is valuable; tie-admitting repeat keys preserves recency among equal-frequency keys without hiding counts.
