# H01

Hill-climb the window size online in the windowed cache to raise hit-rate beyond the fixed ~1% window.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In the cache struct add `window_cap`, `sample_hits`, `sample_reqs`, `prev_rate: f64`, `step: i64`. Replace the fixed 1% window capacity with a variable `window_cap` initialised to 1% of capacity, bounded to [1, 20% of capacity]. Every sample period (10 * capacity requests) compute the hit rate for the period; if it improved over `prev_rate` continue moving `window_cap` in the same direction by `step` (initially 1% of capacity), otherwise reverse direction and halve step (min 1). When the window shrinks, demote surplus window entries through the existing window-to-probation duel; when it grows, shrink the protected segment first, then probation, to keep total size == capacity. Preserve the sketch-gated duel and segment promotion logic unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 2 showed a statically larger window loses hit-rate; adaptation can stay near the 1% optimum on this stable trace while adjusting on others.
