# H03

Reducing SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR from 4 to 8 on the baseline-derived design will retain most of the wide-row hit-rate gain at half the sketch metadata, targeting hit-rate per byte.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the baseline sketch module, change `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` from 4 to 8, and nothing else. This brackets the cycle 7 winner (16) and the baseline (4), giving the width/hit-rate curve point at 8 to judge diminishing returns. Preserve all admission and segment logic.
