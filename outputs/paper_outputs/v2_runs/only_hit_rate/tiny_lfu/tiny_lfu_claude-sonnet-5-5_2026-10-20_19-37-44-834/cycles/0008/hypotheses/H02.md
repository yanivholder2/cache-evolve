# H02

Adding very infrequent halving of the wide sketch (reset period of about 32x cache capacity in increments) will raise hit-rate by letting stale frequencies fade without hurting estimate quality.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Starting from v001, add an increment counter to the sketch. When it reaches 32 * capacity (capacity = cache size, 2048 in the evaluation, so about 65536 increments), halve every counter (right shift by 1) and reset the counter. Earlier aging attempts lost, probably from resetting too often; this period is deliberately several times longer than a typical aging sample size. Keep u8 counters, conservative update, and the admission rule unchanged. Make the period a named constant so it is easy to tune.

## Rationale (optional)

Tests whether a never-decaying sketch is the limiter, with a gentle period that earlier failed aging variants did not use.
