# H02

Split the resident LRU into a 1% admission window plus a segmented main region (probation/protected) to raise hit rate by changing how entries move between buckets.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, replace the single LRU list with three intrusive lists over the same `nodes` arena: window (~1% of capacity, min 1), probation, and protected (~80% of main). Add a `seg: u8` tag to `Node` and per-list head/tail/len. New keys always enter the window (no admission test). When the window overflows, its LRU entry becomes a candidate: if probation has room it moves there, else compare `sketch.estimate(candidate)` with the probation LRU victim's estimate and evict the loser (candidate admitted only if strictly greater). A `get`/`put` hit in probation promotes the node to protected MRU; if protected is full, demote its LRU to probation MRU. Hits in window or protected move to their own MRU. Sketch increments stay on every get/put. Preserve the map/arena indexing, total resident count <= capacity, and the public API.
