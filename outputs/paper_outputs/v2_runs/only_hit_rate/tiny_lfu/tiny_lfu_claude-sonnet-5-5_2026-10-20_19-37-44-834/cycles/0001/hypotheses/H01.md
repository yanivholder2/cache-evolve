# H01

Add periodic halving (aging) of the count-min sketch counters to raise hit rate on workload-a by letting stale frequencies decay.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, add `additions: usize` and `sample_size: usize` fields to `CountMinSketch` (sample_size = 10 * capacity, passed via `CountMinSketch::new(width, sample_size)` from `try_new`). In `increment`, after updating the counters, bump `additions`; when it reaches `sample_size`, call a new `reset()` that right-shifts every counter in every row by 1 (`*c >>= 1`) and sets `additions = additions / 2` (or 0). Keep `estimate`, `position`, the admission rule (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`), the LRU list and `sketch_heap_bytes` unchanged. Do not touch tiny_lfu_jni.rs.

## Rationale (optional)

Baseline counters only grow, so old hot keys permanently beat newcomers; aging is the standard TinyLFU freshness mechanism.
