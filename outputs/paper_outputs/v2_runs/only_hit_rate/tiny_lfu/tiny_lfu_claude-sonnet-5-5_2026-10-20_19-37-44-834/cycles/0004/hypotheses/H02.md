# H02

Resizing the protected segment to a larger share of main (80% to 90% of main capacity) will raise hit-rate on the frequency-driven workload.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

In the v002 overlay's cache constructor, change the capacity split so the protected segment takes a larger fraction of main (the protected share rises from the current value to about 90% of main, probation keeps the remainder, minimum 1 slot). Keep the window size fixed. Keep the tie-admit duel and the empty-probation fallback. Preserve the invariant that window + probation + protected capacity equals total capacity. Promotion from probation to protected on a hit stays as is. When protected overflows, demote its LRU entry to probation as before.

## Rationale (optional)

The v002 insight says segment sizing is a likelier source of gains than the duel. Window size is already near its optimum, so only the main split is varied.
