# H02

Enlarge v002's window from about 1% to about 10% of capacity to raise hit-rate on recency-biased traces.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In the v002 overlay, change the window size computation from ~1% of capacity to max(1, capacity / 10). The probation and protected regions share the remaining capacity, with protected at 80% of the main region as before. Keep the sketch-gated window-to-probation duel unchanged and keep total entries <= capacity. Do not touch anything else.

## Rationale

The 1% window gave only a marginal gain, and workload-a may have more recency than the small window captures.
