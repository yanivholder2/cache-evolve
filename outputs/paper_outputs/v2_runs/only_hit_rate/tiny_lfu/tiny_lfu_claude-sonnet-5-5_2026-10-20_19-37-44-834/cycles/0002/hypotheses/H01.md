# H01

Add periodic halving (aging) of the frequency sketch on top of v002's window plus segmented main region to raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In the v002 overlay's sketch, add a sample counter incremented on every sketch `increment`/access. When it reaches 10 * cache capacity (the sample size), halve every counter (`c >>= 1`) and reset the counter to 0. Do not change the window, probation or protected logic, the window-to-probation duel, or the public API. Preserve existing capacity and key-lookup invariants. Counters must stay saturating, and the halving pass must be O(table size), run only at the threshold.

## Rationale

Aging was never evaluated in cycle 0001 because H01 failed on an infrastructure error. Stale frequencies should hurt admission on shifting workloads.
