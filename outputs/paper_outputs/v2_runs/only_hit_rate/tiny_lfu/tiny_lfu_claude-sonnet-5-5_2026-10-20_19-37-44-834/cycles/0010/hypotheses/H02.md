# H02

Remembering recently rejected admission candidates in a small ghost ring and granting them a frequency bonus on re-request will raise hit-rate by rescuing entries that lost duels unfairly.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Add a fixed-size ring buffer (e.g. capacity = cache capacity / 4) of key hashes to the policy struct. When the window-to-probation duel rejects a candidate, push its key hash into the ring. On a subsequent miss/insert of a key whose hash is in the ring (linear or small open-addressed set lookup), increment its sketch estimate by one extra count before the duel, and remove it from the ring. Leave the tie-break, sketch layout, segment sizes, and threshold otherwise unchanged. Ring entries are overwritten FIFO.
