# H01

Quadrupling the count-min sketch width (SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR 4 to 16) will reduce collision noise and raise hit-rate above 0.7377, at a measurable metadata-size cost.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` from 4 to 16 so `try_new` computes `width = (capacity*16).max(16).next_power_of_two()`. Leave everything else unchanged: u32 counters, depth 4, tie-admit rule (`cand_freq > victim_freq || (cand_freq >= 2 && cand_freq == victim_freq)`), window and segment logic. `sketch_heap_bytes` keeps reporting depth*width*4. Past cycles showed narrower rows lose, so this tests whether wider rows gain, i.e. whether the sketch is still collision-limited.

## Rationale (optional)

Narrowing always lost; widening was never tried.
