# H02

Counting each access once in the sketch (not twice on get-miss followed by put) will make frequency estimates more accurate and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, `get` increments the sketch on every call and `put` increments again, so a missed key is counted twice while a hit is counted once. Remove the `self.sketch.increment(&key)` at the top of `put` for the new-key path (key not in `map`). Keep an increment in `put` only when the key already exists in `map` (update of a present key without a prior get). Concretely: move the increment inside the `if let Some(&idx)` branch, before `touch`. Do not change the admission rule, so the tie-admit threshold `cand_freq >= 2` now requires a genuine second access. Preserve all list invariants.

## Rationale (optional)

The double count inflates first-time keys to freq 2 and lets one-hit wonders tie-admit. This tests whether that is harmful or helpful.
