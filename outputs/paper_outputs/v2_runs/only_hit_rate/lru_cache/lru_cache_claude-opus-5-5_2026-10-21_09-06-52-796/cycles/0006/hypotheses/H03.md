# H03

Shrinking the fixed probation queue from capacity/10 to capacity/20, while the ghost stays at capacity/8, raises hit-rate on workload-a because `main` gets about 5% more of the cache for proven-reuse keys.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, `LruCache::try_new`: change
`let small_target = std::cmp::max(1, capacity / 10);` to
`let small_target = std::cmp::max(1, capacity / 20);`. Update the
`log::info!` line so it reports the new split. At size 2048 this gives
`small_target = 102`, down from 204.

Keep `ghost_cap = max(1, capacity / 8)` (256 at size 2048) unchanged, so
the ghost is now about 2.5x the size of `small`. Leave these unchanged
too:
- `MAX_FREQ`
- one-hit promotion and the freq reset in `evict_one`'s `small` branch
- ghost-hit admission straight into `main`
- `push_ghost`

The `small.len() >= small_target || main.is_empty()` condition in
`evict_one` stays as written.

Check the in-file tests that depend on `small_target`:
- `one_hit_wonders_evicted_from_small_first` and
  `accessed_small_entry_promoted_to_main` (capacity 10)
- the ghost tests (capacity 16)

At capacity 10 and 16, `max(1, cap/20)` is 1, the same value as
`cap/10`. Their sequences and asserts therefore stay the same. Confirm
the `assert_eq!(cache.small_target, 1)` checks still pass, and change
nothing else in the tests.

## Rationale (optional)

The "~10% split holds up" finding was established when the ghost was
near capacity. It came from adaptive resizing (cycle 2) and LIRS (cycle
3), never from a direct fixed-size probe on the current S3-FIFO. With a
long ghost, a small `small` leaked through the ghost path. Now that the
ghost is short and has proven a reliable reuse signal, keys whose reuse
gap is a little longer than `small` are caught by the ghost (256 slots)
and still reach `main`. That covers the failure mode cycle 4 blamed on
over-strict admission, and it frees space in `main`. The test separates
this knob from ghost size, which H01 probes. If hit rate drops, the 10%
split is confirmed as a real optimum and not a leftover from the old
configuration.
