# H03

Replace strict LRU with the LIRS policy (LIR stack plus a 1% resident-HIR queue, with non-resident HIR history), which should beat S3-FIFO's hit rate on the loop-heavy workload-a LIRS trace.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Rewrite the policy in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` as LIRS. Keep the public surface unchanged: `LruCache`, `Node`, `try_new`, `new`, `get`, `put`, and the `framework::Cache` impl.

- **Storage.** Use a `Vec<Node>` arena indexed by `u32`. `Node` holds `key`, `value: Option<V>` (`None` means non-resident HIR), `status: {Lir, HirResident, HirNonResident}`, and two pairs of intrusive `u32` links: `s_prev`/`s_next` for stack S and `q_prev`/`q_next` for queue Q, with `u32::MAX` as null. A `HashMap<K, u32>` indexes every node, resident or not. Keep a free list for reclaimed slots.
- **Sizing.** `hir_cap = max(1, capacity/100)` and `lir_cap = capacity - hir_cap`. For capacity 1, `lir_cap = 0` and all entries are resident HIR. Bound the non-resident entries in S at `2 * capacity`. When that bound is exceeded, remove the oldest non-resident node from the bottom-scan of S and free its slot.
- **`get(key)`**: miss if the node is absent or non-resident.
  - On an LIR hit, move the node to the top of S. If it was at the bottom, run `prune_stack()`, which pops nodes from the bottom of S while they are not LIR, freeing non-resident ones and leaving resident HIRs in Q.
  - On a resident-HIR hit: if the node is in S, promote it to LIR, remove it from Q, demote the bottom LIR of S to resident HIR at the tail of Q, and prune. If it is not in S, push it onto S and move it to the tail of Q.
- **`put(key, value)`**:
  - Existing resident key: update the value and apply the same access logic as `get`.
  - Miss: while the LIR set is not full, admit as LIR. Otherwise evict the front of Q: set its value to `None` and mark it non-resident if it is still in S, or free it if not. Then insert the new key. If it was a non-resident node in S, promote it to LIR and demote the bottom LIR to Q, then prune. Otherwise insert as resident HIR at the top of S and the tail of Q.
- **Invariants.** The resident count is always ≤ `capacity`. The bottom of S is always LIR after pruning. Do not use `unsafe` or external crates. Port the baseline tests whose expected evictions are policy-agnostic. Replace the tests that assume LRU order with LIRS-specific tests: loop-of-(capacity+1) keeps most keys hitting, and a scan does not evict LIR entries.

## Rationale (optional)

S3-FIFO raised hit rate to 0.727 through admission filtering alone. LIRS uses inter-reference recency, which was designed for exactly this loop- and scan-heavy trace family. Starting from the baseline makes this a distinct policy family to compare against the v001 lineage.
