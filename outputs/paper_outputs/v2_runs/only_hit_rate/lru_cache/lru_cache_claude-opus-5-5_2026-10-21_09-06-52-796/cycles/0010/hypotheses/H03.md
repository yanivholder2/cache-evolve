# H03

Reverting only `MAX_FREQ` from 7 to 3 on cycle 9 v001, while keeping its `small`-to-`main` credit carryover, will show whether carryover alone keeps the hit-rate sliver over cycle 8 v003. Hit-rate should stay within 0.0002 of 0.7387 if the carryover was the source, and fall back toward cycle 8 v003's level if the cap was.

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In cycle/0009/v001's `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 7;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;`. Update the comment to cite H03 (cycle 10) as a cap-versus-carryover ablation.

Make no other code changes:

- The `main` branch, ghost path, `small_target` (capacity / 80) and `ghost_cap` (13 * capacity / 80) all stay as they are.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 9 v001 changed two things at once: the cap went 3 -> 7, and the promotion reset became `-= 1`. Cycles 9's insight and the cross-cycle insights both list "which part caused the sliver" as an open question. They also note that single-knob variants give clean answers. Cycle 7 found that the cap on its own overlaps with the other levers.

This is a pure ablation: one constant changes. It is also a different kind of experiment from H01 (representation) and H02 (aging rate).

How to read the result:
- Hit-rate holds: carryover was the cause, and the cap can go back to 3. That lets a key's freq fit in 2 bits, which a future compact node layout could use.
- Hit-rate drops: the gain came from the cap, and the carryover can be removed in a later simplification.
