# H02

Making `main`'s CLOCK reinsertion age credits by halving (`freq >>= 1`) instead of decrementing by one will raise hit-rate above 0.7387, because keys that were hot and have gone cold leave `main` within about 3 passes instead of up to 7.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

Make a single change in `evict_one()` in cycle/0009/v001's `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`. In the `main` branch (`else { let idx = self.main.pop_front() ... }`), when `node.freq > 0`, replace `node.freq -= 1;` with `node.freq >>= 1;`, then `push_back` as before.

Credit now follows 7 -> 3 -> 1 -> 0 -> evict. A key that saturated at 7 survives at most 3 extra rotations, where it used to survive 7. A key with freq 1 still gets exactly one reinsertion. Zero-credit arrivals keep their full-rotation grace pass unchanged, because they are not touched until they reach the head with freq 0.

Leave all of the following unchanged:
- `MAX_FREQ = 7`
- the saturating increments in `get()`/`put()`
- the `small` branch, including the `node.freq -= 1` carryover on promotion
- `small_target`, `ghost_cap`, the ghost path, and `push_ghost()`

The termination argument still holds, because halving strictly decreases any positive freq. Update the doc comment on `evict_one()` to say so.

Add a unit test: insert a key, `get` it 7 times so freq == 7, and force it into `main`. Then drive evictions and assert that after one rotation its freq is 3, after the next it is 1, and after the next it is 0. Keep every existing test passing.

## Rationale (optional)

v001's own insight names a built-in cost: with `MAX_FREQ = 7`, a key that was hot and then went cold can keep a `main` slot for up to 7 rotations. Earlier cycles changed the cap level (3 vs 7) and the entry credits. None changed the aging rate, so this is a different retention mechanism. It keeps the high cap's ability to tell keys apart at entry, and it makes stale credit expire on a logarithmic schedule instead of a linear one. It also does not touch zero-credit arrivals, which the SIEVE result (cycle 8) showed need their grace pass.

How to read the result:
- Hit-rate rises above 0.7387: the extra cap mainly pinned stale keys.
- Hit-rate falls: long, steady reuse on workload-a needs linear aging.
