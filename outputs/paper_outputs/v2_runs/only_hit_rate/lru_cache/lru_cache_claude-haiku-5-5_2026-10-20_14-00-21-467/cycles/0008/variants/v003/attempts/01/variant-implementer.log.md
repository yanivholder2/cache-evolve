**What the overlay does**
- **Frequency sketch:** a count-min sketch with 4 rows of `u8` counters, width `capacity.next_power_of_two()`, and a fixed seed per row. It hashes with `DefaultHasher`, so no external crates or `unsafe`.
- **Increments:** every `get()` and every `put()` bumps the key's counters. Counters halve after every `10 * capacity` increments.
- **Admission gate:** on a new key into a full cache, the new key displaces the LRU head only if its estimate is strictly greater than the victim's. Otherwise the new key is dropped and the victim stays. Ties reject.
- **Recency:** unchanged. Hits still move to MRU, and the map and arena stay in step.
- **Counters:** `admitted` and `rejected` are exposed through `admission_counts() -> (u64, u64)`. They count gate decisions only, i.e. inserts into a full cache. A `Drop` impl logs them.

**Gate risk**
- The universal and invariant suites (P1 capacity bound, P2 value fidelity, P3 no phantoms) should tolerate dropped inserts, since none of them requires a put key to remain resident. The invariant gate reads these properties from `src/test_utils/invariants.rs`.

**Known issue: existing unit tests**
- Several unit tests in `lru_cache.rs` assume unconditional admission. For example, `eviction_removes_lru` puts keys 1, 2, 3 into a capacity-2 cache. Each key's estimate is 1 at the point of comparison, so key 3 is rejected and key 1 stays. The test's `is_none` assertion for key 1 then fails.
- I left these tests unchanged because they are not gates and changing them would alter the hypothesis. Expect `cargo test --lib` to fail on them under this variant.

**Not measured**
- The hit rate on workload-a at size 2048 against the 0.665 baseline is unknown.
- The sketch adds about 8 KB for capacity 2048 (4 × 2048 bytes), plus a few words to `LruCache`. `Node` is unchanged.
