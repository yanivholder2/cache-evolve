# H01

Widening the frequency-aware tail window from four to eight entries will raise workload-a hit-rate at size 2048 above the incumbent's 0.735 by letting the least-hit victim search reach re-referenced keys that sit deeper in the tail.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

In the victim-selection path of the incumbent (the bounded tail-window scan that picks the least-hit entry among the four oldest), change the window length from 4 to 8. Keep the scan read-only over the list: it must not reorder the recency list, and only the chosen victim is removed.

Tie-breaking: when several entries in the window share the minimum hit count, evict the oldest of them (closest to the LRU end). This keeps the strict-recency fallback the baseline relies on whenever frequency gives no preference.

Preserve the per-entry hit counter's update rule and the hit path exactly as in the parent. Do not add new counters, lists, or admission checks. Make the window length a single named constant so it can be changed without touching the scan logic.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0007's v003 gave a marginal gain with a four-entry window. The cycle 0007 insight lists "does the gain hold with a larger window" as an open question, and the frequency signal has only been tried at one window length. A wider window tests whether the gain scales with how far the scan can see, without changing per-entry memory.
