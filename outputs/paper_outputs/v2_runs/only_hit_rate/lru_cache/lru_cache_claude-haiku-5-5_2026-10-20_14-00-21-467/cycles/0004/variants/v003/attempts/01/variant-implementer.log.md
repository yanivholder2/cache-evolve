**What the overlay does (H03, segmented LRU):**
- Entries are kept in two segments within one arena: probation and protected. Node layout gains an `in_protected` flag.
- A new key enters probation at the MRU end.
- A hit in probation, or a `put` on an existing key, moves the entry to protected's MRU end. If protected then exceeds `capacity * 4 / 5`, its LRU entry is demoted to probation's MRU end.
- A hit in protected moves the entry to protected's MRU end.
- Eviction takes probation's LRU entry, falling back to protected's LRU when probation is empty.
- The public API (`try_new`, `new`, `get`, `put`, and the `framework::Cache` impl) and the JNI file are unchanged.
