# H03

Shrinking the protected segment from 80% to 50% of capacity raises hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by giving probation more room to hold the scan stream.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

Change only the protected-segment share; leave the ghost filter, victim window, and recency handling as in the parent.

- Replace the protected-cap computation in `try_new()`, currently `prot_cap: (capacity.saturating_mul(4) / 5).max(1)`, with `prot_cap: (capacity / 2).max(1)`. Introduce named constants `PROT_NUM: usize = 1` and `PROT_DEN: usize = 2` next to `VICTIM_WINDOW`, and compute `prot_cap` as `(capacity.saturating_mul(PROT_NUM) / PROT_DEN).max(1)` so the share is a single tunable.
- Update the module header comment that says the protected segment is "~80% of capacity" to say the share is `PROT_NUM / PROT_DEN` of capacity.
- Leave `touch()`, the overflow demotion loop, `unlink()`, `push_back()`, and `select_victim()` unchanged. Demotion still moves the protected LRU to the probation MRU on overflow.
- Leave `prot_len` accounting unchanged.

Invariants to preserve: `prot_len <= prot_cap` holds at all times through the existing demotion loop; `prot_cap >= 1` when capacity is at least 1; the victim is still taken from the probation head when probation is non-empty, falling back to the protected head otherwise. Existing unit tests must still pass; the `access_updates_lru_order` and `get_middle_node_promotes_correctly` tests assume a protected cap of at least one node, which 50% of capacity 2 or 3 still satisfies.

## Rationale (optional)

The 80% protected share has never been varied; the cycle 0005 segmented result that lost hit-rate used a different structure, so it does not rule out this ratio. At 80% the probation list holds only about one-fifth of the cache, so a burst of new keys must evict from a short list. At 50% probation holds half the cache and absorbs more one-time keys before they reach the protected set. If the gain is absent, the protected share is not the limiting factor on this trace.
