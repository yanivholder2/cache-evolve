# H01

Stop counting rejected misses in the admission sketch of cycle/0005/v001 to raise hit-rate by keeping one-touch scan keys from building frequency.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the insert/admission path, when the admission gate rejects a candidate (its sketch estimate is below the probation victim's), do not let that rejection's miss increment the sketch beyond what was already recorded on the first access. Concretely: increment the sketch only on hits and on admitted inserts, not on every miss lookup. Keep the periodic halving, the 95% protected share and the gate comparison unchanged. A genuinely new hot key must still be admitted: if the candidate's estimate equals the victim's, admit (use `>=`) so ties favour newcomers. Preserve all cache-API behaviour.
