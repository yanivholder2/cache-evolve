# H01

Adding a bounded ghost list of recently evicted probation keys that lets re-referenced keys re-enter directly into protected will raise hit-rate over v003 on scan-heavy traces.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` (v003), add a ghost FIFO of evicted keys: a `VecDeque<K>` plus a `HashSet<K>`, bounded to `capacity` entries (keys only, no values). In `put()`, when evicting a victim from the PROBATION list, push its key onto the ghost FIFO (drop oldest if over bound, removing it from the set). When inserting a new key that is found in the ghost set, remove it from the ghost and push it to the PROTECTED MRU (run the same demotion loop as `touch` while protected len > protected_cap) instead of PROBATION. Evictions from PROTECTED are not recorded. `get` behaviour, the public API, and the capacity invariant (`map.len() <= capacity`) stay unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Keys re-requested shortly after eviction are probably hot but were evicted before their second hit. Ghost history recovers them, as in 2Q/ARC.
