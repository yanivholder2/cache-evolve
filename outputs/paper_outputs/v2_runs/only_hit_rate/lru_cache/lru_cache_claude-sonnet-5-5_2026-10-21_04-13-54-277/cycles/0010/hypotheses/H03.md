# H03

Gate probation-to-protected promotion on a sketch estimate of at least 2 to keep one-touch keys out of protected and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline cache source, restructure it into a segmented design only as needed: since baseline is strict LRU, implement in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` a two-list arena SLRU (probation + protected, protected_cap = 95% of capacity) with a 4-row count-min sketch (u8 counters, saturating at 15, halved every 10*capacity additions) that is recorded on get-miss and put of a non-resident key. In `touch()`, for a probation-resident node, promote to protected only if `sketch.estimate(hash(key)) >= 2`; otherwise move it to the probation MRU end. Admission on full cache: candidate admitted if `ce > ve` (victim = probation head, else protected head). Resident hits do not record in the sketch. Public API and Cache trait impl unchanged.
