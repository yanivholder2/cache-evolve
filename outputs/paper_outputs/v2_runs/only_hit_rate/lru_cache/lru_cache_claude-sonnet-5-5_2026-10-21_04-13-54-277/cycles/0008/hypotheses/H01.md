# H01

Adding an ARC-style adaptive protected-segment share, driven by a small ghost list of probation evictions, will raise hit-rate over v003's fixed share.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

In v003's cache, replace the fixed protected-capacity constant with a mutable `protected_cap` field. Add a bounded ghost FIFO (capacity = cache capacity / 4, keys only, with a HashSet or the existing map) recording keys evicted from probation. On a `put()`/miss whose key is found in the ghost list, decrease `protected_cap` by 1 (never below 50% of capacity) so probation grows, and remove the key from the ghost list. On a hit in the protected segment's LRU-tail quarter (approximate with a rank counter or a tail-region check), increase `protected_cap` by 1 (never above 95% of capacity). When `protected_cap` shrinks below the current protected length, demote the protected LRU tail to probation MRU. Keep the sketch gate (counting rejected misses) unchanged. Initial value equals v003's current share. Invariants: total entries never exceed capacity; get/put semantics unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Fixed share is saturated at 90-95% on this trace; adaptivity tests whether any trace-phase variance can be exploited.
