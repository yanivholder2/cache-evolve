# H02

Adding a small W-TinyLFU-style window LRU (about 1% of capacity) in front of v003's sketch-gated probation segment will raise hit-rate by letting bursty new keys be hit before facing admission.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

In v003, add a third list, the window, with capacity max(1, capacity/100). New keys on `put()` always enter the window MRU (no sketch check at entry). When the window overflows, its LRU victim becomes an admission candidate: compare `sketch.estimate(candidate)` to `sketch.estimate(probation LRU head)`; if the candidate's estimate is strictly greater, evict the probation head and insert the candidate at probation MRU; otherwise drop the candidate. Hits in the window just move to window MRU (and increment the sketch). Hits in probation promote to protected as in v003; protected size shrinks by the window size so total capacity is unchanged. Keep sketch aging unchanged and keep counting rejected misses. Total entries must never exceed capacity.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 gates admission at entry, which can reject keys with imminent short-term reuse; a window defers the decision.
