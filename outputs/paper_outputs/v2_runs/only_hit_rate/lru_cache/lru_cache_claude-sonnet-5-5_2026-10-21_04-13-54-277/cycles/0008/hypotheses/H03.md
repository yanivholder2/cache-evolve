# H03

Using conservative-update count-min increments with saturating 4-bit counters packed two per byte in v003's sketch will cut metadata-size by about half and sharpen frequency estimates without losing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

In v003's sketch, change the counter storage from 4 rows of u8 to 4 rows of 4-bit nibbles (two counters per byte, saturate at 15), halving the sketch bytes. Make `increment(key)` conservative: compute the 4 row indices, find the minimum current value, and increment only the counters equal to that minimum (skip if already 15). `estimate` remains the min across rows. Adjust the aging step to halve every nibble (`(b >> 1) & 0x77`) and scale the aging window to the new max of 15 (sample window = 10 × sketch width, as before in ratio to counter max). Leave the SLRU logic and the admission comparison untouched. Preserve the behaviour of counting rejected misses.

## Rationale (optional)

Targets the metadata-size dimension while testing that conservative updates reduce collision-inflated counts that cause stale-key blocking.
