# H01

Add a TinyLFU-style frequency admission filter on full-cache inserts to raise hit-rate by rejecting one-hit scan keys that are colder than the probation victim.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the overlay `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, add a small count-min sketch field (4 rows of u8 counters, total about 4*capacity.next_power_of_two() entries, hashed with `std::collections::hash_map::DefaultHasher` seeded per row by XORing a constant). Increment the sketch on every `get` and `put` of a key (hit or miss). Halve all counters after `10*capacity` increments (aging). In `put`, for a new key when `nodes.len() >= capacity`: compute the estimated frequency of the candidate and of the probation LRU victim (`lists[PROBATION].head`). If candidate freq < victim freq, drop the insert (do not evict, do not insert; the sketch is already incremented). Otherwise evict and insert as before. Keep the 95% protected share, `touch`, `unlink`, `push_back` unchanged. No unsafe, no external crates.

## Rationale (optional)

The scan-heavy trace rewards keeping one-touch keys out; admission filtering extends that beyond segmentation, acting on probation entry.
