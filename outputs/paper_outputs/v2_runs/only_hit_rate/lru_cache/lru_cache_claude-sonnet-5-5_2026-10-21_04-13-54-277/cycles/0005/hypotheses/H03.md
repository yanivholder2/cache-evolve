# H03

Pick the lowest-frequency victim among the 4 oldest probation entries using a saturating 2-bit hit counter to raise hit-rate over pure probation-LRU eviction.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In overlay `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, add `freq: u8` to `Node` (init 0 on insert and when a slot is reused). In `touch`, increment `freq` saturating at 3 (for both probation and protected hits). In `put` eviction branch, when probation is non-empty, walk from `lists[PROBATION].head` along `next` up to 4 nodes and choose the one with minimum `freq` (ties go to the earliest, i.e. oldest). On demotion in `touch`, set the demoted node's freq to 0. Use the chosen node as `victim` in the existing unlink/reuse logic; fall back to protected head as before when probation is empty. Keep protected_cap at 95%.

## Rationale (optional)

A node that was demoted then gets hit once more would only be saved by recency today; a short frequency-aware scan adds a cheap LFU signal in probation without changing the structure.
