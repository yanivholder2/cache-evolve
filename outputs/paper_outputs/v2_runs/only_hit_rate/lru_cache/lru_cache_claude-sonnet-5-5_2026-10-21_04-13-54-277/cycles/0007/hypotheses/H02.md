# H02

Lengthening the sketch aging window from 10x to 40x capacity (and clearing the doorkeeper on its own shorter 10x-capacity cycle) should raise hit-rate on long-distance-reuse keys.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In `try_new` of `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, set `sample = capacity.saturating_mul(40)`. Add a field `door_ops: usize` and `door_period = capacity*10`. In `record()`, increment `door_ops` on every call; when it reaches `door_period`, zero the `door` bitset and reset `door_ops`. Remove the door clearing from the halving block in `sketch_inc`, so halving only shifts counters. Nothing else changes. Invariants: counters saturate at u8::MAX, door vector length unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 noted clearing the doorkeeper at every halving forgets first sightings, and a slower decay retains frequency history for recurring keys.
