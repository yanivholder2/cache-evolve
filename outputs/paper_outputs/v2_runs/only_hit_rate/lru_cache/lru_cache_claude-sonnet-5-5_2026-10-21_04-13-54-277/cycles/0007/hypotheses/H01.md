# H01

Choosing the coldest of the 4 oldest probation entries as the eviction victim (by sketch estimate) should raise hit-rate over strict probation-LRU victim selection.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In `put()` of `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, replace the single victim `lists[seg].head` with a bounded scan: walk up to 4 nodes from the LRU head of the chosen segment via `next`, compute `sketch_est(key_hash(node.key))` for each, and pick the node with the lowest estimate (ties go to the earliest, i.e. LRU-most). Use that node as `victim` in the gate comparison `sketch_est(h) < sketch_est(vh)` and in the unlink/reuse path. `unlink` already handles mid-list removal. Keep everything else (95% protected share, doorkeeper, halving) unchanged. Invariants: map/arena consistency, capacity bound, victim never the key being inserted.

## Rationale (optional)

Sketch frequency now persists across segments, so the earlier "frequency-aware scan was inert" failure (no persisted frequency) does not apply.
