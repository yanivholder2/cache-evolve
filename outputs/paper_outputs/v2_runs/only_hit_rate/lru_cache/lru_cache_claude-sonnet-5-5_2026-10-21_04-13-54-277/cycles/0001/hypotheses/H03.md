# H03

Replace single-list LRU with a segmented LRU (probationary and protected segments, promotion on second hit) to raise hit rate on the scan-heavy LIRS workload.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, keep the arena and HashMap but maintain two linked lists over the same arena: probation and protected, each with its own head/tail. Add a `segment: bool` flag to `Node`. Protected capacity = capacity * 4 / 5 (min 1 when capacity > 1). New keys are inserted at the MRU end of probation. A `get` or update hit on a probation node moves it to the MRU end of protected; if protected exceeds its limit, demote the protected LRU node to the MRU end of probation. A hit on a protected node moves it to protected MRU. Eviction takes the probation LRU (falling back to protected LRU if probation is empty); reuse its arena slot as in the baseline. Total entries must never exceed `capacity`. Keep the public API and JNI-visible behaviour unchanged. Note: this deviates from strict LRU order, so check the invariant gate; if it requires strict LRU, the variant will fail fast, which itself is informative.
