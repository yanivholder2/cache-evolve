# H01

Raising the protected segment share of the segmented LRU above 80% will raise hit-rate on the scan-heavy trace.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In the `LruCache` constructor, change `protected_cap` from its current fraction of capacity to 90% of capacity (minimum 1, and always leaving at least 1 probation slot when capacity >= 2). Leave promotion-on-second-hit, probation-LRU eviction, and the demotion loop unchanged. Preserve all API behaviour and capacity invariants (total len never exceeds capacity).

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 showed that shrinking the protected share lowered hit-rate, so the opposite direction is the natural test.
