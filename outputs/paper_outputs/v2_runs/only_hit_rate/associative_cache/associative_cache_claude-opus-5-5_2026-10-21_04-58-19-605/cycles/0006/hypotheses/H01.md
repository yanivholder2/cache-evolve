# H01

Shrinking v003's per-bucket ghost ring from 4 entries to 2 will hold hit rate on workload-a at or above 0.731 while cutting per-bucket metadata-size and miss-path scan cost.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes. Keep everything else from
cycle/0004/v003 exactly as it is: the `clock_mode` gate (ClassicLRU only), `CLOCK_MAX = 3`,
saturating increments in `get_from_position`, `clock_find_victim`, the in-place
`std::mem::replace` with `self.hand = idx` resting on the newcomer, the admission rule in
`admission_counter` (counter 1 on a ghost hit with the entry consumed, 0 otherwise), calling
`admission_counter` before the eviction, and `record_ghost` at both GCLOCK eviction sites.

1. Change `const GHOST_LEN: usize = 4;` to `const GHOST_LEN: usize = 2;`. Update the struct doc
   comment to say the ring holds 2 keys.
2. `ghost_pos` stays a `u8` and `record_ghost` keeps its modulo-`GHOST_LEN` advance. Keep the
   lookup in `admission_counter` as a plain linear scan (now 2 compares). Do not add hashing,
   fingerprints or any other change: this tests ring capacity only.
3. Invariants: the bucket never holds more than `max_size` items; the ring is bounded at 2 keys;
   `get` never consults the ring; the sweep terminates within `CLOCK_MAX * len + 1` steps;
   non-ClassicLRU policies keep baseline behaviour.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0005 showed ring size has an interior peak at or below 4 (16 regressed), and the open
questions ask directly whether 2–3 entries match 4. Only the most recent returners appear to carry
useful signal. If 2 matches 0.731, the CLOCK+ghost line gets the same hit rate for half the ghost
memory and half the miss-path scan. Refutation: hit rate below 0.731 means returners at eviction
distance 3–4 contribute materially and 4 is near the optimum.
