# H03

On the 1/8-depth LRU+ghost line, recording into the ghost ring only keys that are evicted without ever being hit will raise hit rate on workload-a above 0.7226 by keeping the ring narrow and focused on newcomers that narrowly missed their reuse.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes. Keep cycle/0005/v002's
`midpoint_insert` gate (ClassicLRU only), the 1/8 insert depth for ordinary newcomers, the
ghost-hit insert at `items.len() * GHOST_DEPTH_NUM / GHOST_DEPTH_DEN` (1/4, index 3 at len 15),
`GHOST_LEN = 4`, `in_ghosts` (linear scan, consume on match, called before the eviction), and the
hit path that promotes to MRU via `self.eviction_policy.get_from_position`.

1. Per-item "was hit" mark: reuse the existing `lfu_counter` field on the item (unused on this
   line). In `put`'s `midpoint_insert` branch, set `item.lfu_counter = 0` before inserting it.
   In the bucket's get/hit path, when `self.midpoint_insert` is true, set the hit item's
   `lfu_counter = 1` (do it on the item at the found position before or after delegating to
   `get_from_position`, making sure to target the same item after the MRU move). Do not add a new
   field to the item struct.
2. Selective recording: in `put`, where v002 does `if let Some(ev) = self.evict_item() {
   self.record_ghost(ev.key) }`, only call `record_ghost` when `ev.lfu_counter == 0`. Keys that
   were hit at least once and then aged down to index 0 are long-reuse residents and are no
   longer recorded.
3. Invariants: the bucket never exceeds `max_size`; ring bounded at 4 keys; `get` never consults
   the ring; when `midpoint_insert` is false, behaviour is unchanged from baseline; no new
   per-item or per-bucket memory beyond v002.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0005 showed the ghost signal works best when narrow: a bigger ring diluted it with
long-reuse keys. On the LRU line, ring slots are also spent on keys that were promoted to MRU and
then slowly aged out — exactly the long-reuse population that hurt v001 in cycle 0005. Filtering by
"never hit" makes each of the 4 slots hold a short-reuse candidate without enlarging the ring. This
is the secondary line and differs from H01/H02 (CLOCK line, where victims are already zero-count).
Refutation: hit rate at or below 0.7226 means returning previously-hit keys were contributing as
much as never-hit ones.
