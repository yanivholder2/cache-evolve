# H03

Lowering the GCLOCK saturation ceiling `CLOCK_MAX` from 3 to 2 will raise hit rate on workload-a above 0.731, because once-hot keys lose protection faster after a LIRS phase change, and the shorter sweep also cuts the worst-case miss path.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes, and only one constant:

1. Change `const CLOCK_MAX: usize = 3;` to `const CLOCK_MAX: usize = 2;`. Update the doc
   comments that mention the counter range (`0..=CLOCK_MAX`) and the sweep bound
   (`CLOCK_MAX * len + 1` steps), so they stay correct.
2. Leave everything else from cycle/0007/v001 unchanged: the saturating increment in
   `get_from_position` (it now saturates at 2), plain newcomers at counter 0, ghost-admitted
   newcomers at counter 1 (still strictly below the cap, so the ghost reward still ranks below a
   proven-hot key), the 4-entry 16-bit fingerprint ring with consume-on-match,
   `clock_find_victim`, the hand resting on the newcomer, and both eviction sites recording ghosts.
3. Invariants: counters stay in `0..=2`. The sweep terminates within `2 * len + 1` steps. The
   bucket never holds more than `max_size` items. Non-ClassicLRU policies keep the baseline
   behaviour exactly.

## Rationale (optional)

Every tuned knob on the CLOCK line has been bracketed from both sides: ring size (2, 4, 16),
ring admission, newcomer leniency and hand placement. The counter ceiling has not. It was set to
3 in cycle 2, when GCLOCK beat 1-bit CLOCK, and has never been probed since. Every cycle shows
that protection beyond the strict newcomer filter does not pay on workload-a (cycles 3, 4 and
7). That suggests a smaller ceiling could free slots held by keys that were hot in an earlier
loop phase. Keeping the ceiling at 2 rather than 1 preserves the ordering ghost (1) < hot (2),
so cycle 4's capped ghost bonus still means something. This is a bracket test: H01 in this
cohort probes the opposite direction (more protection after the first hit). Together the two
locate the hit-side optimum. Refutation: a hit rate below about 0.729 means the third
protection level shields keys that really are reused. A tie at 0.731 still favours ceiling 2
once per-op-time is measured, because the worst-case sweep is a third shorter.
