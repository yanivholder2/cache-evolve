# H01

Replacing v003's `[Option<K>; 4]` ghost ring with a ring of four non-zero 16-bit key fingerprints will cut per-bucket metadata-size while keeping hit rate on workload-a within noise of 0.731.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes. Keep everything else from
cycle/0004/v003 as it is: the `clock_mode` gate (ClassicLRU only), `CLOCK_MAX = 3`,
`GHOST_LEN = 4`, the saturating increment in `get_from_position`, `clock_find_victim`, the
in-place `std::mem::replace` with `self.hand = idx` resting on the newcomer, and the rule that
a ghost hit admits with counter 1 and consumes the entry.

1. Fingerprint helper: add a private `fn ghost_fp(key: &K) -> u16`. Hash the key with a small
   local hasher, for example a private `struct FpHasher(u64)` that implements
   `std::hash::Hasher` with FNV-1a over `write` bytes plus a `write_u64` fast path. Do not use
   `DefaultHasher`, which is too slow on the miss path. Finish with a 64-bit mix (e.g.
   `x ^= x >> 33; x = x.wrapping_mul(0xff51afd7ed558ccd); x ^= x >> 33;`). Take the **top** 16
   bits, so the fingerprint does not share the low bits used for bucket indexing, and map 0 to
   1 so that 0 can mean "empty".
2. Storage: change `ghosts: [Option<K>; GHOST_LEN]` to `ghosts: [u16; GHOST_LEN]` and initialise
   it to `[0; GHOST_LEN]` in `new`. Keep `ghost_pos: u8`. The per-bucket ghost state drops from
   4 × `size_of::<Option<K>>()` (64 bytes for a u64 key) to 8 bytes.
3. `record_ghost(&mut self, key: K)`: store `ghost_fp(&key)` at `ghost_pos`, then advance the
   ring as now.
4. `admission_counter(&mut self, key: &K)`: compute `fp = ghost_fp(key)` once, then scan the
   4 slots for `*g == fp`. On a match set `*g = 0` and return 1. Otherwise return 0. Call it at
   the same two sites as now, before the eviction.
5. Invariants: the bucket never holds more than `max_size` items. The ring stays bounded at 4
   entries. `get` never reads the ring. Non-ClassicLRU policies keep the baseline behaviour. A
   false positive (probability about 4/65535 per miss) only gives a newcomer counter 1 instead
   of 0. It can never affect correctness or a stored value.

## Rationale (optional)

Hit rate on workload-a has been flat at 0.731 for two cycles, and cycle 0006 says the deciding
axes are now metadata-size and per-op-time. The ring holds only ghosts, and ghosts need
membership, not identity. A 16-bit fingerprint keeps the 4-entry ring that cycle 0006 found
locally optimal, and keeps v003's hit-rate behaviour (collisions are negligible), at about
one-eighth of the memory. The scan also compares u16s instead of `Option<K>`. Refutation: a
hit rate below about 0.729 (outside the noise band seen in cycle 0006) would mean
fingerprinting changes admission more than expected. No metadata-size reduction would mean
the measure does not count the ghost ring.
