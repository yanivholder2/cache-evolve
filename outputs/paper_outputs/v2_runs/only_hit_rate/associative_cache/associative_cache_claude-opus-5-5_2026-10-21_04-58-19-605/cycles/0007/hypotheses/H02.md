# H02

On the 1/8-depth LRU+ghost line, inserting ghost-hit keys at 3/8 depth instead of 1/4 will raise hit rate on workload-a above 0.723 by giving returners whose reuse distance is just past the bucket enough room to survive until their next access.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes. Keep everything else from
cycle/0005/v002 as it is: the `midpoint_insert` gate (ClassicLRU only), the 1/8 insert depth
for ordinary newcomers (`INSERT_DEPTH_NUM = 1`, `INSERT_DEPTH_DEN = 8`), `GHOST_LEN = 4`,
`record_ghost` on every miss-path eviction (no admission filter, since cycle 0006 v003 showed
filtering hurts), `in_ghosts` (linear scan, consume on match, called before the eviction), and
the hit path that promotes to MRU through `self.eviction_policy.get_from_position`.

1. Change the ghost-hit depth constants from `GHOST_DEPTH_NUM = 1`, `GHOST_DEPTH_DEN = 4` to
   `GHOST_DEPTH_NUM = 3`, `GHOST_DEPTH_DEN = 8`. With the post-eviction length of 15, a ghost
   hit is then inserted at index `15 * 3 / 8 = 5` instead of index 3. Ordinary newcomers stay
   at index 1.
2. Keep the index computed after the eviction, as now, and keep the insert going through
   `self.items.insert(idx, item)`. Guard with `idx.min(self.items.len())` so short buckets
   (non-full push path) never go out of bounds. On the non-full path keep v002's existing
   behaviour, just with the new constants.
3. Update the doc comment on the constants to say "capped reward: 3/8 instead of 1/8".
4. Invariants: the bucket never holds more than `max_size` items, because eviction still runs
   before the insert. The ring stays bounded at 4 keys. `get` never consults the ring. When
   `midpoint_insert` is false, behaviour is the baseline's.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0006's continuation names ghost-hit insert depth as the only hit-rate lever left untested
on the LRU line. On this line, the ring is filled largely by hit keys that aged to index 0, so
their reuse distance is just past one bucket's worth of misses. Index 3 gives them only two
more misses of grace than an ordinary newcomer. Index 5 is still well short of the midpoint and
far from the MRU promotion that lost in cycle 0002, so the reward stays capped. Refutation: a
hit rate at or below 0.723 means 1/4 is already at or past the peak of the ghost-depth curve,
and the LRU line cannot close its gap to CLOCK+ghost (0.731) through depth alone.
