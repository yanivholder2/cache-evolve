# H01

Growing v003's per-bucket ghost ring from 4 to 16 entries (one per bucket slot) will raise hit rate on workload-a above 0.731, because more returning keys with reuse distances longer than four bucket evictions will get the capped counter-1 admission.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Only `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` changes. Keep everything else from
cycle/0004/v003 exactly as it is: the `clock_mode` gate (ClassicLRU only), `CLOCK_MAX = 3`,
saturating increments in `get_from_position`, `clock_find_victim`, the in-place
`std::mem::replace` with `self.hand = idx` resting on the newcomer, the admission rule (counter 1
on a ghost hit, 0 otherwise), consuming the ghost entry on a match, and calling
`admission_counter` before the eviction.

1. Change `const GHOST_LEN: usize = 4;` to `const GHOST_LEN: usize = 16;`. A full bucket holds
   16 items, so the ring now holds one bucket's worth of evicted keys.
2. `ghost_pos` stays a `u8`; 16 fits easily. The modulo in `record_ghost` stays as it is. Update
   the doc comment on the struct to say the ring holds 16 keys.
3. Keep `admission_counter` a linear scan over `self.ghosts`. Do not add hashing or a
   fingerprint filter. This hypothesis tests ring capacity only, so the lookup method must not
   change.
4. Invariants: the bucket never holds more than `max_size` items. The ring is bounded at 16 keys.
   `get` never consults the ring. Non-ClassicLRU policies keep baseline behaviour.

## Rationale (optional)

The v003 insight and the cycle 0004 open questions both name ring size as the next thing to test,
and point out that 4 was a guess. If keys that return after more than 4 evictions in their bucket
still benefit from a single extra sweep pass, a larger ring will catch them. If the larger ring
refutes the claim, with a hit rate at or below 0.731, the useful returners are mostly short-distance
ones and a large ring is just more space on the miss path. That would point toward 4 or fewer
entries. Cost: 12 more `Option<K>` per bucket and a 16-entry scan on every miss. Neither is
measured yet.
