# H01

Capping the bucket victim scan at 8 sampled slots cuts per-op-time on workload-a while holding hit-rate within 0.002 of 0.664.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Keep the fixed-width 64-slot bucket layout from `BucketGeometry::layout` unchanged. Modify the victim selection in `evict_one()` so that, on a miss into a full bucket, it does not scan all 64 slots. Instead, keep a per-bucket rotating cursor (a small integer stored alongside the bucket) and examine at most 8 occupied slots starting at the cursor, wrapping around. Among those 8, evict the slot with the lowest recency or frequency counter, the same ordering key the parent uses. Advance the cursor past the evicted slot after each eviction. If fewer than 8 slots are occupied, scan the occupied ones. Do not change the promotion logic, the hit path, or the fallback split for budgets not divisible by 64. The cursor must be reset to 0 when the bucket is cleared.

## Rationale (optional)

The 0001 insight notes that the 64-slot scan cost was predicted but never measured, and that conflict misses are a minor share of misses. That suggests the exact-minimum scan buys little hit-rate over a sampled minimum, while its per-op cost grows with bucket width. This hypothesis tests whether the scan cost can be cut without giving up the small gain that earned v001 its place.
