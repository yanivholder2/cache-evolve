# H03

Replacing the bucket's boxed `dyn EvictionPolicy` dispatch in its hot get path with statically dispatched enum matching will lower get p50 latency below the baseline 69 ns without changing hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Overlay `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` only. Keep the `AssociativeCacheBucket` struct name, its `new(settings)` constructor, and the `Bucket<K, V>` trait impl signatures unchanged.

Change the `eviction_policy` field from `Box<dyn EvictionPolicy<K, V>>` to a concrete enum `BucketPolicy<K, V>` with one variant per policy (`ClassicLRU(ClassicLRU)`, `TimestampLRU(TimestampLRU)`, `Lifo(Lifo)`, `Lfu(Lfu)`, `Fifo(Fifo)`, `Swap(Swap)`), constructed in `new` from `settings.eviction_policy` by matching on `EvictionPolicyKind`. The policy structs are imported from `crate::shared::eviction_policy`. Implement `EvictionPolicy<K, V>` for `BucketPolicy<K, V>` by delegating each method (`name`, `get_from_position`, `eviction_candidate_index`, `promotion_candidate_index`) to the wrapped struct with a `match`.

In `get_from_position`, call the inner policy through the concrete enum so the call is statically dispatched. `get_eviction_policy(&self) -> &dyn EvictionPolicy<K, V>` must still return a trait-object reference; return `self.eviction_policy` as `&dyn` (the enum implements the trait), so its signature stays unchanged.

Invariants: no change to eviction order, promotion order, or the `Item` layout; behaviour must be identical for every policy kind. Keep `max_size` and `items` as they are. `Vec::with_capacity(settings.bucket_size)` allocation stays as in the baseline.

Expected effect: removes one indirect vtable call per hit on the get path, targeting the get p50 (69 ns) and p99 (101 ns) in the per-op-time dimension. Hit rate should be unchanged, so this hypothesis is a pure latency test. If the cache core calls eviction methods through `get_eviction_policy()` on the put path, that dispatch remains dynamic; the expected gain is confined to the bucket's own `get_from_position`, so measure get and put separately.
