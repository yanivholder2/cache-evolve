# H02

Bounding the probationary-region victim scan to 8 sampled slots in v003 will cut per-op time without reducing hit-rate below 0.7145 on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's two-region bucket and its probationary-first eviction order. Change only the victim-selection routine that scans the probationary region: instead of visiting every probationary slot in the bucket, visit at most 8 slots, starting from a rotating cursor stored per bucket and advanced by the number of slots visited, and evict the best candidate among those visited (using v003's existing ordering key). If the probationary region has 8 or fewer slots, the scan is exhaustive and behaviour matches v003 exactly.

Fall back to the protected region only when the probationary region is empty, as v003 already does. Locate the scan in the parent's source; the cursor is the only new state and is a single integer per bucket.

Make sure the bounded scan is actually reached on the evaluated path. The overlay must be confirmed active (for example, by a debug counter in the variant only, not in the gate surface) before any flat hit-rate is read as a result.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle-0003 sampled-scan variant (v001) left hit-rate roughly flat at 0.664 (activation not confirmed), which suggests exact-minimum victim selection buys little. This hypothesis carries that cost-side idea onto the incumbent and targets per-op time, which no variant has yet measured. Hit-rate is a guardrail here, not the target: the variant should be judged on per-op time with hit-rate at or above the incumbent.
