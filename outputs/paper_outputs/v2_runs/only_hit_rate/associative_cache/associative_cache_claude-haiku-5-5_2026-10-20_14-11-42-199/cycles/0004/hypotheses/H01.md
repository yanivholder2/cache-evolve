# H01

Adding a protected sub-region with promotion on second hit to the two-region bucket will raise hit-rate on workload-a above the incumbent's 0.7145 by keeping re-referenced entries out of reach of single-use inserts.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's two-region bucket layout and probationary-first eviction unchanged. Add a third state to the entry metadata, `protected`, that is set only when an entry in the probationary region is hit a second time (a hit on an entry already marked as having been hit once moves it to protected). Entries in the protected region are never chosen by the victim-selection routine while any probationary entry exists in the same bucket.

Cap the protected region at half the bucket's slots. When a promotion would exceed the cap, demote the least recently hit protected entry back to the probationary region (it keeps its hit mark) before promoting the new one.

Locate the promotion logic in the parent's source and extend it; do not change the victim-scan order within the probationary region. Preserve the existing insert path and the hit-counting used for v003's region choice. The only new per-entry state is the protected flag, and it must not change the fitness-vector schema.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 showed that evicting probationary entries first improves hit-rate. This hypothesis tests whether a second reuse signal, one hit versus two, separates the working set from one-shot entries more sharply than a single region split does. It keeps the scan-resistance mechanism of the incumbent and adds a protection tier, so any gain is attributable to the promotion rule.
