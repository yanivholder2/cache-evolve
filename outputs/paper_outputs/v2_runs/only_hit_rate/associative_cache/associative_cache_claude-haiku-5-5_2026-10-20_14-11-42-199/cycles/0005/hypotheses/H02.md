# H02

Removing demote-on-promotion from the second-hit protected tier keeps protected entries shielded and raises workload-a hit-rate above the cycle 0003 incumbent.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Keep the probationary-first eviction and the half-bucket protected cap from cycle 0004 v001. Change the promotion path so that protected entries are not pushed back into the eviction pool:

- When an entry reaches its second hit and the protected half-bucket is already full, do not demote the least recently hit protected entry. Instead, skip the promotion: the new entry stays in the probationary region with its hit recorded, so it can be promoted later when a protected slot frees.
- A protected entry leaves the protected region only through eviction, and only when the bucket has no probationary entry left to evict. Preserve that ordering.
- Do not change the cap size, the promotion trigger (second hit), or the probationary-first victim selection.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 v001 reported that the demote-on-promotion step, not the second-hit trigger, is the likely cause of its hit-rate loss. The insight says the protected working set keeps cycling back into the eviction pool. This hypothesis tests that cause directly by removing the forced demotion. It is a retry of the protected tier with a changed demotion rule, as the cycle 0004 continuation recommends.
