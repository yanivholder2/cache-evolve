# H01

Adding the shared-frequency admission gate to the incumbent's probationary-first eviction raises workload-a hit-rate above 0.7145.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Start from the cycle 0003 v003 two-region bucket split, which evicts from the probationary region first. Add the admission gate from cycle 0004 v003 as a separate step in the miss path:

- Keep one frequency counter that covers both incoming keys and resident keys, so both are compared on the same scale. Update it on every access, hit or miss.
- On a miss where the victim is chosen by the existing probationary-first rule, admit the incoming key only if its counter value is strictly greater than the victim's counter value. If it is not, bypass the insert: serve the miss without displacing the victim.
- Do not add periodic halving or any other aging step to the counter. Aging-based LFU variants gave no hit-rate gain in cycles 0001 and 0002.
- Leave the probationary-first victim selection and the promotion logic unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The incumbent improved hit-rate by changing which entries get displaced. Cycle 0004 v003 showed that an admission gate on a shared counter also helps, but on its own it reached only 0.6956, below the incumbent. The two mechanisms act on different decisions (whether to admit versus which resident to evict), so they may stack.
