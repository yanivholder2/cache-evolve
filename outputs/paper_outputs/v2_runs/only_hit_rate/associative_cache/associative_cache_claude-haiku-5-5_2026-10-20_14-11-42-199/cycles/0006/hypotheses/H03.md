# H03

Shrinking the protected region of the segmented bucket from about 75% to 50% of slots will raise workload-a hit-rate above 0.7427 by giving probation room to absorb one-shot entries.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`, change the capacity split computed in `AssociativeCacheBucket::new`.

- Replace `let prob_cap = ((max_size + 2) / 4).max(1);` with `let prob_cap = (max_size / 2).max(1);`. The protected capacity `prot_cap = max_size.saturating_sub(prob_cap)` is then about 50% of `max_size`, where it was about 75%.
- Update the doc comments on the struct field `prot_cap` and on the `items` docs from "≈75%" and "≈25%" to "≈50%" and "≈50%".
- Keep every other mechanism unchanged: probationary-first eviction, promotion on a probation hit, the demotion of the protected victim when `protected_len() == prot_cap`, and the admission gate in `mod.rs`.
- Keep the `prot_cap == 0` branch in `touch` working. With the new formula `prot_cap` is 0 only when `max_size` is 1, so the existing guard stays valid.
- Do not change the eviction policy, `get_from_position`, or any hash or index logic.

The protected-tier cap is the suspect named in cycle 0004 and cycle 0005 for the protected-tier failures. This hypothesis isolates the cap-size half of that question. It does not change the demotion rule, so a flat or negative result would point at the second-hit signal instead.
