# H03

Driving v002's AgedLfu decay by cache-wide insert count instead of per-bucket hits makes the aging actually reorder evictions, raising hit-rate above 0.6611 on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Keep v002's `AgedLfu` policy type and its halving of `lfu_counter` values. Change the trigger: remove the per-bucket hit counter that currently fires the halving every 64 bucket hits, and instead keep one cache-wide insert counter in the cache struct. When the insert counter reaches a period `P = max(64, capacity / 8)` (capacity is the total slot budget, 2048 in the evaluated configuration), halve every `lfu_counter` in all buckets and reset the counter to 0. Compute `P` once at construction, not per operation. Eviction order logic (the minimum-counter victim scan) stays as in v002. Do not touch the eval selection path or any test or harness files; if the aging policy is not selected by the harness, record that in the insight rather than editing the harness.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's insight found hit-rate identical to baseline and could not tell whether the decay never fired or never changed eviction order. A period tied to insert volume and capacity makes the decay fire many times over a 2048-slot run, so the effect either shows up in hit-rate or is refuted. The cycle 0001 continuation asked for re-testing the aging policy only after confirming it changes eviction, so this variant also reports how often the halving fires in its insight.
