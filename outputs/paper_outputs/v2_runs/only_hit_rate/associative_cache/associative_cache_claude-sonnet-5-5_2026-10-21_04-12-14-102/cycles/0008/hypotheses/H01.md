# H01

Insert new entries at the LRU end of the probation segment (LIP-style) in the v001 design to raise hit rate on scan-heavy traces.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In the insert path of the associative cache overlay (the code that places a missed key into a full or non-full bucket), change placement so a newly inserted entry goes to the eviction-end of the probation segment instead of its MRU end. Victim selection stays probation-first (evict the probation entry at the eviction end), so an entry never touched again is the next victim, while a hit still promotes into the protected segment via the existing first-hit promotion. Keep the probation size at slots/16 of the (doubled) associativity, the protected-overflow demotion logic, and slot legality (everything stays within the hashed bucket). Only the insertion position changes.

## Rationale (optional)

One-hit-wonder scan entries then cannot displace other probation entries that may still receive their first re-reference.
