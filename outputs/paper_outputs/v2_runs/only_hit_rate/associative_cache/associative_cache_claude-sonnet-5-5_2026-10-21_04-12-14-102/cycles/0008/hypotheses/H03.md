# H03

Make protected-segment eviction recency-biased by demoting only the protected LRU entry into probation when probation is full, with promotion to protected requiring a second hit only while the protected segment is under-filled, to raise hit rate over the baseline.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline plain move-to-back LRU bucket, add a lean positional segmented design with a different promotion rule than earlier winners: the bucket is an ordered array; the first slots/16 positions from the eviction end are the probation zone. On a hit in the probation zone, move the entry to the back (MRU) as the baseline does. On a hit in the rest of the bucket, move it to the back only if it is not already within the most-recent quarter of the bucket (skipping the move otherwise, which avoids shifting work). On insert into a full bucket, evict the entry at the eviction end and place the new entry just above the probation zone boundary rather than at MRU. No extra per-entry metadata, no ghost state, and every slot stays within the hashed bucket.

## Rationale (optional)

Tests a promotion-by-position family distinct from first-hit promotion, from the baseline, as a diversity probe.
