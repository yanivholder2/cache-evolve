# H01

Add a per-bucket probationary segment (new entries inserted at the LRU-side segment, promoted to the protected segment only on a second hit) on top of v001's 32x64 geometry to raise hit-rate on the LIRS trace.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

In the bucket logic of the overlay, keep v001's 32 buckets x 64 ways and full move-to-back on a hit for protected entries. Partition each bucket's Vec logically at an index `probation_len` (about 16 of 64 slots, tracked as a small per-bucket counter or by a one-bit flag per entry). New inserts go into the probationary region. A hit on a probationary entry moves it to the back (protected MRU end). When the bucket is full, evict the oldest probationary entry first, and only evict the protected LRU if probation is empty. Do not weaken promotion for protected entries. Keep the public API and total capacity unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0001 showed that shallower promotion lost hit-rate. A separate admission region changes the eviction/admission choice instead.
