# H02

Replace ClassicLRU's move-to-back-on-hit with a one-step-toward-back transposition (and middle insertion left unchanged) to cut per-op get time while keeping hit rate near baseline.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`, change `ClassicLRU::get_from_position` so that instead of `items.remove(position); items.push(item)` (an O(n) shift), it swaps the hit item with its right neighbour (`items.swap(position, position+1)` if `position + 1 < len`) and returns the new index. Keep `eviction_candidate_index` = 0 and `promotion_candidate_index` = len-1. Do not change the enum, names, indices, or `build`, so the existing unit tests stay valid. Hit-path becomes O(1) memmove-free; frequently-hit items gradually migrate toward the protected end (transposition heuristic, mirror of `Swap` but toward the back).

## Rationale (optional)

Targets per-op-time p50 for get (69ns) with a hit-rate cost that should be small on skewed workloads.
