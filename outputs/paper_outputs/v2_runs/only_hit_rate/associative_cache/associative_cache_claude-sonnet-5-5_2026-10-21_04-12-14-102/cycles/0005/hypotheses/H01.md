# H01

Shrinking the per-bucket probation segment of cycle/0004/v003 from 1/16 of slots to exactly 1–2 slots raises hit-rate on the LIRS trace.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In the overlay's segmented-LRU bucket code (the file v003 replaced in src/associative_cache/), change the probation-size computation from `slots/16` (min 1) to a constant `PROBATION_SLOTS = 2`, clamped to `[1, slots-1]`. Keep first-hit promotion (hit in probation moves the entry to the protected MRU end, demoting the protected LRU entry into probation if protected is full), insertion into probation, and eviction taking the probation LRU first. Add no new per-entry state. Preserve slot-legality: all placement stays inside the hashed bucket.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 4 showed the trend toward smaller probation is flattening. This probes the lower end to find the turn.
