# H03

Removing all residual ghost, counter and adaptive state from the baseline-plus-split design, leaving a lean 4-slot-probation segmented LRU on baseline geometry, preserves hit-rate while cutting metadata-size and per-op-time.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline source, implement a segmented LRU in each bucket with a fixed probation size of 4 slots (clamped to slots-1). New entries are inserted at the probation MRU end. A hit in probation moves the entry to the protected MRU end and, if protected is full, demotes the protected LRU entry to the probation MRU end. A hit in protected moves the entry to the protected MRU end. Eviction takes the probation LRU first, then the protected LRU if probation is empty. Represent segment membership by slot position within the bucket's ordering (the first 4 positions from the LRU end are probation) rather than a per-entry flag, so no additional metadata is added. Add no ghost, counter, or adaptive state. Keep all placement inside the hashed bucket.

Tests whether the lean split matches v001 and v003 while keeping the baseline's metadata footprint of 40 and its per-op-time.
