# H01

Shrinking the probationary segment from 16 to 8 of 64 slots per bucket will raise hit-rate on the LIRS trace by leaving more room for protected re-referenced entries.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In the v001 bucket implementation, change the probation-size constant (about 16 of 64 slots) to 8 (one eighth). Keep everything else identical: new entries enter probation, a second hit promotes to protected, eviction takes probationary entries first (LRU order), falling back to protected LRU when probation is empty. If probation size is derived from a ratio or shift, change that expression; ensure that bucket capacity and the public API are unchanged and that probation never exceeds its cap (demote or evict the oldest probationary entry on overflow as in v001).

## Rationale (optional)

Directly answers the open question on probation sizing; tests whether the optimum is smaller than a quarter.
