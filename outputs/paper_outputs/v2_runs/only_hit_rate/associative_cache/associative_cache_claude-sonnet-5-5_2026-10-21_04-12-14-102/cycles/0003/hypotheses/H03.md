# H03

Making the probation segment adaptive per bucket (grow on probation-evicted-key re-misses, shrink on protected-victim re-misses) will raise hit-rate beyond fixed-size probation on the LIRS trace.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline bucket (plain move-to-back LRU), implement a 2Q-style probationary segment from scratch within the bucket file, with a per-bucket adaptive probation target `p` stored as a small integer (initial 16 of 64, clamped to [4, 32]). New entries enter probation; a second hit promotes to protected (moved to back of the protected region). Eviction takes the oldest probationary entry when probation size > p or protected is empty, otherwise the protected LRU entry. Adaptation: keep a tiny ghost ring (8 keys) of recent probation evictions; a miss on a ghost key increments p by 1 (probation too small); a hit on the protected LRU-most quarter of entries decrements p by 1. No placement outside the hashed bucket; public API and capacity unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

ARC-like self-tuning avoids hand-picking the probation share; starting from baseline gives an independent implementation path from v001.
