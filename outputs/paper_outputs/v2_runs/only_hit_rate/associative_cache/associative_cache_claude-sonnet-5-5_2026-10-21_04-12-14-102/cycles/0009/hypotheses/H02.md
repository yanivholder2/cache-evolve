# H02

Skipping the move-to-protected-MRU on a protected-segment hit when the entry is already in the top quarter of the protected segment will cut per-op-time at near-equal hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In the hit path for entries located in the protected segment, compute the entry's distance from the MRU end. If it is within the top protected_len/4 positions, return the value without rotating/shifting the bucket; otherwise move it to protected MRU as today. Probation hits still promote to protected MRU (unchanged), insertion, demotion, eviction and sizes unchanged. No new per-entry state is added (metadata-size must not grow). Intended to reduce memmove/rotate work in wide 4x buckets; hit-rate should stay within noise of 0.7304.

## Rationale (optional)

Per-op-time has never been judged; wide buckets raise rotate cost. Earlier "weakened promotion" failures were in plain LRU, not on protected segment hits of the split design.
