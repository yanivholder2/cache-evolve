# H01

Inserting new keys at the middle of the probation segment instead of its top will raise hit-rate by evicting one-touch keys sooner while still giving them residency.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In the insert path of the bucket (the branch that places a missed key into the bucket after eviction), change the insertion position from "just above the probation boundary" (the probation MRU end) to the middle of the probation segment: index = boundary - probation_len/2 (clamped so it stays within the probation segment, and so probation_len==1 behaves as before). Keep hit-path promotion (probation hit -> protected MRU), demotion, probation size (slots/16 of the 4x-wide bucket) and victim choice (evict probation LRU first) unchanged. Preserve the slot-legality invariant: every key stays in its single home bucket and the rotate/shift must only move entries inside that bucket.

## Rationale (optional)

Open question in cross-cycle insights: is a mid-probation insertion better than either extreme? Single-knob change, easy to attribute.
