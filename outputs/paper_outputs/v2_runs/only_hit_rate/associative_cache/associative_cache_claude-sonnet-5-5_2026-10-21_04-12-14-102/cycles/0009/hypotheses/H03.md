# H03

Growing the probation segment from slots/16 to slots/8 only for the 4x-wide buckets on a lean positional split will raise hit-rate by giving first-touch keys more residency to earn a re-reference.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Rebuild the lean segmented LRU on the baseline with 4x ways per bucket (bucket count halved, same total capacity): each bucket's slots are ordered with a positional boundary; probation = the last slots/8 positions (absolute count, e.g. 4 of 32 ways if ways=32), protected = the rest. New keys insert just above the probation boundary (probation MRU), a probation hit moves the entry to protected MRU and demotes the protected LRU entry to probation MRU, eviction takes the probation LRU entry. No ghost, counters or other per-entry state. Compare against the slots/16 setting used in cycle/0008/v002 to test whether the optimum probation fraction shifts upward at wider associativity.

## Rationale (optional)

Probation optimum was found at narrower buckets; at 4x the absolute probation size at slots/16 may be too short for the trace's reuse distances.
