## Changes

Keep the whole overlay of cycle/0009/v002 unchanged (4x-wide buckets, positional probation/protected split, first-hit promotion, skip-move for hits already in the protected top quarter). Change only the probation-size computation (the constant or expression deriving probation length from slots per bucket, currently slots/16) to slots/8. Make sure it is clamped to at least 1 and at most slots-1. Do not change insertion point, promotion or demotion.
