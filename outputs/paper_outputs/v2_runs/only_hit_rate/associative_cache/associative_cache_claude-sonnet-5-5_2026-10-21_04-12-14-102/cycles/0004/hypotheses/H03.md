# H03

Combining the baseline's wider-bucket geometry with a fixed 4-slot probation segment per bucket will raise hit-rate by testing whether the split gain depends on the cycle 1 geometry.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Start from the baseline overlay-free source. Implement, in the allowed associative cache file, a probation/protected split inside each bucket with a fixed probation size equal to one sixteenth of the bucket's slots (minimum 1). New entries are inserted at the back of probation. Eviction takes the LRU entry of probation when probation is full, otherwise the LRU entry of protected. A hit in probation moves the entry to the back of protected and demotes the LRU protected entry to the back of probation if protected is full. A hit in protected moves the entry to the back of protected. Add no ghost, counter or adaptive state. Use only the baseline's bucket count and associativity and keep slot-legality intact.

## Rationale

This is an independent re-derivation of the split from the baseline with the smallest probation share. It checks whether the incumbent's gain carries over without the accumulated cycle 1-3 changes.
