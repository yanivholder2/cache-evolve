# H02

Promoting an entry from probation to protected only on its second hit within probation, tracked by a one-bit flag, will raise hit-rate by keeping weakly reused entries out of protected.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

In the cycle/0003/v001 overlay, add a per-entry one-bit "seen once" marker, stored in existing per-slot metadata where possible. On a hit to a probationary entry whose marker is clear, set the marker and move it to the back of probation. Do not promote it. On a hit to a probationary entry whose marker is set, promote it to protected as v001 does now. Clear the marker on insertion and on any demotion. Protected-segment hit handling, eviction order and the probation size of 8 stay unchanged. Do not touch the ghost-ring code. This is the stricter promotion rule that the cross-cycle insights name as untested.

## Rationale

Cycle 0003 suggested promotion needs a stronger reuse signal. A second-hit requirement within probation is a cheap version of that signal and adds no ghost state.
