# H02

Reducing per-op-time by implementing promotion and demotion as a single slice rotation (`rotate_left`/`rotate_right` over the affected index range) rather than element-by-element swaps, with identical eviction behaviour, keeps the 0.7267 hit-rate while lowering per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the bucket hit path and insert path of the associative-cache overlay file, find every loop that moves an entry across slots one swap at a time (move-to-back, promotion into the protected segment, shifting on insert). Replace each with one `slice::rotate_*` call on the minimal range `[i..=j]`, so each hit or insert does one contiguous memmove. On a hit in the protected segment, skip the move entirely when the entry is already at the MRU slot. On a miss with an empty slot available, write the key/value into the first free slot without shifting. The resulting slot order and the chosen victim must be bit-for-bit identical to v001, so hit-rate must stay 0.7267. Add no fields and do not change the bucket layout, so metadata-size stays the same. Do not touch the JNI file or factory.

## Rationale (optional)

Per-op-time and metadata-size have never been measured. The best hit-rate designs reorder entries on every hit, so this cuts that cost without changing the policy.
