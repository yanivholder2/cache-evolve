# H03

Doubling bucket associativity while keeping the 4-slot probation segment fixed on the lean split will raise hit-rate by cutting conflict misses in the protected segment.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline source, do two things in the associative-cache overlay. First, change the bucket associativity (ways per bucket) to twice the baseline value, and reduce the bucket count by half, so that total capacity is unchanged. Second, add the lean positional segmented-LRU split: the first 4 slots in recency order from the LRU end form the probation segment, and new keys are inserted at the probation MRU position. A hit in probation promotes the entry to the protected MRU and demotes the protected LRU entry to the probation MRU. A hit in protected moves the entry to the protected MRU. Eviction takes the probation LRU entry first. Use no counters, ghost state or stamps. Keep every key in its hashed bucket (the slot-legality invariant). Total slots must equal the baseline's. Do not edit the JNI file, factory.rs or Cargo.toml.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 1 found that wider buckets gave a small real gain, and the 4-slot probation was tuned at the current width. This tests whether the two gains stack when the probation share stays fixed in slots, not as a fraction. Since this is built on the baseline, it is a test of stacking, not an increment on v003.
