# H03

Adding an early-exit single-pass lookup and avoiding redundant hash/index recomputation in the rotate-based segmented policy lowers per-op-time without changing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Keep the policy bit-for-bit (same hits/evictions). In the get/put paths: compute the bucket index once and reuse it; scan the bucket keys once with early exit on match, scanning protected region first (more likely to hold hot keys) then probation; perform promotion with a single `rotate_left`/`rotate_right` over the minimal sub-range between the hit position and the segment boundary rather than shifting the whole bucket; on insert, reuse the miss-scan result to find the victim instead of rescanning. Add no per-entry metadata, so metadata-size stays at the parent's value. Hit-rate must remain 0.7267.

## Rationale (optional)

Per-op-time has never been improved from baseline (69); this targets the cost dimension while holding the hit-rate winner.
