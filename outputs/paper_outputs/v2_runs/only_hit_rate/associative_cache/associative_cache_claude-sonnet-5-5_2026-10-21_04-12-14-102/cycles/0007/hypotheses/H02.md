# H02

Decoupling probation size from bucket width (fixed ~4 slots) on a doubled-associativity bucket raises hit-rate over the incumbent.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Double ways per bucket at constant total capacity (halve bucket count, adjust index mask). Unlike H01, keep probation segment at an absolute 4 slots rather than scaling with bucket width, because the earlier sweet spot was ~4 slots on short buckets and may be an absolute scan-length effect. Leave promotion (first hit moves entry to protected MRU, demoting protected LRU into probation), eviction order and lookup logic unchanged. Preserve in-bucket placement invariant.

## Rationale (optional)

Distinguishes whether the optimum is slots/16 or an absolute count, a question left open by the cycle 0006 wider-bucket result.
