# H03

Making S3-FIFO's direct-mapped ghost 2-way set-associative with 2× the slots will raise hit-rate by keeping returning keys' fingerprints that collisions now overwrite.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, replace the direct-mapped ghost. Right now `ghost: Vec<u32>` has `capacity.next_power_of_two()` slots and is indexed by `h & gmask`, so with a full ghost, roughly a third of fingerprints are lost to slot collisions before they can be hit.

- In `try_new`, allocate `ghost: vec![0u32; 2 * gsize]` where `gsize = capacity.next_power_of_two()`. Treat it as `gsize` sets of 2 ways: set `s = (h as usize) & gmask` covers indices `2*s` and `2*s + 1`. Keep `gmask = gsize - 1`.
- Ghost lookup in `put` (still done before eviction): compute `fp = fingerprint(h)` and check both ways of the set. On a match, zero that way and admit to main, as now.
- Ghost insert in `evict()` (unvisited small-head eviction only): within the set, move way 0 to way 1 and write the new fingerprint into way 0. This gives FIFO replacement inside the set, so the oldest of the two is dropped. If one of the ways already equals `fp`, just move it to way 0 rather than duplicating it.
- Main-queue SIEVE evictions still do not feed the ghost. Leave unchanged: `small_target`, promotion logic, `find_victim`, the Fx hasher, and the 24-byte `Node`.

Tests: update `in_ghost` to check both ways of the set. Add a test that inserts two fingerprints which collide in one set (find two keys with equal `h & gmask` by search in the test) and asserts both stay in the ghost. Add a test that a third colliding key pushes out the oldest. Keep all existing tests passing.

## Rationale (optional)

Ghosts proved useful only within the S3-FIFO split (cycle 5), and the gain was modest. Part of that modesty may be an artefact of the ghost implementation rather than of the workload. The direct-mapped table silently drops entries on collision, so some keys that were reused at the ghost's reach still pay the trip through probation. Doubling the size and adding associativity tests whether the ghost's recall, not the amount of one-hit traffic, is what limits S3-FIFO's gain. Probe cost on a miss grows by one adjacent u32 read in the same cache line, so per-op-time should stay flat. The ghost costs 8 more KiB of heap at capacity 2048, which the per-node metric does not count. Report that cost alongside the result.
