Widening the S3-FIFO small probationary queue from 10% to 25% of capacity will raise hit-rate on workload-a by giving new keys a longer window to earn their second touch before ghosting.

Unit tests that depend on the 10% split must be checked. The existing tests use capacities 1–4 and 10, and `(cap/4).max(1)` equals `(cap/10).max(1)` at capacities 1–4. It differs at capacity 10 (2 vs 1) and in the randomized test at 16 (4 vs 1). Re-derive every hard-coded small/main key expectation under the new target, including `scan_of_one_touch_keys_stays_in_small`, and fix any that change. Also add one test with capacity 40 asserting `small_target == 10`, and keep `randomized_workload_preserves_structure` passing.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 2 showed that shrinking the window in which new keys can earn a second touch (BIP inside SLRU probation) cost hit-rate. The modest S3-FIFO gain in cycle 5 suggests that many keys on workload-a are re-referenced at distances between 10% and 25% of capacity. At 10%, those keys get ghosted and only return to main after a miss. If the hit-rate drops, that confirms the 10% default is already near optimal for this trace.
