# H01

Shrinking arena links from `Option<usize>` to `u32` indices with a sentinel will cut `Node<u64,u64>` from 48 to 24 bytes, improving metadata-size and likely per-op-time via cache locality.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, change `Node.prev`/`Node.next` from `Option<usize>` to `u32` with a `const NIL: u32 = u32::MAX`. Change `LruCache.head`/`tail` likewise to `u32`. Update `unlink`, `push_back`, `get`, and `put` to compare against `NIL` instead of matching on `Option`, and store `map: HashMap<K, u32>`. Convert with `as usize` only when indexing `nodes`. In `try_new`, return `None` (with a `log::error!`) if `capacity >= u32::MAX as usize`. Keep strict LRU semantics, the public API, and the `framework::Cache` impl unchanged. Do not touch `lru_cache_jni.rs`.
