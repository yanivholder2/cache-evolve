# H02

Replacing the single LRU list with a segmented LRU (probationary and protected segments) will raise hit rate on the scan-heavy lirs workload-a trace.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, keep the arena and `HashMap<K, usize>`, but split the list into two segments. Add a `seg: bool` field to `Node` (true means protected), and keep head/tail pairs for each segment plus a `protected_len` counter. The protected capacity is `capacity * 4 / 5` (at least 1 when `capacity > 1`).

- New keys are inserted at the MRU end of the probationary segment.
- A `get` or `put`-update hit on a probationary node moves it to the MRU end of the protected segment. If protected is over its limit, demote the protected LRU node to the MRU end of probationary.
- A hit on a protected node moves it to the protected MRU.
- Eviction takes the probationary LRU. If probationary is empty, take the protected LRU.
- Reuse the evicted slot in place, as the baseline does.

Preserve `capacity` as the total bound, the public API, and the `framework::Cache` impl. Update the unit tests in `mod.rs` that assume strict LRU order, or keep them passing where they still hold. The risk is the invariant gate, so keep size bounds and get-after-put correctness intact.
