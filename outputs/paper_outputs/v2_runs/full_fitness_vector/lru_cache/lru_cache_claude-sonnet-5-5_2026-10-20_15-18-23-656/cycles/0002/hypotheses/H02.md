# H02

Encode the segment flag in a spare bit of an existing node field in v002 to cut metadata-size back toward 48 bytes while keeping hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In v002's node struct, remove the separate segment field (bool or enum). Store the segment bit in the high bit of the `prev` link (or the `u32`/`usize` field that has spare range), with small accessor helpers `segment()`, `set_segment()`, `prev_idx()`. Mask the bit in every link read and write. Keep the policy unchanged: new entries go to probation, a hit promotes to protected, and overflow from protected demotes to the probation head. Also replace per-node counters of segment sizes with the two existing length fields if duplicated. The goal is a smaller node with the same hit-rate.
