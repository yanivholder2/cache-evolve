# H02

Raising the protected share from 3/4 to 7/8 of capacity on top of v002's ghost-gated LRU-end insertion will raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

In `try_new`, compute `protected_cap` as `(capacity - capacity / 8).min(capacity - 1).max(1)` for `capacity > 1` (0 otherwise), instead of `capacity - capacity / 4`. Probation then holds about 1/8 of capacity, and since cold keys already enter at the LRU end of probation, scan keys churn only that small slice while re-referenced keys keep more room. Leave the ghost logic, promote/demote, and hasher untouched.
