# H03

Tune the protected-segment size fraction in v003's segmented LRU to raise hit-rate with no change to the hot-path code.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In v003, locate the constant or expression that sets the protected segment's capacity (its share of total capacity). Change it to 3/4 of capacity (computed once at construction, e.g. `cap - cap / 4`), keeping the probationary segment at the remainder with a minimum of 1. Change nothing else: hasher, links, promotion and demotion logic stay as they are. Per-op work stays the same, so per-op-time and metadata-size should hold, and the claim is a hit-rate change on the scan-heavy trace.

## Rationale (optional)

Segment ratio is an untested single-parameter axis. Cycles so far changed mechanics, not this ratio.
