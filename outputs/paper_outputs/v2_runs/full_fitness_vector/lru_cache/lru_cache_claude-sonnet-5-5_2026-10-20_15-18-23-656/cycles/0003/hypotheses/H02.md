# H02

Adding segmented-LRU scan resistance onto the u32-link plain LRU, with the segment flag stored in the spare high bit of the u32 `next` link, will lift hit-rate toward 0.72 while keeping metadata-size at 24 and per-op-time near 27.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Start from v003 (u32 sentinel links, folded Fx hasher, head-touch early return). Split the single list into probation and protected segments (protected capped at ~80% of capacity, as in v001's ratio) by adding a second head/tail pair to the cache struct. New entries are inserted at the probation head; a `get` hit in probation promotes the node to protected head; if protected exceeds its cap, demote the protected tail to the probation head. Eviction takes the probation tail (fall back to protected tail if probation is empty). Store the segment flag as bit 31 of the `next` u32 (capacity limited to 2^31-2, NIL = 0x7FFF_FFFF masked), exposed via two small `#[inline(always)]` helpers `next_of(n)` / `set_next(n, v)` that preserve the flag. Do not grow `Node` beyond v003's size. Keep the head-touch early return applying only to the protected head.
