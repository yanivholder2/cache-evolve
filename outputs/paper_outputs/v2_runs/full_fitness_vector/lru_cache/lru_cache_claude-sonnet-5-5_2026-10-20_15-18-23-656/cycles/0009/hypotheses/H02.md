# H02

Recycling the evicted node's storage for the incoming key in `put` will cut the number of allocator and map operations per put and lower median per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v003

## Changes

In `put` for a new key when the cache is full: instead of removing the victim node (freeing its slot / dropping its entry) and then allocating a new node, take the victim's node slot (index or box) in place, overwrite its key and value with the new ones, relink it into the probation segment at the position chosen by the existing ghost-gate rule, and update the map by removing the victim key and inserting the new key pointing at the reused slot. Preserve: the single victim hash computed for the ghost record, the ghost lookup gated on a full cache, the second-chance/segment eviction choice of victim, and all hit-rate-relevant ordering. Do not touch `get`. Invariants: len never exceeds capacity; no stale map entries for the victim key; free-list (if any) unchanged in size.

## Rationale

Insight from cycles 0007-0008: hash trims only moved the tail; the median put is dominated by map insert plus node allocation. This targets the allocation/free pair structurally while leaving policy untouched.
