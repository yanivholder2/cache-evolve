# H01

Raising the protected share from 3/4 to 7/8 of capacity will further raise hit-rate on the scan-heavy trace, with no change to metadata-size or per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In `LruCache::try_new` in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, change the `protected_cap` formula from `capacity - capacity / 4` to `capacity - capacity / 8`. Keep the `.min(capacity - 1).max(1)` clamps and the `capacity > 1` guard. Touch nothing else (no hot-path edits). This probes the direction in which v003 (1/2 to 3/4) had the best hit-rate in the incumbents, to find where the trend turns.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 holds the best hit-rate. The cycle-0005 note called the split a weak lever, so the single-constant change tests whether the trend continues or reverses.
