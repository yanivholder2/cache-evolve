# H01

Skipping the relink for protected-segment hits that are already at or adjacent to the protected head will cut get/put per-op-time on the segmented cache without changing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Single change only: in the get/touch path of the segmented-LRU code in `src/lru_cache/lru_cache.rs` (the parent's promotion/touch function), add an early return when the hit node is in the protected segment and is the protected head OR its `prev` is the protected head. In that case do no unlink/relink. Probation hits and promotion logic, eviction, hasher and link layout stay untouched. Preserve list invariants (len counters, head/tail). Expect hit-rate to stay within noise of the parent since eviction order only differs for the second-from-head node.

## Rationale (optional)

Addresses the open question on cheap promotion by skipping relinks near the head.
