# H03

Reducing the hasher on the packed segmented cache to a single Fx multiply with high-bit selection (no fold) will lower per-op-time while keeping hit-rate and metadata-size, isolating the fold's cost.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Single change only: in the hasher used by the key-to-index map in `src/lru_cache/lru_cache.rs`, remove the xor/shift avalanche fold in `finish()`. Keep the Fx multiply (`h = (h.rotate_left(5) ^ x).wrapping_mul(K)`) and have the map use the high bits of the product (for example, by making `finish()` return `h.rotate_left(26)` so hashbrown's top-7-bit tag and low-bit bucket index both draw on well-mixed bits). Do not touch node layout, the packed segment bit, or the promotion/demotion code.

## Rationale (optional)

Tests the open question of whether the fold is responsible for the speed loss on segmented variants, with one change for clean attribution.
