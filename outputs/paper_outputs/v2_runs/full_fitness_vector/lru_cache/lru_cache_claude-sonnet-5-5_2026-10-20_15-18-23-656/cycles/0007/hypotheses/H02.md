# H02

Insert never-before-seen keys at the LRU end of probation (LIP-style), gated by a ghost fingerprint table, to improve hit-rate under scans.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` add `ghost: Vec<u32>` (power-of-two length >= capacity, 0 = empty, fingerprints `| 1`) to `LruCache`. Record fingerprints of every evicted key (from `(hash_one(&evicted_key) >> 32) as u32 | 1` at index `hash & mask`) in the eviction branch of `put`. On a put-miss for key k: if the ghost slot matches k's fingerprint, clear it and `push_back(idx, PROB)` as today (MRU of probation); if not matched (first-time key), insert at the LRU end of probation (add a small `push_front(idx, PROB)` helper that links at `heads[PROB]`), so a one-touch scan key is the next victim. A subsequent `get` still promotes to protected via `promote`. Keep the eviction rule (probation head first). Preserve behaviour of existing unit tests; note `hit_protects_from_eviction` evicts key 2 either way. Do not change protected_cap or hashing.

## Rationale (optional)

Differs from H01: ghost hits alter insertion position within probation for cold keys rather than jumping to protected.
