# H01

Keeping PROTECTED and REFERENCED in the top bits of `prev` while confining the index mask to one inline helper will recover the per-op-time loss of cycle/0007/v003 and keep its 24-byte node, improving `get` p50 and tail at unchanged hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

Keep the packed layout (flags in the top two bits of `prev`, `Node<u64,u64>` at 24 bytes, `LruCache` untouched). The cost in v003 came from every index read and write stripping the flag bits. Change it as follows:

- Add one `#[inline(always)]` helper, `link_index(prev) -> u32`, that returns the masked index, and one `INDEX_MASK` constant. Route every index read in `unlink`, `push_back`, `select_victim`, and `demote_prot_head` through this helper, and make the sentinel check compare the masked value.
- On the `get` hit path, set REFERENCED with a single bitwise OR on the raw `prev` word (`prev |= REFERENCED_BIT`) without extracting the index. Do not read the index on the hit path beyond what is needed to locate the node.
- Leave `put`'s flag logic and the eviction order unchanged. Do not add any new field to `Node` or `LruCache`.

Invariants: hit-rate must stay at 0.7058 on workload-a at 2048 (the change is policy-neutral, so any hit-rate movement means the diff altered behaviour). Node size must stay 24 bytes. Measure `get` and `put` p50/p99 against the parent in the same run and report both.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 showed the 8-byte padding saving is real but the mask was paid on every index access. Limiting the mask to one helper and making the hit-path flag update a plain OR tests whether the size-for-latency trade can be made free on `get`.
