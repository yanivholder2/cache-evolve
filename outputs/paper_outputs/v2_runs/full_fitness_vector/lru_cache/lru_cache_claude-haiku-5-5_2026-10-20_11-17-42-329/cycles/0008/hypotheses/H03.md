# H03

Re-admitting keys that hit the ghost ring as probationary rather than protected will recover the hit-rate that cycle/0006/v003 gave up on workload-a at 2048, at no extra per-op cost on the hit path.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

Keep the bounded ghost ring and its lookup exactly as in the parent. Change only the admission decision for a miss whose key is found in the ghost ring:

- In the miss path where the parent inserts a ghost-hit key directly into the protected segment (setting PROTECTED and linking into the protected list), instead link it at the probationary head with PROTECTED clear. Keep the REFERENCED bit clear on this insert so the key must earn protection with a real hit.
- Leave the victim selection, `demote_prot_head`, the protected-segment size, and the hit path (`get`) unchanged. Do not add a second ghost lookup or any hashing on the hit path.
- Add a counter for ghost hits and report it in the variant insight, so the ghost's own hit rate can be read against the variant's overall hit rate. Cycle 0007 flagged that this measurement was missing.
- Report the ghost ring's heap size (ring capacity times entry size) alongside `LruCache` struct size, since the struct-size metric does not count it.

Invariants: `get` p50/p99 should stay within noise of the parent. The miss path may change only by the single admission-list choice. The ring's bound must not grow.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0007 v002 showed that promoting ghost hits straight into protected lost hit-rate on workload-a. Its continuation suggested keeping ghost hits probationary and measuring the ghost's hit rate before changing admission. This hypothesis tests that single change on the ghost-ring lineage that first introduced the idea.
