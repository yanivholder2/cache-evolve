# H01

Giving a spared protected head a two-skip spare credit in its node flags should recover the hit-rate that the flag-only demotion retry gave back, while keeping its per-operation gain (targets hit-rate).

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Work in the segmented (probationary and protected) overlay of cycle/0004/v001, in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`. Keep every list operation as it is now. No new relinks on any path.

- Add a 2-bit spare-credit field to `Node.flags`, at bits 2–3 (`CREDIT_SHIFT = 2`, `CREDIT_MASK = 0b1100`). `flags` stays a `u8`, so `Node<u64,u64>` stays 40 bytes. Define `SPARED` only through this field, not as a new bit.
- In `select_victim()`, when a probationary head is rescued into the protected tail, set its credit to 0 (as its bits are cleared on rescue).
- In `demote_prot_head()`, keep the one-skip-per-call structure. Decide on the protected head as follows:
  1. If the head has `REFERENCED`: clear `REFERENCED`, set its credit to 2, and spare it (victim becomes `next`, as now).
  2. Else if the head's credit is greater than 0: decrement the credit by 1 and spare it (victim becomes `next`).
  3. Else: the head is the victim, as now.
  If the chosen skip target is `NIL` (the head is the only protected node), always demote the head itself.
- The demoted victim goes to the probationary MRU end with `REFERENCED` and credit both cleared, as in v001 today.
- `get()` and `put()` stay unchanged: hits still only set `REFERENCED`.
- Update the module header comment and the `demote_prot_head` doc comment to describe the credit.
- Add one unit test: a protected head that was rescued while referenced survives exactly two demotion passes that do not reference it again, and is demoted on the third. Keep every existing test passing unchanged.

Expected effect: hit-rate moves back toward the 0.71 that cycle/0003/v001 reached with full MRU rotation on the rescued head (the cycle 0004 insight attributes the current gap to that rotation). per-op-time should stay near v001's 46, since the extra work is one bit test and a 2-bit decrement on the demotion path only.
