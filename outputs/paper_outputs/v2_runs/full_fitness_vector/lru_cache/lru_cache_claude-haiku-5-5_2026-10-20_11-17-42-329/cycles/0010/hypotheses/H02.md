# H02

Bounding the CLOCK eviction sweep to two slots per miss recovers the put tail and part of the hit rate lost by the single-ring CLOCK, while keeping `Node<u64,u64>` at 16 bytes.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, keep the single ring, the `refs: Vec<u8>` parallel array, the 2-bit saturating counter, the `hand: u32` field, and the `FastBuildHasher` map unchanged. Change only the victim selection on a `put` of a new key at capacity.

Replace the unbounded multi-slot sweep with at most two slots examined per miss. Let `i = hand`. If `refs[i] == 0`, the victim is `i`. Otherwise decrement `refs[i]` and let `j = (i + 1) % n`, where `n` is the ring length. If `refs[j] == 0`, the victim is `j`. Otherwise decrement `refs[j]` and take `j` as the victim regardless of its counter. Set `hand = (victim + 1) % n` after the victim is chosen.

After the victim is chosen, remove its key from `map`, overwrite the slot's key and value in `nodes`, set `refs[victim] = 0`, and insert the new key into `map` at that index. Keep the `get` increment (saturating at 3) and the existing-key `put` increment as they are.

Keep `try_new`, the capacity check, and the `framework::Cache` impl unchanged. The `refs` array is a heap allocation outside the Node struct; report its size alongside the node size, since the metadata-size dimension does not count it.

The insight should report hit rate against cycle/0009/v003 (0.6871), metadata-size (expected to stay 16), and `put` and `get` p50 and p99 against the parent. Say whether the bounded sweep moves the put tail more than hit rate, because the parent's put tail is the cost this change targets.
