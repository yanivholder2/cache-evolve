# H01

Raising the protected share from 4/5 to 9/10 of capacity lifts hit-rate on workload-a at 2048 without changing metadata-size or per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, change `protected_cap()` from `(self.capacity * 4 / 5).max(1)` to `(self.capacity * 9 / 10).max(1)`. Update the doc comments on `protected_cap` and `select_victim` that describe the 4/5 share so they match the new ratio.

Keep everything else unchanged: `select_victim`, the single second chance for the probationary head, `demote_prot_head` with its one spare per call, the packed `PROTECTED_BIT` and `REFERENCED_BIT`, and the `get` and `put` hit paths. No new fields, so `Node<u64,u64>` stays 24 bytes and the `LruCache` layout does not change.

Capacity edge cases: at capacity 1 and 2 the new formula gives the same cap as 4/5 after `.max(1)`, so `try_new` needs no change. Confirm that `protected_cap() < capacity` for every capacity above 2 so the probationary list keeps at least one slot for new keys. If it would not, clamp to `capacity - 1`.

The insight should record the hit-rate delta against the parent, and any `put` or `get` movement, separately from the change.
