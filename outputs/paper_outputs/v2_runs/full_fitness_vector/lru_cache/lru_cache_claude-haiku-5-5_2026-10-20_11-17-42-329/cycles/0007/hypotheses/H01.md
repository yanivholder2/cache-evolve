# H01

Fold the high 32 bits of the FastHasher state into the low bits in `finish()` so the key map's bucket-index bits depend on every input bit, to tighten the get and put p99 tails on the per-op-time dimension with hit-rate unchanged at 0.7058.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In `FastHasher` (the `Hasher` impl), change `finish()` from returning `self.0` to returning a folded value: `self.0 ^ (self.0 >> 32)`. Leave `add_to_hash`, `write`, `write_u64`, and `write_usize` unchanged, so the per-word fold stays identical. The multiply in `add_to_hash` pushes entropy toward the high bits, while the map's bucket index uses the low bits, so this fold moves that entropy down.

Do not change anything in the policy: `get`, `put`, `select_victim`, `demote_prot_head`, `unlink`, `push_back`, the node layout, `protected_cap`, and the `FastBuildHasher` type stay as they are. Hit-rate must remain exactly 0.7058 on workload-a at 2048, since the map only affects lookup cost and not which key is evicted.

Keep `FastBuildHasher` zero-sized so the `LruCache` struct stays at 88 bytes. Update the doc comment on `FastHasher` to say the finish step folds high bits into low bits for bucket spread.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0006 v002's insight notes that the raw finish leaves the low bucket-index bits least mixed, so its speedup may depend on how keys spread. This variant tests whether a one-line fold closes that gap and tightens the tail (get p99 and put p99 in the fitness vector) without giving back the p50 gain. The expected result is no hit-rate change and a neutral or lower p99; a p50 regression larger than a few ns would count against it.
