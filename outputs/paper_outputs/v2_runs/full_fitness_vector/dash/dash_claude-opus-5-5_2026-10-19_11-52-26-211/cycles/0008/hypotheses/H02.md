# H02

Add a scan-free, target-only rescue of stash victims to cycle-7 v002, placing a victim at its target's LRU end only when that target has a free slot, to raise hit-rate above 0.7095 toward 0.7118 while keeping get p50 within about 3 ns of v002's 69 ns.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

Edits go in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, plus a small helper in `src/dash/segments/mod.rs`.

1. **Rescue hook in `stash_insert`.** When both `s0` and `s1` are full, `stash_insert` falls through to `core.put_stash_at_index(s0, pos, item)` and gets a victim back. Today that victim leaves the cache. Add `stash_insert_with_rescue(core, pos, item)`, which calls `stash_insert`. If it returns `Some(victim)`:
   - compute `t = get_index(&victim.key, core.segment_size)` (one hash);
   - if `!core.buckets[t].is_full()`, insert the victim into `buckets[t]` at `InsertPosition::Front` through a counter-aware native insert (e.g. `put_normal_known(t, /*is_foreign*/ false, Front, victim)`, or `put_at(Slot::Target, Front, victim)`), and `debug_assert!` that the insert evicts nothing;
   - otherwise drop the victim, as today.

   **Do not rescue into the probing bucket.** That keeps `foreign` unchanged by rescue, so the probing gate keeps skipping most probing scans. It also avoids the second `is_full` check and the second bucket write. Route all three stash insert sites through the wrapper: the fresh put in `DashSegment::put`, and both `stash_insert(core, DEMOTE_TO_STASH_POS, …)` calls in `demote_from_normal_bucket`.

2. **No recursion and no cascades.** Rescue only fills an existing hole, so it never evicts and never calls `demote_from_normal_bucket`. A rescue insert can't trigger another stash insert.

3. **Leave everything else unchanged.** Keep the lookup order `s0` → target → probing (only if `foreign != 0`) → `s1` (only if `borrowed != 0`). Keep both counters and the stash placements (fresh at Front, demotees at Back).

4. **Tests:**
   - (a) A stash victim whose target has a free slot lands at target index 0, and `foreign` and `borrowed` stay exact.
   - (b) A stash victim whose target is full is dropped, even if its probing bucket has a hole.
   - (c) Extend both random-op tests to run through the wrapper, and assert `check_foreign_counts`, `check_borrowed_counts`, `check_slot_legality` and `check_capacity_bound` after every op. Also assert that `get` finds every resident key.

## Rationale (optional)

Full rescue into target or probing (c5 v002, c6 v001, c7 v001) gives the best hit-rate at 0.7118, but it costs 14–18 ns of get p50. Some of that cost comes from writing foreign items into probing buckets. In v002 those items would turn the `foreign` gate on, and they also make two-bucket rescue checks and fuller probing buckets more common. Target-only rescue keeps the lossless probing gate intact, so it isolates how much of rescue's hit-rate comes from target holes alone. The cross-cycle open questions ask for exactly this cheaper rescue. **Confirmed** if hit-rate is clearly above 0.7095 (≥0.711) and get p50 is ≤72 ns. **Refuted** if hit-rate barely moves, meaning most rescues went into probing holes, or if get p50 rises to rescue-lineage levels (≥80 ns), meaning fuller target buckets alone carry the cost.
