**Hash count on the common path.** A stash hit that evicts from a full target and sends the evictee to the stash now hashes twice: once for the looked-up key and once for the evictee. Before, it hashed about seven times. The only extra hash left is for a stash victim when the stash overflows.

**What changed (H01 items 1–6):**
1. **One hash, many indices.** `key_hash(k)` returns `hash(k) as u64`, and `index_from_hash(h, n)` returns `(h as usize) % n`. This is the same arithmetic as `get_index`, so results match bit for bit. A new test checks the identity over 10k keys (plus a few extreme ones) for the stash size, the segment size, 8, 24 and other sizes. I didn't need to touch `utils.rs`.
