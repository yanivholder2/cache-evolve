## Rationale (optional)

With `s1 = s0 + 1`, overflow behaves like linear probing. A hot run of adjacent stash buckets overflows into its own neighbours, which are themselves likely to be under pressure, so the second choice often finds no room and `s0`'s LRU is evicted. Independent two-choice hashing is the standard fix ("power of two choices"): with random independent choices, the chance that both candidates are full is close to the square of the per-bucket full rate, rather than the correlated rate for adjacent buckets. This is the stash structural lever that cross-cycle insights list as never tried, and it uses capacity that is already there, the only lever that has raised hit-rate since cycle 2. **Confirmed** if hit-rate rises clearly above 0.7118, by more than one or two trace hits. **Refuted** if hit-rate is unchanged or lower, meaning stash pressure is uniform rather than clustered and the stride was not losing items. Expect get p50/p99 to rise slightly from the extra hash on misses. If H03 confirms, a later cycle can port it onto the counter-gated lineage, with `borrowed` redefined as "items whose `s0` is not this bucket".
