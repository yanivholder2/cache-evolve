# H03

Shrinking the per-item metadata of `Item<u64,u64>` from 40 bytes to 32 bytes by packing the bookkeeping fields into the key/value slot will reduce metadata-size, a dimension no cycle has moved yet.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change only the layout of the `Item<u64,u64>` record that the metadata-size dimension reports as 40 bytes. Target 32 bytes.

- Identify every non-key, non-value field stored in `Item<u64,u64>` (for example, a separate counter, flag, or padding word) and fold them into the unused high bits of the key or value word, or into a single `u8` field placed to remove alignment padding.
- Keep key and value semantics bit-identical: lookups, comparisons, and returned values must not change. Any field that cannot be packed without losing information stays as is.
- Do not change the Dash segment layout (reported at 24 bytes), the eviction order, the admission path, or the promotion path. This keeps hit-rate expected to match the parent exactly. Any hit-rate movement is a regression signal, not a gain.
- Do not edit the gate-integrity surface or the metadata-size measurement code.

Success means metadata-size reported at 32 or below, with hit-rate exactly equal to baseline's 0.663 and per-op-time not worse than baseline's 183 ns.

## Rationale (optional)

Every cohort so far has traded hit-rate against latency, and metadata-size has stayed at 40 since baseline. A pure layout change is the one direction that can improve a dimension without touching eviction behaviour, which makes it a clean control for the cohort.
