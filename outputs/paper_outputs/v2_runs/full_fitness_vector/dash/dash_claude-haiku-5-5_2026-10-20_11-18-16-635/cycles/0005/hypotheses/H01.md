# H01

Replace v002's full lowest-counter scan in `evict_one()` with a two-choice sample so put-side eviction cost stops growing with bucket size while keeping the frequency-aware hit-rate gain.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Keep the saturating access counter that v002 adds to bucket residents and keep its bias of `evict_one()` away from recently hit slots. Change only the victim search. Instead of scanning every slot in a full bucket for the lowest counter, sample two slots from the bucket (a fixed pair derived from the key's hash, or two indices chosen from a small per-bucket rotating cursor) and evict the one with the lower counter, breaking ties by the existing slot order. The sample-based search must avoid a heap or sorted structure. The counter update on hit, the insert path, the promotion logic, and the get path stay exactly as in v002. Preserve the stash-to-target promotion and the `Item<u64,u64>` layout unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's cycle 0004 insight names the put-path lowest-counter scan as its only cost, and the open question asks whether that cost grows with bucket size. A two-choice sample bounds the comparisons per eviction at two, so the test is whether put p50 falls back toward the parent's level while hit rate stays near 0.69.
