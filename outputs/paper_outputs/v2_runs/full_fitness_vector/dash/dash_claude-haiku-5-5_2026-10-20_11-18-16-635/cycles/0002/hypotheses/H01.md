# H01

Guard the stash-to-target promotion insert position on target-bucket occupancy so that hit rate recovers on fuller buckets while keeping v002's gain on sparse ones.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `DashSegment::get`, the promotion call currently passes `InsertPosition::Back` unconditionally to `promotion_get`. Change the choice to depend on the target bucket's occupancy at the moment of promotion:

- If the target bucket has at least one free slot, keep `InsertPosition::Back` (v002's behaviour).
- If the target bucket is full, pass `InsertPosition::Front` instead, so the promoted key enters at the LRU end and is the first candidate for the next eviction rather than displacing the bucket's current LRU resident into the demotion cascade.

Keep the occupancy check local to the promotion path: do not change `evict_one()`, the demotion cascade, or the probing-recovery path in `promotion_get`. Do not alter the stash scan order introduced by v003 (this hypothesis is built on v002, which does not include that reorder). Preserve all existing invariants on slot ownership and bucket counts.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 showed that MRU insertion helps one-shot reuse on the LIRS trace, while v001 showed that MRU insertion into a full target bucket displaces hot residents and loses hit rate. Gating the insert position on fullness tests whether the two effects can be separated by occupancy alone.
