# H02

Defer moving a stash-hit key into its target bucket until its second stash hit to cut get-path latency on stash-resident keys.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `promotion_get()`, when a lookup hits a stash slot, return the value and leave the entry where it is. Only promote the entry into its target bucket on the second stash hit for that key. Track the first hit with a single bit in unused segment padding, not with a per-bucket counter, so no write-side maintenance is added to insert, remove, or demotion paths. Keep v002's tier order (target and probing scans before the stash) and its target-hit behaviour unchanged. Do not change `InsertPosition` handling, victim selection, or metadata layout.

The experiment: measure get p50 and p99 on a trace or subset where most hits land in the stash, comparing against cycle/0001/v002. Also measure hit rate on workload-a at size 2048 (v002 reports 0.6787). The hypothesis is refuted if stash-hit get latency does not drop, or if hit rate falls, because deferred promotion lets a stash entry be evicted before it is promoted.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle 0001 insight left open whether v002's latency gain survives stash hits, which pay for two earlier misses. Cycle 0002 showed that adding occupancy bookkeeping on the write path costs more than it saves, so this change avoids new write-side state. It removes the promotion write from the stash-hit read path, which is where the cost sits.
