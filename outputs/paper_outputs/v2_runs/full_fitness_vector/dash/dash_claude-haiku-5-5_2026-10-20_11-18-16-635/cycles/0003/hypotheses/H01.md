# H01

Break ties among equal-counter victims by LRU age in `evict_one()` to raise hit rate on workload-a without changing per-op cost.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In `evict_one()`, keep the 2-bit saturating access counter that v003 uses to pick the victim, but resolve ties between slots with equal counter values by choosing the least-recently-used of them, instead of whichever tied slot the scan reaches first. Recency can come from the existing LRU order the baseline already maintains, so no new per-slot fields are needed. Leave the counter increment, the decay step, the promotion logic, and the get path unchanged. Metadata must stay at 40 bytes per item and 24 bytes per segment.

The experiment: run workload-a at size 2048 and compare hit rate against v003 (0.6706). The hypothesis is refuted if hit rate does not move, or if get or put p50 regresses against v003.

## Rationale (optional)

v003's counter ties are common at low counter values, and the scan order decides which of them is evicted. Using recency as the tie-break keeps the frequency signal that gave v003 its hit-rate gain and adds the recency signal that a counter alone does not provide.
