# H01

Caching the lowest-counter slot per full bucket will cut put-path per-op time below v002's 99 ns p50 while keeping its hit rate at 0.6909 exactly.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Keep the saturating access counter and the victim ranking from cycle/0004/v002 unchanged, but stop rediscovering the minimum on every eviction. Add a per-bucket cached index of the lowest-counter resident (for example a small `min_slot: u8` alongside each bucket's metadata). Update it on the write paths that change a resident's counter or occupancy: insert into a free slot, counter increment on a target hit in the saturating-counter update, and any removal or replacement inside `evict_one()`. When a bucket is full, `evict_one()` reads the cached slot and only falls back to a full scan if the cache is marked invalid. Tie-breaking must match v002 exactly (same slot wins among equal counters), so the chosen victim is identical to v002 on every eviction. Leave the get path, the deferred stash-to-target promotion, and the record layout untouched. Preserve the invariant that the cache is never stale when `evict_one()` runs: if a counter changes without the cache being refreshed, mark the bucket invalid rather than guess.

Success check: the hit-rate summary must equal v002's 0.6909 on workload-a, and put p50 should fall from v002's 99 ns. Get should stay at v002's level.
