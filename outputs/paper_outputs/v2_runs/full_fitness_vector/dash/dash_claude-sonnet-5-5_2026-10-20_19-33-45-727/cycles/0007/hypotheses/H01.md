# H01

Enlarge the bit-packed second-hit doorkeeper in the cycle 0005 incumbent so more cold keys are filtered on put, targeting per-op-time while holding hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the overlay of the admission/put path (the second-hit filter), keep the existing gate semantics: a key is admitted into a segment only on its second sighting. Cycle 0006 showed a small lossy doorkeeper cut put-side work but cost hit-rate because of false positives/collisions. Instead, size the doorkeeper at a larger power-of-two number of bits (e.g. 4x the current size, chosen from capacity), indexed by two independent hash bits of the key hash (a 2-hash Bloom-style check), and clear it by an aging reset once its popcount passes ~50% of bits (amortised, done inside the existing put under the lock already held). Do not add fields to the item struct; metadata-size must stay 40. Do not touch the get path.

## Rationale

Answers the open question "can a larger or aged doorkeeper keep put-side savings without losing hit-rate".
