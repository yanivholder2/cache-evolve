# H03

Strip remaining dead per-put bookkeeping from the cycle 0001 incumbent (which was never cleaned) to reduce per-op-time without changing policy.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Apply only deletions, no new branches: in the overlay files, remove writes and reads that the policy never consumes on the put and eviction paths (unused timestamp/counter updates, unused stat increments, redundant re-reads of the same bucket field, redundant fingerprint recomputation of the key hash that is already computed by the caller). Reuse the already-computed hash through the put path instead of rehashing. Do not alter admission, insert position, or eviction order, and do not add guards or early returns (these have repeatedly cost latency). Item layout must remain 40 bytes. This is also a measurement of how much of the cycle 0004 gain came from dead-bookkeeping removal versus the filter.

## Rationale

Cycle 0004 found dead-code removal helped once; cycle 0001's variant predates that finding.
