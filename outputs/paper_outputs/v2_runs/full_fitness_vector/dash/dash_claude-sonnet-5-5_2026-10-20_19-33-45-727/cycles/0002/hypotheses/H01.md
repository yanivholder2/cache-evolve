# H01

Packing v003's second-hit flag into spare bits of an existing Item field will keep the admission-filter hit-rate and speed gains while restoring metadata-size from 48 to 40 bytes.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In v003's overlay, remove the `hit: bool` field from `Item` so the struct returns to its baseline layout and size (40 bytes for `Item<u64,u64>`). Store the "has been hit once in stash" flag in a spare bit of an existing field instead. Pick whichever existing field has spare bits (for example a fingerprint/tag or a small counter/metadata byte). If none exists, encode it in the bucket's per-slot metadata. Add small inline helpers `item_hit(&Item) -> bool` and `set_item_hit(&mut Item)` and use them where v003 reads or writes `hit`. Mask the bit off wherever the field is compared or used for its original purpose, such as fingerprint matching. Keep the get logic identical: on the first stash hit, refresh recency in place and set the flag, skipping `remove_at` and `promote_to_target`. On the second hit, promote. Clear the flag when an item is inserted or demoted. Do not change any other struct, and preserve all key-matching behaviour.
