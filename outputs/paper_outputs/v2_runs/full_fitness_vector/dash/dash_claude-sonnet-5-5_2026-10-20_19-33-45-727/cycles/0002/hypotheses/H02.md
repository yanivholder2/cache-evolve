# H02

Combining v001's uniform MRU (Back) insertion for demotion cascades with a cold (Front) first stash admission will test whether position gains stack, targeting hit-rate at zero metadata cost.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Starting from v001 (where `get` passes `InsertPosition::Back` for stash→target promotion and cascades also use Back), change only the stash→target promotion call in `get`/`promote_to_target` to `InsertPosition::Front`, so first-time admissions land cold. Leave the target→probing promotion and every `demote_from_normal_bucket` cascade at `InsertPosition::Back`. Do not touch struct layouts, so metadata-size stays at 40. This differs from v002, which used Back for target→probing promotion but a different cascade choice. Here cascades stay MRU, which isolates the effect of demotion position from promotion position. The experiment compares hit-rate against v001 and v002.
