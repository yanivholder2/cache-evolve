# H03

Stacking v003's second-hit admission filter on baseline-free insertion at Front for cascaded demotions will improve hit-rate beyond v003 by keeping demoted items cold, at a cost of 48-byte items.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's `hit: bool` second-hit filter in `get` exactly as is. Additionally, in `demote_from_normal_bucket` and any cascade path, pass `InsertPosition::Front` (LRU end) for demoted items, so they are first to be evicted, while items promoted on their second hit are inserted at `InsertPosition::Back` (MRU) in the target bucket. Reset `hit` to false when an item is demoted, so a demoted item must prove itself again. Leave the Item layout alone. The experiment is whether the admission filter and tiered positions stack on hit-rate, as asked in the cycle 0001 open questions.
