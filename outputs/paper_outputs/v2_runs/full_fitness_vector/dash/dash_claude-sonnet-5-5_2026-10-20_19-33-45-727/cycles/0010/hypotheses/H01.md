# H01

Replace the victim choice in the DASH segment eviction path with a frequency-aware pick (lowest 2-bit saturating counter among bucket slots) to raise hit-rate on top of the incumbent doorkeeper.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In the eviction function of the DASH segment overlay (the one that picks a victim slot when the target bucket and its probing/stash buckets are full), keep the cycle-0008 one-cache-line aged two-probe doorkeeper on the put path untouched. Pack a 2-bit saturating counter per slot into spare metadata bits (metadata-size must remain 40). Increment on get hit (saturating at 3, plain non-atomic or Relaxed update, no extra branches beyond the saturating add). In the eviction scan, choose the slot with the lowest counter, ties broken by first found; after choosing, decrement (age) the counters of the other scanned slots by 1 when above 0. Do not change lookup control flow, locking, or the doorkeeper. Invariants: capacity, key/value semantics, and metadata-size unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Insights say admission is saturated and eviction/promotion is the untested lever; this is an eviction-only change.
