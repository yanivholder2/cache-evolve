# H02

Shrink the incumbent doorkeeper's reset work by clearing it incrementally (one word per put) instead of a full wipe at the aging threshold, to cut put-latency spikes while holding hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the doorkeeper/admission code of the put path, locate the aging step that clears or resets the filter bits after a threshold of insertions. Replace the bulk reset with an incremental scheme: keep a rotating cursor (stored in existing spare bits, metadata-size stays 40) and on each put that touches the filter clear exactly one 64-bit word at the cursor then advance it modulo the word count. Remove the bulk-reset branch. Do not alter probe count, hash functions, or filter size, and do not touch the get path. Invariants: second-hit admission semantics (a key must be seen once before admission) preserved; filter stays sparse.

## Rationale (optional)

Amortizes aging cost; per-op-time is the targeted dimension, with hit-rate expected flat.
