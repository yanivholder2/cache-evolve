# H02

Taking the put/insert path of the best-hit-rate incumbent and avoiding the victim scan on non-full buckets will reduce per-op-time while keeping hit-rate near 0.684.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Starting from cycle/0002/v002 (hit-rate 0.684), modify only the insert/put path. Check for a free slot in the target bucket with a single pass, using the bucket's occupancy or a bitmask, and write directly into the first free slot without invoking the eviction/victim-selection routine. Run victim selection only when the bucket and its stash are full. Merge the duplicate-key check and the free-slot search into one scan over the bucket. Keep the admission logic, the bit-packed flag layout and the item size (40) unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The put path is the other half of per-op-time. It is unexplored, because earlier cycles changed policy rather than the mechanics of insertion.
