# H02

Replacing the second-hit filter's remove-and-promote on a flagged hit with an in-place flag clear and no slot movement will lower per-op-time without losing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

In cycle/0002/v002 (best hit-rate, 0.684), find the get-hit branch that handles an item whose second-hit flag bit is already set. Today it removes the item and re-inserts or promotes it. Change this so the item stays in its current slot. Clear or flip the packed flag bit with a single write, and do no bucket shifting or reordering. Keep the first-hit path (set the flag, no promotion) unchanged. Keep put, eviction and metadata layout unchanged, at 40 bytes. This is a removal of work, not an added fast path: the branch body shrinks and no new condition is added. Report hit-rate, because skipping promotion may shift which items are evicted.

## Rationale (optional)

Promotion moves are the most expensive remaining work on hits. Earlier position-only levers showed small hit-rate effects, so dropping promotion is likely cheap in hit-rate.
