# H03

Shrinking lock hold time in the cleaned-up minimal filter variant by moving hashing and key comparison setup outside the critical section will reduce per-op-time at flat hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Starting from cycle/0004/v001, in the get and put entry points, compute everything that does not depend on bucket state before taking the bucket or segment lock. That means the key hash, the bucket index, and the fingerprint or tag used for matching. Drop any recomputation of these values inside the locked region. Do not add atomics, guards or early returns. Do not change the lookup scan, the second-hit filter or the eviction policy. Keep metadata at 40 bytes. The edit is a reordering of existing statements plus passing precomputed values down. It should leave hit-rate bit-identical, so any hit-rate difference means a bug.

## Rationale (optional)

Nothing has measured where get and put time goes. Lock hold time is the likeliest contributor, and reordering adds no new branches.
