# H01

Switching DashSegment::get from InsertPosition::Front to InsertPosition::Back (promotions and demotions land at MRU) raises hit-rate on the lirs workload at no per-op cost.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, change `DashSegment::get` to call `promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Back)` instead of `InsertPosition::Front`. Nothing else changes. Stash-hit items then land at MRU of the target, so a single further hit promotes them to probing. Demoted and cascaded items also land at MRU of their destination bucket, so they are not the first evicted. The `promotion_get` doc already describes the Back semantics. Keep the unreachable invariants valid (the key must be resident after promote). Unit tests that hardcode Front positions call helpers directly and are unaffected.

## Rationale (optional)

Baseline's cold-landing is the most conservative option. On a recency-heavy LIRS trace, giving hits an MRU position may be better. This is the simplest probe of the insert-position axis.
