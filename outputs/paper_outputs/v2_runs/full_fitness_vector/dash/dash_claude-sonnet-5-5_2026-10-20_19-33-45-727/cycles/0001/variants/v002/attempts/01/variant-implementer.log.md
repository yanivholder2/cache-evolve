`promotion_get` no longer uses one insert position for every path:
- **Stash hit:** the item is promoted to its target bucket at `InsertPosition::Front`, so it has to earn MRU before it can go further.
- **Target-hottest hit:** the item is promoted to probing at `InsertPosition::Back`. The demotion cascade from that move (probing → true target → stash) inherits `Back`.
- **Stash admission cascade:** `demote_from_normal_bucket` is unchanged and receives the originating promotion's position, so a cascade from a stash-hit admission stays at `Front`.

The post-insert residency lookups and their `unreachable!` invariants are kept. `DashSegment::get` still passes `Front`, but `promotion_get` ignores that argument (renamed `_default_pos`).
