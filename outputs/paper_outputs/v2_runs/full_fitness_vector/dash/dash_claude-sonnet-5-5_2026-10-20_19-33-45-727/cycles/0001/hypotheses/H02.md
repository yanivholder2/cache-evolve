# H02

Using split insert positions (stash-hit admission lands cold at Front, but target→probing promotion and its demotion cascade land hot at Back) improves hit-rate through scan resistance for first-time hits while protecting proven-hot items.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, modify `promotion_get` so it no longer uses one `insert_pos` for every path. In the stash-hit branch, call `promote_to_target(core, data, InsertPosition::Front)`; the item must earn MRU before it is eligible for probing. In the target-hottest branch, call `promote_to_probing(core, data, InsertPosition::Back)`. Items evicted from probing then reach their true target at Back (one more chance, hot), and cascades to stash land at Back. Keep `demote_from_normal_bucket` unchanged and let it receive the `insert_pos` of the originating promotion. `DashSegment::get` still passes a nominal position, which is ignored or used only as a default. Preserve the post-insert residency lookups and their `unreachable!` invariants.

## Rationale (optional)

Distinct from H01 because it mixes the two positions by tier instead of picking one for all moves.
