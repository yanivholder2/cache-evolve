# H01

Lower the aging threshold of the one-cache-line doorkeeper from ~50% fill to ~25% fill to raise hit-rate by keeping the filter sparser (fewer false second-hits).

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In the doorkeeper of cycle 0008 v001 (512-bit, one 64-byte aligned line per segment, two probes from one hash), change the aging trigger: reset (clear the 8 u64 words) when the set-bit counter exceeds ~128 bits (25% fill) instead of ~256. Change nothing else: no edits to get paths, eviction, promotion, or per-item layout. Keep metadata-size at 40.

## Rationale (optional)

A sparser filter yields fewer false positives that admit one-hit keys, at the cost of forgetting true second touches slightly more often. Tests the aging-threshold open question.
