# H01

Upgrade the packed one-bit second-hit flag to a 2-bit saturating hit counter in spare Item bits, requiring two re-hits before stash→target promotion, to raise hit-rate with no metadata growth.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

In the overlay's Item helpers (`item_hit` / `set_item_hit`), widen the packed flag to a 2-bit saturating counter stored in the same spare bits of the existing field (item size must stay at 40 bytes; do not add fields). Add `item_hits()` and `bump_item_hits()`. On a hit in the stash bucket, increment the counter; promote stash→target (still at `InsertPosition::Front`, as in v002) only when the counter reaches 2; otherwise just bump the counter in place without remove-and-promote. Keep cascades and target→probing at Back. Counter resets to 0 when an item is demoted. Preserve all existing invariants on key/value bits (mask carefully).

Cross-cycle insights name a richer free-metadata admission signal as the top open question.
