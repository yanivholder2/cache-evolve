# H02

Revert v002's cold first admission to Back insertion on the best-latency incumbent while skipping promotion work for already-hot targets, to cut per-op-time without losing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Starting from v001 (bit-packed second-hit flag, uniform Back inserts), make the promotion path cheaper: in the get-hit path, if the item is already in its target bucket and already at the MRU end (front/most-recent slot), return immediately without remove-and-reinsert. Also only perform the second-hit flag write (`set_item_hit`) when the bit is currently clear, avoiding redundant stores. Do not change admission semantics or item layout (stays 40 bytes). Goal: lower get latency while hit-rate stays ~0.6787.
