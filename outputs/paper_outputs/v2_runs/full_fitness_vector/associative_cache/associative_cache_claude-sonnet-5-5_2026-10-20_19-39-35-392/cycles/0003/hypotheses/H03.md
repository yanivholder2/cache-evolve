# H03

Apply low-position insertion plus partial-free hit promotion by one step (transpose) instead of full MRU on v001's wide buckets to cut per-op-time without losing hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

Starting from v001's overlay (wide buckets), in the get-hit path replace the move-to-front/remove+push with a swap of the hit element with its immediate predecessor (transpose heuristic; no-op if already at index 0). On insertion place the new key at index len/2 after evicting the last element. Do not add metadata. The swap touches two slots only, avoiding shifting a wide bucket, so the hypothesis is that get time drops while hit rate stays near v001's or improves through scan resistance.
