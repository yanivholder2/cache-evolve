# H01

Tune the low-position insertion point from len/4 to the LRU-most slot (BIP-style with a rare MRU insert) to raise hit rate on scan-heavy workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the bucket insert path of v003's associative cache overlay (the put/insert branch that currently places a new key at index ~len/4), change the insertion position to the LRU-end slot (index len-1 after eviction). Add a bimodal rule using a cheap deterministic counter derived from the key hash (e.g. `hash & 31 == 0`): on that 1-in-32 case insert at MRU instead. No per-slot metadata may be added (metadata-size must stay 40). Keep hit promotion to MRU and eviction of the last element unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 showed insertion position is the lever; moving further toward LRU tests whether more scan resistance helps, while the rare MRU insert protects against never-promoted new working sets.
