# H02

Moving the insertion point from about len/4 to about len/3 on cycle/0004/v002's swap-based insertion will raise hit rate by giving new entries a better chance to prove reuse.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

In the put/insert path, which uses the O(1) swap-based mid-insertion, change the target insertion index from the current (about len/4) to len/3 (integer division, clamped to at most len-1). Keep the swap-based mechanism (swap the new entry into the target slot and move the displaced entry to the vacated victim slot), the transpose-by-one promotion on hit, and eviction of the LRU-end slot unchanged. Add no metadata and no new branches on the get path. Cycle insights say extreme LRU-end insertion starves new entries while higher insertion may lose scan resistance, so this probes the optimum between them.
