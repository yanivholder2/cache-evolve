# H01

Drive the insertion-depth adaptivity only from the put-miss path (not from get hits) to keep hit rate while cutting per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the parent's bucket put/insert path, keep the swap-based mid-bucket insertion and transpose-by-one promotion on hits. Add a small saturating miss-pressure counter stored in existing padding (no metadata growth). Update it only inside the put-miss branch: if the victim evicted was never promoted since its insertion (infer from the victim sitting at or below the insertion slot), increment; otherwise decrement. When the counter is above a threshold, insert at len/4; otherwise insert at len/2. Do not touch the get/hit path at all. Preserve metadata-size 40 and all existing eviction semantics.

## Rationale (optional)

Cross-cycle insights say per-hit additions taxed the hot path; cycle 0008's adaptive insertion used a global hit/miss counter.
