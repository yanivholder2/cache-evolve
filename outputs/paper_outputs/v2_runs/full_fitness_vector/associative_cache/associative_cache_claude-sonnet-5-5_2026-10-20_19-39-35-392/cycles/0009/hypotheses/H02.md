# H02

Promote an entry by a two-slot jump only on its second consecutive hit (position-derived, no flag) to raise hit rate over plain transpose-by-one.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the parent's get-hit promotion code, keep transpose-by-one for entries located at or below the mid-insertion slot (len/4 from the head). For hits on entries already above that slot, swap with the neighbour two positions toward the head instead of one (clamped at slot 0). This uses position as an implicit "has been hit before" signal, requiring no per-entry flag or counter and adding only one compare on the hit path. Leave put/insertion/eviction unchanged. Metadata size must stay 40.

## Rationale (optional)

Open question on a middle ground for promotion; unconditional geometric jumps hurt, so gate by position.
