# H02

Replace the mid-bucket shifting insert with a swap-based insert (victim slot overwritten, then swapped with the mid slot) to cut put time while keeping the hit rate of cycle 0003 v001.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

In the put path, where a new entry is placed at the mid-bucket position by shifting about half the bucket, replace the shift with: overwrite the victim (LRU-end) slot with the new entry, then swap that slot with the slot at the mid-bucket index. This is an O(1) operation rather than a half-bucket memmove or rotate. Leave the promotion-on-hit logic and the key/value layout untouched. Preserve the invariant that the victim is always the last slot. The eviction order among the displaced items changes slightly, which is acceptable. Do not change the get path.

## Rationale (optional)

The insights name the median put shift as the cost of mid-insertion. This tests whether an approximate order keeps the gain at lower cost.
