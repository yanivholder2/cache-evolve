# H03

Cut get-path fixed overhead in the baseline by using a cheaper hash-to-bucket mapping and an early-exit key compare, to lower per-op time.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the get and put paths, replace the bucket index computation with a single multiply-and-shift (mask when the bucket count is a power of two) on the existing hash. Remove any modulo or division. Compute the hash once per operation and pass it down instead of rehashing. In the bucket scan, compare a cached hash or tag first, and only compare full keys on a tag match, exiting the loop on the first hit. Mark the hit path as the expected branch. Do not change replacement policy, so hit rate must stay equal to the baseline and only per-op-time is targeted. Keep metadata-size at or below the baseline's.

## Rationale (optional)

Item movement was shown not to dominate get time. Dispatch, hashing, and lookup are the unmeasured suspects.
