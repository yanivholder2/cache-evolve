# H01

Add a one-bit-per-slot ghost/admission filter to the best incumbent so one-hit-wonders are not inserted, raising hit rate on scan-heavy workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the put path of the associative cache overlay (the function that inserts a new key into a bucket), keep the parent's insertion and eviction logic unchanged. Add a small per-bucket "ghost" record of the hash fingerprint (e.g. 8 bits) of the most recently evicted key. On a put miss, if the new key's fingerprint matches the ghost, insert at the parent's normal position, since it was recently evicted and is therefore reused. Otherwise insert at the LRU end, one slot above the victim, so a scan item is the next victim unless it is hit. Do not add any per-hit work. Keep metadata-size growth to at most a few bytes per bucket, and keep the get path identical to the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

Ghost admission is the untested policy family named in the open questions. It targets hit rate without adding the cost of stacked mechanisms.
