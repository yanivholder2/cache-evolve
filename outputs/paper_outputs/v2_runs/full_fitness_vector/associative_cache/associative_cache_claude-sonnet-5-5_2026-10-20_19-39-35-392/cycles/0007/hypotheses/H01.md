# H01

Replace transpose-by-one hit promotion with a geometric halving jump toward the MRU end to raise hit rate on scan-heavy traces.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In the get/hit path of `src/associative_cache/associative_cache.rs` (the overlay's transpose-on-hit code), change the promotion target. A hit at bucket position `i` (0 = MRU) currently swaps with `i-1`. Instead, move it to position `i - max(1, i/2)` using a single swap with that target slot (swap-based, no shifting, as in the insertion path). Positions 0 stays put. Leave the insertion point (len/4, swap-based), eviction victim (last slot), hashing, and the 64-byte alignment untouched. No new per-slot or per-bucket state; metadata-size must stay 40.

## Rationale (optional)

Insertion tuning is exhausted; the open question is whether the hit-promotion path can beat insertion position. Entries reused after surviving in the low region should reach the protected region faster, which transpose-by-one makes slow.
