# H03

Evict the lower-ranked of the last two bucket slots chosen by key-hash parity, to improve hit rate without extra state.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the eviction/victim selection of the put-miss path, replace "always evict the last slot" with: candidates are slot `len-1` and slot `len-2`; pick `len-2` as victim when a bit of the incoming key's already-computed hash is set (e.g. `hash & 1 == 1`, reuse the hash computed for indexing, do not rehash), otherwise `len-1`. Then insert the new entry at the existing insertion index (len/4) with the existing insertion mechanism, filling the freed slot by swap. The goal is to give the entry at the LRU end a second chance against scan pollution with zero metadata. Keep promotion on hit and all other behaviour unchanged; metadata-size must stay 40.

## Rationale (optional)

Ghost filters and reference bits stacked badly, but a stateless randomised victim among the two oldest slots adds no per-access work and softens strict LRU-end eviction.
