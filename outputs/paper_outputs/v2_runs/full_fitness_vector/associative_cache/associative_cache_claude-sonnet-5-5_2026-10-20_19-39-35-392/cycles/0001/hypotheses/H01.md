# H01

Repartitioning the fixed 2048-item capacity into fewer, wider buckets (e.g. 32 buckets x 64 ways) in `BaselineGeometry::layout` will raise hit rate on workload-a by reducing hash-induced conflict evictions.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, change `BaselineGeometry::layout(&self, total_capacity)` to ignore the harness split and return `(n, total_capacity / n)` with `n = 32` (clamped so n >= 1, n divides total_capacity, else fall back to the nearest divisor; also handle total_capacity < 32 by returning (1, total_capacity)). Keep the `BaselineGeometry` type name, its public fields and `for_settings` unchanged. Preserve the invariant that num_buckets * bucket_size == total_capacity. Eviction policy stays ClassicLRU. Expect a hit-rate gain (closer to fully-associative LRU) at the cost of higher get/put per-op time (longer linear scans of a Vec of 64).

## Rationale (optional)

Baseline is 128 x 16; narrow buckets suffer load imbalance. Tests the hit-rate vs per-op-time trade of associativity.
