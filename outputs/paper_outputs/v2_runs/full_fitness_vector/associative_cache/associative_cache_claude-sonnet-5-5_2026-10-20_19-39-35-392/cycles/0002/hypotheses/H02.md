# H02

Add a small saturating frequency counter per slot and evict the lowest-frequency item in the bucket (LFU with LRU tie-break) to raise hit rate on skewed workloads.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the cache overlay's slot/entry struct, add a 2-bit saturating counter, stored as a `u8`. On `get` hit: increment the counter (saturating at 3) and still do the baseline's move-to-back, so recency order is kept for tie-breaks. On `put` of a new key into a full bucket: pick the victim by scanning from the LRU end and taking the first slot with the minimum counter. Insert the new item with counter 0. Every 8th eviction in that bucket (or when all counters are saturated), decrement all counters in the bucket by 1 so stale hot items can age out. Keep the update-in-place path unchanged. Metadata-size is expected to rise slightly, so keep the counters packed.

## Rationale (optional)

Frequency is an untested signal (cycle 0001 open question). Hot items that survive a burst of one-hit scan traffic should raise hit rate, at the cost of a bucket-sized scan on eviction only.
