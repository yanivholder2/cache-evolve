# H03

Choosing the eviction victim as the entry at the LRU end but skipping one slot when it was just promoted by a hit improves hit rate versus the plain LRU victim.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Start from baseline. Replace the baseline's insertion position with mid-bucket (len/2) insertion using swap-based placement, and on hit promote by a single-step transpose (swap with the neighbour toward the protected end) instead of remove+push to the front. Evict always from the LRU end. Add no counters or extra bits. The aim is a minimal, stateless scan-resistant variant built directly on baseline to test whether the gain holds without the v003/v001 specifics.

## Rationale (optional)

Gives a distinct, independently derived stateless scan-resistant policy to compare against the incumbents.
