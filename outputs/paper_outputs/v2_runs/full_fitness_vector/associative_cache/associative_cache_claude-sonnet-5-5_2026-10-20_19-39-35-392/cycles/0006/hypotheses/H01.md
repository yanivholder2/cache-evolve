# H01

Lowering the insertion point from len/4 to len/5 in the swap-based insertion raises hit rate on scan-heavy traces.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the put path where a new entry is placed on a miss (the O(1) swap-based insertion at index len/4), change the target insertion index to `len/5` (integer division, clamped to at least 1 so the new entry is never at the pure LRU end). Keep the victim handling, swap mechanics, and the transpose promotion on hit unchanged. Do not add any new state; metadata size must stay 40.

## Rationale (optional)

Open question in cross-cycle insights: the optimum may lie below len/4. Moving toward len/3 lost hit rate; len/5 tests the other direction.
