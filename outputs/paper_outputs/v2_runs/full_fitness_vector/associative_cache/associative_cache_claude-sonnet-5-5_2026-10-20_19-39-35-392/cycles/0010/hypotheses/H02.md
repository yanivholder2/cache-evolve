# H02

Cascading the displaced len/4 occupant to the len/2 slot (instead of the tail) during swap insertion will keep swap's cheap puts while recovering the hit rate that plain swap insertion lost.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the put-miss insertion path of the bucket (the variant that writes the new entry at index len/4 with fewer shifts), replace the "occupant at len/4 moves to the victim/tail slot" step with a three-way rotation: new entry -> index len/4; old occupant of len/4 -> index len/2; old occupant of len/2 -> the victim slot (the tail, which is being evicted/overwritten anyway). This is two extra element moves versus a plain swap, still no half-bucket shift. Handle small buckets by clamping len/2 to at most len-1 and falling back to the plain swap when len/2 == len/4 or the bucket is not full (use the first empty slot as the victim then). Do not change get or promotion logic and add no metadata.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0009 showed swap insertion demotes the len/4 occupant to the tail, where it is evicted next, and that costs hit rate. The cascade moves the demotion to a colder middle entry.
