# H02

Approximating the frequency victim minimum with a cached two-candidate pair per bucket, refreshed only on full scans, cuts the full-bucket put median while holding hit-rate at 0.7278.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Keep the frequency-based victim selection of cycle/0002/v003 (per-slot access counters, periodic halving schedule unchanged). Change only how the victim is found on a full-bucket put:

- Add a small cache per bucket of the two lowest-counter slot indices, recorded during the last full victim scan. Store it as a compact field (two u8 slot indices plus a valid flag) in the bucket or alongside the bucket array, not per item.
- On a full-bucket put, if the cached pair is valid, choose the one of the two cached slots with the lower current counter as the victim, without scanning the bucket. Evict it and invalidate the cache for that bucket.
- If the cache is invalid, run the existing full scan, record the two lowest-counter slots, and evict the lowest.
- Do not invalidate the cache on get-hit counter increments. A stale pair is acceptable because it is only a candidate; the selection still picks between the two current counter values. Do invalidate on any insert into a cached slot.
- Keep the get path and the periodic halving exactly as in v003. Do not add extra decay.

Invariants to preserve: eviction still never chooses a slot whose counter is greater than the minimum of the two cached candidates, and the halving schedule is unchanged. The cache is only a shortcut for the scan.

Expected measurement: put p50 drops on full-bucket puts with hit-rate within noise of 0.7278. If hit-rate drops, the approximation is losing the frequency protection that C2 and C3 relied on, and the hypothesis is refuted.
