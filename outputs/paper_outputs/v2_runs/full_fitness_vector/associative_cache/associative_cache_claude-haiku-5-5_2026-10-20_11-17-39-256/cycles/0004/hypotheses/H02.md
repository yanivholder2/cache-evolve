# H02

Limiting cycle/0002/v003's frequency victim search to a bounded window of 8 slots per put will cut put-side p50 below the parent's measured put cost while keeping workload-a hit-rate within 0.01 of 0.7278, targeting per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's frequency counters, their saturation at 3, their increment on get hit, and its periodic halving every 256 insertions unchanged. Change only which slots the put-side victim search examines.

- Add one `u8` cursor per bucket. It names the first slot of an 8-slot window that rotates through the 32-slot bucket.
- On a put into a full bucket, scan only the 8 slots in the window starting at the cursor. Pick the minimum-counter slot among them, breaking ties by the first in window order, as v003 breaks ties in slot order.
- After each eviction, advance the cursor by 8 (mod 32) so successive evictions cover different windows across the bucket.
- If the bucket is not full, keep the existing insert path unchanged.
- Do not change the get path beyond v003's existing counter increment on hit. Do not add per-slot state, a cached minimum, or a second pass.

Expected test: put p50 should drop, because the full-bucket min scan becomes an 8-slot scan. Hit-rate on workload-a may fall slightly because the victim is no longer the global minimum. The claim is that it stays within 0.01 of 0.7278. Get p50 should stay within noise of v003's.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 and 0003 both left the put-side full-bucket scan as the open cost of the frequency line, and the cached-minimum route failed because its maintenance cost more than the scan. This hypothesis attacks the scan width instead of its bookkeeping. It tests whether a partial scan keeps most of the frequency protection, which is the open question about making the victim search cheaper without losing the hit-rate gain.
