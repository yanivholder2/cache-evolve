# H01

Repartitioning the fixed 2048-slot capacity into 64 buckets of 32 slots instead of 128 buckets of 16 raises hit-rate on workload-a by reducing hashed set-conflict misses.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Overlay `src/shared/geometry.rs` only. Keep the `BaselineGeometry` struct name, its fields, `for_settings()`, and the `BucketGeometry` impl signature. Change the body of `layout(&self, total_capacity: usize)` to:

- if `total_capacity % 32 == 0`, return `(total_capacity / 32, 32)`;
- otherwise fall back to `(self.num_of_buckets, self.bucket_size)`.

For the swept size of 2048 this yields 64 buckets × 32 slots. The product `num_of_buckets * bucket_size` must equal `total_capacity` exactly, since the geometry seam must conserve the capacity budget and the invariant gate checks it. Do not touch the eviction policy or bucket files; the buckets remain the stock `AssociativeCacheBucket`, which will simply hold up to 32 items each.

Expected trade-off: hit-rate should rise because each key has a larger set of slots to live in. `get`/`put` scan a bucket linearly via `get_position`, so per-op-time p50 should increase roughly in proportion to bucket width. `metadata-size` should be unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The geometry seam exists precisely to repartition the same capacity. The baseline's 16-slot buckets may be in a narrow-bucket conflict regime for this trace; this hypothesis tests whether fewer, wider buckets escape it at a measurable per-op cost.
