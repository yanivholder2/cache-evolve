# H03

Inserting ghost-miss cold keys at probation LRU (index 0) in place of the evicted victim, instead of at the probation middle, will raise hit-rate by letting demoted-protected and ghost-hit keys outlive one-hit-wonders.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Change only the cold-key branch of `pair_put_tagged` in
`src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`, which runs after
`take_ghost` misses and when `len0 > 0`. All of v001's other behaviour stays
the same.

- **Full bucket (today):** v001 evicts `items[0]`, writes the new item and
  tag into slot 0, then runs `rotate_left(1)` on `items[0..=mid]` and
  `tags[0..=mid]` with `mid = len0 / 2`. With the usual 1–2 slot
  probation, this puts the cold newcomer above the item that was just
  demoted from protected, and that demoted item is evicted on the next
  miss.
- **Full bucket (new):** keep the `mem::replace` of `items[0]` and
  `tags[0]`, then drop the rotate entirely. The cold newcomer sits at
  probation LRU and is the next victim unless it is hit first. `len0` is
  unchanged, and `record_ghost(old_tag)` is still called.
- **Not full (new):** insert the cold key at index 0 (`items.insert(0, ..)`
  and `tags.insert(0, ..)`, then `len0 += 1`) instead of at `mid`.

Leave these unchanged:
- **Ghost hits:** they still insert at probation MRU (`len0`). A key
  evicted cold and seen again therefore gets a full probation lifetime,
  which works as a cheap two-strike admission.
- **Other paths:** the `len0 == 0` fallback, `hit`, promotion and
  demotion, the 7/8 cap with a floor of 2 at 16+ slots, the ghost ring
  length, the tag-first `find_tagged`, and the 16-byte `Item`.

Update `cold_key_lands_at_probation_middle` so it expects index 0 for cold
keys, both when filling and when full, and rename it to match. The
`slru_bucket` and `mod.rs` test `slru_second_hit_protects` must still pass.
Keep "exactly one scan key was evicted": with a 4-slot bucket, A in
protected and three probation keys, that count still holds.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle open questions ask whether admission can take over from
the flattening cap as the hit-rate lever, and whether mid-probation entry
still means anything when probation is this thin. This tests LIP/BIP-style
LRU insertion for keys with no history. Unlike every failed hit-rate
attempt, it never shrinks or displaces protected. Removing the rotate also
makes the miss path cheaper. This is a different axis from H01 (cap size)
and H02 (memory layout).
