# H02

Replacing v003's ghost-to-protected admission with an ARC-style adaptive `protected_cap`, driven by ghost hits and late protected hits, will raise hit rate above 0.718.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Work in `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`. Keep v003's per-bucket ghost ring of probation-victim fingerprints (its `u16` ring of `max_size` entries, plus the hashing it already does on eviction), but change what a ghost hit does:

1. **Stop promoting ghost keys to protected.** In `pair_put`, a key whose fingerprint is in the ghost ring is inserted like any other new key, at probation MRU (`items.insert(len0, ..)`, `len0 += 1`, or the eviction path). Remove the `push`-into-protected branch.
2. **A ghost hit shrinks protected.** On a ghost hit, clear that ghost entry and do `protected_cap = protected_cap.saturating_sub(1).max(min_cap)`. A ghost hit means probation was too small to hold the key until its reuse. If protected now holds more than `protected_cap` items, demote protected LRU lazily: `len0 += 1`. This moves the boundary and moves no data.
3. **A late protected hit grows protected.** In `hit()`, on a protected hit where `pos < len0 + (items.len() - len0) / 4` (the LRU quarter of protected), do `protected_cap = (protected_cap + 1).min(max_cap)`. Such a hit means protected nearly lost a reused item.
4. **Bounds.** Add `min_cap = max(1, max_size / 4)` and `max_cap = max_size - 1`, computed in `with_layout`, which keeps at least one probation slot. Start `protected_cap` at v003's 3/4 value. Store `protected_cap` as a field (it already is) and make it mutable. Buckets with `max_size <= 2` keep a fixed cap.
5. Leave the promotion and demotion code in `hit()` unchanged apart from reading the now-variable `protected_cap`. Keep the non-SLRU fallback untouched.

Invariants: `0 <= items.len() - len0 <= protected_cap` after every op, except transiently inside a single call; at most one copy of each key per bucket; capacity is unchanged. Add unit tests that `protected_cap` stays inside `[min_cap, max_cap]` under random ops, and that a ghost hit no longer lands in protected.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003 showed that a ghost hit is weak evidence and must not displace protected items. The open question was whether ghost history helps if it only resizes probation. The fixed 3/4 split has never been tuned. Letting each bucket adapt toward recency (more probation) or frequency (more protected) targets hit rate on the LIRS-style trace. It adds no per-item metadata.
