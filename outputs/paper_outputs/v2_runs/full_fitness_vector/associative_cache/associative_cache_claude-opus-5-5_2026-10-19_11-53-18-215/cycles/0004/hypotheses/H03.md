# H03

Inserting first-seen keys at the middle of probation, and only ghost-hit keys at probation MRU, will raise hit rate on v001's 24-byte SLRU by shielding probation from one-shot scan keys.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Work in `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`. Leave `Item` (24 bytes), protected-segment logic, promotion and demotion, and `protected_cap = 3/4` unchanged.

1. **Per-bucket ghost ring.** Add a fixed-size `ghosts: [u16; G]` array, or a `Vec<u16>` of length `max_size / 2` rounded up to at least 1, plus a `ghost_head: usize` cursor. When the SLRU miss path evicts a probation item, write a 16-bit fingerprint of its key to `ghosts[ghost_head]` and advance the cursor cyclically. For the fingerprint, take the upper 16 bits of `crate::shared::utils::hash(&key)` and map 0 to 1, so 0 can mean "empty".
2. **Insertion position by history (SLRU branch of `pair_put`, new key only).**
   - **Ghost hit** (the fingerprint is in `ghosts`): clear that entry and insert at probation MRU, as today. Never insert into protected.
   - **Ghost miss** (cold key): insert at probation index `mid = len0 / 2`.
     - Bucket full and `len0 > 0`: evict `items[0]` and place the new item at `mid` with one short move, `let old = mem::replace(&mut items[0], item); items[0..=mid'].rotate_left(1)`, where `mid'` is the target index after eviction. `len0` stays unchanged.
     - Bucket not full: `items.insert(mid, item); len0 += 1`.
     - All-protected fallback (`len0 == 0`): keep v001's behaviour.
3. Probation hits still promote to protected MRU through the existing `hit()`. A cold key re-referenced while it sits in the lower half of probation is promoted exactly as before.
4. Non-SLRU fallback untouched.

Invariants: every key stays in its single hashed bucket, with no duplicates and capacity unchanged. The ghost ring stores fingerprints only, never items. Update `Bucket::put` so its re-find still locates the key wherever it was inserted. Add unit tests:
- a cold key lands at `len0 / 2`
- a key that is evicted and then re-put lands at probation MRU
- a sequential scan longer than the bucket never evicts protected items

## Rationale (optional)

This tests "ghost history acts only on probation", the alternative cycle 0003 recommended, through insertion position rather than split size. That makes it distinct from an adaptive-split design. On a LIRS-style trace, loop and scan keys are mostly first-seen. Inserting them deeper in probation lets keys that really are reused within a short window survive longer, because they come back as ghost hits and are inserted at MRU. Promoted keys never get displaced by this mechanism. A cold key's move spans only half of probation, so latency should stay flat or improve.
