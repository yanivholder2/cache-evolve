# H03

Pairing adjacent buckets, so an item hit in the partner bucket is pulled back into its home bucket by swapping with the home bucket's LRU item, will raise hit rate above the baseline 0.6611 while keeping get latency close to the 69 ns p50.

baseline

## Changes

Edit `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` (`AssociativeCache::put`, `get_and_update_item`) and `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`.

1. **Fixed partner bucket.** Each key's home bucket stays `h = get_index(key, n)`. Its partner is `h ^ 1`, the adjacent bucket in the pair. If `n` is odd and `h == n - 1`, there is no partner, so fall back to single-bucket behaviour. No second hash is computed; the partner is free to derive.

2. **Put: demotion on eviction.**
   - If the key exists in `h` or `h ^ 1`, update it in place.
   - Otherwise insert into home `h` with `bucket.put(...)`.
   - If that evicts item `e`, and `e`'s own home is `h` (it always is in this scheme unless `e` was a demoted guest, see below), try to *demote* `e` into the partner `h ^ 1`:
     - If the partner has a free slot, push `e` there.
     - If the partner is full, push `e` only if `e.timestamp` is newer than the partner's eviction-candidate timestamp. Evict and drop the candidate.
     - Otherwise drop `e`.
   - An evicted item that was already a guest in its partner bucket (its home is not the bucket it was evicted from) is dropped and never demoted again. This bounds every put to at most one extra move.

3. **Get: promotion back home.**
   - Probe home `h` first. On a hit, apply ClassicLRU's `get_from_position` as today.
   - On a miss, probe the partner `h ^ 1`. On a partner hit, *promote* the item:
     - Remove it from the partner with `remove_at`.
     - Insert it into home `h` with `put`.
     - If home was full, its evicted LRU item `x` takes the vacated slot in the partner, but only if `x`'s home is `h`, which keeps the at-most-two-buckets invariant. If `x` is itself a guest from the partner, it goes back to its home, which is the partner anyway, so it is always legal. Either way the partner's slot count is preserved: a swap, not a drop.
   - Return a reference to the item's value from the home bucket after promotion.

4. **Recency signal.** Change ClassicLRU's `get_from_position` to refresh `items[position].timestamp = Instant::now()`. Then the demotion test in step 2 compares last-access times across buckets.

5. **Invariants to keep:**
   - A key is only ever in `h` or `h ^ 1`, and never in both.
   - Bucket sizes stay at most `max_size`.
   - Total capacity is unchanged; do not touch `geometry.rs`.
   - A promotion swap never loses an item.

   Update the editable in-file unit test `eviction_when_bucket_full` in `mod.rs` to reflect that the first item may survive as a guest in the partner bucket.

## Rationale (optional)

Unlike H01 (load-aware placement with two independent hashes) and H02 (cuckoo displacement chains), this scheme keeps hot items in their home bucket, so most get hits cost a single probe. The partner acts as a victim area that each pair of buckets shares. Partner buckets are adjacent in the `Vec`, so the second probe also has better cache locality than a random second hash.
