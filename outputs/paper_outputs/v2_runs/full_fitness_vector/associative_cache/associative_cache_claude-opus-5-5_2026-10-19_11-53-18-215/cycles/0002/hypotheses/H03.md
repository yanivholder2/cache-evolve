# H03

Replacing `Item`'s 16-byte `Instant` timestamp and wide LFU counter with 32-bit logical fields will cut metadata-size for `Item<u64,u64>` from 40 to 24 bytes and lower per-op time, with hit rate unchanged.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

Edit the `Item` struct (in `src/shared/item.rs`, or wherever `crate::shared::item::Item` is defined) and the policies in `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`. Leave v003's bucket algorithm in `associative_cache_bucket.rs` the same, apart from the timestamp comparison type.

1. **Item layout.** Change `timestamp: Instant` to `timestamp: u32` and `lfu_counter` to `u32`, so `Item<u64,u64>` is `8 + 8 + 4 + 4 = 24` bytes with no padding. Order the fields so the two `u32`s are adjacent. Remove any other per-item field that no policy reads. Keep the field names so call sites still compile.
2. **Logical clock.** Add a module-level `thread_local! { static CLOCK: Cell<u32> = Cell::new(0) }` with `pub fn tick() -> u32`, which does a wrapping increment and returns the new value. `Item::new` sets `timestamp = tick()` and `lfu_counter = 0`. This replaces every `Instant::now()` call.
3. **Policies.** In `ClassicLRU::get_from_position_in_slice` and `TimestampLRU::get_from_position_in_slice`, replace `Instant::now()` with `crate::shared::item::tick()`. In `Lfu`, use `lfu_counter = lfu_counter.saturating_add(1)`. Remove the `use std::time::Instant` import.
4. **v003 demotion comparison.** In `pair_put`, change `e.timestamp > self.items[c].timestamp` to a wrap-safe test: `(e.timestamp.wrapping_sub(self.items[c].timestamp) as i32) > 0`. Apply the same wrap-safe ordering in `TimestampLRU`'s `min_by_key` / `max_by_key`. Key on `t.wrapping_sub(base)`, where `base` is the minimum over the slice found by the same wrap-safe comparison, or simply keep a plain `u32` compare and document that wrap needs 4 billion ops.
5. **Tests.** Add a unit test asserting `std::mem::size_of::<Item<u64, u64>>() == 24`. Update `classic_lru_slice_touch_moves_to_back_and_refreshes` to assert `items[2].timestamp != before`. Do not change any JNI or gate-surface file. If `Item` crosses JNI only through key/value, nothing else changes.

Invariants: ordering semantics of every policy are preserved, recency comparisons stay monotonic within 2^31 ticks, and capacity and slot legality are unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Metadata-size hasn't moved in any cycle. In v003, 24 of the 40 bytes per item are timestamp and counter, and a monotonic `Instant::now()` is a vDSO call on every hit and insert. That call is likely a visible part of v003's 56 ns get. A logical tick gives the same ordering for much less. This targets a different fitness dimension from H01 and H02, which both target hit rate.
