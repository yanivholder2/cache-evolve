# H02

Reusing v003's two segments of each merged bucket as a key-independent segmented LRU (probation then protected, promote on second hit) will raise hit rate above baseline by protecting re-referenced items from one-shot scans on the LIRS workload-a trace.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

Keep v003's single-hash, contiguous-`Vec`, in-slice-update mechanics and its `len0` split. Change what the two segments mean. They are no longer per-key home and partner halves chosen by a hash bit. They become SLRU segments shared by all keys of the physical bucket: `items[0..len0]` is **probation** and `items[len0..]` is **protected**. Edit `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`:

1. **Capacities.** Let `max_size` be `2 * bucket_size` when paired, else `bucket_size`. Set `protected_cap = max_size * 3 / 4` and keep at least 1 probation slot. Store it as a field in place of `half_size`, and drop `divisor` and the hash-bit half selection (`home_half_of_hash`, `home_half_of_key`). The unpaired trailing bucket uses the same rule with its smaller `max_size`.
2. **get (`pair_get`).** Scan the whole bucket once for the key.
   - Hit in protected: rotate the item to the back of the protected range, which is the MRU position, in place with `items[pos..].rotate_left(1)`.
   - Hit in probation: promote. Remove it from probation (`len0 -= 1`) and push it to the protected MRU position. If protected now holds more than `protected_cap` items, demote protected's LRU item (index `len0`) to probation's MRU end. Do this by moving the boundary: `len0 += 1` makes that item the last probation slot with no data move. The total count is unchanged, so nothing is dropped on a get.
3. **put (`pair_put`).** If the key exists in either segment, update its value and apply the same hit rule as get. Otherwise:
   - If the bucket is full, evict probation's LRU item (`items[0]`). If probation is empty, evict `items[len0]`, the protected LRU, instead.
   - Insert the new item at probation's MRU end (index `len0`, then `len0 += 1`).
4. These moves don't use the boxed policy's ordering for SLRU. Bypass `eviction_policy` in `pair_get` and `pair_put` when the configured kind is `ClassicLRU`. For any other kind, keep a single-segment fallback (plain one-list behaviour through the policy, with `len0 = items.len()`) so the other policies stay correct. Remove the `Instant::now()` refresh from `ClassicLRU::get_from_position_in_slice`, because SLRU doesn't use timestamps.
5. **Tests.** Replace `partner_hit_promotes_home` with `slru_second_hit_protects`. In a ClassicLRU cache with `n = 2` and `bucket_size = 2` (one 4-slot bucket), put A, then get A (promoted). Then put B, C, D, E, which streams through probation. Assert that A still hits. Keep `pair_capacity_and_slot_legality_under_overfill`, and keep `eviction_when_bucket_full` passing (3 keys fit in 4 slots).

Invariants: every key stays in its hashed physical bucket, `len0 <= items.len() <= max_size`, there are no duplicates, and a get never drops an item.

## Rationale (optional)

LIRS traces are built to punish pure LRU with loops and scans. SLRU is the cheapest scan-resistant policy that fits inside one bucket and needs no per-item metadata: segment membership is encoded by the `len0` boundary v003 already keeps. This is a different direction from H01 (plain LRU) and H03 (metadata shrink).
