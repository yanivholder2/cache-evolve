# H01

Replace the 16-byte `Option<usize>` prev/next links in `Node` with `u32` indices (sentinel `u32::MAX`) to cut the metadata-size dimension below 48 without changing hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change `Node.prev` and `Node.next` from `Option<usize>` to `u32`, with a `const NIL: u32 = u32::MAX`. Change `head` and `tail` in `TinyLfuCache` the same way. Update `unlink`, `push_back`, `get` and `put` to convert with `as usize` at indexing sites and compare against `NIL`. Do not change the sketch, hashing or admission logic (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`). Keep `try_new` returning `None` for capacity 0, and also return `None` if capacity >= u32::MAX. Preserve the LRU order semantics exactly, so hit-rate must be identical to v001.

## Rationale

Cycle 0002 noted that the headline metadata number tracks Node size. It has been stuck at 48 since the baseline. Two `Option<usize>` fields cost 32 bytes. Two `u32` fields cost 8, which should reduce Node size by 24 bytes or so, depending on K and V. Fewer cache lines touched per list operation may also help per-op-time.
