# H02

Choose the eviction victim as the lower-frequency of the two least-recently-used entries to raise hit-rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `put()` in `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, in the full-cache branch, look at both `self.head` and the node after it (`nodes[head].next`) when that node exists. Estimate the sketch frequency of each with the existing `CountMinSketch::mix` and `estimate`. Take as victim the one with the lower estimate, breaking ties toward `head` (the older one). Then apply the existing admission test, `incoming_freq > victim_freq`, against that victim. Removing a non-head victim uses the existing `unlink`. Leave the sketch, hashing and `get` path untouched. Add one extra hash and estimate per eviction attempt, and nothing on hits.

## Rationale

Plain LRU-head victim selection can pick a moderately hot entry that has just gone cold. Sampling two candidates is a cheap approximation of frequency-aware eviction. It targets admission quality, which the cycle 0002 insight flagged as the lever for hit-rate gains.
