# H01

Pre-reserving map and node-slab capacity at construction time will lower per-op-time on the incumbent by removing rehash/grow and allocation work from the put path.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In the constructor of the cache in the tiny_lfu overlay (the `new`/`with_capacity` path), size the key->index map with `with_capacity(capacity + capacity/8)` (or the equivalent for the hasher in use) and `Vec::with_capacity(capacity + 1)` for the node slab, so no resize occurs during steady state. In the eviction/put path, reuse the evicted node's slab slot in place for the incoming entry (overwrite key/value, relink at the MRU position) rather than pop-and-push or free-list churn. Do not change the sketch, the admission rule (strict greater-than against the LRU head), or the Node layout. Preserve the public API and the JNI-facing behaviour.

## Rationale (optional)

Insights say only changes that make existing machinery cheaper pay off, and that put tail latency is the main cost. Hit-rate and metadata-size should be unchanged.
