# H03

Skipping the sketch increment for keys whose estimated frequency is already at the u8 cap, combined with a prefetch-friendly single-cache-line counter placement, will lower per-op time on hot keys with hit-rate unchanged.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Starting from the baseline, in the sketch increment path: compute the single hash once, derive the index with a mask, read the counter, and return early if it is already saturated (u8 255 or the baseline cap) instead of performing the conservative-update pass over all rows. Replace the multi-row index derivation with one hash split into row indices via bit-shifts of the same 64-bit value (no re-hash). Do not change the admission rule (strict greater-than, LRU-head victim) nor the counter decay policy (none). Combines the cheap-hash idea from cycle 0002 into the baseline as an independent lineage for comparison.

## Rationale (optional)

Gives a distinct baseline-rooted speed experiment to test whether the incumbents' gains are additive or noise.
