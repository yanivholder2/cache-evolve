# H01

Reverting v003's 4x wider sketch to the original sketch size while keeping its cheap hasher and MRU short-circuit will cut per-op time and heap with hit-rate unchanged.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In the sketch constructor of the overlay, restore the sketch width to the pre-widening size (undo the 4x multiplier), leaving everything else in v003 untouched: the cheap map hasher, the MRU short-circuit on hit, the u32 links, u8 counters, single-hash conservative update, and strict-greater admission against the LRU head. The smaller table improves cache locality in the increment/estimate hot path. Keep the mask-based index derivation valid (width stays a power of two). Expect hit-rate within ~0.001 of 0.742 and lower per-op time.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 insight says widening gave only a marginal gain at large hidden heap cost.
