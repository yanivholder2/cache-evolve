# H02

Split the resident LRU into a small admission window plus a main segment, so new keys get a trial period, to raise hit rate on scan/recency-heavy traces.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, add a per-node `seg: u8` field (0 = window, 1 = main) and a second pair of list pointers (`win_head`, `win_tail`) plus `win_len`. The window holds ~1% of capacity (min 1). In `put` for a new key: always insert at the window tail. If the window then exceeds its size, take the window head (the candidate) and compare `sketch.estimate(candidate)` with `sketch.estimate(main_head)` (the main LRU victim). If candidate > victim, evict the main victim and move the candidate into main at its tail. Otherwise, evict the candidate. In `get`, a hit in the window moves the node to the window tail; a hit in main moves it to the main tail. Keep the sketch, the `increment` calls, and the total capacity bound unchanged. Total nodes must never exceed `capacity`. Nodes are still arena-backed with indices. `Node` grows by 1 byte, which may pad it.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline drops a newcomer immediately when it is cold, so a key that would become hot never builds frequency. A window gives it time to build frequency, as in W-TinyLFU.
