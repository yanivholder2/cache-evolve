# H01

Add periodic halving (aging) of the count-min sketch to raise hit rate on workload-a by letting stale frequencies decay.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, add `additions: usize` and `sample_size: usize` fields to `CountMinSketch` (sample_size = 10 * capacity, passed into `CountMinSketch::new`). In `increment`, after bumping counters, increment `additions`; when it reaches `sample_size`, call a new `reset()` that right-shifts every counter in every row by 1 (`*c >>= 1`) and sets `additions = additions / 2`. Leave the admission rule in `put` (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`) and the LRU list logic unchanged. Sketch dimensions are unchanged, so metadata size is unchanged. The aging pass is amortised over `sample_size` ops.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline sketch only grows (u32 saturating), so old hot keys keep high estimates forever and block newer hot keys from admission.
