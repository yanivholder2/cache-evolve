# H03

Replace the 4-row Vec<Vec<u32>> sketch with a single flat table of u8 saturating counters and one hash computation per op, to cut per-op time and metadata size.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, rewrite `CountMinSketch` to store `counts: Vec<u8>` of length `depth * width`, indexed `row * width + pos`. Counters saturate at 255 (`MAX_COUNT` becomes `u8::MAX`). In `position`, stop constructing a `DefaultHasher` per row. Hash the key once per call, then derive the four row indices from that single 64-bit hash, for example by mixing with distinct odd multipliers or by rotating it and applying a splitmix64 finalizer per row. Add a `hash_once` helper that `increment` and `estimate` call once. Use conservative update in `increment` (only bump the counters equal to the current minimum). Update `heap_bytes` to `depth * width` bytes. Keep the `put`/`get` admission logic and the LRU list unchanged. Width stays derived from capacity, so heap bytes drop 4x.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline calls SipHash 4 times in `increment` and 4 times in each of two `estimate` calls per full-cache put, which dominates per-op time. The u8 counters cut the 65536-byte sketch to a quarter of that.
