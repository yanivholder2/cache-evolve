# H02

Skip the sketch increment for keys on the hit path of a main-LRU head entry, and cache the victim's frequency estimate, to cut per-op-time on cycle 0007 v001.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In `get` hit handling, when the hit entry is already in the main LRU and within the top eighth of recency (it is near the head, so relinking is a no-op or cheap), skip the move-to-head relink and only call the sketch increment. In `put`, when the cache is full and an admission decision is needed, store the current victim's estimated frequency in a field `victim_freq_cache` together with the victim's key. Reuse it if the victim is unchanged and no sketch increment has happened since the last estimate (use a `u32` sketch-epoch counter bumped on each increment). Otherwise recompute it. Keep the strict greater-than admission rule, the sketch and the Node layout unchanged. The goal is fewer sketch estimate calls and fewer pointer writes per op, and this is testable by per-op-time p99 on put. Hit-rate must stay within 0.001 of the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

No profile exists. This targets two hot-path costs, relinking and repeated estimates of the same victim.
