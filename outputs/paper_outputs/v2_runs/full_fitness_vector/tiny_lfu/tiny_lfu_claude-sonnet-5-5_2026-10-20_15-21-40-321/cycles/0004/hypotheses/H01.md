# H01

Replacing the std SipHash `HashMap` hasher with a cheap custom BuildHasher will cut per-op-time on the compact 24-byte layout without changing hit-rate or metadata-size.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, add a zero-sized `FoldBuildHasher` (implements `std::hash::BuildHasher`, `Default`) that builds the existing `FoldHasher` seeded with `0x9E37_79B9_7F4A_7C15`. Extend `FoldHasher::finish` so the map hash is well-spread: apply one multiply-xorshift fold (`z ^ (z >> 32)` after the final multiply) when used by the map. Change the field to `map: HashMap<K, usize, FoldBuildHasher>` and construct it with `HashMap::with_capacity_and_hasher(capacity, FoldBuildHasher)`. Do not touch Node, list links, sketch, or admission logic. A ZST hasher keeps `TinyLfuCache` and `Node` size unchanged. Also in `get`, skip `unlink`+`push_back` when `idx as u32 == self.tail` (already MRU).

## Rationale (optional)

Every get and put does one or two SipHash map lookups, which likely dominate latency on a 2048-entry working set. Cycle 0003 showed the u32 layout lost latency, so this recovers it from a different place.
