# H03

Widening the sketch from 4x to 16x capacity per row will reduce counter collisions and raise hit-rate on the compact 24-byte layout, at no change to metadata-size.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` from 4 to 16 (width = `(capacity*16).max(16).next_power_of_two()`; for capacity 2048 this is 32768 per row, 128 KiB of u8 counters at depth 4). Keep depth 4, u8 counters, conservative update, `mix`/`index` bit slicing (the `bits` field adapts via `width.trailing_zeros()`; verify 4*bits <= 64 so row slices stay disjoint, otherwise the rotate wraps harmlessly). No changes to Node, list, or admission.

## Rationale (optional)

Sketch heap is not counted by metadata-size, which tracks Node size. Cutting precision cost hit-rate in earlier cycles; this tests the opposite direction and measures the per-op-time cost of a larger table on cache locality.
