## Changes

Leave the sketch, the hasher, the compact Node layout and the strict-greater admission comparison untouched. Only the split constants and any derived eviction-trigger conditions should change. Preserve the invariant `window_len + main_len <= capacity`. Test: compare hit-rate against the parent on workload-a; the claim is refuted if hit-rate does not exceed 0.7421. Per-op-time and metadata-size are expected to stay flat (no new state).
