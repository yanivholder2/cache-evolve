# H03

Replace the `fmix64` finaliser in `key_hash` with a single xor-shift fold of the Fx product, shortening the dependency chain in front of the four sketch RMWs and lowering `get` p50 per-op-time with hit rate within noise.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

- In `key_hash<K>()`, delete the `fmix64(hasher.finish())` call. Instead, `let h = hasher.finish(); h ^ (h >> 32)`. For `u64` keys, `FxLikeHasher` gives `k * FX_SEED`, whose high 32 bits are well mixed but whose low bits depend only on the key's low bits. The fold puts high-bit entropy into the low 32 bits. The fold costs one shift and one xor, where `fmix64` costs two 64-bit multiplies and three shift-xors in a serial chain.
- `CountMinSketch::indices()` is unchanged: `h1 = h as u32` becomes `lo ^ hi`, and `h2 = (h >> 32) as u32 | 1` stays the high half. Rows stay independent through double hashing with odd `h2`.
- Remove `fmix64` if nothing else uses it, so there is no dead code. Don't touch the `HashMap`'s `FxBuild`; the map hashes independently.
- Update `conservative_update_bumps_only_minimum_counters`, which derives indices from `key_hash`. It needs no logic change, because it recomputes `h1` and `h2` from the same `key_hash`.
- `get`, `put`, the duel, conservative update, the `u8` counters and the CLOCK logic stay byte-for-byte the same.

Acceptance: `get` p50 drops below v001's 36 ns when the two run side by side, and hit rate stays within ±0.0005 of 0.7422. If hit rate falls further, the weaker mixing is producing correlated collisions and the hypothesis is refuted.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the reverse of the lesson from cycle 0009 v003. Adding one more `fmix64` to the sketch path raised the median, because the hash sits on the critical path before any counter load can issue. `get` p50 has not moved under changes to relinking, sketch size or layout. So the serial ALU chain in `key_hash` → `indices()` is the one untested part of the per-access path that can be removed rather than added to.
