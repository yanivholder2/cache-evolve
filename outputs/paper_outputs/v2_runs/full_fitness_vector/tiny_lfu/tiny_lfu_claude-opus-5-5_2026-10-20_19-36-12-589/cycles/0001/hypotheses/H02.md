# H02

Adding periodic sketch aging (halving every counter after a sample of 10 × capacity increments) will raise hit rate by letting formerly-hot resident keys lose their admission advantage so the LRU victim can be displaced by currently-hot newcomers.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, keep the single LRU resident set and the
admission-contest logic in `TinyLfuCache::put` exactly as is; change only the
`CountMinSketch` frequency bookkeeping:

- Add fields `additions: usize` and `sample_size: usize` to `CountMinSketch`.
  Set `sample_size = 10 * capacity` (pass capacity into `CountMinSketch::new`
  alongside `width`, clamping `sample_size` to at least `MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH`).
- In `CountMinSketch::increment`, after bumping the rows, increment
  `additions`; when `additions >= sample_size`, call a new `reset()` that
  right-shifts every counter in every row by 1 (`*c >>= 1`) and sets
  `additions /= 2` (the Caffeine reset convention).
- Since counters can no longer grow unbounded in a meaningful way, lower the
  saturation cap: replace `MAX_COUNT = u32::MAX` with `MAX_COUNT = 15` (a
  4-bit-style ceiling) so a single very hot key cannot dominate for many
  aging periods. Keep the storage type `u32` so metadata size is unchanged
  and the diff isolates the aging effect.
- Leave `estimate`, `position`, the hash function, `heap_bytes`, depth and
  width unchanged.

Invariants: the sketch is still incremented on every `get` and `put` before
any residency change; `heap_bytes()` reports the same value; the admission
rule remains strict `incoming > victim`. The existing unit tests use
capacity 2 or 4 with at most about 10 sketch increments. With the clamp,
`sample_size` is at least 16 (capacity 2 gives 20), so those tests never
trigger a reset and their expectations stay unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline never decays its counters, so on a long trace with phase
changes (LIRS workloads include looping and shifting patterns) residents that
were hot early accumulate huge counts and block admission indefinitely. Aging
is the TinyLFU paper's freshness mechanism and is absent here.
