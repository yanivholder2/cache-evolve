# H03

Making the admission duel compare the newcomer against the colder of the first two CLOCK candidates with clear ref bits will raise hit rate on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

Change only the victim choice in `TinyLfuCache::put`, after the existing CLOCK sweep. `get`, the sketch and the strict `>` rule stay as they are.

1. Run the existing sweep unchanged. It yields candidate `a`, the first slot at or after the hand whose ref bit is clear, and it has already cleared the set bits it passed.
2. Find a second candidate `b` cheaply, **without clearing any more bits**:
   - Look in the ref-bit word that holds `a`, and only at bit positions above `a`'s.
   - Build the candidate set as `!word & (!0u64 << ((a & 63) + 1))`, ANDed with a valid-slot mask for the last word. Use the empty set if `a & 63 == 63`, which avoids a shift overflow.
   - Pick its lowest set bit with `trailing_zeros`. If no such bit exists, or if `capacity == 1`, there is no `b` and the behaviour is exactly as in v001.
   - Cap the scan at that one word, so the miss path does at most one extra word load.
3. Estimate both candidates with `self.sketch.estimate_hash(key_hash(&node.key))`. The victim is the candidate with the lower estimate. On a tie, take `a`, which keeps v001's order.
4. Admit when `incoming_freq > victim_freq`, as before:
   - Replace the victim slot.
   - If the victim was `a`, move the hand to `a + 1` (wrapping).
   - If the victim was `b`, leave the hand at `a`. `a` keeps its clear bit and will be the first candidate next time.
   - On rejection, leave the hand at `a`, as now.
5. Add unit tests:
   - When `a` and `b` share a word and `b` is colder, `b` is evicted and `a` survives.
   - A rejected newcomer leaves both candidates resident.
   - Update `eviction_prefers_hot_items` only if its expected outcome changes, and document why in the test.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 found that LRU-end sampling gave no information, because adjacent LRU-tail entries had near-identical estimates. The candidates here come from CLOCK. They are not recency-adjacent: they are any two slots near the hand that went unreferenced for a full revolution, so their sketch estimates should vary more. The test is whether, under CLOCK's coarser order, a frequency tiebreak between two cold candidates finally gives the duel useful information. The cost is limited to one extra `key_hash` plus `estimate_hash` on full misses, and no extra sweep.
