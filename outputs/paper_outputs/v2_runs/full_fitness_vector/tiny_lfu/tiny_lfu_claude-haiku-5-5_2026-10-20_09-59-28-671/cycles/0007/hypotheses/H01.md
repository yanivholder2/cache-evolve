# H01

Shrinking the protected segment's capacity share so the protected-tail demotion branch actually fires will raise hit-rate on workload-a at size 2048 by at least 0.001 over the parent while keeping per-op-time within the parent's range.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Keep the protected-tail demotion from v002 exactly as written (demoted entries go to the probation MRU). Change only the protected-segment capacity constant or its computation in the segmented resident list so the protected share is roughly half of its current value. Do not add a cap that evicts directly from the protected segment; overflow must continue to flow through the demotion branch. Keep the sketch, the admission gate, the seed tables and all metadata structs unchanged. Add no new fields. The point of the change is to make the demotion branch reachable on this trace, since v002 showed zero hit-rate movement and the cause was never confirmed.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003 showed that capping the protected segment without demotion lost hit rate, but that variant never demoted, so the cap removed entries outright. Pairing a smaller protected share with demotion tests whether turnover is the missing ingredient. If hit-rate is unchanged again, the demotion branch is likely still not firing and the question should be treated as settled as untestable on this trace.
