# H02

Keeping v003's probation/protected split while packing the protected flag into an existing Node field and using only two list anchors restores metadata-size to 48 bytes and keeps v003's hit-rate gain.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

Remove the `protected: bool` field added to `Node` by v003. Instead, store the protected flag as the high bit of a field that `Node` already carries (use an existing small integer field, or the index field if it has spare range), with accessor helpers `is_protected()`, `set_protected()`, and `clear_protected()` used by `touch()`, `unlink()`, and the promotion path. Reduce the four list anchors v003 added to `TinyLfuCache` to two: keep the probation head and tail anchors from the original resident list and add only the protected head anchor and a protected length (`prot_len`) field. Derive the protected tail from the probation/protected boundary when needed, or keep the existing tail anchor for whichever segment is last. Keep the victim selection in `get()`/`put()` that prefers the probation head, and keep the promotion-on-probation-hit rule. The goal is that `size_of::<Node<u64,u64>>()` returns 48 and the `TinyLfuCache` fixed overhead drops from 192 back toward 152. Do not change the sketch or the admission gate.

## Rationale (optional)

v003's insight says its hit-rate gain comes from victim selection preferring the probation head, and that its metadata growth came from the bool and the extra anchors. If the same victim order can be kept with less bookkeeping, the cohort gets a hit-rate result at baseline-level metadata. The leaderboard shows metadata-size 48 held by baseline and v002, so this would be a Pareto improvement over v003 if it holds.
