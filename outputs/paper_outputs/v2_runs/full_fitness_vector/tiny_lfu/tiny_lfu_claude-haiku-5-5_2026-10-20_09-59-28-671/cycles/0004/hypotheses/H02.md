# H02

Inlining the protected-flag accessors on the `touch()`, `unlink()`, and promotion paths will recover per-op-time lost to the flag checks while keeping metadata-size and hit rate at the parent's values, targeting per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Parent v002 packs the protected flag into an existing `Node` field and reduces the resident-list anchors from four to two. Its insight records that get and put tail latencies rose because the flag accessors are called on every `touch()`, `unlink()`, and promotion step, adding work per operation even though the hypothesis aimed at metadata.

Change only the access pattern of the protected flag on those hot paths:
- Replace calls to the flag accessor functions inside `touch()`, `unlink()`, and the promotion branch with direct reads or writes of the packed field, using a shared bit mask constant, so each path performs one load and one mask test instead of a function call that may not be inlined.
- Mark the small accessor functions `#[inline(always)]` (or equivalent) if they remain in use elsewhere.
- Keep the probation/protected split, promotion and demotion rules, and anchor layout exactly as in the parent.

Invariants to preserve:
- The packed field layout and bit positions are unchanged, so `Node` size and the metadata-size figure stay at 32.
- Eviction order and hit rate must match the parent exactly. Any hit-rate movement means the change is not behaviour-preserving.
- Do not change the sketch, the admission gate, or the aging logic.

The parent's source was not available during hypothesis authoring. Before editing, confirm which functions read or write the protected flag and that the packed field is the only storage for it, so that the mask-based access is equivalent to the accessor it replaces.
