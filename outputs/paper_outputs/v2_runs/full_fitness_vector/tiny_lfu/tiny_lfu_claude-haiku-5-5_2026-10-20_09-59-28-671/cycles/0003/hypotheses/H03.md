# H03

Shortening the frequency sketch's aging period from the baseline's reset threshold to half of it raises hit rate on lirs workload-a by letting the admission gate track recent popularity faster, with no change to metadata or per-op cost.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change only the aging constant that controls when the count-min sketch counters are halved:

- Locate the sample counter and its reset threshold in the sketch's increment path (the place where the sample count is compared against the threshold and the counters are halved). Note the baseline's current threshold value in the variant's insight.
- Set the threshold to half of that value. Keep the halving operation itself (right shift or divide by two) and the counter width unchanged.
- Do not add fields, do not change the sketch dimensions, and do not touch the admission check, eviction, or node layout. This keeps metadata-size and per-op-time at baseline values, so any movement is attributable to the aging period alone.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 v003 showed that the doorkeeper, which keeps one-hit keys out of the sketch, barely moved hit rate. That suggests stale frequency counts may matter more than pollution from first sightings. A faster aging period is a single-constant test of that idea, and it does not reuse the doorkeeper direction that the cycle 0002 insight dropped. Baseline is the parent because it is the cleanest reference for measuring the aging effect alone.
