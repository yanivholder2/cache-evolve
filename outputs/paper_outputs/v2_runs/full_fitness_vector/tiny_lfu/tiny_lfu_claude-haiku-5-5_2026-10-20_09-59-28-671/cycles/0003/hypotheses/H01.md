# H01

Capping the protected segment at half the cache capacity raises hit rate over cycle/0002/v002 on lirs workload-a while keeping its packed-flag metadata at 32 bytes per node.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Keep v002's probation/protected split and its packed protected flag exactly as they are. Change only the promotion and demotion logic that moves entries between the two segments:

- In the hit path (`touch()` or the equivalent get-hit handler), promote a probation entry to protected only while the protected count is below a fixed cap of `capacity / 2`.
- When the protected count is at the cap and a probation hit arrives, do not demote the protected tail to make room. Instead, move the promoted entry to the MRU end of probation and leave the protected list unchanged.
- Add a single counter or reuse the existing length field for the protected count. Do not add a new `bool` or anchor to any node or to the cache struct, so `Node` stays at 32 bytes and `TinyLfuCache` stays within its current size.
- Leave admission (the frequency-sketch gate in `put()`) and eviction victim selection untouched.

The intent is to stop a hot working set from crowding out probation entries that are still gaining frequency. Verify the protected-count field name and the anchor layout in the overlay before editing.

## Rationale (optional)

v002 kept v003's hit rate (0.7379) with lower metadata. Cycle 0001 v003 showed that segmentation is the only source of hit-rate movement so far, but its protected segment was not capped. A cap is the cheapest way to test whether the segmentation gain is limited by protected-segment size.
