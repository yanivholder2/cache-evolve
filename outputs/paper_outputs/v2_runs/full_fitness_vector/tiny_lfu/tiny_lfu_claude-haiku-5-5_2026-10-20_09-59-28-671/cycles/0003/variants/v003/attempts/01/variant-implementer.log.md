There is no threshold to read. I added the aging step and set the period to `5 × capacity`, which is half the canonical TinyLFU sample size of `10 × capacity`. Any finite period is "faster" than the baseline's infinite one, so this is the closest reading of the hypothesis's intent. The period is my choice, not a value from the baseline.

- `CountMinSketch` gains `sample_size` and `samples` fields. Each `increment` bumps `samples`, and when it reaches `sample_size`, every counter is shifted right by one and `samples` resets.

- The public API, the admission check, eviction, node layout and the sketch dimensions are unchanged.
