# H03

Narrowing the frequency-sketch counters to 8-bit saturating values at the same byte budget, and spending the freed bytes on about eight times as many columns, should lower counter collisions more than the one-row-fewer reshape while keeping the metadata footprint at or under baseline.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Change the `CountMinSketch` counter storage from its baseline 64-bit counters to 8-bit counters, saturating at 15 to mimic the 4-bit counters used by standard TinyLFU, so that a saturated counter stops incrementing. Keep the same number of rows as baseline. Increase the column count per row by a factor of eight, keeping the total counter bytes within the baseline sketch allocation of 65536 bytes, so the new column count must be a power of two that fits the budget (the baseline row length times eight, rounded to a power of two).

Update the column-index modulus to use the new column count, keeping the per-row seeded index derivation as the baseline computes it. Keep the existing aging and halving behaviour, scaling its trigger threshold only if the baseline ties it to counter capacity. Do not change the admission comparison, the segment logic, the node layout, or the eviction order.

Record the resulting row and column counts and the sketch byte total in the variant's insight so the footprint can be checked against the baseline. The expected result is a hit-rate change on workload-a at size 2048, positive if collision spread dominates and negative if the saturation cap at 15 loses frequency resolution that the admission gate needs. Metadata-size should stay 48 or below, consistent with baseline.
