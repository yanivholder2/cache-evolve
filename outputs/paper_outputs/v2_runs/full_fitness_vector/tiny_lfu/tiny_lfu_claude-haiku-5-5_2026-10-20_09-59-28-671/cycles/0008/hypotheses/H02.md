# H02

Memoizing the victim's frequency estimate within a single put will reduce put-path latency at both p50 and p99 without changing any admission or eviction decision.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

Within `put()`, compute the admission-side frequency estimate for the eviction victim at most once per call, and store it in a local variable that both the admission comparison and any subsequent victim-selection step read. If the current put path reads the victim's estimate in more than one place (for example in the admission compare and again while choosing or confirming the victim in `evict_one()`), replace the repeat reads with the local. Do not change what is compared, the comparison direction, the tie behaviour, or the order of list operations. Keep the split unlink/promotion helper from the parent as it is. Add no struct fields and no state that persists between calls; the local lives on the stack for the duration of one `put()`. Keep sketch indices and aging untouched. If the victim's estimate is read only once already, make no change and say so in the variant's insight rather than forcing an edit.

## Rationale (optional)

The parent's put-side gain came from shortening the shared unlink and promotion path. The remaining put cost is the per-row estimate work, which earlier cycles showed scales with the number of sketch reads. This variant removes repeated reads on the put path only. Because hit rate and metadata are expected to stay at the parent's values, any movement in them would indicate that the decision logic changed by mistake, which makes this a useful check as well as a speed test. Repeat the put timing at least three times, since single runs have not separated put changes from noise in earlier cycles.
