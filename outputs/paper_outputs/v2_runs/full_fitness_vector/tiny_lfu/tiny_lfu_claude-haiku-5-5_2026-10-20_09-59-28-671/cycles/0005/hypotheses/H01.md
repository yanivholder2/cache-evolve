# H01

Removing the put-side cost of the protected-flag accessor calls in `unlink()` and the promotion step will lower put p50 latency below the parent's 152 ns while keeping the get-path gain, at unchanged hit rate and metadata size (per-op-time).

## Parent

cycle/0004/v002

## Changes

Keep the get-path inlining of the protected-flag accessors from the parent (`touch()` and the get hit path stay as they are). On the put path only, change `unlink()` and the promotion step so that the protected flag is read once into a local and the node is relinked with direct field access, rather than calling the accessor again per branch. Put this specialisation behind a separate helper (for example `unlink_for_evict()`) that is called only from `evict_one()` and from the put-side eviction branch, so `get`/`touch` keep the single shared `unlink()` path. Do not change eviction order, the admission gate, the sketch counter indices, or the packed flag layout. Do not add fields to `TinyLfuCache` or `Node`.

Measure with repeated runs (at least three) on workload-a at size 2048. Accept only if hit-rate stays at 0.7379, metadata-size stays at 32, and put p50 falls below the parent's 152 ns without the get p50 rising above 93 ns.

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0004/v002 showed a get gain and an unpredicted put regression, and the cycle-0004 insight asked for the put timing to be re-run before the inlining is accepted. This hypothesis tests whether the put regression comes from the shared `unlink()` path, and if so, removes it without giving up the get gain.
