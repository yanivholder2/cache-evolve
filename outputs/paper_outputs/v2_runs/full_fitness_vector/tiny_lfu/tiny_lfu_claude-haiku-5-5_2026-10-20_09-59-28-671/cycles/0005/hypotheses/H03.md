# H03

Deriving each row's sketch index from the single precomputed key hash with the public per-row mixing function will restore the hit rate lost by the hoisted-hash variant to baseline parity while keeping its put-path speedup (hit-rate).

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

Keep the hash computed once per operation and reused across the admission check and the sketch update, as the parent does. Change the hashed-key helper that computes the per-row counter indices so that each row index is produced from that one hash by the same per-row seeded mixing that the public sketch method uses, instead of by the shortcut the parent uses. The seeds for this mixing come from a `const` array, so no per-instance seed storage is added. Confine the change to the helper; do not change `put()`'s control flow, the protected flag layout, the eviction order, or the aging period.

Verify equivalence on a sample of keys by comparing the helper's indices with the public method's indices for each row, and keep the check in the overlay's own tests (not `tests/`).

Accept only if hit-rate returns to at least 0.7360 (the baseline value) and the per-op-time summary stays at or below the parent's 54 ns; the put p50 must not exceed the parent's measured put p50 by more than run-to-run noise (re-run three times before judging).

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0002/v001 is the best per-op-time incumbent, but its hit-rate drop (0.7349 vs 0.736 for baseline) is the only open defect in that line. The cycle-0003 result showed that matching the public per-row indexing restores hit rate but costs speed. This hypothesis tests whether the cost can be avoided by deriving the public indices from the single hash, rather than by re-hashing per row.
