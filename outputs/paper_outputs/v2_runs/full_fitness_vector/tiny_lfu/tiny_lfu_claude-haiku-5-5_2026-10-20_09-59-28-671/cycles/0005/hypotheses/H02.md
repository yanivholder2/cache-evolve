# H02

Moving the per-row sketch seeds from a per-instance field into a `const` table will keep the cycle-0004 seed-caching speedup while bringing the `TinyLfuCache` struct back to its parent's size (metadata-size).

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In the sketch type, replace the per-instance seed array that `new()` fills at construction with a module-level `const` array of the same length, with values equal to the seeds that the public per-row indexing computes. Make every seed read in the sketch's index path (the helper used by both the admission check and the counter update) index into this `const` array. Remove the seed field from the sketch struct and from `TinyLfuCache`, and remove any construction-time loop that populates it.

Before relying on the table, add a check (in the overlay's own tests, not in `tests/`, which is off-limits) that for a sample of keys the const-table indices equal the indices from the public per-access derivation. Do not change the counter storage, the aging logic, the eviction order, or the protected flag layout. Hit rate must not move.

Accept only if hit-rate stays at 0.7365, per-op-time get p50 stays at or below the parent's 86 ns, and the reported `TinyLfuCache` byte count returns to 176 or lower.

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0004/v001 kept the seed speedup with no hit-rate change, but its insight records the per-instance seed array as the reason the struct grew from 176 to 440 bytes. The cycle-0004 continuation and the open question on `const` seed storage both point to this change. Its distinct claim is about storage layout, not about how hashes are computed; the hash reuse is covered by H03.
