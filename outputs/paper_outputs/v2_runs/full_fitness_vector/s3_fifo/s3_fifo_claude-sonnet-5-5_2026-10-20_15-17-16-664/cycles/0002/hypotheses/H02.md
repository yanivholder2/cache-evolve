# H02

Raising v002's small-queue ratio from 10% to 15% improves hit rate by retaining more new objects long enough to be re-referenced.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In the overlay file, change only the constant that sets the small-queue capacity fraction (currently 10% in v002) to 15%, ensuring the small capacity is computed with integer rounding and clamped to at least 1 and at most capacity-1. Leave the promotion threshold, ghost sizing, and eviction logic untouched. This is a sweep point to find whether 10% is a local optimum (compare with the 5% sweep in H03).

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 1 flagged the optimal small ratio as workload-dependent; this tests the upper neighbour.
