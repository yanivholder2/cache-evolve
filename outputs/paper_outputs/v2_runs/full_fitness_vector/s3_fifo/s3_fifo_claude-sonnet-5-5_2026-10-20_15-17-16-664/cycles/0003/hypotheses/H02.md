Leave eviction logic, MAX_FREQ=3 and promotion threshold `freq > 1` untouched. Update nothing else; existing unit tests use capacity 4/5 where small_size is still 1.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 2 showed that a larger small queue lost hit rate; the optimum may lie below 10%.
