# H01

Reduce per-op-time by replacing the double linear scans in `get`/`put` of the cycle-1 winner with a single pass and an early ghost-membership check, without changing eviction decisions.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`: `put` currently calls `find_entry_mut` (scan of small+main), then scans `ghost` with `.iter().any(...)`, then `retain` (a second full scan) on a ghost hit. Change it to: (1) keep the entry lookup; (2) locate the ghost key once with `ghost.iter().position(..)` and remove it with `ghost.remove(idx)` (single scan, no `retain`). In `get`, search `main` first and `small` second only if not found (hot items are mostly in main), instead of chaining small then main; the behaviour (freq increment capped at MAX_FREQ) must stay identical. Do not change struct fields, MAX_FREQ, the 10% small ratio, or the promotion threshold (freq > 1), so hit rate stays 0.7248 and only latency changes.

## Rationale (optional)

Latency is dominated by O(n) VecDeque scans; this targets per-op-time while preserving the best hit rate.
