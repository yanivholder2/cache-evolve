# H03

Make main-queue eviction cheaper and smarter by capping the second-chance rotation to improve per-op-time without losing hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

In `evict_main` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` (as modified by the parent), bound the rotation loop: allow at most `MAX_FREQ + 1` pops-and-reinserts of freq>0 entries per call; if the bound is reached, evict the current tail regardless of freq (drop it, do not ghost it). Additionally, when reinserting a decremented entry, decrement by 1 as before (no change). Keep small ratio, promotion threshold, ghost handling and struct fields of the parent unchanged. Hypothesis: a worst-case full-queue rotation when many main entries have freq>0 is the latency tail, and the bound removes it at negligible hit-rate cost.

## Rationale (optional)

Parent v002 (cycle 2) has the second-best per-op-time (103); bounding unbounded rotation targets the remaining cost.
