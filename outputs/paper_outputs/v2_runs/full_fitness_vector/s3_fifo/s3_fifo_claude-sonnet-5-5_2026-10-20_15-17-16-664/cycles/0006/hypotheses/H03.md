# H03

Skip the freq increment and ghost work for repeated hits on keys already at the freq cap, and make small-to-main moves allocation-free, to reduce per-op-time on the 5% small-queue stack.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In the overlay's S3-FIFO source, in the `get` hit path, only write the entry's frequency counter when it is below the cap (`if f < MAX_FREQ { f += 1 }` as a branch that avoids the store when saturated), keeping the 5% small-queue ratio. In the small-to-main promotion path in `put`/eviction, move the existing entry (value and key) by `pop_front`/`push_back` of the owned item instead of cloning or reconstructing it, and avoid temporary `Vec`/`Option` allocation. Pre-reserve the VecDeque capacities for small and main at construction (`with_capacity`) so no growth reallocations occur in steady state. Do not change thresholds, queue ratios, ghost size, or eviction order; hit rate must stay identical to the parent.
