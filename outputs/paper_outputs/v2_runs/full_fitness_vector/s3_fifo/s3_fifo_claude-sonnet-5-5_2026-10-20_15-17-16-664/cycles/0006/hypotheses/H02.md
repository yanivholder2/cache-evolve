# H02

Collapse the separate small/main/ghost membership lookups on `put` and `get` into a single index lookup that stores each key's queue location, to cut per-op-time without changing hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

In the overlay's S3-FIFO source, keep one `HashMap<Key, Loc>` where `Loc` is a small enum/tag (`Small`, `Main`, `Ghost`) rather than probing separate per-queue indexes. `get` does one lookup and branches on the tag; `put` does one lookup to decide existing-update, ghost-hit-admit-to-main, or new-insert-to-small, and updates the tag in place on small-to-main promotion, main eviction, and ghost insertion/removal (rather than remove+insert in different maps). The ring-buffer ghost stays as is, but ghost expiry must update the tag entry (remove it) in O(1). Preserve all queue ordering, thresholds, the 0 to 3 freq cap, and the reported metadata size. Hit/miss sequence must be identical to the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

The incumbents' remaining put tail is hash-map traffic; fewer map operations per op is a direct mechanism.
