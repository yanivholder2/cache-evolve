# H03

Lowering MAX_FREQ from 3 to 2 will let cold main entries age out faster and raise hit rate, with no change to small-to-main admission.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3` to `2`. Leave the small-to-main promotion test in `evict_small` (`tail.freq > 1`) as it is, so admission to main still needs two hits in small and stays strict. Main rewinds in `evict_main` now cost at most two decrement passes per entry, which shortens the worst-case rotation. Change nothing else: keep the hasher, the index, the ghost structure and the small ratio inherited from the parent. The experiment is a single-constant hit-rate and tail-latency comparison; the prior cycles' failures involved loosening admission, whereas this tightens residency in main only.
