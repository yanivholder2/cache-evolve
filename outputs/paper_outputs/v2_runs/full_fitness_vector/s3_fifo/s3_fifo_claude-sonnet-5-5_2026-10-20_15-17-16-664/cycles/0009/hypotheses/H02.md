# H02

Adapting the small-queue size online from ghost-hit feedback (ARC-style) will raise hit rate without loosening main admission.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, make `small_size` mutable, bounded to [max(1, capacity*1/100), capacity*10/100], and starting at the current 3%. Add two small counters, `ghost_hits` and `small_evict_to_ghost`, reset every `capacity` puts. On a ghost hit in `put` (the `ghost_map.remove` branch), increment `ghost_hits`. At each window boundary compute the ghost-hit ratio against ghost insertions. If the ratio is above 0.5, items evicted from small were being re-requested, so grow `small_size` by max(1, capacity/200). If the ratio is below 0.1, shrink it by the same step. Keep `main_size = capacity - small_size` for the resident bound only: when `small_size` shrinks, evict extra small entries through the existing `evict_small` path; when it grows, let main drain naturally via `evict_main` on the next put. Leave the ghost bound fixed at the initial `main_size` so `ghost_map` and the ring need no resizing. Keep the promotion threshold (freq>1), MAX_FREQ and the hasher unchanged. The experiment is a hit-rate comparison against the fixed 3% parent.
