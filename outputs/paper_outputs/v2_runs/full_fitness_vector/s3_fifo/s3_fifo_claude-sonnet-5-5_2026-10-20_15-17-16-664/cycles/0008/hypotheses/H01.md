# H01

Combining v001's split ghost map with a cheap Fx-style hasher (no map pre-sizing) will lower per-op-time on both `get` and `put` without hurting hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Starting from v001 (split ghost map + tagged index), replace the default SipHash `BuildHasher` on the tagged main index and on the ghost map with a multiply-xorshift hasher: `h = (k as u64).wrapping_mul(0x9E3779B97F4A7C15); h ^ (h >> 32)` in `finish()`. Do NOT pre-size either map beyond what v001 already does (v003's pre-sizing is suspected of causing the `put` tail regression). Do not change eviction, promotion, ghost bound, or queue ratios. Preserve the public API and all behaviour; hit rate must stay at 0.7275.
