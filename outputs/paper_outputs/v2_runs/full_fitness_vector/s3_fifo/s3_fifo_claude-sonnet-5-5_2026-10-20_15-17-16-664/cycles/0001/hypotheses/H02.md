# H02

Shrinking the small queue from 25% to ~10% of capacity in `try_new` raises hit rate by leaving more room for the main queue.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, `try_new()`: replace `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` with `(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` (keep the capacity==1 special case). `main_size = capacity - small_size` stays derived, so ghost capacity (main_size) grows accordingly. No other logic changes. Invariants: small_size >= 1, small_size + main_size == capacity. Unit tests using capacity 4/5 are unaffected (small_size clamps to 1; capacity 4 gives 1 either way).

## Rationale (optional)

The S3-FIFO paper recommends ~10% small queue; baseline's 25% may waste main capacity at size 2048.
