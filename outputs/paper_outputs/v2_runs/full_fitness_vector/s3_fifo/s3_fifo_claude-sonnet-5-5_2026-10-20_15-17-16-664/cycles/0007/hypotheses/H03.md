# H03

Stacking the cheap Fx-style hasher with exact-capacity pre-sizing on the tagged-index base will lower per-op-time and `get` tail at identical hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

On the tagged-index variant, replace the default SipHash `BuildHasher` of the index map with a multiplicative Fx-style hasher (a simple `u64` multiply-rotate implementation written inline in the overlay, no new dependencies). Pre-size the map with `with_capacity_and_hasher` to cache capacity plus ghost bound times about 1.25 so no rehash occurs during the run. Mark hot index lookup/insert helpers `#[inline]`. Do not change any eviction, promotion or ghost logic, so hit rate remains identical to the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0006 suggested the hasher gain should be tested on top of the tagged index.
