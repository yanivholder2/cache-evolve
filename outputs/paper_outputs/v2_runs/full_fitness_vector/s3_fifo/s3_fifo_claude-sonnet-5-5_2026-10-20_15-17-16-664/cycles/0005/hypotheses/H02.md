# H02

Batch main-queue eviction by reusing a persistent scan cursor instead of rescanning from the head to cut per-op-time at unchanged hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

In the main-queue eviction routine (`evict_main` or equivalent), the freq-decrement/reinsert loop currently restarts from the queue head on each eviction. Make each eviction pop the head entry; if freq > 0, decrement it and push it to the tail (CLOCK-style), repeating until a freq-0 victim is found. Avoid any auxiliary scans or Vec shifts, and use VecDeque pop_front/push_back only. Do not change the promotion threshold, the freq cap, the ghost size or the small ratio. The eviction decisions must stay equivalent to the parent's, so the hit rate is unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Targets the put-tail cost, using a different parent from H01 and a different mechanism (main eviction rather than ghost).
