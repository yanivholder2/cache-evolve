# H03

Reduce the small-queue ratio to 5% on the baseline to raise hit rate, by testing whether the earlier small-ratio gain holds without other changes.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In the baseline S3-FIFO source, change the small-queue capacity constant from its current value to 5% of total capacity (at least 1 entry). Leave promotion threshold, ghost sizing, freq cap and all other logic untouched. This isolates the small-ratio effect from the scan and index changes, answering the open question of whether 5% beats 10% beyond noise.

## Rationale (optional)

A clean single-constant experiment from baseline. It will confirm or refute the cross-cycle note that 5% may be better than 10%.
