# H02

Shrink `small` by two slots per ghost hit instead of one, with the ghost ring pinned, to raise hit-rate by moving the split's balance point toward a larger main.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

This is one policy lever on the ghost-hit shrink side of the adaptive split. Cycle 0006 suggested this side sets the balance point. Nothing else changes.

- Add `const SHRINK_STEP: usize = 2;`. Add a `#[cfg(test)] shrink_step: usize` override field, initialised to `SHRINK_STEP` in `try_new`, in the same style as `floor_div`.
- In `put`'s ghost-hit branch, replace the single-slot shrink with:
  `let step = self.shrink_step().min(self.small_size - self.small_floor());` behind the existing `self.adaptive() && self.small_size > self.small_floor()` guard. Then `self.small_size -= step; self.main_size += step;`. The step can never go below the floor.
- Replace the single `if self.small.len() > self.small_size { self.evict_small_inner(false); }` fix-up with a `while` loop. Do the same for the `main.len() > main_size` fix-up (while loop calling `evict_main`). Keep `adapt = false` on the fix-up small evictions.
- Leave the grow-small side alone: +1 per graduate-while-main-full, upper clamp `capacity/2`.
- Keep the ghost pinned: `ghost_ring.len()` and `ghost_cap` stay at the *initial* `main_size` from `try_new`. Do not tie them to the new split.
- Keep the `capacity/64` floor, the `freq > 1` graduation rule, `MAX_FREQ` and the layout exactly as in the parent.
- Tests: `adaptive_split_keeps_bounds` must still pass. Extend `replay`/`h01_split_report` into an `h02_split_report` that runs `shrink_step` in {1, 2} on the same Zipf streams and prints `hit_rate`, `settled_small`, `min_small`, `shrinks`, `floor_blocks` and `grows` for each. The insight MUST quote the settled `small_size` for step 1 vs step 2.

## Rationale (optional)

Every hit-rate gain since cycle 0001 has come from giving main more room: the adaptation itself, then the `capacity/64` floor. Damping the grow-small trigger (0006 v003) was inert. A stronger shrink signal is the untested mirror image. It changes the shrink/grow ratio, and that ratio sets where the split settles. If it is flat, the floor (not the trigger ratio) is what binds, and the settled-size report will show that.
