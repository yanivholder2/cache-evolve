# H03

Raising the main-queue frequency cap `MAX_FREQ` from 3 to 7 lets hot keys survive more CLOCK laps in main, raising hit-rate on skewed workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

This is a single policy lever: the saturation ceiling of the per-entry access
counter. Everything else in 0006 v002 is unchanged. That covers the split
adaptation and its clamps (`capacity/64` floor, `capacity/2` ceiling), ghost
sizing pinned to the initial main size, the `freq > 1` small→main graduation
test, and the `Entry`/index layout. `freq` stays a `u8`, so `Entry` stays at 16
bytes.

- Change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 7;`. `get`
  continues to do `freq = (freq + 1).min(MAX_FREQ)`. `evict_main` continues to
  decrement by one per pass and rotate to the front while `freq > 0`.
- Do not change the graduation threshold. `evict_small_inner` still promotes
  on `freq > 1`, so admission behaves exactly as in the parent. Only how long a
  resident main key survives changes.
- Add a test-only `max_freq` override (mirroring `floor_div`) and an
  `h03_split_report` test. It replays the parent's Zipf streams with
  `MAX_FREQ` in {1, 3, 7, 15} and prints `hit_rate`, `settled_small`,
  `shrinks`, `floor_blocks`, `grows`, and the mean number of rotations per
  `evict_main` call. The rotation count quantifies the `put` cost of the longer
  CLOCK sweep.
- Keep `repeated_get_clamps_frequency` asserting against `MAX_FREQ`, so it
  picks up the new value.

Rationale: `MAX_FREQ` is an S3-FIFO lever that has never been tested in seven
cycles. Every trigger-rate lever was inert. A higher cap separates very hot keys
from lukewarm ones that are reused only once or twice in main. It should do
this without strengthening the ghost path, which has backfired every time. The
risk is more rotations per main eviction (`put` latency) and slower demotion of
keys that have gone stale.
