# H01

Combining the 20% small-queue share with a lowest-frequency victim fallback should raise hit rate above 0.7195 on workload-a at capacity 2048 while keeping put p50 at or below the v003 level, targeting hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Start from cycle/0002/v003 and keep its small-queue share of 20% of capacity, with the main and ghost queues sized as v003 sizes them. Port the post-budget victim choice from cycle/0002/v002 into the main-queue eviction path: when the second-chance rotation in the main queue has used its 8-step budget, buffer the rotated entries (up to the 8-slot pass) and evict the one with the lowest frequency counter instead of always dropping the tail. Ties go to the entry closest to the tail, so the choice matches the tail-drop behaviour when all frequencies are equal. Do not change `freq > 1` in `evict_small()`, the ghost-queue structure, or `get()`. Metadata layout must stay as v003 (Entry 24 bytes). Keep the rotation budget at 8 so the worst-case put work stays bounded.

## Rationale (optional)

v003 changed only the partition constant and got the best hit rate in the lineage (0.7195). v002 recovered hit rate lost to tail-drop once the budget ran out, but its gain was measured against baseline, not against the 20% share. The two edits touch different branches (partition sizing and post-budget victim choice), so they should compose without interfering. The open question from cycle 0002 is whether the victim-choice gain persists when the budget runs out more often; this hypothesis tests it on top of the stronger parent.
