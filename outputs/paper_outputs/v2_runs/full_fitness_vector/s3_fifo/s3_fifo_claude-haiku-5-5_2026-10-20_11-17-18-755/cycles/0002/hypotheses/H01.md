# H01

Replace the ghost queue's O(n) pop-back and position-based removal with an O(1) sequence-numbered ghost FIFO to lower put-path cost while keeping hit-rate identical to the parent.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Keep `ghost_index` (the `HashSet<K>` added in v002) for O(1) ghost-membership tests, but stop maintaining the ghost queue as a `Vec`/deque that is scanned and removed from by position. Instead:

- Store ghost entries in a `VecDeque<(K, u64)>` where the `u64` is a monotonically increasing insertion sequence number, and change `ghost_index` to a `HashMap<K, u64>` mapping each key to its current sequence number.
- When a key is pushed into the ghost queue, record `ghost_index.insert(key, seq)` and increment `seq`.
- When the ghost queue overflows, pop from the front (O(1)); only remove the key from `ghost_index` if the stored sequence number equals the popped sequence number, so a re-inserted key is not dropped by a stale entry.
- When a ghost hit occurs on `put`, remove the key from `ghost_index` and mark it as consumed; do not search the queue by position. Stale `VecDeque` entries whose key is no longer in `ghost_index` with a matching sequence number are skipped lazily when they reach the front.
- Preserve the existing ghost-hit semantics exactly: a key admitted from ghost goes to main, and the ghost capacity bound is unchanged. Do not touch `evict_small()`, `evict_one()`, `get()` or `find_entry_mut()`.
- Keep `MAX_EVICT_SCAN` behaviour from the parent as-is.

Invariants to preserve: hit-rate must match the parent on workload-a at capacity 2048; `Entry<u64,u64>` stays 24 bytes. Report the extra `u64` per ghost key as the only expected metadata change.
