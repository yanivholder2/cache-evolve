# H02

Replacing the two per-queue resident indexes in cycle/0005/v002 with one id-keyed map that also stores the queue tag shrinks the `S3Fifo` container below 320 bytes while keeping get p50 near 45 ns and hit rate at 0.7195.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Start from the id-indexed lookup in cycle/0005/v002, which keeps two `HashMap` indexes (one for `small`, one for `main`) and two head counters, with position computed as `index = head - id`. Replace the two `HashMap` indexes with a single `HashMap<u64, Slot>` keyed by id, where `Slot` records the queue (`small` or `main`) and the stored position needed for `index = head - id`. Keep one head counter per queue, since the position formula depends on it. Update the insert in the push path for both queues, the removal in the eviction path (`evict_one()` and the main-queue rotation), and the promotion move from `small` to `main`, so each key is inserted and removed from the single map exactly when it enters or leaves a queue. The get path remains one hash lookup followed by one indexed access. Do not change eviction, ghost, or promotion decisions, so hit rate should match the parent. Keep `Entry` unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0005/v002 showed that the index structure is the main metadata cost in the container, and its insight attributes the cost to the extra `HashMap` and head counters. Collapsing the two maps into one tests whether the get and put speedup survives a smaller footprint.
