# H03

Growing the small queue to 20% of cache capacity from baseline, with no victim-choice change, raises hit rate on workload-a at 2048 without the put-path or metadata cost of the ghost variants.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Start from the baseline eviction path. Change the small-queue share constant (the value that splits capacity between `small` and `main`) from the baseline value to 20% of total capacity, so the small queue holds one fifth of entries and the main queue holds the rest. Leave the second-chance rotation budget in the main queue, the lowest-frequency victim selection, the promotion threshold (`freq > 0` stays as baseline), and the ghost structure unchanged. Do not add the post-budget victim choice that was stacked in cycle/0002 and cycle/0003, since that stack lost hit rate on the trace tested. Keep `Entry` and the container layout as in baseline, so the metadata footprint should match baseline's 24-byte `Entry` and its container size.

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0002 reported a small hit-rate gain from a larger small-queue share, but the change was bundled with a victim choice that cycle/0003 later showed does not compose with it. The open question from cycles 0002 and 0003 is whether the share increase helps on its own. This isolates that single variable from baseline so the result is attributable.
