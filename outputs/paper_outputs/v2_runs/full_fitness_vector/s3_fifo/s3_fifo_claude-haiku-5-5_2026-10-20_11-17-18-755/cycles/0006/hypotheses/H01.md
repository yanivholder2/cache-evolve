# H01

Capping the fingerprint ghost at half the main-queue capacity on top of cycle/0005/v003 raises hit rate above 0.7195 while keeping v003's put-path gain and 16-byte `Entry`.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

Start from the fingerprint ghost in cycle/0005/v003 (ghost held as 64-bit fingerprints in a `VecDeque` plus `ghost_set` and `ghost_copies`). Add the half-main ghost bound that cycle/0005/v001 introduced as `ghost_cap: usize`, set to half the main-queue capacity. In the ghost-push path (the code that runs when a small-queue victim is pushed as `fingerprint(&tail.key)`), after pushing, drain from the front while the live ghost population exceeds `ghost_cap`. For each drained fingerprint, remove it from `ghost_set` and decrement or remove its entry in `ghost_copies` so the set and the copy count stay consistent with the queue. Keep the existing front-drain of stale copies before the push. Leave the ghost-hit lookup, the frequency side-array, and the promotion rules unchanged. Do not add any per-entry field, so `Entry` stays at 16 bytes. Record the whole `S3Fifo` footprint, including the new `ghost_cap` word, in the metadata measurement.

## Rationale (optional)

cycle/0005/v003 kept the put-side gain but its hit rate matched the parent. cycle/0005/v001 showed that a half-main ghost cap gave a small hit-rate gain on its own, and the open questions ask whether that gain survives once the ghost is stored as fingerprints. This tests the combination directly.
