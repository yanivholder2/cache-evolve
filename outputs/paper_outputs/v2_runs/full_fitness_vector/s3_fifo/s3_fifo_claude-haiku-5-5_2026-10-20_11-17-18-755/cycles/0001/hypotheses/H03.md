# H03

Capping the main-queue second-chance scan at a fixed number of steps bounds the worst-case put latency of the S3-FIFO main eviction with negligible hit-rate loss.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, rewrite `S3Fifo::evict_main()` to take at most `MAX_EVICT_SCAN` second-chance steps, with `const MAX_EVICT_SCAN: usize = 8;` added next to `MAX_FREQ`. Keep the loop shape: pop the back entry; if its `freq > 0`, decrement it and push it to the front, counting the step; if its `freq == 0`, drop it and return. Once the step count reaches `MAX_EVICT_SCAN` and the current tail still has `freq > 0`, pop that tail and drop it unconditionally so exactly one entry is removed per call. Handle an empty main queue by returning without panicking (the existing `while let` on `pop_back()` already does this). Leave `evict_small()`, `put()`, `get()`, the ghost handling, and the promotion threshold (`freq > 1`) unchanged. Do not edit `tests/` or the JNI file.

## Rationale (optional)

In the baseline, `evict_main()` is unbounded: when many resident entries have `freq > 0`, a single put can rotate most of the main queue before it evicts, which shows up in the p99 `put` latency (4150 ns in the baseline fitness vector). Bounding the scan trades a small amount of second-chance precision for a fixed worst-case cost. This targets per-op-time, with hit rate expected to move only slightly.
