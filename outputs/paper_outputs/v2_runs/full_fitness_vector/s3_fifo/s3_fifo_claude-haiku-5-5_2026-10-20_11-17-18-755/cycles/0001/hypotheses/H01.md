# H01

Lowering the small-to-main promotion threshold to any reuse (freq >= 1) raises hit rate on workload-a at capacity 2048 by admitting single-reuse keys into the main queue sooner.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change the promotion test in `S3Fifo::evict_small()` from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 0`, so an entry from the small queue is promoted to main after one `get` hit instead of two. Leave `get()` (saturating increment capped at `MAX_FREQ`), `evict_main()` (second-chance decrement), `put()` ghost-hit re-admission to main, and the small/main/ghost sizing in `try_new()` unchanged. The ghost push for freq-0 entries stays as is. Keep the existing `small_eviction_promotes_to_main_after_multiple_hits` and `main_eviction_uses_frequency_second_chance` tests compiling and passing; do not edit anything under `tests/`.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline needs two hits in the small queue before promotion, so a key reused exactly once is dropped to the ghost queue and must be re-seen to re-enter. Promoting on one reuse is a cheap, single-line change to the bucket-movement policy and isolates the effect of the promotion threshold alone.
