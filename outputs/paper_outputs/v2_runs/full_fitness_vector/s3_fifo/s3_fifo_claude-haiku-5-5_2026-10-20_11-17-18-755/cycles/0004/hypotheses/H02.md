# H02

Storing 64-bit key fingerprints in the ghost queue instead of full keys, on top of the 20% small-queue share, should reduce put median and tail latency by removing per-eviction key clones while hit rate stays at 0.7195 on workload-a at capacity 2048, targeting per-op-time.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Start from cycle/0002/v003 and port the fingerprint ghost from cycle/0003/v003 onto it. Change the ghost queue to hold `u64` fingerprints of evicted keys, computed once with the cache's existing hasher, rather than cloned `K` values. Ghost membership is a hash set of fingerprints, so the membership test stays O(1). On overflow, pop the oldest fingerprint from the ghost queue. The ghost capacity stays as v003 sizes it, not as the cycle 0004 H01 variant would size it.

Keep the 20% small-queue share, the main-queue second-chance budget, the `freq > 1` promotion condition in `evict_small()`, and `get()` exactly as v003 has them. Do not add a tombstone or sequence tag. Keep `Entry` at 24 bytes.

Report hit rate before and after the fingerprint change. A ghost hit on a fingerprint collision is accepted as a ghost hit, so any hit-rate drift is the collision effect and should be recorded as such. Avoid the linear `retain` over the ghost queue on ghost hits that cycle 0003/v003 used: locate the fingerprint in a position-free way (for example, leave the stale fingerprint in the queue and remove it lazily on overflow, using the hash set's count) so a ghost hit does not scan the queue.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003/v003 was the only variant with an attributable put-path gain, and it came from dropping key clones on the put miss path, not from hit rate. Its cost was the linear ghost-hit scan and the heavier bookkeeping, which the 20% share does not change. Porting the fingerprint onto v003 tests whether the put-path win composes with the hit-rate winner. The lazy removal targets the cycle 0003 tail-latency risk directly.
