# H01

Sizing the ghost queue to the full cache capacity instead of the main queue should raise hit rate above 0.7195 on workload-a at capacity 2048, because more evicted keys stay remembered so that re-references are admitted straight to main, targeting hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Start from cycle/0002/v003 and keep its 20% small-queue share and its freq thresholds unchanged. In the constructor where the ghost queue capacity is set, change the ghost capacity from the main-queue size to the total cache capacity (the full `capacity` value passed to the constructor). Only the ghost capacity constant moves; the small queue, main queue, `evict_small()`, `evict_one()`, the promotion condition, and `get()` stay as v003 has them. Ghost overflow still drops the oldest key, and a ghost hit still routes the key to main as v003 does.

Do not add any new fields and do not change `Entry` or `S3Fifo` layout, so metadata-size stays at 24 and the struct stays at its v003 size. Note that ghost keys are not part of the `Entry` size measured by metadata-size, so the extra ghost memory is a cost the fitness vector will not show; the rationale below accepts that.

Invariants: total resident entries never exceed capacity; ghost entries are never counted as resident; eviction order within each queue is unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The ghost queue has never been varied. Cycles 0001 to 0003 changed its membership index, its key storage, and its sequence tagging, but kept its capacity tied to the main queue. With the small queue now at 20%, a main-sized ghost forgets keys sooner than the larger cache can use. A full-capacity ghost is the natural next dial, and it touches a different branch from the partition constant that v003 changed.
