# H03

Lowering the saturating frequency ceiling from 3 to 2 in `cycle/0007/v001` should raise hit rate above 0.7195 on workload-a at 2048 by shortening second-chance rotations in the main queue, with metadata-size and get-path structure unchanged.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

This changes only the ceiling of the `freq` counter. It is the policy knob that `cycle/0007/v001` moved into the index record, so the change stays local to that record.

- In `get()`, the saturating bump on a resident's `freq` caps at 2 instead of 3. Keep the bump as a single saturating add so the get path stays one index lookup.
- In `evict_small()`, keep the promotion rule as it is: a tail entry with `freq > 1` is promoted to main, and otherwise its fingerprint goes to the ghost. A key therefore still needs two hits to be promoted.
- In `evict_main()`, the second-chance loop still decrements `freq` on each rotation and breaks on the first entry with `freq == 0`. With a ceiling of 2, a hot entry takes at most two rotations to reach zero, not three.
- Keep the ghost, the small/main sizing, the index layout, and `Entry` exactly as `cycle/0007/v001` has them.
- Keep the `check_indexes` test helper working.

Expected result: hit rate above 0.7195, and per-op-time within noise of the parent's 41 ns p50 (no new work on the hit path). If hit rate falls instead, record the direction, since that would show the three-level ceiling is the one that matters. Metadata-size should stay at 16.
