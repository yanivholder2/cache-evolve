# H02

Expanding the second-chance scan to cover the full bucket depth (`scan_depth = len`) instead of a fixed 8-position cap will improve hit rate by finding and evicting never-accessed items that have drifted past the 8-position boundary toward the hot zone through passive displacement during other items' promotion swaps.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

In `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`, `ClassicLRU::eviction_candidate_index`, change the single line:

```rust
let scan_depth = len.min(8);
```

to:

```rust
let scan_depth = len;
```

The loop, fallback, and everything else in the method remain identical. The effect is that the scan inspects every position in the bucket on each eviction call, not just the coldest 8.

Also include `src/shared/item.rs` in the overlay, carrying it forward verbatim from the `cycle/0006/v003` overlay (32-byte `#[repr(C)]` struct: `key`, `value`, `lfu_counter: usize`, `_pad: u64`). Do not modify it.

No changes to `get_from_position`, `promotion_candidate_index`, the trait, or any other file.

## Rationale

In the current 8-slot scan, the loop iterates `(len - 8..len).rev()` — covering only the 8 coldest positions. Items with `lfu_counter == 0` that have been passively displaced toward the hot zone (positions 0 through `len - 9`) by repeated 3-swap promotions of their neighbours are invisible to the scan and can only be removed as the positional fallback. Expanding to `scan_depth = len` exposes those stale-cold items to second-chance eviction for the first time. On workloads where bucket depth exceeds 8, this directly increases eviction candidate quality; on 8-deep buckets it is a provably safe no-op, making the test low-risk. The trade-off is increased scan work per eviction (and thus higher put overhead), which is the mirror image of H01 — together the two bracket the optimal scan depth.
