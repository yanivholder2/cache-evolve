# H02

Implement segment-based LRU eviction within 64×32 buckets to bound scan depth and reduce p99 tail latency.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Refactor each 64-item bucket into 8 segments of 8 items each, maintaining an independent LRU order within each segment. In the eviction path, select the segment containing the oldest-accessed item (using LRU timestamps aggregated per segment), then evict the least-recently-used item within that segment. This bounds the maximum scan depth during eviction to 8 items instead of 64, reducing worst-case latency outliers. Keep the overall bucket geometry: 64 buckets with ~64 items total per bucket. Preserve the per-hit update logic and LRU semantics, but localize them within segments.
