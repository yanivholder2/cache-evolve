# H03

Reduce characterization phase overhead by terminating early when access patterns stabilize (256 operations) and skipping timestamp comparisons for older items to preserve hit-rate gain at lower latency cost.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

Add a `stability_count: u32` field to `AssociativeCache` to track consecutive operations without hit-rate change during characterization. Modify the characterization termination logic: instead of fixed CHARACTERIZATION_PHASE_THRESHOLD = 512, compute an early-exit condition as `if hot_phase_count >= 256 AND (stability_count >= 32 OR hot_phase_count >= 512)`, then transition to normal phase. In `get_and_update_item()`, optimize the timestamp comparison loop to skip items older than 50% of the recent_item_insertions window (i.e., only compare timestamps for the most recent half of inserted items), reducing comparison count by ~50%. Concretely, wrap the age check with `if recent_item_insertions.len() > RECENT_WINDOW_SIZE / 2 { ... only compare if in top 50% by insertion time ... }`. This reduces both the duration of expensive per-operation tracking (terminates as early as 256 ops if stability is detected) and the per-operation cost (fewer timestamp comparisons), directly targeting cycle 0009's finding that continuous, comprehensive tracking is the latency bottleneck.

## Rationale

Cycle 0009 v003 showed that 8.1% hit-rate improvement is achievable through characterization, but tracking overhead over 512 operations accumulates into 2–3× latency degradation. The hypothesis proposes two independent latency reductions: (1) shorter characterization window (terminate at 256 ops if patterns stabilize) reduces total tracking duration by up to 50%, (2) selective timestamp comparison (only recent 50% of items) reduces per-operation overhead by proportional amount. Unlike cycle 0009 v001's amortization (which failed by consolidating spikes), this approach reduces tracking frequency uniformly and terminates early if patterns are detected quickly. Testing whether access patterns stabilize within 256 operations will reveal whether full 512-operation characterization is necessary, or whether half-cost characterization captures sufficient temporal-locality information.
