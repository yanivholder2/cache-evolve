# H03

Extend ultra-wide bucket geometry (16×128) with early-exit and cache-line alignment to push hit-rate frontier.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Modify the geometry layout in `BaselineGeometry::layout()` to divide the bucket count by 8 instead of 4, yielding 16 buckets with 128 items per bucket on a 2048-entry cache. Maintain the AssociativeCacheBucket's 64-byte cache-line alignment (`#[repr(align(64))]`) and early-exit optimization in `get_position()` from the parent. Keep ClassicLRU eviction policy unchanged.

Cycle 0004's v003 proved that wide-bucket geometry (32×64) becomes viable when micro-optimized, achieving both hit-rate gain (+0.003 relative to ClassicLRU) and latency control through implementation cleverness. This hypothesis tests whether the early-exit and cache-line-alignment strategy generalizes to even more extreme bucket widths (16×128). If latency remains controlled via early-exit, the larger buckets may increase hit-rate further by improving cache density. This directly addresses the open question: how wide can we scale while maintaining the latency benefit through micro-optimization?
