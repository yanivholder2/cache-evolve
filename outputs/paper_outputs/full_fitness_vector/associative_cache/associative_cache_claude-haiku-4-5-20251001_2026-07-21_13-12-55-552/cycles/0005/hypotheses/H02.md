# H02

Apply early-exit and cache-line alignment to 64×32 geometry to salvage hit-rate gain while controlling latency regression.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Modify the AssociativeCacheBucket struct to align to 64-byte cache-line boundaries by adding `#[repr(align(64))]`. Implement early-exit optimization in the `get_position()` method: return immediately upon finding the key, avoiding unnecessary memory accesses for the remaining bucket items. Preserve the 64×32 bucket geometry from the parent and keep ClassicLRU eviction policy unchanged.

Cycle 0002's v002 achieved a promising hit-rate gain (+0.24%) on the wider 64×32 geometry but suffered a 45% latency regression. Cycle 0004's v003 demonstrated that the same geometric advantage can be realized without latency penalty through careful implementation. This hypothesis applies v003's proven latency-reduction techniques to the narrower-but-still-wider 64×32 geometry, testing whether micro-optimization generalizes and whether we can capture the hit-rate gain while restoring latency to acceptable levels.
