# H01

Switch to LFU eviction policy to increase hit-rate by preserving frequently-accessed items in Zipfian workloads instead of relying solely on recency.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_settings.rs` in the `Default` implementation: change the `eviction_policy` field from `EvictionPolicyKind::ClassicLRU` to `EvictionPolicyKind::Lfu`. This shifts the eviction strategy from moving items to the back on access (recency-based) to incrementing a frequency counter on each access (frequency-based).

The Lfu policy is already implemented in `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` and evicts the item with the lowest `lfu_counter` when the bucket is full. Items that are accessed frequently will accumulate higher counters and persist longer in the cache, which should better match the access pattern of Zipfian workloads where a small set of items account for the majority of requests.

Keep all other settings unchanged: bucket geometry (128 buckets × 16 items), bucket layout logic, and the Bucket trait implementation.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline achieves 0.6611 hit-rate under a Zipfian workload. ClassicLRU assumes "recently accessed = most important," but Zipfian traces exhibit items with vastly different popularity levels. LFU directly optimizes for frequency, which better matches the underlying access distribution. An item accessed 10 times is more valuable to retain than an item accessed once, even if the once-accessed item was recent.
