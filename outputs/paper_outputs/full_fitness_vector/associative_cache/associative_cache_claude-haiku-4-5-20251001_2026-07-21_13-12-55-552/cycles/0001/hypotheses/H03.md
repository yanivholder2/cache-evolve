# H03

Switch to Swap eviction policy to increase hit-rate by maintaining efficient recency positioning through low-cost adjacent swaps rather than expensive list manipulation.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_settings.rs` in the `Default` implementation: change the `eviction_policy` field from `EvictionPolicyKind::ClassicLRU` to `EvictionPolicyKind::Swap`.

The Swap policy is already implemented in `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`. On each cache access, Swap swaps the accessed item with its predecessor (moving it one position closer to the front of the bucket). On eviction, it removes the front item (position 0). This creates a "bubble-up" mechanism where frequently-accessed items gradually migrate toward the front and become less likely to be evicted.

Unlike ClassicLRU, which removes an item from an arbitrary position and pushes it to the back (O(n) operation), Swap performs a constant-time swap. This reduces CPU overhead per operation, which should allow the eviction policy to be responsive without impacting other cache operations.

Keep all other settings unchanged: bucket geometry (128 buckets × 16 items), geometry logic, and the Bucket trait implementation.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline's ClassicLRU achieves 0.6611 hit-rate but incurs O(n) cost per access due to item removal and re-insertion at the back. Swap provides an alternative recency-based strategy that bubbles frequently-accessed items forward at O(1) cost per access. The efficiency gain could preserve more cache resources and allow for faster response to access patterns.

Additionally, Swap's gradual positioning (one swap per access) creates a gentler gradient than ClassicLRU's binary "back = hot" placement, potentially better matching Zipfian workloads where items have varying degrees of popularity. Items with moderate frequency gently rise, while the rarely-accessed items (at the front) are quickly evicted when new items arrive.
