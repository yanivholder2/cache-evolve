# H02

Decouple first-touch from promotion by gating move-to-back behind the reuse bit, making the bit non-redundant with position and pushing hit-rate past the 0.7267 incumbent.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

v001 carries a per-bucket `reuse: Vec<bool>` parked off `Item` and evicts the
first LRU-front slot whose reuse bit is `false`. Cycle 4 showed that once a
hit also moves the item to the back, the bit re-encodes what position already
says. Break that redundancy by making the bit a promotion *gate* rather than a
duplicate of position:

- In `get_from_position`, on a hit read the item's parallel `reuse` entry:
  - If `reuse == false`: set it to `true` and leave the item **in place**
    (return `position` unchanged). The item stays in the probationary region,
    having earned only its first chance.
  - If `reuse == true`: relocate the item to the protected back. Move the item
    and its `reuse` entry together using an **index-preserving swap sequence**
    (rotate the single element to the tail and rotate the matching bool the
    same way) — never a key-matched rebuild, which cycle 4 confirmed is
    quadratic and blows out the p99 tails.
- Keep front admission (insert at index 0 with `reuse = false`) and the
  existing reuse-aware eviction candidate selection.

This gives a genuine two-hit probation: one touch protects from eviction but
not from staying probationary; a second touch is required to reach the
protected zone. The bit now encodes hit-count-1, information position alone
cannot carry under a single-step layout.

Invariants: `Item` stays key+value (16-byte floor); `reuse` stays on the
bucket struct; every relocation keeps `items[i]` and `reuse[i]` aligned; per
hit stays O(bucket_size) at worst with no per-access rebuild.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 4 proved the reuse bit and position are the same lever *when both fire
on the first hit*. Firing them on different hits (bit on the first, position
on the second) turns two redundant signals into a two-tier frequency filter
without adding any measured state.
