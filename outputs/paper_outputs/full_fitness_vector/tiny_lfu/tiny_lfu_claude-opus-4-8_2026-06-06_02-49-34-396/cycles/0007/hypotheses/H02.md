# H02

Shrinking the eviction victim sample from 5 to 3 trims the put-path forward walk and its p99 tail, lowering per-op-time at near-zero hit-rate cost.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `SAMPLE_VICTIMS` from `5` to
`3`. This is the only edit. In `TinyLfuCache::put`, the eviction path
walks forward from `head` along `next` collecting up to `SAMPLE_VICTIMS`
candidates and calls `self.sketch.estimate` on each to pick the
lowest-frequency victim; fewer candidates means a shorter walk and fewer
`estimate` calls per eviction, directly cutting the put p99 tail the
cross-cycle log flags as the persistent soft spot. Keep the
strictly-`<` victim-replacement comparison, the head-first walk order
(so recency still breaks ties toward older candidates), the
`incoming_freq > victim_freq` admission test, and all sketch geometry
unchanged. The premise (from cycle/0005/v003's finding that deepening the
walk to 10 bought only marginal hit-rate at real p99 cost): eviction
quality is near-saturated and only weakly sample-depth-bound on this
trace, so reducing depth below 5 should shed put-path latency while the
sketch estimate — the actual binding constraint — keeps hit-rate
essentially flat. Preserve the `debug_assert!(self.head != NIL)` guard.
