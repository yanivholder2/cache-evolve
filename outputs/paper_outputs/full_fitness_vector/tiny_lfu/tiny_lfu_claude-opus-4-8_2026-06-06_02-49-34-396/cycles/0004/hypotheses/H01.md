# H01

Fold v002's u32-sentinel node layout onto v003 to cut metadata-size from 48 to 24 bytes while preserving v003's 0.7369 hit-rate and ~43ns per-op.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

Port the compact node encoding from cycle/0003/v002 into v003's source. v003
currently uses `prev: Option<usize>` / `next: Option<usize>` in `Node<K,V>`
(48-byte node) together with the fast hasher, u8 counters, double-hashing, and
sampled-coldest eviction. Replace the link representation with v002's scheme:

- Change `Node.prev` / `Node.next` from `Option<usize>` to `u32`, and the
  cache's `head` / `tail` fields from `Option<usize>` to `u32`.
- Add `const NIL: u32 = u32::MAX;` as the "no link" sentinel and initialise
  `head`/`tail` to `NIL` in `try_new`, and new nodes' `prev`/`next` to `NIL`.
- Rewrite `unlink` and `push_back` to use `!= NIL` branch tests and
  `as usize` indexing exactly as v002 does.
- Update the sampled-coldest eviction walk in `put`: the cursor that follows
  `next` links is currently `Option<usize>`; convert it to walk `u32` links,
  stopping when the link equals `NIL`. Preserve the sampling semantics
  unchanged — still collect up to `SAMPLE_VICTIMS` candidates from the LRU head
  and keep the strictly-lower-estimate tie-break.

Leave the FastHasher, u8 CountMinSketch, double-hashing `position_for`, and the
`incoming_freq > victim_freq` admission test untouched. This is a pure layout
composition of two proven winners and should be regression-free on hit-rate and
per-op-time while halving the per-entry node footprint.
