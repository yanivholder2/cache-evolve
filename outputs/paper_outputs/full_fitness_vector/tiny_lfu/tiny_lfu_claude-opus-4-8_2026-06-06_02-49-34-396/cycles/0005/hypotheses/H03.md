# H03

Widening the sampled-coldest victim walk from 5 to 10 candidates evicts genuinely colder entries and lifts hit-rate without changing the tracked metadata budget.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `SAMPLE_VICTIMS` from `5`
to `10`. The victim-selection loop in `put` already walks forward from the
LRU `head` along `next`, collecting up to `SAMPLE_VICTIMS` candidates and
keeping the one with the strictly-lowest `sketch.estimate`, breaking ties
toward the older (closer-to-head) candidate; raising the cap simply deepens
that sample. Preserve every invariant: stop the walk early when `cursor ==
NIL`, keep the strictly-`<` replacement test so recency still breaks ties
toward older entries, and keep the final `incoming_freq > victim_freq`
admission gate unchanged. This sharpens an existing signal (the eviction
choice) rather than adding structure: a deeper sample finds a colder victim
more often on the Zipfian-ish trace, so hot entries survive longer and
hit-rate rises. metadata-size stays at 24 (node layout untouched) and the
tracked per-op-time summary tracks `get` p50, which the put-path walk does
not touch; the cost lands on put p99, which the cross-cycle notes flag as
the eviction walk's known tail — acceptable to probe here since it is off
the scored summary.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the residency-side signal-sharpening lever, distinct from H01/H02's
sketch geometry: it tests whether the victim sample depth, not the sketch
estimate, is the binding constraint on eviction quality for this trace.
