# H03

Replacing the second seeded-hash call in cycle/0004/v001 with a three-step bijective remix chain will preserve its hit-rate advantage over single-hash variants while eliminating one SipHash invocation from every sketch operation.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Add the `remix` function (from cycle/0005/v002) immediately after `seeded_hash`:

```rust
#[inline(always)]
fn remix(h: u64) -> u64 {
    h.wrapping_mul(0x9e3779b97f4a7c15_u64)
        .rotate_right(32)
        ^ h
}
```

In `CountMinSketch::increment` and `CountMinSketch::estimate`, replace the two-call computation (`h0 = seeded_hash(key, 0)`, `h1 = seeded_hash(key, 1)`) and the existing four 16-bit-window positions with a four-stage remix chain where each position takes the low bits of a successive full-width bijective transformation:

```rust
// Replace this (parent):
let h0 = seeded_hash(key, 0);
let h1 = seeded_hash(key, 1);
let mask = self.width - 1;
let pos0 = (h0 as usize) & mask;
let pos1 = ((h0 >> 16) as usize) & mask;
let pos2 = (h1 as usize) & mask;
let pos3 = ((h1 >> 16) as usize) & mask;

// With this:
let h0 = seeded_hash(key, 0);
let h1 = remix(h0);
let h2 = remix(h1);
let h3 = remix(h2);
let mask = self.width - 1;
let pos0 = (h0 as usize) & mask;
let pos1 = (h1 as usize) & mask;
let pos2 = (h2 as usize) & mask;
let pos3 = (h3 as usize) & mask;
```

Remove the `seeded_hash(key, 1)` call. All cache policy logic, the `TinyLfuCache` struct, and `seeded_hash` remain unchanged from cycle/0004/v001.

## Rationale

cycle/0004/v001's two-seeded-hash approach achieved the highest hit rate of any incumbent (0.7372) by providing genuine pairwise independence between row pairs. cycle/0005/v002 showed that a single Fibonacci multiply-rotate-XOR remix is sufficient for row-pair independence at the cost of intra-pair correlation (pos0 and pos1 share all but 16 bits of h0). The three-step chain gives each of the four row positions a distinct full-width hash value — each derived from a complete bijective transformation of the previous — providing better inter-row independence than the 2×16-bit-window approach while costing three ALU instructions rather than a second SipHash invocation. The goal is to match or exceed cycle/0004/v001's hit rate at lower hash-computation cost.
