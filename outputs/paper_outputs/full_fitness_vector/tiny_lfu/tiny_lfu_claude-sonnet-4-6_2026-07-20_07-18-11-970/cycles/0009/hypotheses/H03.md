# H03

Increasing the protected-segment capacity from 20% to 30% of total cache capacity in `cycle/0008/v002` will improve hit rate by shielding a larger fraction of the hot Zipfian working set from the eviction victim pool.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v002

## Changes

In `try_new`, change the single line that initialises `prot_capacity` from:

```rust
prot_capacity: (capacity / 5).max(1),
```

to:

```rust
prot_capacity: (capacity * 3 / 10).max(1),
```

At `capacity = 2048` this yields a protected zone of 614 slots (≈30%) instead of 409 (≈20%). The integer arithmetic `capacity * 3 / 10` uses truncating division, which is correct for this purpose.

Every other line of `cycle/0008/v002` — including `prot_flags: vec![false; capacity]`, the chain-management helpers (`prot_unlink`, `prot_push_back`), the promotion/demotion logic in `get` and `put`, the `Vec<bool>` flag storage, and the admission gate comparison (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`) — remains byte-for-byte identical to the parent.

This isolates the ratio variable: if hit rate improves, the protected zone was undersized at 20%; if hit rate regresses or the probationary victim pool becomes too shallow, 30% is too large and the optimal fraction lies below 30%.
