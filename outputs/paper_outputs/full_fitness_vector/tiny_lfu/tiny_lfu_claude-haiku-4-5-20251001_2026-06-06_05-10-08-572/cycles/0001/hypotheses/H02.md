# H02

Reduce sketch depth to halve hash operations per get/put, lowering per-op latency.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Replace `SKETCH_DEPTH` (currently 4) with 2 in src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs line 8.
This reduces the number of hash rows in the count-min sketch from 4 to 2. Each increment()
call in get() and put() will compute 2 hash positions instead of 4, and each estimate()
call will read 2 counters instead of 4, reducing CPU cycles per operation. Frequency
estimates will be less accurate (fewer independent hash functions = higher collision
probability), but the tradeoff is measurable latency improvement. Invariants remain
satisfied as long as increment() and estimate() stay in sync.
