# H02

Introduce frequency-aware item ordering within LRU eviction to improve hit-rate by deprioritizing cold items during eviction candidate selection.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` and related bucket eviction logic (if present in overlayable files) to track a frequency counter for each item alongside its LRU timestamp. When evicting from a bucket, replace pure LRU ordering with a composite score: items with both low recency (LRU position) and low frequency (access count) are preferred for eviction first. Implement frequency counter increments in the `get` path (via existing promotion logic) and use this counter as a secondary sort key during eviction. This decouples eviction policy from promotion selectivity, allowing independent optimization of what gets evicted versus what gets promoted.

## Rationale

Cycles 0001–0008 have extensively tuned bucket geometry and promotion thresholds, but the LRU eviction policy has remained constant. The cross-cycle insights note that "LRU eviction policy remains constant across all cycles. No variants tested alternative eviction strategies (LFU, frequency-biased, adaptive recency-vs-frequency)." This hypothesis targets a new optimization axis: within the tier cascade during demotion, frequency information can guide eviction order, prioritizing hot items for retention even within the same recency band. Zipfian workloads with tail access patterns benefit from frequency-aware retention; this change preserves the existing tier structure while refining eviction selectivity within each bucket without modifying promotion logic or threshold constants.
