# H01

Refine bucket geometry to 79/21 allocation within the identified optimal band to fine-tune latency while preserving hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/geometry.rs` in the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` method to compute an adjusted geometry: rebalance the normal-to-stash ratio from 80/20 to 79/21 while keeping the divisor 2 promotion gating from v001. This targets the frontier between 80/20 and the saturation boundary at 85/15, testing whether a single-point narrower geometry can improve v001's per-op-time (81 ns) while maintaining or improving its hit-rate (0.7166). The change should allocate stash buckets as `(settings.num_of_stash_buckets * 21) / 100` and normal buckets as the complement, preserving total capacity.

## Rationale

v001 holds the cross-cycle hit-rate lead at 0.7166 but carries latency cost (81 ns). Cycle 0006–0008 insights show the optimal geometry lies within 75/25–80/20, with 80/20 as a clean midpoint. This hypothesis tests whether granular movement within that band (79/21 shifts stash capacity from 20% toward 21%, favoring normal buckets by 1 percentage point) can tune the eviction pressure and improve latency without triggering the hit-rate cascades observed at 85/15+ in cycle 0005. The change is mechanically isolated to geometry initialization, orthogonal to promotion selectivity, and testable against v001's established baseline.
