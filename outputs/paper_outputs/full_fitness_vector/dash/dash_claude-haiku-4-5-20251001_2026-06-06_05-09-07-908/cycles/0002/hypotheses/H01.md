# H01

Combining back-insert promotion with geometry rebalancing amplifies both hit-rate and latency gains.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Apply both v001's back-insert promotion strategy and v002's geometry rebalancing together:

1. In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, line 68: change the `get()` method to pass `InsertPosition::Back` to `promotion_get()` (already done in v001 at lines 157, 170).

2. Keep `src/shared/geometry.rs` at the v002 configuration: 16 normal + 16 stash buckets (50/50 split) instead of baseline's 8/24 split.

The combination layers back-insert's benefit (keeping promoted items at MRU end longer) with geometry's benefit (reducing stash misses via wider indexed tier). Both changes operate independently: promotion timing is orthogonal to bucket allocation. Expected outcome: higher hit-rate from geometry + lower latency from back-insert, without diminishing returns.

## Rationale

Cycle 0001's insight notes the three winners are orthogonal with no apparent conflicts. This hypothesis tests whether v001 and v002 compose beneficially. v002 achieved the highest hit-rate (0.6965) but with slightly higher per-op-time (120 ns). v001 achieved the best per-op-time (104 ns). Combining them should yield the hit-rate strength of v002 + the latency strength of v001 without trade-offs, since back-insert reduces demotion cascades (v001's mechanism) while wider normal buckets reduce stash traversals (v002's mechanism).
