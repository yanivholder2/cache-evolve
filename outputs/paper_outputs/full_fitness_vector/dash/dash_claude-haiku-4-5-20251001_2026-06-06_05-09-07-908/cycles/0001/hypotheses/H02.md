# H02

Reshape cache geometry to allocate more normal buckets and reduce stash size, raising hit-rate by improving hash distribution in the primary tier.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/shared/geometry.rs`, modify the `BaselineGeometry::for_settings()` function (lines 23–32) to return a `DashGeometry` with reallocated capacity:

- `num_of_normal_buckets: 16` (increased from 8)
- `num_of_stash_buckets: 16` (decreased from 24)  
- `bucket_size: 64` (unchanged)
- `num_of_segments: settings.num_of_segments` (unchanged)

Total capacity stays constant: (16 + 16) × 64 = (8 + 24) × 64 = 2048 slots. This shift allocates more of the fixed budget to the hash-indexed normal tier and less to the unindexed stash, improving the proportion of cold misses that land in their target/probing bucket vs. falling through to the stash. Keep the `for_settings()` signature and the `BucketGeometry` trait implementation unchanged.

## Rationale

The baseline allocates 75% of capacity to the stash (24 of 32 buckets), which means most initially cold items miss their target and land in the stash. Widening the normal tier reduces cold misses by 2× (more hash buckets to absorb initial traffic) and raises the effective working-set capacity. The tradeoff is a smaller stash (accepting some demotions that can't retry), but the baseline's conservative demotion path already sends many items to the stash anyway.
