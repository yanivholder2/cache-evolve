# H02

Clear promotion heat at the target→stash demotion site so `put` can drop its per-insert side-table probe, lowering put-path latency.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, move the stale-heat reset off the
hot fresh-insert path and onto the only path that actually strands heat.

Today `put` unconditionally calls `core.target_heat.clear(&item.key)`
(the `TargetHeat` direct-mapped clear) on every non-resident insert, even
though a freshly-inserted key is normally cold. The reason the clear is
needed at all is that `demote_from_normal_bucket` moves a target resident
down to the stash **without** clearing its heat, so a later re-`put` of
that same key could otherwise inherit stale heat.

Make two coordinated edits:
1. In `demote_from_normal_bucket`, in the `true_target == source_idx`
   branch (the target→stash demotion) and in the cascade branch that
   pushes the overflow `cascade` item into the stash, call
   `core.target_heat.clear(&item.key)` / `clear(&cascade.key)` for the
   item being placed into the stash, immediately before the
   `put_at(Slot::Stash, ...)` call. This clears heat exactly at the
   moment a key leaves the target tier for the stash.
2. Remove the `core.target_heat.remove`/`clear` call from `put`, since
   no key can any longer reach the stash carrying live heat.

Net effect: the gate decisions are byte-for-byte identical (a key in the
stash always has heat 0 in both designs), but the side-table touch is
removed from the high-frequency `put` path and paid only on the rarer
demotion events. Keep the `debug_assert!(!core.contains_key(...))` in
`put`. Do not alter `promotion_get`, the heat bump, or `PROMOTE_AT`, so
hit-rate and metadata-size stay at their floors.

This is distinct from cycle/0007/v003, which relocated the per-put
`remove` wholesale to the *eviction* site and turned a cheap non-resident
lookup into a real resident remove behind new branches (regressing both
put metrics). Here the clear stays a cheap targeted O(1) array clear and
lands on the demotion path, not the eviction-victim path.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle-0007 insight pinned the live frontier on the put path. v003
proved naive relocation backfires; this version clears heat precisely
where it is stranded, removing work from `put` without manufacturing a
new resident remove.
