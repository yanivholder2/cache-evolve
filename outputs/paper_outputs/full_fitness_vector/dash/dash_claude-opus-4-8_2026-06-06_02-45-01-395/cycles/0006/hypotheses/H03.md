# H03

Eliminate the side-table hash entirely by storing promotion heat in a per-bucket `Vec<u8>` kept position-aligned with the bucket's items, improving locality while holding the 16-byte floor.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

This is the "structural change that removes indirection while preserving or
improving locality" the open questions ask for. Instead of a hashed
`target_heat: HashMap<K, u8>` probe on every target hit, carry the heat as a
parallel byte vector inside the bucket that already holds the item — so the heat
is read by index, lives in the same cache line neighborhood as the items, and
needs no hashing at all.

Concretely:

1. In `src/dash/dash_bucket.rs`, add a field `heat: Vec<u8>` to `DashBucket`,
   constructed with the same `with_capacity(max_size)` as `items` and kept
   strictly index-aligned with `items` through every mutation:
   - On `put_at` push/insert, push/insert a `0` at the matching index.
   - On `remove_at`/`remove` and on the eviction path, remove the same index.
   - In `get_from_position` (both the devirtualized and trait-object paths), when
     the ClassicLRU remove+push reorders an item, apply the identical remove+push
     to `heat` so alignment is preserved.
   Expose `fn heat_at(&self, pos: usize) -> u8` and
   `fn bump_heat_at(&mut self, pos: usize) -> u8` (saturating add) on the bucket
   (add them to the `Bucket` trait in `src/shared/traits/bucket.rs` with default
   bodies that operate on a slice accessor, or as concrete methods used via
   downcast — prefer trait methods with a `heat_slice_mut` accessor to keep the
   trait-object call site simple).
2. In `src/dash/segments/mod.rs`, delete the `target_heat` HashMap field and all
   `target_heat.entry/remove` calls. In `promotion_get`'s target branch, after
   `get_position(key)` yields `item_idx`, read/bump heat via the bucket at that
   index instead of the map. Promotion at `PROMOTE_AT` removes the item (which
   removes its aligned heat byte automatically). The "reset on re-insert"
   semantics now fall out for free: an evicted key loses its heat slot, and a
   re-inserted key gets a fresh `0`, so the explicit `remove` resets in `put` and
   on promotion are no longer needed — verify this reproduces the parent's gate
   decisions exactly on the existing segment unit tests.
3. Keep the gate threshold, MRU placement, and demotion cascade unchanged.

Invariants: `Item<u64,u64>` must stay 16 bytes (heat lives in the bucket
container, never in `Item`); hit-rate must stay 0.6794; the parallel-vector
alignment must hold under every reorder path (the existing
`target_hit_below_threshold_promotes_within_bucket`,
`target_hit_at_threshold_promotes_to_probing`, and
`reinserted_key_starts_with_cleared_heat` tests are the alignment oracle).

## Rationale (optional)

If alignment holds, this removes the hash probe that bounds v001's latency
entirely and replaces it with an in-bucket indexed read — a locality win rather
than added indirection — potentially beating v002's 81 ns p50 while staying at
the 16-byte metadata floor. Higher risk than H01/H02, but the largest upside.
