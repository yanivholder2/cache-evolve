# H02

Removing the `lfu_counter` field from `Item` entirely drops metadata-size below v003's 24 (toward 16 bytes for `Item<u64,u64>`) since the active `ClassicLRU` eval policy never reads it.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

v003 already removed `timestamp` and shrank `lfu_counter` to `u8`, landing
metadata-size at 24 — but the byte after the two `u64`s still pads the
struct out. The active eval policy is `ClassicLRU`, which is purely
positional and never touches `lfu_counter`; the field is dead weight under
the exercised config.

In `src/shared/item.rs`, delete the `lfu_counter` field, its initialiser
in `Item::new`, and its mention in the `Display` impl. With only
`key: K` and `value: V` left, `Item<u64,u64>` should pack to 16 bytes.

This requires `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` to stop referencing
`item.lfu_counter`: rewrite the `Lfu` policy's `get_from_position`,
`eviction_candidate_index`, and `promotion_candidate_index` to use a
positional fallback (mirror `ClassicLRU`'s implementations) so the file
compiles and `Lfu` still satisfies the `EvictionPolicy` trait. Keep the
`EvictionPolicyKind::Lfu` enum variant, its index/name, and the `build`
arm intact so the JNI ordinal mapping and gate tests are unaffected.

Preserve `Item`'s `PartialEq`/`Eq` (key+value comparison) and the
`#[cfg(test)]` expectations that don't read `lfu_counter`.

## Rationale (optional)

The cycle-0001 insight flagged that positional-only `ClassicLRU` makes any
frequency/recency metadata pure overhead; this pushes the metadata-size
axis as far as the struct allows without changing the exercised behaviour.
