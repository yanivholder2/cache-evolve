# H02

Replacing v001's positional promotion gate with a frequency threshold driven by the already-present-but-unused `u8 lfu_counter` will protect repeat-warm keys and raise hit-rate at zero extra metadata cost.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

v001's `Item<u64,u64>` still carries a `u8 lfu_counter` (it costs nothing
extra — the struct is 24 bytes either way due to alignment), but the
positional `ClassicLRU` eval policy never reads it. Put it to work as a
non-positional promotion signal, entirely inside
`promotion_get` (src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs):

1. In the target-tier branch, before deciding whether to promote, bump the
   resident's counter:
   `core.buckets[target_idx].get_items_mut()[item_idx].lfu_counter =
   core.buckets[target_idx].get_items()[item_idx].lfu_counter.saturating_add(1);`
2. Replace the `is_hottest == promotion_candidate_index()` test with a
   frequency gate: promote to probing when the resident's `lfu_counter`
   has reached a threshold `PROMOTE_AT` (start at `2`). Below threshold,
   fall through to the existing intra-bucket touch
   (`get_from_position(item_idx)`) so the key still climbs positionally.
3. When an item is moved out of target via `promote_to_probing`, reset its
   `lfu_counter` to `0` (clear it on the removed `data` item before the
   call) so the heat signal is per-tier-residency, not lifetime.

Keep v001's `InsertPosition::Back` placement, the slim `Item` layout, and
the stash/probing/demotion paths unchanged. Do not touch the
`EvictionPolicy` trait or `eviction_policy.rs` — the counter is read and
written only in the segment's `get`.

## Rationale

Cycle 0002's open question 2 asked for a gate *shape* that is
recency/frequency-weighted rather than positional. The `u8` counter is
free footprint under the current 24-byte floor, so spending it on a
"promote after N proven hits in target" rule tests whether genuine repeat
warmth beats positional luck at steering keys into the protected probing
tier — without regressing size or needing a wider scan.
