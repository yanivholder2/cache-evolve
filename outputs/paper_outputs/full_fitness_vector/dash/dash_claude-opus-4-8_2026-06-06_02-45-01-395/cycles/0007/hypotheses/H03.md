# H03

Drop the unconditional per-put `target_heat.remove` and clear heat at the cache-exit (stash-eviction) site instead, removing a guaranteed side-table probe from every put to lower put-path latency.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

Today `DashSegment::put` (`src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`) runs
`core.target_heat.remove(&item.key)` on **every** put — a guaranteed
side-table touch for the freshly-inserted (always non-resident) key,
needed only to clear *orphaned* heat left behind when a key with heat
was evicted from the cache entirely. Relocate that clear to the single
place a key actually leaves the cache: stash-bucket eviction.

Concretely:
- Remove the `core.target_heat.remove(&item.key)` line from
  `DashSegment::put`. Keep the `debug_assert!` and the
  `put_at(Slot::Stash, InsertPosition::Back, item)` call, but capture
  its returned `Option<Item>` and, if `Some(evicted)`, call
  `core.target_heat.remove(&evicted.key)`.
- In `demote_from_normal_bucket`, apply the same treatment to both stash
  inserts: the direct `put_at(Slot::Stash, ...)` and the cascade
  `put_at(Slot::Stash, cascade)` — clear heat for any item those inserts
  evict out of the cache.

This is bit-for-bit on gate decisions. Every terminal cache exit is a
stash eviction (target/probing evictions always cascade downward and the
final drop lands in stash), so clearing the evicted key's heat there
guarantees `target_heat` never holds an orphaned, non-resident key —
exactly the invariant the per-put `remove` maintained lazily. A heat
entry is only ever *read* for a target-resident key, and an evicted key
is non-resident until re-put, so eager-clear-at-eviction and the
parent's lazy-clear-at-reput produce identical reads. The bounce case
(target→stash demotion where the key stays resident in stash) is
untouched: we clear only the *evicted* key, never the demoted one, so
heat still carries across a target→stash→target bounce as before.

Net effect: puts that do not evict (cache still warming) do zero
side-table work; puts that evict do one remove for the *exit* key
instead of the *inserted* key — strictly ≤ the parent's one-remove-per-put.
hit-rate (0.6794) and metadata-size (16) must stay identical; only
put-path latency (p50/p99) should move. This also directly probes the
cycle-0006 open question of what bounds the put-p99 tail.

## Rationale (optional)

The put path is the least-explored latency surface and the only one the
side-table hasher and capacity reservation did not reach. Removing a
guaranteed per-put probe is a clean, hit-rate-neutral test of whether
that probe is a component of put latency.
