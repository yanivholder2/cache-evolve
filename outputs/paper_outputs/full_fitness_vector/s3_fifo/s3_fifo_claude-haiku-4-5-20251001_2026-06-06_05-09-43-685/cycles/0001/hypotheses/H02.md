# H02

Route ghost hits through small queue instead of direct main promotion to apply additional filtering and improve hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, modify the `put()` function to reintroduce ghost-hit keys into the small queue instead of directly promoting them to main queue. When `in_ghost` is true, instead of removing the key from ghost and pushing directly to main:
1. Remove the key from the ghost queue
2. Check if small queue is full and call `evict_small()` if needed
3. Push the entry to small queue with `freq = 0` (as if it were a new entry)

This gives re-admitted keys a second opportunity to prove their hotness in the small queue before graduating to main, reducing re-admission of keys that were evicted for good reason.

## Rationale

Current ghost hits jump directly to main queue with zero frequency, making them immediately vulnerable to the next main eviction. This creates noisy re-admissions that flood main queue with marginally-hot items. By routing ghost hits through small queue, they must re-establish hotness (freq > 1 or higher with H01) before main promotion. This reduces "flapping" of borderline items and improves main queue stability and overall hit rate.
