# H01

Increase small-queue promotion threshold to reduce false positives in main queue admission and improve hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, modify the `evict_small()` function to raise the frequency threshold for promoting items from small queue to main queue. Change the condition from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 2`. This ensures only items that have been accessed at least 3 times in the small queue (since freq starts at 0) graduate to the main queue, making the admission filter more selective and reducing eviction pressure on the main queue.

## Rationale

The current small-queue filter is relatively loose: items with frequency > 1 (meaning just 2 accesses) promote to main. This may admit false-positive hot items, flooding main queue and causing excessive evictions. Raising the threshold to freq > 2 creates a stricter filter, ensuring main queue capacity is reserved for items with stronger hotness signals. This trade-off between small-queue residence time and main-queue stability should improve hit rate on Zipfian workloads.
