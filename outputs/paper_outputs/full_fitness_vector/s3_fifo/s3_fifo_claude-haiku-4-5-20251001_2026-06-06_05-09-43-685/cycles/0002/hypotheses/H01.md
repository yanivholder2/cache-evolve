# H01

Raise v001's promotion threshold from freq > 2 to freq > 3 to further reduce main-queue population and improve latency on Zipfian workloads.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, modify the `evict_small()` function to increase the promotion threshold. Change the condition from `if tail.freq > 2` to `if tail.freq > 3`. This ensures only items that accumulate at least 4 accesses in the small queue (since freq starts at 0) graduate to the main queue, further tightening admission filtering and reducing main-queue contention.

## Rationale

v001 succeeds by using freq > 2 as a strict admission filter, achieving the best latency (138ns) while maintaining hit-rate. Further raising the threshold to freq > 3 eliminates more marginal items from main queue, potentially improving latency further by reducing eviction overhead. Trade-off: Some legitimately warm items with moderate frequency may be ghosted instead of promoted, slightly impacting hit-rate. This explores whether v001's tight filtering can be pushed further for latency gains on sparse working sets.
