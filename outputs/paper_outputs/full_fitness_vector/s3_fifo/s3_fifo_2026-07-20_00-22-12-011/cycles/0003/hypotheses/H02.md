# H02

Shrink the small-queue fraction from 1/10 to 1/20 of capacity to give the main queue an additional 5 percentage points of residency and improve hit-rate on Zipfian traces.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the single line that computes `small_size` from:

```rust
(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)
```

to:

```rust
(capacity / 20).max(1).min(capacity - 1)
```

`main_size` is derived as `capacity - small_size`, so it automatically expands from 90 % to 95 % of capacity with no further edits.

Every other line in the file is identical to `cycle/0002/v001`: the parallel-freq-deque layout, the `ghost_set` O(1) membership oracle, the lazy-deletion tombstone guard in `evict_small()`, the second-chance loop in `evict_main()`, and the `MAX_FREQ = 3` cap all remain unchanged.

For capacity = 2048: `small_size` goes from 204 → 102 slots and `main_size` goes from 1 844 → 1 946 slots. The ghost deque capacity (also `main_size`) expands by the same margin.

The `with_capacity` hints on all five deques should be updated to match the new sizes:

```rust
small:      VecDeque::with_capacity(small_size),
small_freq: VecDeque::with_capacity(small_size),
main:       VecDeque::with_capacity(main_size),
main_freq:  VecDeque::with_capacity(main_size),
ghost:      VecDeque::with_capacity(main_size),
ghost_set:  HashSet::with_capacity(main_size),
```

(These lines are already correct in v001; no textual change is needed there beyond deriving from the new `small_size`.)

## Rationale

The cross-cycle record shows a monotone improvement as the small-queue fraction shrank: baseline (1/4) → cycle-0001-v001 (1/10) improved hit-rate from 0.716 to 0.725. The open question explicitly flagged after cycle 0002 is whether 1/10 is at or past the optimum. For workload-a (Zipfian skew), the hot working set is a small fraction of the key space and benefits from as much contiguous residency in main as possible. A smaller admission filter (102 vs 204 slots) shortens the `get()` scan through small before reaching the denser hot region and may further reduce cold-key interference with main-queue residents. The risk is that the narrower funnel lets fewer once-warm keys into the ghost window, slightly reducing ghost-hit promotion opportunities.
