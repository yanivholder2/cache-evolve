# H01

Lazy-batch bucket updates to reduce get fast-path overhead while maintaining V002's hit rate gains.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

V002's current design updates node frequency buckets on every get() and put() call, which causes pointer-chasing overhead (get p99: 67→147 ns). Defer bucket reorganization to batch updates: track pending frequency increments in a separate queue and only move nodes between buckets during eviction decisions or periodic flush operations (e.g., every 64 accesses). This decouples the frequency counter increment (cheap) from the bucket-list manipulation (expensive), allowing the frequency signal to inform eviction decisions without paying the pointer-chasing cost on every access. Keep the frequency-aware eviction logic and bucket structure intact, but reduce the frequency of bucket reorganization on the critical path.

Specifically, modify the LruCache struct to add a `pending_freq_updates` queue and implement a new `flush_pending_updates()` method. In get() and put(), increment frequency counters and queue nodes for update, but defer actual `link_to_bucket()` and `unlink_from_bucket()` calls. Call `flush_pending_updates()` only before `find_evictable_node()` in put() eviction path, and periodically (e.g., every 64 total accesses tracked by an `access_counter`).
