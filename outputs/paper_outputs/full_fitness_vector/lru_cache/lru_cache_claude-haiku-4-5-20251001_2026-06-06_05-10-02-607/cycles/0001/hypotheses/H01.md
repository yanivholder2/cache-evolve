# H01

Implement segmented LRU (SLru) with two zones to raise hit rate on Zipfian traces by protecting frequently accessed items from immediate eviction.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Partition the cache into two zones: a hot zone (top 30% capacity) and a main zone (remaining 70%). On eviction, always evict from the main zone's LRU. When accessing an item in the main zone, promote it to the hot zone if there is space; items in the hot zone are only evicted when both zones are full and demand requires it.

Modify the `LruCache` struct to track which zone each node belongs to (add a `zone: u8` field to `Node`, where 0=main, 1=hot). Adjust `put()` to check if the item is being promoted from main to hot, and adjust eviction logic in `put()` to first evict from the main zone before touching the hot zone. Update `get()` to promote accessed items in the main zone toward the hot zone boundary. This preserves the invariant that the number of items in hot zone never exceeds `capacity * 0.3`.
