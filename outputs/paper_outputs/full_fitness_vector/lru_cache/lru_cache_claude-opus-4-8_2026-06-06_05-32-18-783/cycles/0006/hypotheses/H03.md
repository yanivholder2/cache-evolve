# H03

Widen the protected soft cap from 4/5 to 7/8 of capacity to recover part of the 0.73 hit-rate ceiling at less latency cost than the full geometry move.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In `src/lru_cache/mod.rs`, change the protected-segment sizing in
`try_new`. The parent sets `prot_cap = (capacity * 4 / 5).max(1)
.min(capacity.saturating_sub(1))`. Change the fraction to `capacity * 7 / 8`
(keeping the same `.max(1)` floor and `.min(capacity.saturating_sub(1))`
clamp so at least one probation slot always remains). Nothing else changes:
single-hit promotion, the protected-MRU fast path, eviction order, hashing,
and node layout all stay exactly as in the parent (node remains 24 bytes).

This is a deliberate verification probe for the standing open question: the
hit-rate record of 0.73 is held by cycle/0004/v003's wider-protected
variant, but it paid ~52 ns per op versus the champion's 27 ns. By taking a
smaller geometry step (7/8 instead of v003's larger protected share), the
hypothesis tests whether a fraction of that hit-rate gain reproduces on
workload-a while staying nearer the champion's latency. Confirmed if
hit-rate rises above the parent's 0.7241 with per-op-time staying well below
v003's 52 ns; refuted (and the "geometry-widening starves the fast path"
pattern reconfirmed) if latency regresses to v003 levels without a hit-rate
gain.
