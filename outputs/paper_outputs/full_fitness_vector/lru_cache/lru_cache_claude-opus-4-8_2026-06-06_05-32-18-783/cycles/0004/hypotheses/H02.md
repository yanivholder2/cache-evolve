# H02

Soften the ghost-history re-admission to land in probation (not protected) and gate on a repeated ghost hit, recovering the reuse-distance signal to push hit-rate above the 0.724 record without over-promoting.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

Start from the ghost-history SLRU variant (`cycle/0003/v002`), whose
unconditional re-admission straight into the protected segment overshot —
transient post-eviction re-references evicted genuinely hot protected keys
and dropped hit-rate *below* plain SLRU. Make admission gentle in two
ways:

1. On a ghost hit during `put` of a new key, re-admit the key into the
   **probationary MRU** (the normal new-entry position) rather than
   straight into protected. The reuse-distance signal still earns the key
   a normal SLRU promotion the next time it is referenced, but a single
   transient re-reference no longer displaces a hot protected key.

2. Gate any accelerated treatment on **repeated** ghost evidence: track a
   small per-ghost hit count in the ghost structure and only grant the
   faster path (e.g. inserting at the protected LRU end instead of plain
   probation) once a key has been seen in the ghost history at least twice.
   A single ghost hit gets only normal probation admission.

Keep the ghost store keys-only, non-resident, bounded, and never counted
toward `capacity`; keep the existing bound/eviction discipline on the
ghost queue. Preserve all resident SLRU invariants (segment geometry,
`prot_cap`, victim selection, fast paths). The claim is that gentler
admission turns the reuse-distance signal from a hit-rate regression into
a gain over the 0.724 SLRU baseline; measure hit-rate as the target axis
and watch that the extra per-put ghost bookkeeping does not erase the win.
