# H01

Reducing probationary capacity from `capacity/8` to `capacity/32` (96.875% protected) will further improve hit-rate by continuing the monotonic quota-compression trend while the probationary pool remains large enough to supply graduation diversity.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the single line that computes `prob_cap`:

```
// before (cycle/0006/v001):
let prob_cap = (capacity / 8).max(1);

// after:
let prob_cap = (capacity / 32).max(1);
```

No other changes. At `capacity=2048` this gives `prob_cap=64`, leaving 1984 protected slots (96.875%). The rest of the policy — probationary FIFO, one-hit graduation to protected LRU, overflow demotion to MRU of probationary, eviction from probationary first — is unchanged.

This probes the next step beyond the `capacity/16` (93.75%) result confirmed in `cycle/0007/v001` (hit-rate 0.7316). The diminishing-returns pattern (75%→87.5% gained more than 87.5%→93.75%) suggests a reversal point is near; this test locates whether `capacity/32` is still on the beneficial side of that plateau or has crossed it.
