# H02

Adding a third "elite" protected tier promoted on a second protected hit and evicted only after ordinary protected keys hardens retention of the hottest keys and raises hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v002

## Changes

The trace rewards "once-hot immortality," and the parent already grants durable
protection on the first hit — but among protected keys it discriminates only by
recency (the all-protected fallback evicts protected-LRU at index 0). This adds
a *finer retention tier* for keys proven hot more than once, so that when a
bucket is full of protected keys, the merely-warm ones are sacrificed before the
hottest ones. Crucially this is protection-*additive*: it never raises the bar
to reach protection (still promote-on-first-hit) and never demotes — it only
reorders eviction priority within the already-protected set, which is the
opposite of the protection-erosion levers (two-hit threshold, caps+demotion,
LFU-min global scan) that failed in prior cycles.

Edit `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` (the parent's
segmented bucket):

1. In `get_from_position`, on a hit to an already-protected item
   (`lfu_counter >= 1`), increment `lfu_counter` up to a cap of 2 (so the tiers
   are: 0 = probationary, 1 = protected, 2 = elite). The existing MRU fast path
   (`position == len-1 && lfu_counter >= 1`) must be amended so it still bumps
   the counter toward 2 on an at-back hit even though it skips the memmove —
   otherwise elite status can never be earned by the hottest (already-MRU) key.

2. Rework `eviction_candidate_index` to choose the victim by tier, then recency:
   evict the *oldest probationary* item (`lfu_counter == 0`, highest such index)
   if any exist; else evict the *lowest-index* (LRU) item whose `lfu_counter` is
   the minimum among the resident protected items — i.e. prefer a `counter == 1`
   victim over a `counter == 2` victim, breaking ties by lowest index. A small
   linear scan over the bucket is acceptable here (hit-rate is the only scored
   fitness dimension; the scan cost ties), so the strict O(1) contiguous-region
   arithmetic the parent uses may be relaxed.

3. Keep `evict_item`/`protected_count` consistent: `protected_count` should
   count items with `lfu_counter >= 1` and decrement when a protected/elite
   victim is removed. If the prefix/suffix counter invariant is no longer
   maintainable across three tiers, drop `protected_count` and derive everything
   from a scan instead.

Invariants to preserve: `put` must still insert new keys at the probationary
front and return `(&inserted, Option<evicted>)`; the bucket must never exceed
`max_size` (P1 capacity_bound); routing is untouched (P2 slot_legality holds).

## Rationale (optional)

If the all-protected churn at the LRU end is discarding still-hot keys, an elite
tier that survives one extra round of pressure should recover them. It is a
distinct strategy from H03 (which reserves probationary space) and from the
failed LFU fallback (which scanned a global min-count and let counter-1
newcomers dominate) — here probationary keys are still always evicted first.
