# H02

Halve the frequency decay interval from capacity × 16 to capacity × 8 operations to enable faster adaptation to workload phase shifts.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In the `check_and_perform_frequency_reset()` method, change the decay threshold constant from `(self.capacity as u64) * 16` to `(self.capacity as u64) * 8` (line 135 in the variant implementation). This causes frequency counters to be right-shifted (divided by 2) twice as frequently, allowing new hotspots to emerge faster as old frequencies decay at double the rate. The decay mechanism itself (right-shift by 1 bit per reset) remains unchanged. Eviction selection (minimum frequency with LRU tie-breaking), MRU promotion, and initial frequency assignments are all preserved. This tests whether the original `capacity * 16` interval is over-conservative and whether faster phase-shift responsiveness improves hit-rate.

## Rationale

The current `capacity * 16` interval may be too conservative, allowing ancient hotspots to retain excessive influence during workload phase shifts. Decaying at `capacity * 8` instead provides 2× faster frequency reset, enabling the cache to forget old patterns sooner and weight new access patterns more quickly. The periodic decay still maintains the same decay curve (right-shift); only the frequency is increased.
