# H01

Increase multiplicative decay coefficient to 0.8× to preserve frequency discrimination and slow the forgetting of historical access patterns.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Modify the frequency decay in `check_and_perform_frequency_reset()` to use a 0.8× (4/5) multiplicative coefficient instead of the current 0.75× (3/4). This means each node's frequency will be scaled by multiplying by 4 and dividing by 5:

```
node.freq = (node.freq as u16 * 4 / 5) as u8;
```

Replace the line:
```
node.freq = (node.freq as u16 * 3 / 4) as u8;
```

The decay interval remains `capacity * 16`. The hypothesis tests whether gentler frequency forgetting—by raising the decay coefficient from 0.75× closer to 1.0×—allows the cache to retain discriminative power across more operations while still adapting to workload changes. This explores the hypothesis that 0.75× may be slightly too aggressive for this trace.

## Rationale (optional)

The Cycle 0004 insights note that decay curve shape is critical and recommend sensitivity testing of coefficients. The 0.75× multiplicative decay outperformed the harsher 0.5× right-shift, suggesting that gentler decay preserves frequency discrimination. Testing 0.8× (between 0.75× and 1.0×) extends this exploration upward to find if an even gentler curve yields further improvements by slowing how quickly historical hotspots are forgotten.
