# H02

Partition the cache into a protected hot zone (top 1/3 capacity) and cold zone (bottom 2/3); promote items from cold to hot only after 2+ accesses to reduce thrashing on mixed-access patterns.

## Changes

Add two fields to the `Node` struct: `gen: u8` (generation; 0=cold, 1=hot) and `access_count: u16` (counter incremented on each access).

Add two fields to `LruCache`: `hot_capacity: usize` (set to `capacity / 3` in `try_new()`) and `op_counter: u64` (incremented on each operation, used for time-windowed decisions).

In `get()`, increment the accessed node's `access_count` counter. If the node is in the cold generation (gen=0) and `access_count >= 2`, unlink the node, set its generation to 1 (hot), and re-insert it at the tail of the linked list (treating hot items as MRU).

Modify eviction in `put()` when at capacity: count how many nodes currently exist in the cold zone (gen=0). If the cold zone is not empty, evict the LRU node from the cold zone only. If the cold zone is empty (all nodes are hot), then evict the LRU node from the hot zone. When inserting a new node, always start with gen=0 (cold) and access_count=0.

Ensure that the hot zone never exceeds `hot_capacity` nodes in steady state, and the total cache size never exceeds the specified `capacity`. Preserve all existing invariants: capacity bound, value fidelity, and no phantom items.
