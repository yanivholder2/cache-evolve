# H01

Increase the frequency decay reset interval from capacity × 16 to capacity × 24 to preserve frequency discrimination longer across workload phase shifts and improve hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In the `check_and_perform_frequency_reset()` function, replace the threshold calculation:

```rust
let reset_threshold = (self.capacity as u64) * 16;
```

with:

```rust
let reset_threshold = (self.capacity as u64) * 24;
```

This reduces the frequency of decay cycles by 50%, allowing frequencies to accumulate longer before the periodic multiplicative decay (0.75×) is applied. The decay coefficient and mechanism remain unchanged; only the interval between decay cycles is extended.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 tested faster decay (capacity × 8) and it actively regressed, indicating acceleration hurts hit-rate. The cross-cycle insights note the capacity × 16 interval is "robustly tuned" but explicitly ask: "Does the capacity × 16 decay interval generalize across different workload traces and cache sizes, or is it tuned to this specific scenario?" This hypothesis explores the untested direction: a longer interval for slower, more conservative decay. The hypothesis tests whether the current interval is over-tuned toward aggressive forgetting, and whether a gentler adaptation curve (decaying less frequently) preserves historical frequency signals that help identify truly hot items.
