# H01

Cap frequency counters at 32 to prevent ancient hotspots from saturating counter space, allowing newly-hot items to remain competitive in eviction decisions and improving hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Modify the frequency increment logic in the `get()` and `put()` methods to cap the maximum frequency at 32. Replace instances of `self.nodes[idx].freq = self.nodes[idx].freq.saturating_add(1)` with `self.nodes[idx].freq = std::cmp::min(self.nodes[idx].freq.saturating_add(1), 32)`. This applies to the frequency increment in `get()` (around line 60) and in `put()` for the update-in-place case (around line 76). All other logic remains unchanged: eviction selects the node with minimum frequency (ties broken by LRU head-to-tail scan), frequency decay still occurs every `capacity * 16` operations via right-shift by 1, and new items are inserted with freq=0. This tests the hypothesis that limiting saturation prevents historical hotspots from dominating the cache indefinitely.

## Rationale

The u8 frequency field saturates at 255, potentially allowing ancient hotspots to monopolize the frequency ranking. Lowering the saturation point to 32 reduces the dynamic range, keeping newly-hot items (freq 1-32) more competitive against old patterns without sacrificing frequency discrimination. The periodic decay at `capacity * 16` intervals still gradually resets all frequencies, enabling phase shifts.
