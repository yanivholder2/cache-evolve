# H03

Expand hot zone size from 1/3 to 1/2 of cache capacity to increase protection for frequently-accessed items and test whether stronger frequency-based partitioning improves hit rate.

## Changes

Modify zone-based hybrid approach (from v002) by increasing hot zone capacity: change `hot_capacity = capacity / 3` to `hot_capacity = capacity / 2`, with cold zone becoming `capacity / 2` instead of `2 * capacity / 3`. Maintain the same promotion rule (freq >= 2 to cold→hot transition) and eviction strategy (LRU from cold zone, min-frequency from hot zone). Adjust cold-zone eviction logic to account for the new larger hot zone and ensure proper zone balance. This tests whether a larger protected set of frequently-accessed items can capture more value without sacrificing cold-item fast-track benefits.
