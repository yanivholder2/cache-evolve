# H01

Combine periodic frequency decay with zone-based partitioning to capture both phase-shift adaptation and access-skew structure, improving hit-rate beyond single-policy approaches.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Integrate zone-based partitioning (from cycle/0002/v002) with frequency decay mechanism. Implement a two-zone structure: cold zone (2/3 capacity) with LRU eviction, hot zone (1/3 capacity) with frequency-based eviction. Add independent frequency decay for each zone: decay hot-zone frequencies every `capacity * 8` operations (right-shift by 1), decay cold-zone frequencies every `capacity * 16` operations. Promote cold-zone items to hot zone when frequency >= 2. Preserve node promotion logic and zone boundary maintenance during decay to ensure both temporal locality (cold zone) and phase-shift responsiveness (hot zone) work in concert.
