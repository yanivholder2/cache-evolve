# H02

Dynamically adjust frequency decay interval based on observed frequency counter distribution to maintain responsiveness to workload phase shifts without over-decaying.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Replace fixed `capacity * 16` decay interval with an adaptive threshold computed from frequency distribution statistics. Track frequencies of items accessed in a rolling window (last 512 operations). On each operation, compute the current average frequency. Set decay interval dynamically as `max(capacity * 8, min(capacity * 32, 2 * avg_freq))` to keep decay responsive when frequencies are generally low (phase shift in progress) and less frequent when frequencies are high (stable phase). Trigger decay when operation counter reaches the dynamically-computed threshold. Preserve all other frequency-based eviction logic (saturating counter increments, min-frequency victim selection with LRU tie-breaking).
