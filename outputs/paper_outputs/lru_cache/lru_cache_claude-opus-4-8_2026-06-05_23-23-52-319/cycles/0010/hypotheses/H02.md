# H02

Promoting an A1in key to the Am main list on its first in-window reuse, instead of 2Q's ghost-only delayed promotion, raises hit-rate by matching the incumbent's first-hit promotion signal.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v003

## Changes

This is the promotion-signal half of the "race 2Q against the 95/5
incumbent" thread. The 2Q parent's `get` treats an `A1IN` hit as a FIFO
no-op (returns the value, does not reorder or promote); a key only
reaches Am after a full A1in lifetime and a later A1out ghost hit — an
effectively delayed, two-hit-like gate. The cross-cycle record shows the
incumbent's *single-hit* promotion is the winning signal and that
loosening/delaying the promotion gate regresses. Change the `A1IN` arm
of `get`:

- On a get-hit whose node is in `A1IN`, promote it to Am MRU
  immediately: `self.unlink(idx); self.push_tail(idx, AM);` then return
  `self.nodes[idx].value.as_ref()`. This mirrors the incumbent's
  `push_back_protected` on first access.
- Keep `kin = capacity / 4` and `kout = capacity / 2` (canonical 2Q
  geometry) so this variant isolates the promotion lever from H01's
  geometry lever.
- Leave the A1out ghost path in `put` intact: a key that was paged out of
  A1in before any reuse, then returns, still promotes to Am. The change
  only adds the fast in-window promotion path.

Preserve invariants: resident count `lens[AM] + lens[A1IN] <= capacity`,
ghost `value == None`, slot reuse unchanged. Note that promoting out of
A1in early shrinks A1in occupancy, so `evict_if_needed` will lean more on
the Am-LRU eviction branch — that is intended. Confirmed if hit-rate
exceeds the parent's 0.7103 toward 0.7313; refuted if early promotion
admits one-hit-wonders into Am and erodes hits.

## Rationale (optional)

Tests whether 2Q trails the incumbent because of its delayed
(ghost-gated) promotion rather than its geometry. Orthogonal to H01: one
moves the size lever, the other the promotion-timing lever, so the pair
separates the two candidate causes of 2Q's gap.
