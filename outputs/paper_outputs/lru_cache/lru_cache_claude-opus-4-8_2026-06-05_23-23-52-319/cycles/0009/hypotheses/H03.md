# H03

Rebuild the cache as a 2Q (A1in FIFO + A1out ghost + Am LRU) skeleton to filter one-hit-wonders through a FIFO admission queue before they reach the main list, raising hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Replace the baseline single LRU list in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` with a
from-scratch 2Q implementation (Johnson & Shasha), a distinct skeleton not
yet tried in this run. Maintain three structures over the shared arena plus
a ghost list:

- `A1in` — a FIFO queue of newly admitted keys (recency-once), sized to a
  small fraction of capacity (start at `capacity / 4`, the classic Kin).
- `Am` — the main LRU list holding keys that proved reuse.
- `A1out` — a key-only ghost FIFO of keys evicted from `A1in`, sized to
  roughly `capacity / 2` (classic Kout), carrying no value.

Behaviour:
- On a **miss for a wholly new key**: if `A1out` contains the key, it has
  demonstrated reuse across an A1in lifetime — admit it to `Am` MRU.
  Otherwise admit it to the tail of `A1in` (FIFO).
- On a **hit in `Am`**: promote to `Am` MRU (standard LRU).
- On a **hit in `A1in`**: do **not** move it (canonical 2Q keeps A1in
  strictly FIFO); just return the value. This is the key behavioural
  difference from an LRU admission window and from W-TinyLFU's window,
  which the cross-cycle notes record as losers — here admission is FIFO and
  promotion is driven by A1out ghost hits, not by in-window recency or a
  frequency sketch.
- **Eviction** when the resident count (`A1in` + `Am`) would exceed
  `capacity`: if `A1in` is over its target size, evict its FIFO head into
  `A1out` (recording the ghost, dropping A1out's head if it overflows);
  otherwise evict the `Am` LRU outright.

Keep the no-unsafe, no-external-crate, in-place-slot-reuse arena style of
the baseline, and preserve the existing `framework::Cache` impl and all
public method signatures. Net resident count must stay within `capacity`.

## Rationale (optional)

LIRS and ARC both reported structural lifts but lost; 2Q occupies the
untested middle ground — a FIFO admission filter that drains one-hit-wonders
without the protected-tier sizing the SLRU axis has exhausted, probing
whether any structural axis beyond the 95/5 incumbent remains.
