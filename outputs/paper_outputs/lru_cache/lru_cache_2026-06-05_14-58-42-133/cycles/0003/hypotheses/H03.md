# H03

Adding a TinyLFU frequency doorkeeper to ARC's T1 admission raises hit-rate by blocking one-hit-wonders from displacing residents on a full miss.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

Augment the ARC variant (v003) with a count-min frequency sketch and gate its
full-miss admission — the previously-untested "frequency sketch on top of ARC"
open question. Add a fixed sketch to the `LruCache` struct (4 rows x 16384
saturating `u8` counters, distinct per-row seeds, halved every `10 * capacity`
recorded accesses), plus `bump(&key)` and `estimate(&key)` helpers identical in
shape to v002's. Call `bump(&key)` on every `get` hit and at the top of `put`
for the incoming key, so frequency is tracked for residents and newcomers alike.
Gate ONLY ARC's Case IV full-miss path (the `None` arm of the `ghost_map` match,
where a brand-new key would `alloc_resident(key, value, T1)` after a `replace`).
Before performing the replace+admit there, identify the resident that `replace`
would evict (the T1-LRU when `res_len[T1] > p`, else the T2-LRU) and compare
`estimate(&new_key)` against `estimate(&victim_key)`; if the newcomer's estimate
is strictly lower, reject admission — skip the `replace` and `alloc_resident`
entirely, leaving all four lists and `p` untouched, but still having bumped the
newcomer so a persistently-reused key is admitted on a later attempt. Leave the
B1 (Case II) and B2 (Case III) ghost-hit arms unchanged: a returning ghost has
proven reuse and bypasses the doorkeeper, preserving ARC's `p` adaptation. Do not
gate ghost-hit promotions, and do not alter `replace`'s T1/T2 victim choice.
Invariants: `map.len() <= capacity`, the ARC size relations (`|T1|+|B1| <= c`,
total `<= 2c`) still hold on every path, and the public/JNI surface is unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle notes flag "a frequency-sketch admission gate on top of ARC" as
never evaluated. v002 already proved a TinyLFU doorkeeper lifts SLRU; this tests
whether the same orthogonal frequency signal compounds with ARC's adaptivity or
merely adds overhead, exploring from a proven-but-non-winning base.
