# H02

Adding a coarse visit counter (capped at 8, halved on demotion) as a secondary tie-breaker for entries with equal IRR values in the cycle/0007/v003 eviction scans raises hit rate by recovering long-term warmth information that pure IRR discards.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v003

## Changes

Start from the cycle/0007/v003 overlay (SLRU + IRR timestamps, 80/20 split, LRU-quarter scan in protected, LRU-half scan in probationary, no ghost set, timestamps preserved across demotion).

**Add a coarse visit counter to each node:**
- Add `visit_count: u8` to `Node`.
- Add `const MAX_VISITS: u8 = 8;` at the top of the file.
- On new insertion in `put()` (both fresh arena slot and recycled slot path): set `visit_count = 1`.
- In `get()`, after updating IRR timestamps: `self.nodes[idx].visit_count = (self.nodes[idx].visit_count + 1).min(MAX_VISITS);`
- In the defensive re-put branch of `put()` (resident key, contract-violating path): also increment `visit_count` with the same cap.

**Update victim selection in both scan functions:**

Replace the plain `cur_irr > victim_irr` comparisons with a composite `(irr, inverted_visit_count)` comparison that makes `visit_count` a secondary tie-breaker. The logic: a candidate beats the current victim if its IRR is strictly higher, OR its IRR is equal AND its visit_count is strictly lower (fewer historical accesses = colder). In Rust:
```rust
let beats_victim = cur_irr > victim_irr
    || (cur_irr == victim_irr && cur_vc < victim_vc);
```
Apply this in `evict_from_probationary()` and in `demote_prot_head()`. The scan length and scan starting point (LRU head) remain unchanged.

**Halve visit_count on demotion, preserve on eviction:**
- In `demote_prot_head()`, after moving the victim from protected to probationary MRU tail: `self.nodes[victim_idx].visit_count /= 2;`. This mirrors the `freq /= 2` halving used in the frequency baseline — it preserves relative warmth information across segment transitions without letting the counter become unfairly large.
- On eviction from probationary (in `evict_from_probationary()`), no explicit reset is needed since the node's slot is freed.

**Keep unchanged:**
- IRR timestamp mechanics (`last_access`, `prev_access`, `op_clock`, the `irr()` function).
- 80/20 protected/probationary split (`prot_cap = capacity * 4 / 5`).
- LRU-quarter scan in protected (`scan_len = (prot_count / 4).max(1)`).
- LRU-half scan in probationary (`scan_len = (prob_count / 2).max(1)`).
- Single-hit promotion from probationary to protected.
- Timestamp preservation across demotion (do NOT reset `last_access` or `prev_access` when demoting).
- All linked-list helpers and the arena slot-reuse mechanism (`free_slots`).

## Rationale

Pure IRR cannot distinguish an entry with IRR=50 accessed once long ago versus an entry with IRR=50 accessed dozens of times across its residency history. Both look equally cold by IRR alone. The coarse visit counter adds a lightweight long-term warmth signal — equivalent to the frequency counter's contribution in cycle/0006/v001 but without the staleness problem of unbounded accumulation. The cap at MAX_VISITS=8 and halving on demotion mirror the frequency-era design decisions that proved load-bearing (cycles 3-6): the counter remains bounded, and relative warmth survives segment transitions. Because `visit_count` only breaks ties among equal-IRR candidates, it cannot override the sharper IRR signal where IRR values differ.
