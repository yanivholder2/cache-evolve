# H01

Widening the protected soft cap from 7/8 to 15/16 of capacity recovers additional hit-rate without re-triggering fast-path starvation.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

In `try_new` change the single sizing fraction that computes `prot_cap`.
Currently:

```rust
let prot_cap = (capacity * 7 / 8).max(1).min(capacity.saturating_sub(1));
```

Replace the `7 / 8` factor with `15 / 16`, keeping the `.max(1)` and
`.min(capacity.saturating_sub(1))` clamps exactly as-is so probation is
never fully starved and the cap stays at least 1. Touch nothing else:
promotion, demotion (`demote_protected_lru`), eviction victim selection,
the protected-MRU fast path, node layout, and the packed `prot` flag all
stay byte-for-byte identical. This isolates the geometry probe the cycle
0006 insight called for — a single step into the headroom between the
proven 7/8 cap and the larger shares that previously starved the
protected-MRU fast path and regressed latency. The hypothesis is
confirmed if hit-rate rises above v003's 0.7291 while get p50 holds at or
near 26 ns, and refuted if latency regresses (starvation reappears) or
hit-rate fails to move.
