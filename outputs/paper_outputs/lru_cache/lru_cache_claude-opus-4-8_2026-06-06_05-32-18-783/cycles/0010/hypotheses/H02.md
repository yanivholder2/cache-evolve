# H02

Deleting the dead NIL self-writes from `unlink` cuts two node-link stores per promote/demote/evict, lowering per-op-time on the named link-surgery hot path.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v003

## Changes

In `unlink` (the helper that detaches a node from its segment), remove the
two trailing self-writes that clear the node's own links:
`self.nodes[idx].set_prev_link(NIL);` and
`self.nodes[idx].set_next_link(NIL);`. After this change `unlink` only
repairs the neighbor (or head/tail) links and leaves the node's own
`prev`/`next` words untouched.

This is safe because every caller of `unlink` immediately follows it with
`push_back`, which unconditionally overwrites both link words
(`set_prev_link(tail)` then `set_next_link(NIL)`): the get protected-refresh
path, the probationary-to-protected promotion path, the put in-place-update
path, `demote_protected_lru`, and the eviction victim path (which also
rewrites key/value/segment before pushing). The two NIL stores are pure
dead writes in all of them.

Invariants to preserve: do not alter `push_back` — it must keep writing
both links so the node is always fully re-linked after detach. Do not touch
the `PROT_MASK` segment-flag handling in `set_prev_link` (the flag must
still survive a detach untouched). Keep the 7/8 geometry, FastHasher, and
24-byte node layout identical; this is a pure store-elimination change with
no policy or footprint effect.

Confirm: get/put p50 drop below the parent's 26 ns while hit-rate and
metadata-size hold exactly. Refute: latency is flat or worse, indicating
the dead stores were already elided by codegen and the change only
perturbed register/instruction scheduling.

## Rationale (optional)

Targets link surgery — the one hot-path cost cycles 0008 and 0009 both
flagged as never directly attacked — with a minimal, risk-bounded edit that
removes work rather than adding a branch or store (the move that has
historically paid off), and does not grow the `LruCache` footprint.
