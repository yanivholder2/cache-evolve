# H02

Use 20% stash capacity with FIFO eviction to locate the efficiency frontier for hit-rate improvement between 10% and 25%.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Implement a bounded stash mechanism in AssociativeCache with capacity calculated as `total_capacity / 5` (20% of total cache), filling the gap between the baseline's 0%, cycle 0001's 10%, and cycle 0002's proven-best 25%. Insert all evicted items into the stash using straightforward FIFO semantics: when at capacity, remove the oldest item (index 0) to make room for the new eviction. Perform secondary stash lookup on primary bucket misses as in prior variants. This variant probes the capacity saturation curve; if 20% hits a sweet spot better than 25%'s cost-benefit ratio, it isolates the efficiency frontier. If not, it confirms that raw capacity scaling continues to be productive up to at least 25%.
