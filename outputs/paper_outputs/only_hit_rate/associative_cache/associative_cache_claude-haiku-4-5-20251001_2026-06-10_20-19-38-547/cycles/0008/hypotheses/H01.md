# H01

Increase hot-tier allocation to 20% and reduce cold tier to 5% to provide more capacity for the workload's hot-tier items at the cost of reduced cold-tier headroom, improving hit rate on frequently re-accessed evictions.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Modify the partition calculation in `AssociativeCache::try_new()` to allocate more capacity to the hot tier. Change the hot_stash_capacity calculation from `std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 18 / 100)` to `std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 20 / 100)`, and the cold_stash_capacity calculation from `std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 7 / 100)` to `std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 5 / 100)`. The comment describing the partition sizes (lines 47-50) should also be updated to reflect the new allocation.

This is a direct continuation of Cycle 0007's asymmetric partition discovery, testing whether the workload's re-access patterns benefit from an even greater skew toward hot-tier retention. The promotion logic and stash management mechanisms remain unchanged; only the capacity allocation shifts.

## Rationale

Cycle 0007 v001 succeeded by shifting from symmetric (12.5/12.5) to asymmetric (18/7) allocation. The Cycle 0007 continuation guidance suggests testing nearby ratios (15–20% hot, 5–10% cold). This hypothesis tests the upper bound of the local region: 20% hot provides more overflow protection for repeatedly re-accessed items, while 5% cold is sufficient for initial eviction capture given the workload's concentrated re-access subset.
