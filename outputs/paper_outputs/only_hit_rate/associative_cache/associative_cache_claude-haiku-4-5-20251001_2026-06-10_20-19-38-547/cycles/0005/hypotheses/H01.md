# H01

Increase the geometric reduction factor from 0.71 to 0.75 to achieve more aggressive bucket-width expansion and collision reduction, targeting improved hit rate through refined geometric optimization.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/geometry.rs` to increase the `reduction_factor` constant from 0.71 to 0.75. This makes the bucket-width rebalancing more aggressive, reducing the target bucket count by a higher percentage (25% vs. 29% under the current 0.71 factor), resulting in fewer but proportionally wider buckets. The change keeps the total capacity conservation logic intact and the rest of the layout algorithm unchanged. The hypothesis expects this refinement to push further into the collision-reduction design space, testing whether the current 0.71 factor is conservative and whether 0.75 yields incremental gains by reducing collision clustering more aggressively while still maintaining balance. Retain the 25% stash FIFO mechanism and secondary lookup from v001 unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004's v001 validated that 0.71 is a productive point in the geometric design space, but the open question remains whether this represents the local optimum or whether further refinement in either direction yields gains. This hypothesis tests the high side of the factor search space to bound the saturation point.
