# H03

Implement collision-aware selective stash insertion to preserve only evicted items from high-contention buckets, targeting improved hit rate by reducing stash pollution from low-value evictions and synergizing with geometric collision reduction.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Modify `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` to add per-bucket collision tracking and conditional stash insertion. Add a collision counter to each `AssociativeCacheBucket` that increments when an eviction occurs (i.e., when a new item cannot fit without displacing an existing item). In the `put()` method, after a bucket eviction, only add the evicted item to the stash if its source bucket's collision count is at least 2 (indicating persistent contention). Items from buckets experiencing 0–1 collisions are discarded rather than preserved, reducing stash churn. Preserve the FIFO eviction policy and 25% stash capacity from v001; secondary lookup on primary bucket misses remains unchanged. Reset collision counters periodically (e.g., per 1000 operations) to reflect current workload patterns, or keep unbounded and use a simple threshold.

The hypothesis expects collision-focused insertion to filter out low-value recoveries: items from lightly-loaded buckets are natural noise that clogs the stash; items from hot (collision-heavy) buckets are more likely to be re-accessed. By pairing this filtering with v001's geometric collision reduction, the cache preserves stash capacity for the highest-value re-uses, improving hit rate and stash utilization.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0003 tested occupancy-aware filtering (avoid capturing from under-occupied buckets) and it yielded zero improvement. H03 shifts the filtering axis from occupancy to collision pressure—a signal orthogonal to occupancy that may become meaningful once geometry reduces collision frequency but concentrates remaining collisions in specific hot buckets. This explores a previously untested filtering strategy grounded in the insight that collision clustering is the binding constraint.
