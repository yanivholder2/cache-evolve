# H02

Three-tier stash with access-count-based promotion (cold → warm → hot) to reduce spurious hot-tier entries and improve stash utility.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

Extend `AssociativeCache` to add a third `warm_stash` tier between cold and hot. Add the following fields to the struct:

- `warm_stash: Vec<Item<K, V>>` to hold intermediate-value items
- `warm_stash_capacity: usize` to track its max size
- `stash_promotion_count: HashMap<K, usize>` to track how many times each item has been re-accessed while in stash

In `try_new()`, initialize three tiers with capacities totaling 25%:
- `hot_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 6 / 100)`
- `warm_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 10 / 100)`
- `cold_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 9 / 100)`

Modify `get_and_update_item()` to implement tiered promotion:
- When an item is found in cold_stash: increment `stash_promotion_count[key]`
  - If count == 1: promote from cold to warm (remove from cold, append to warm)
  - If count >= 2: promote from warm to hot (remove from warm, append to hot)
- When an item is found in warm_stash: increment count; if count >= 2, promote to hot
- Maintain FIFO eviction within each tier when capacity is reached (remove oldest item first)

This creates a filtering mechanism where items must accumulate at least two re-accesses before entering the hot tier, reducing pollution of hot capacity by one-time or low-frequency stash hits while preserving the two-tier organization's promotion-on-hit principle.
