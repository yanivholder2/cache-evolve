# H01

Asymmetric tier partition in two-tier stash (18% hot / 7% cold) to prioritize frequently re-accessed items and improve hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

In `AssociativeCache::try_new()` (around lines 48–50), adjust the stash partition calculation to allocate asymmetric capacities:

- Change `hot_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity / 8)` to `hot_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 18 / 100)`
- Change `cold_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity / 8)` to `cold_stash_capacity = std::cmp::max(1, total_capacity * 7 / 100)`

This reallocates 5.5% capacity from cold (12.5% → 7%) to hot (12.5% → 18%), prioritizing frequently re-accessed items while maintaining the 25% overall stash budget. The underlying promotion-on-hit mechanism (where cold items move to hot on re-access) remains unchanged. The asymmetric split reflects the hypothesis that the workload's evicted items exhibit skewed re-use patterns: most evictions enter cold, but the items that are re-accessed multiple times should occupy more hot capacity to avoid eviction from the hot tier.
