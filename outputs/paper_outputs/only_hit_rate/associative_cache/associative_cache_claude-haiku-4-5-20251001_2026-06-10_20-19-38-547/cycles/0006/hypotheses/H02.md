# H02

Partitioning the 25% stash into hot and cold tiers with promotion on secondary-lookup hit will improve hit rate by concentrating capacity on high-frequency re-accessed items.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Modify `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` to implement a two-tier stash structure. Partition the 25% stash capacity (`total_capacity / 4`) into a hot tier (12.5%, `total_capacity / 8`) and a cold tier (12.5%, `total_capacity / 8`). On eviction, insert new items into the cold tier using FIFO order. On secondary stash lookup (when primary bucket miss occurs), when a hit is found:
- If the item is in the cold tier, move it to the hot tier, displacing the oldest item from the hot tier if necessary
- If already in the hot tier, leave it there (optionally update its position to LRU-like recency within hot tier)

On stash eviction due to capacity pressure, evict from the cold tier first using FIFO; only evict from the hot tier when cold tier is fully empty. Retain the secondary lookup logic and 0.71 geometric factor from v002 (inherited from cycle/0004/v001).

The hypothesis expects this stratification to preserve frequently re-accessed items in the hot tier while using the cold tier as a staging area for lower-probability re-uses, improving effective stash utilization and overall hit rate.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycles 0003 and 0005 tested filtering strategies (occupancy-aware, collision-aware) and both failed to improve over unconditional 25% FIFO capture. This hypothesis takes a different escape path: rather than filtering what to capture, it organizes the stash internally to give frequent re-accesses preferential treatment. The promotion-on-hit mechanism creates a learned priority signal orthogonal to the collision-count or occupancy-count signals tested before, and keeps all 25% capacity in use without the architectural constraints that blocked capacity scaling in Cycle 0003.
