## Changes

Modify `src/shared/geometry.rs` to set the `reduction_factor` constant from 0.71 to 0.70. This reduces the bucket count by a slightly smaller percentage (70% vs. 71%), resulting in proportionally wider buckets with finer collision-reduction tuning. Keep the total capacity conservation logic and rest of the layout algorithm unchanged. Retain the 25% stash FIFO mechanism and secondary lookup from v001 intact.

The hypothesis tests the lower bound of the 0.68–0.73 fine-grained search space identified in Cycle 0005, verifying whether 0.71 is the precise optimum or whether 0.70 yields incremental gains by reducing collision clustering at a slightly different scale.
