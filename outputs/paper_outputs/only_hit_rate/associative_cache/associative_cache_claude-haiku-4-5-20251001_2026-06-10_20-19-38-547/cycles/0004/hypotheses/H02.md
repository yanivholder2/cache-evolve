# H02

Implement 25% stash capacity with LRU eviction policy instead of FIFO to better preserve recently-accessed items in the recovery buffer, potentially increasing hit rate by keeping hot stashed items longer.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Implement a bounded stash mechanism in `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` with capacity set to 25% of total cache capacity (`total_capacity / 4`). Capture all evicted items into the stash on primary bucket evictions. Modify stash eviction policy from FIFO to LRU: track the access order of stashed items (e.g., via timestamp or explicit order), and when the stash reaches capacity, remove the least-recently-accessed stashed item (not the oldest-inserted item). On secondary stash lookup, update the access time of the found item. Perform secondary stash lookup on primary bucket misses as in the baseline stash variants. The hypothesis expects LRU ordering to preserve hot items (those re-accessed within the stash window) longer than FIFO, improving the utility of the 25% capacity by aligning stash eviction with item value rather than insertion order.
