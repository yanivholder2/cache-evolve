# H01

Combine wider-bucket geometry with the 25% stash to reduce collision-driven evictions while maintaining strong recovery coverage, targeting improved hit rate via synergy between geometry and eviction recovery.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/geometry.rs` to overlay `BaselineGeometry::layout()` to prefer fewer, wider buckets. Specifically, compute a rebalanced layout that reduces `num_of_buckets` by a factor of ~0.67–0.75 while increasing `bucket_size` proportionally, keeping total capacity constant. For example, if the harness provides 64 buckets × 4-wide, repartition to 48 buckets × 5.33-wide (or similar within integer constraints). This reduces collision clustering by spreading items across fewer, larger buckets, lowering the rate of evictions that would otherwise flow to the stash. Retain the 25% stash mechanism and secondary lookup from cycle/0002/v002 unchanged. The hypothesis expects the collision reduction to compound with the stash's recovery capability: fewer collisions mean fewer items reach the stash, but those that do are preserved by the proven 25% capacity, yielding better utilization of both levers.
