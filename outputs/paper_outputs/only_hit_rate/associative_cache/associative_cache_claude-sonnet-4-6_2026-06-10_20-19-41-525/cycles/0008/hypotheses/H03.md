# H03

Increasing the bulk-decay magnitude from 1 to 2 on warm-lock creates a larger cold pool after each decay event, reducing warm-lock recurrence frequency and improving hit-rate over the incumbent on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

In `evict_item()` in `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`, change the
decay loop (Step 2, warm-lock path) from:

```rust
for item in self.items.iter_mut() {
    item.lfu_counter -= 1;
}
```

to:

```rust
for item in self.items.iter_mut() {
    item.lfu_counter = item.lfu_counter.saturating_sub(2);
}
```

Use `saturating_sub(2)` rather than `- 2` to prevent underflow; items with
`lfu_counter == 0` or `1` both become 0. No other changes — the cold-first scan
(Step 1) and the `remove_at(0)` warm-lock fallback (end of Step 2) are unchanged.

**Expected effect on counter distribution** (with the cap=4 inherited from the parent):
- With decay-by-1: items at counter 1 → 0 (cold); counters 2, 3, 4 stay warm.
- With decay-by-2: items at counter 1 or 2 → 0 (cold); counters 3, 4 stay warm.

Items that had exactly counter == 2 at the time of warm-lock represent the
"lukewarm" tier — accessed twice since the last decay event, but still frequent
candidates for eviction on a scan-heavy trace. Making them cold immediately
(a) enlarges the cold pool available to the cold-first scan on the next eviction call
and (b) reduces how quickly the cache re-enters warm-lock after the decay event.

**Invariants to preserve**: no underflow (`saturating_sub` guarantees this); the
cold-first scan logic and `remove_at(0)` fallback are unchanged; the counter cap
at 4 from the parent is retained in `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` (no change
to that file).

## Rationale

Counter ceiling (cap=4 vs cap=2) was tested in cycles 0005 and 0007 and found inert.
Decay *magnitude* (how much to subtract per warm-lock event) is a distinct axis that
has never been varied — every prior cycle decayed by exactly 1. With the cap at 4,
only the range {1, 2, 3, 4} can exist at warm-lock time; decay-by-2 converts items
at 1 and 2 to cold without touching items at 3–4. If workload-a's hot set
accumulates counters of 3–4 (many repeated hits) while its "lukewarm" scan items
linger at 2, this change correctly demotes the lukewarm tier in one decay pass
instead of two, shrinking warm-lock duration without harming the true hot set.
