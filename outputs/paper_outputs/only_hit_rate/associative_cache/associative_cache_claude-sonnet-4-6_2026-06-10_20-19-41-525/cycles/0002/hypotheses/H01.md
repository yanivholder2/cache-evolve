# H01

Increasing the bucket-widening factor from 4 to 16 (8×256 instead of 32×64) will further improve hit-rate by giving each per-set LRU an even larger eviction pool, provided the gain is not yet saturated at factor=4.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Overlay `src/shared/geometry.rs`. In `BaselineGeometry::for_settings`, change the single constant from `let factor = 4usize;` to `let factor = 16usize;`. For the standard 128×16=2048 configuration this yields 8 buckets × 256 slots. The capacity-conservation guard (`if total % target_num_buckets == 0`) and the identity-split fallback remain exactly as in v001 — only the factor constant changes. No other file is modified.

## Rationale

v001 proved that the 4× geometry shift raised hit-rate from 0.661 to 0.664 on the LIRS workload-a trace, and the cycle-insight explicitly flagged the direction as "not yet saturated." This hypothesis tests the next step on the same axis: a further 4× reduction in bucket count (from 32 to 8) doubles the per-bucket capacity again (128→256 slots). If the LIRS workload's hot working set still fits comfortably within 256 slots, the LRU will see fewer premature evictions and hit-rate should rise further. If the gain has already plateaued at factor=4, this variant will show a flat or regressed result and bound the geometry search space.
