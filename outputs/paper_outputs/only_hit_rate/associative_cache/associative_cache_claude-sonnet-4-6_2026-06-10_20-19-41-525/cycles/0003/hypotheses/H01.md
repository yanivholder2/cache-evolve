# H01

Combining mid-point insertion with cycle/0002/v002's cold-first eviction on wide-bucket geometry will further reduce scan-pollution hits and raise hit-rate by attacking the scan problem from both the insertion and eviction sides simultaneously.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Overlay `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` with a `put` override that inserts new items at the midpoint of `self.items` (position `self.items.len() / 2`) rather than appending at the back, mirroring cycle/0002/v003's mid-point insertion logic.

Concrete implementation of the `put` method in `AssociativeCacheBucket`:
1. Existing-key update path is unchanged: find the position via `self.get_position(&item.key)`, update `items[position].value`, call `self.get_from_position(position)`, return.
2. When the bucket is full, call `self.evict_item()` to produce the evicted-item `Option`.
3. Compute `let mid = self.items.len() / 2;` on the post-eviction vec.
4. Call `self.items.insert(mid, item)` instead of `self.items.push(item)`.
5. Return `(&self.items[mid], evicted_item)`.

Do **not** re-implement `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` or `src/shared/geometry.rs` — both are inherited unchanged from cycle/0002/v002 (cold-first ClassicLRU with counter tracking, and 4× wider-bucket geometry respectively).

## Rationale

cycle/0002 showed that cold-first eviction (v002) and mid-point insertion (v003) each independently reduced scan pollution, but they targeted different parts of the pipeline: eviction-side vs. insertion-side. Neither was tested in combination. If the two effects are additive — mid-point insertion keeps new cold items out of the fully-protected MRU zone, while cold-first eviction ensures those same items are eviction candidates before warm items — stacking them should produce a further hit-rate gain on top of v002's already-strong baseline.
