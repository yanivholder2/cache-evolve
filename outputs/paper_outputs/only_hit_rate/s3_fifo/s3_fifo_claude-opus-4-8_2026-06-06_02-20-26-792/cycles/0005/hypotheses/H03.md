# H03

Insert newly re-admitted and freshly promoted entries at the tail of main rather than the front, reserving the MRU front exclusively for keys proven hot by real get() reuse, to raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In v003 the MRU front of `main` is shared by two very different populations:
keys proven hot by an actual `get()` (moved to front by the move-to-front
path) and *unproven* keys that were just (re)admitted. The latter enter at
the front via two `push_front` sites:

1. `put()` ghost re-admission: `self.main.push_front(Entry::new(key, value));`
2. `evict_small()` small→main promotion: `self.main.push_front(tail);`

Change both of these to `push_back` so re-admitted and promoted entries land
at the tail (LRU end) of main. Leave the `get()` move-to-front path
untouched: only a genuine reuse hit earns a front (most-protected) position.
Keep the direct-to-main re-admission path itself intact and ungated — this
changes only *where* in main the entry lands, not whether it is admitted.

Effect: an unproven re-admission or promotion must survive at least one
`get()` reuse before it is protected from eviction, so the eviction scan
reaches freshly admitted-but-unreused keys before it reaches reuse-proven
residents.

Preserve invariants: admission/re-admission still happens (no key is
dropped that v003 would have kept on entry), capacity bounds unchanged, and
`evict_main()` is still called before insertion when main is full.

## Rationale (optional)

Cross-cycle: "Reordering (what gets evicted) beats re-admitting or
warm-starting." This is a pure reorder of the insertion position that lets
get()-reuse, not mere (re)admission, decide front protection — sharpening
eviction without adding any protection or memory to weak-signal keys.
