# H01

Promote a small-queue entry into the main queue on any reuse (freq >= 1) instead of requiring freq >= 2, raising hit-rate by retaining reused items longer.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `evict_small()` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change the promotion condition
from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 0`. This means a small-queue tail
that has been accessed at least once (rather than at least twice) graduates
into the main queue; cold entries with `freq == 0` still go to the ghost
queue as today. Keep the rest of `evict_small` unchanged — the main-queue
overflow check (`self.main.len() >= self.main_size` → `evict_main()`) and the
ghost insertion path must stay intact. Do not touch `evict_main`, `get`, or
`put`. This aligns with the original S3-FIFO paper, which promotes on any
reaccess.

## Rationale (optional)

The baseline's `freq > 1` threshold discards items that were reused exactly
once, sending them to the ghost queue where a subsequent hit pays a full
re-admission round-trip. On a Zipfian/LIRS workload many warm-but-not-hot
keys are reused once within a small-queue lifetime; admitting them directly
should reduce misses. This is a pure admission-threshold change, isolated
from sizing and history-depth strategies.
