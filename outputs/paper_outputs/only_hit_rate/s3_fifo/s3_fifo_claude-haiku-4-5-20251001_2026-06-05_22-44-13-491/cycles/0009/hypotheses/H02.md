# H02

Extend frequency-depth protection to MAX_FREQ=6 while preserving the winning extreme structural selectivity at 1/14 to test whether deepening frequency retention unlocks additional compounding gains at the saturation boundary.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v003

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 5;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 6;`. Keep the small-queue sizing at `(capacity / 14).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` unchanged, maintaining v003's proven 1/14 structural selectivity. All other logic identical: frequency increments on access up to the new MAX_FREQ=6 ceiling, freq > 1 threshold for promotion, main-queue eviction with frequency decrement.

## Rationale

Cycle 0008 revealed that frequency-depth protection is the higher-leverage axis at extreme structural selectivity: v002's increase from MAX_FREQ=5 to MAX_FREQ=6 yielded +0.0014 (larger than v001's structural increment of +0.0002). However, v003 won by pairing 1/14 structure with MAX_FREQ=5, suggesting the optimal point may lie beyond this pairing in the frequency dimension. The cycle insight notes "frequency depth still has unlocated leverage" at the saturation boundary. This hypothesis tests whether extending the main-queue item lifetime one additional frequency level (allowing items to survive one more eviction round before final removal) complements the aggressive 1/14 admission gate, potentially unlocking synergistic gains where items passing the tight structural gate benefit from extended tenure.
