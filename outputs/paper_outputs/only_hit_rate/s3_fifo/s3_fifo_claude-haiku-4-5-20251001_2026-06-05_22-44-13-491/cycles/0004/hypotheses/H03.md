# H03

Reduce small-queue capacity from 1/6 to 1/8 to accelerate discovery of the saturation boundary and identify any utilization cliff where the admission gate becomes too constrained.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small-queue admission size more aggressively. Change the line `(capacity / 6).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 8).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This narrows the small queue from 1/6 to 1/8 of total capacity, testing a larger step down from v001's 1/6 baseline. Cycle 0003 confirmed that tighter gates continue improving hit-rate, but the saturation point remains unknown. This more aggressive reduction either discovers continued improvement (suggesting the optimal gate is tighter than 1/6), stalls (indicating approach to saturation), or degrades (revealing the utilization cliff where the queue becomes too constrained to absorb working-set diversity). Rapid boundary discovery here informs whether future cycles should refine within the 1/6–1/7–1/8 range or pivot to orthogonal axes.
