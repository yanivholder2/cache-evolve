# H02

Reduce small-queue capacity from 1/8 to 1/10 to aggressively accelerate saturation-boundary discovery and identify any utilization cliff where the admission gate becomes too constrained.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small-queue admission size. Change the line `(capacity / 8).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This narrows the small queue from 1/8 to 1/10 of total capacity, testing a larger step down from v003's winning boundary. Cycle 0002 through 0004 show monotonic improvement from structural selectivity (1/4→1/5→1/6→1/8), but the saturation point remains unmapped. This aggressive reduction either confirms continued improvement (indicating optimal sizing is tighter than 1/8), stalls (showing approach to saturation), or degrades (revealing a utilization cliff where working-set diversity absorption fails). Rapid boundary discovery here determines whether future cycles should refine incrementally around 1/8-1/9 or pivot to orthogonal axes.
