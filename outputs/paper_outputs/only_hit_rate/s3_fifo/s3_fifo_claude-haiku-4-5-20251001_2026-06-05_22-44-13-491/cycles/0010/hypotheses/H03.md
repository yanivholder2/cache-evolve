## Rationale

Cycle 0009 produced two winners pursuing different optimization axes—v001 pushed structural selectivity to 1/15 while v002 pushed frequency depth to MAX_FREQ=6, both achieving identical 0.7278 hit-rate. The cycle insight explicitly identifies the "unexplored pairing" that merits testing: "Test at least one unexplored pairing (e.g., 1/15+MAX_FREQ=6 or 1/14+MAX_FREQ=7)." This hypothesis tests whether pairing 1/15 structure with MAX_FREQ=6 frequency creates a synergistic interaction stronger than either innovation alone, or whether the improvements are merely additive. If this pairing outperforms both winners, it indicates the true optimum lies in the interaction space between them; if it matches or underperforms, it reveals whether the two dimensions truly compound or represent alternative paths to the same ceiling.
