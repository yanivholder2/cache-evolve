## Changes

Modify the `put()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to assign a modest initial frequency to items re-admitted from the ghost queue. In the branch where `in_ghost` is true (around lines 85-91), change the line `self.main.push_front(Entry::new(key, value));` to first create an entry and set its frequency to 1: `let mut entry = Entry::new(key, value); entry.freq = 1; self.main.push_front(entry);`. This provides a middle ground between the baseline (freq=0) and v002's failed approach (freq=2). Ghost hits now require exactly 2 eviction passes before permanent eviction (vs 3-4 for cold items), reflecting their prior survival while avoiding the upfront crowding that destroyed v002. Items must still earn further protection through continued access patterns.
