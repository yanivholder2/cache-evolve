## Rationale

Cycle 0007 established that saturation lies between 1/12 and 1/14: v001's 1/13 showed minimal improvement (+0.0003), yet v003's 1/14 showed no degradation despite diminishing returns. The open question asks: "Does 1/14 unlock greater gains when paired with MAX_FREQ=5?" Current cycle winner v002 (1/12+MAX_FREQ=5) demonstrates that structure-frequency compounding is viable. This hypothesis aggressively explores the structure dimension at the saturation cliff while leveraging the proven frequency protection, testing whether extreme selectivity can sustain performance when paired with extended item lifetime, or whether the admission gate has truly saturated.
