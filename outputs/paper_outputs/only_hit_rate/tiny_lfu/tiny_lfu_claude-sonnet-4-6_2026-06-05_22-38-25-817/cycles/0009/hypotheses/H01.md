# H01

Reducing PROMO_THRESHOLD from 8 to 6 will admit medium-frequency items into the protected segment that the current gate incorrectly excludes under the now-accurate sketch, raising hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant declaration from:

```
const PROMO_THRESHOLD: u32 = 8;
```

to:

```
const PROMO_THRESHOLD: u32 = 6;
```

No other code changes. The `was_protected` bypass in `get()` is preserved unchanged. The admission gate in `put()`, the k=4 victim scan, and the 90/10 SLRU split are all left intact.

### Rationale

PROMO_THRESHOLD=8 was calibrated in cycle 0003 and carried forward. Threshold=4 was re-probed in cycle 0007/v002, but its parent was cycle/0006/v002 — the conservative-update baseline with the original 4× sketch width. At that narrower width, collision inflation was meaningfully higher: items with true frequency 5–7 frequently had their sketch estimates inflated to 8–10 by collisions, so they already cleared the threshold=8 gate automatically. The threshold=4 test regressed because it promoted genuinely second-tier items that were correctly blocked by threshold=8 under noisy estimates.

Under the cycle/0008/v001 baseline (8× width + conservative update), collision-inflated estimates are largely eliminated. Items with true frequency 6–7 now estimate accurately at 6–7 and are blocked by threshold=8. Threshold=6 is an untested intermediate that has never been evaluated against the full-accuracy sketch. If a non-trivial cohort of medium-hot items stalls in probationary because the sharper estimates correctly place them at freq 6–7 (rather than the previously inflated 8–10), lowering to 6 would let them enter protected and reduce unnecessary probationary churn.

## Preservation invariants

- `was_protected` bypass must remain unconditional (any item previously demoted from protected bypasses the gate regardless of frequency).
- The admission gate in `put()` (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`) is unchanged.
- The k=4 victim scan in probationary is unchanged.
