# H01

Prioritize oldest items strictly by age within the frequency-cold cohort, bypassing frequency weighting in secondary ranking to improve hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

In the victim-selection first pass within the `put()` method, modify the scoring for items in the 25th-percentile frequency-cold cohort to use pure age (oldest first) rather than age/(freq+2).

Specifically:
1. Keep the 25th-percentile frequency-cohort partitioning unchanged.
2. In the first-pass loop (lines 153-165), replace the age/(freq+2) scoring with a simple age metric:
   ```
   let age = self.current_time - self.nodes[idx].last_access_time;
   if age > best_score {
       best_score = age;
       best_victim_idx = Some(idx);
   }
   ```
3. Keep the fallback pass and admission gate unchanged.

The change decouples the secondary ranking from frequency; items in the cold cohort are now strictly ordered by temporal staleness (time since last access), not a frequency-weighted hybrid.

## Rationale

Cycle 0009 demonstrated that the 25th-percentile frequency threshold is well-calibrated for partitioning, but the secondary ranking formula may be suboptimal. The current age/(freq+2) formula weights frequency heavily in the denominator; for items already filtered to the lowest frequencies, this may dilute the signal of absolute staleness. By using pure age as the secondary criterion within the cold cohort, we give strict temporal priority to the oldest (least-recently-accessed) items, potentially better identifying truly dormant cache residents that have completely exited the working set.
