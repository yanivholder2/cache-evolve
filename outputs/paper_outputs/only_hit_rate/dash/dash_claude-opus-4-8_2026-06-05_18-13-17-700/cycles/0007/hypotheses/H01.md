# H01

Grafting v002's one-hop stash relocation onto the winning 3:1-geometry/touch-counter config raises hit-rate by stacking the dwell-time seam on top of the protected-tier seam.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

Take the winner `cycle/0006/v001` exactly as-is (normal-dominant 3:1
`BaselineGeometry`, two-speed promotion, per-key `target_touches` counter,
demotions landing MRU) and add the cuckoo-style stash relocation from
`cycle/0006/v002` on top of it. Concretely:

- Pull in the `get_index_with_salt` / `stash_alt_index` helpers into
  `src/shared/utils.rs` (copy v002's additions verbatim, including the
  `STASH_ALT_SALT` golden-ratio constant and the `size < 2` fallback).
- In `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`, add v002's `stash_insert` free
  function: insert the incoming item into its primary stash bucket via
  `put_at(Slot::Stash, ..)`, and if that overflows, relocate the single
  overflow victim into its `stash_alt_index` bucket at `InsertPosition::Back`
  only when `alt != primary` and that bucket is not full; otherwise drop it
  (baseline behaviour). At most one relocation hop, no cascade.
- Route every stash-bound insert through `stash_insert`: the segment's `put`
  (cold admission, MRU/`Back`) and both stash landings inside
  `demote_from_normal_bucket` (the direct-to-stash branch and the cascade
  branch) must call `stash_insert` instead of `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, ..)`.
- In `promotion_get`, before declaring a stash miss, check both the primary
  (`get_index`) and alternate (`stash_alt_index`) stash buckets for the key,
  exactly as v002 does; promote from whichever bucket holds it.
- Update `SegmentCore::contains_key`, `check_slot_legality`, and the segment
  `mod.rs` imports to treat the alternate stash bucket as a legal second home
  (copy v002's `src/dash/segments/mod.rs` changes).

Preserve the winner's promotion logic, the `target_touches` lifecycle (reset
on stash→target, drop on promote/probing/miss), and the 3:1 geometry untouched.
The only new behaviour is the stash second-home relocation.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the cycle's most important experiment: the cross-cycle open question is
whether the late slivers are additive or chase the same key pool. v001 and v002
moved different seams (protected-tier capacity/promotion vs. cold-key dwell
time) from different parents; combining them tells us directly whether the
relocation gain survives on top of the proven best.
