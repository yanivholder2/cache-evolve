# H01

Combining tier-skipping and MRU positioning for stash hits yields faster promotion to the hot tier and improves hit rate.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Modify the stash-hit promotion path in `promotion_get()` to promote directly to the probing (hot) bucket instead of target, while inserting at the MRU end (Back position) as v001 demonstrated.

Specifically, in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs` at the stash-hit branch (around line 154–157), change the promotion call from:
```
promote_to_target(core, data, InsertPosition::Back);
```
to:
```
promote_to_probing(core, data, InsertPosition::Back);
```

This combines v003's insight (tier-skipping reduces traversal cost) with v001's insight (MRU positioning accelerates promotion eligibility), testing the explicit hypothesis from the cycle 0001 insight that asked whether these two winning ideas synergize. The Back insertion ensures stash-returned items land as immediate candidates for the next tier, not buried at the LRU end.

## Rationale

Both v001 and v003 improved hit rate by targeting stash-hit acceleration, but they diverged: v001 kept the tier hierarchy (target→probing) but improved positioning within it; v003 eliminated the intermediate tier. This variant tests whether the strongest path is neither pure v001 nor pure v003, but the combination: the speed of tier-skipping plus the candidacy of MRU positioning. If successful, it would validate that both dimensions matter and amplify when combined.
