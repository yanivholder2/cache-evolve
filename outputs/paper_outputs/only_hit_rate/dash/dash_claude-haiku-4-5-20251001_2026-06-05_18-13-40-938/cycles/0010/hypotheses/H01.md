# H01

Improve hit rate by replacing LRU-based bucket eviction with FIFO-based eviction to test whether temporal ordering beyond recency dominates this workload's hit patterns.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Modify the eviction policy in `src/dash/segments/segment_core.rs` and the bucket eviction logic in `src/shared/traits/bucket.rs` to replace LRU (Least Recently Used) eviction with FIFO (First In, First Out) eviction:

1. In `segment_core.rs`, when selecting an `eviction_candidate_index()` for a full bucket, change the algorithm from returning the LRU item (tail of the access-order queue) to returning the FIFO item (insertion-order head). FIFO evicts items in the order they arrived, ignoring recent accesses.

2. Preserve all other mechanisms from v001 intact:
   - Three-tier hierarchy (Stash, Target, Probing)
   - Occupancy-aware demotion routing (bypass target when >= 70% full)
   - MRU back-insertion for demoted items (demotion recovery)
   - Front insertion for stash→target promotion
   - Back insertion for target→probing promotion

3. The eviction selection change affects all three tiers uniformly: whenever a bucket is full and an item must be evicted, select the oldest (FIFO) resident rather than the least-recently-used (LRU) resident.

This tests whether the 0.684 ceiling is constrained by LRU's assumption that recent access predicts reuse. FIFO removes recency bias and treats all bucket residents equally, allowing the tier hierarchy and promotion discipline to drive differentiation. If the workload exhibits bursty or non-locality-dominated patterns, FIFO's simpler ordering may be better suited than LRU's recency tracking.
