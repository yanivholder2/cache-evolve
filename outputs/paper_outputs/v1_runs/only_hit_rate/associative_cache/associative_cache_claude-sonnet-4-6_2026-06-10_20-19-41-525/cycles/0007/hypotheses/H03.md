# H03

Reducing the `lfu_counter` cap from 4 to 2 in `ClassicLRU::get_from_position` will raise hit-rate by shortening warm-lock episodes: with a lower ceiling, every item returns to cold eligibility within two decay events rather than four, replenishing the cold pool more aggressively per warm-lock trigger.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

Modify only `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`; all other overlay files (associative_cache_bucket.rs with mid-point insertion, cold-first eviction, and bulk counter decay, and geometry.rs with factor-4 wide-bucket layout) are inherited unchanged from cycle/0005/v003.

In `ClassicLRU::get_from_position`, change the cap from `.min(4)` to `.min(2)`:

```rust
// CURRENT (cycle/0005/v003):
items[position].lfu_counter = items[position].lfu_counter.saturating_add(1).min(4);

// PROPOSED:
items[position].lfu_counter = items[position].lfu_counter.saturating_add(1).min(2);
```

No other line in `eviction_policy.rs` changes.

**What changes in practice:**

| counter before GET | cap=4 result | cap=2 result |
|--------------------|--------------|--------------|
| 0 (cold)           | 1            | 1            |
| 1                  | 2            | 2            |
| 2                  | 3            | 2 (capped)   |
| 3                  | 4            | 2 (capped)   |
| 4                  | 4 (capped)   | 2 (capped)   |

With cap=2, any item that has been accessed at least twice saturates at counter 2. After one bulk-decay event, it drops to counter 1 (still warm). After a second decay event, it drops to counter 0 (cold, eligible for cold-first eviction). This guarantees that no item requires more than two warm-lock events to return to the cold pool, versus four with the prior cap.

**Why this is different from the "threshold tightening" failure (cycle/0003/v002):** that experiment raised the *eviction threshold* — items with counter ≤ 1 were treated as cold-evictable before their counter reached 0, which caused hot-set members accessed just once to be evicted prematurely. This proposal leaves the eviction threshold at `counter == 0` unchanged; only the maximum value an item's counter can accumulate is halved. Items with counter=1 or counter=2 are still fully protected (not cold-evictable) under the same criterion as before.

**Hypothesis:** with a cap of 2 and a bucket size of 64, warm-lock events (all counters >= 1) are resolved more quickly because a higher fraction of items have low counters (1 or 2) and become cold after fewer decay passes. The cold pool replenishes faster, so subsequent evictions find cold candidates without triggering another decay, reducing per-operation overhead during the recovery window. The risk is that highly-accessed items cycle through cold eligibility faster, but given the full-stack's proven robustness to decay events and the counter-cap-inertness observed in cycle/0005/v003, this level of change is worth probing.
