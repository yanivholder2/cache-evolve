# H03

Capping `lfu_counter` at a maximum value of 4 in cycle/0004/v001's `get_from_position` will improve hit-rate by ensuring that even very frequently-accessed items can be returned to cold status within a bounded number of decay events, preventing long-lived hot entries from permanently blocking warm-lock recovery.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` only; all other overlay files from
cycle/0004/v001 (`associative_cache_bucket.rs` with mid-point insertion and
counter-decay `evict_item`, `geometry.rs` with factor-4 wide-bucket layout) are
preserved unchanged.

In the `ClassicLRU` implementation of `get_from_position`, change the counter increment
from unbounded addition to a saturating increment capped at 4:

```rust
fn get_from_position(&self, items: &mut Vec<Item<K, V>>, position: usize) -> usize {
    // Cap counter at 4 so no item requires more than 4 decay events to become cold.
    items[position].lfu_counter = items[position].lfu_counter.saturating_add(1).min(4);
    let item = items.remove(position);
    items.push(item);
    items.len() - 1
}
```

The `eviction_candidate_index` body is unchanged (cold-first scan with `unwrap_or(0)`
LRU fallback, inherited from v001's overlay). The cap constant 4 is chosen to preserve
meaningful frequency discrimination between "touched once" (1) and "accessed many times"
(4) while bounding the worst-case decay-to-cold recovery to 4 warm-lock events.

## Rationale

In cycle/0004/v001, counters grow without bound: an item accessed 100 times reaches
`lfu_counter = 100`. After each warm-lock decay event it drops by 1, so it takes 100
decay invocations before it can become cold and be evicted by the cold-first path. On a
long trace with a stable hot set, these high-counter items accumulate and effectively
become permanently warm — each warm-lock event decays their counters imperceptibly.
Capping at 4 means any item, no matter how hot, returns to cold status within 4
consecutive warm-lock firings. This tightens the feedback loop between warm-lock
detection and cold-pool restoration. The value 4 still distinguishes items accessed once
from genuinely recurrent items (accessed 4+ times), preserving the frequency signal
that cold-first eviction relies on for the LRU fallback.
