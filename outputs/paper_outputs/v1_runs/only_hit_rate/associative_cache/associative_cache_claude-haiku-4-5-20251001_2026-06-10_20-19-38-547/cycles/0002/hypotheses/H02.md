# H02

Increase stash capacity from 10% to 25% of total cache capacity to improve hit rate by capturing more evictions from collision-heavy workloads.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Modify the stash_capacity calculation in AssociativeCache::try_new() to use `total_capacity / 4` instead of `total_capacity / 10`. This increases the stash from approximately 205 items (10% of 2048) to approximately 512 items (25% of 2048), providing more room to preserve evicted items before discarding them. Keep all other stash logic identical: FIFO eviction when at capacity, secondary lookup on cache miss, and re-insertion logic unchanged. The larger capacity trades modest additional metadata overhead for a longer recovery window, assuming the workload exhibits significant working-set re-use of discarded items.
