# H02

Scale stash capacity to 30% of total cache capacity with FIFO eviction to explore saturation beyond the proven 25%, targeting improved hit rate through extended recovery capacity.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` to increase the stash capacity from 25% to 30%. Change the stash_capacity calculation from `total_capacity / 4` to `(total_capacity * 3) / 10`. Preserve the FIFO eviction policy (remove oldest item when full) and secondary lookup behavior on primary bucket misses (when a key is not found in its primary bucket, search the stash and attempt re-insertion if space is available). All other cache logic remains unchanged. The hypothesis directly tests the capacity saturation frontier that Cycle 0003 could not measure due to build failures at 20% and 30%. If 30% yields improvement, it suggests the stash mechanism still has headroom; if it fails to improve, it confirms that 25% is near saturation for this workload.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0002 validated 25% capacity (doubling from 10%) with 1.2% gain; Cycle 0003 could not test beyond due to build failures. This hypothesis probes the next natural capacity point to assess whether gains from stash scaling continue, plateau, or decline.
