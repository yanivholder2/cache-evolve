# H01

Extend age-tracked LRU eviction to the cold tier to improve hit-rate by prioritizing retention of re-accessed items in cold storage, mirroring the hot-tier LRU strategy proven in the parent.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

In the `AssociativeCache<K, V>` implementation, extend LRU-based eviction from the hot tier (as implemented in v002) to the cold tier as well.

**Cold-tier LRU eviction** (in `add_to_cold_stash()`): When the cold stash is at capacity and needs to evict an item to make room for a newly evicted bucket item, instead of removing the oldest item by insertion order (FIFO via `self.cold_stash.remove(0)`), scan the cold tier and remove the item with the **oldest last_accessed_time** (least recently accessed). This mirrors the hot-tier LRU logic already present in `promote_cold_to_hot()`.

**Timestamp tracking**: Ensure that when items enter the cold stash (from bucket evictions), their `last_accessed_time` is set to the current `Instant::now()`. Items already in cold stash retain their timestamp across accesses (do not update on cold-tier access, only on promotion to hot tier).

**Eviction logic**: In `add_to_cold_stash()`, replace the hardcoded `self.cold_stash.remove(0)` with a scan to find the index of the cold-tier item with minimum `last_accessed_time`, then remove that item. This ensures cold stash retains items showing activity patterns, even while waiting in cold storage.

Preserve all other aspects of the two-tier mechanism: the 20%/5% partition, promotion-on-hit from cold to hot, and hot-tier LRU already proven by v002. The change is scoped entirely to cold-tier eviction ordering.

This tests whether temporal recency matters for eviction decisions in the cold tier as well as hot, potentially unlocking further incremental gains by ensuring the stash buffer prioritizes items that demonstrate re-access patterns at any tier.
