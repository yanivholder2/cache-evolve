# H03

Use 30% stash capacity with FIFO eviction to test whether expanding the stash beyond 25% continues to yield hit-rate improvements or plateaus.

## Parent

## Changes

Implement a bounded stash mechanism in AssociativeCache with capacity calculated as `total_capacity / 3.33` (approximately 30% of total cache), extending the capacity scaling axis beyond cycle 0002's proven 25% variant. Insert all evicted items into the stash using FIFO eviction: when full, remove the first item (index 0) to make room. Perform secondary stash lookup on primary bucket misses. This variant tests capacity saturation by extrapolating beyond v002; if hit rate continues to improve significantly, raw capacity remains the binding constraint. If gains flatten or diminish relative to the 10%→25% jump (which yielded 1.2%), it provides evidence of saturation and justifies deprioritizing further capacity expansion in favor of filtering or ordering refinements.
