# H01

Apply occupancy-aware selective insertion to the 25% stash capacity to improve hit rate by combining larger recovery buffer with focus on high-value evictions.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Starting from v002's 25% stash capacity implementation, add occupancy-aware selective insertion filtering. In the `put()` method, after a bucket eviction occurs, check the bucket's post-eviction occupancy against a 75% threshold of bucket_size. Insert the evicted item into the stash only if `(bucket_occupancy * 100) >= (self.bucket_size * 75)`. This prevents low-value evictions from lightly-loaded buckets from polluting the stash, while preserving a large 25% capacity buffer focused on items actually lost to congestion. Maintain FIFO eviction logic within the stash itself. This tests whether selective insertion compounds with capacity expansion—the cycle 0002 insight noted that v003's filtering didn't combine with v001's capacity in direct head-to-head testing; this variant isolates whether filtering becomes synergistic with v002's larger proven-effective buffer.
