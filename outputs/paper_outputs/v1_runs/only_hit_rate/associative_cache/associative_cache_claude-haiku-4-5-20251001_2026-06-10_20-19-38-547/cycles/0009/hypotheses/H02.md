# H02

Implement age-tracked hot-tier eviction using last-access timestamps to preferentially evict older items, enabling more sophisticated temporal prioritization than simple FIFO within the hot tier.

## Parent

cycle/0008/v001

## Changes

Modify the `Item<K, V>` struct in `src/shared/item.rs` to include an optional `last_accessed_time` field tracking the timestamp of the item's most recent access (initialization at item creation or promotion to hot tier).

In `AssociativeCache<K, V>`, replace the simple FIFO eviction mechanism for the hot stash with an age-weighted strategy:

**Hot-tier eviction** (in `promote_cold_to_hot()` and related promotion paths): When the hot stash is at capacity and needs to make room for a newly promoted item, instead of removing the oldest item by insertion order (FIFO), scan the hot tier and remove the item with the **oldest last_accessed_time** (least recently accessed in absolute terms). Maintain the existing promotion-on-hit logic but update `last_accessed_time` whenever a hot-tier item is accessed.

**Initialization**: Set `last_accessed_time` when an item is first created (put operation) or promoted to hot tier (get operation on cold-tier hit). Update it on every hot-tier hit.

Preserve all other aspects of the two-tier mechanism: cold-tier FIFO capture, promotion from cold to hot on re-access, asymmetric 20%/5% partition. The change is scoped entirely to hot-tier eviction ordering.

This allows the cache to prioritize retention of recently used items over older ones, exploiting temporal locality patterns that may vary across the workload's re-access distribution.
