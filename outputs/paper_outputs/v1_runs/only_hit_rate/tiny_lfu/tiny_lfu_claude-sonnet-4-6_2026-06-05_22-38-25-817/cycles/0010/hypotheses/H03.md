# H03

Widening the probationary segment from 10% to 15% of capacity (reducing protected to 85%) will give borderline-frequency items more residence time to accumulate the 6 accesses required for promotion, raising hit rate by reducing premature eviction of warming items.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, inside `try_new()`, change:

```rust
let protected_capacity = (capacity * 9) / 10;
```

to:

```rust
let protected_capacity = (capacity * 17) / 20;
```

This yields protected_capacity ≈ 85% and probationary_capacity ≈ 15% of total capacity. At capacity=2048 this changes protected from ~1843 slots to ~1741 slots and probationary from ~205 slots to ~307 slots (~50% larger probationary buffer).

Update the struct-level doc comment to reflect the new 85/15 split and the rationale. All other constants and logic remain unchanged: PROMO_THRESHOLD=6, `was_protected` bypass, k=4 probationary victim scan, conservative CMS update, 8× sketch width.

### Direction rationale

Previous split-ratio tests probed the direction of *increasing* the protected segment: 80/20 → 90/10 produced a marginal gain (cycle 0002); 90/10 → 95/5 produced no gain (cycle 0005). The opposite direction — *shrinking* protected and *widening* probationary — has not been evaluated. Under PROMO_THRESHOLD=6, an item must be accessed at least 6 times before promotion. With only ~205 probationary slots available at the current 90/10 split, any item that takes more than ~205 eviction cycles to accumulate 6 accesses will be victimised before it qualifies. Widening probationary to ~307 slots reduces this competition pressure and gives the k=4 victim scan more space within which to find a genuinely cold eviction candidate rather than an unlucky warm one.
