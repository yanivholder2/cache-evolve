# H01

Enlarging the SLRU protected segment from 80% to 90% of capacity gives hot items a larger stable sanctuary and raises hit rate on Zipfian traces.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v003

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the protected-capacity formula from 80 % to 90 %:

```rust
// before (v003)
let protected_capacity = (capacity * 4) / 5;

// after (H01)
let protected_capacity = (capacity * 9) / 10;
```

`probationary_capacity` is derived as `capacity - protected_capacity`, so it shrinks from 20 % to 10 % automatically — no other field changes are needed.

The 80/20 split in v003 was chosen arbitrarily and the cycle-insight flagged it as the most immediate lever to probe. With only 20 % probationary capacity, items that are demoted out of protected frequently displace items that haven't yet had a chance to re-enter protected, reducing effective protection. A 90/10 split extends the protected zone at the cost of a smaller probationary staging area; the hypothesis is that on a Zipfian workload — where a small hot set dominates — the extended protected zone covers more of that hot set and the reduced probationary capacity is an acceptable trade.

Preserve all other logic unchanged: the existing frequency-gated admission (`incoming_freq > victim_freq`), the SLRU demotion on protected overflow (`prot_head` demoted to `prob_push_back`), and the eviction from `prob_head` when the cache is full.
