# H01

Quadrupling the count-min sketch width again (factor 16→64) further reduces estimator collisions and raises hit-rate, or reveals the fidelity plateau.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR` from `16`
to `64`. Leave every other value and every policy mechanic untouched:
`SKETCH_DEPTH` stays `4`, the `try_new` width formula
`(capacity * SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR).max(MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH).next_power_of_two()`
is unchanged, and the increment / estimate / victim-selection logic is
identical to the parent. This is a pure memory-for-accuracy scaling of the
estimator. The parent (v003) won by moving the factor 4→16; this probes
whether the same upstream lever keeps paying off at 64 or whether the
accuracy gain plateaus once sketch cells stop colliding with the trace's
distinct-key count. Preserve the `next_power_of_two()` rounding and
`MIN_SKETCH_WIDTH` floor so small capacities behave unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Estimator fidelity is the single durable lever identified across cycles 0001
and 0002. Width is the cleanest axis to sweep and v003's win sits exactly on
it; one more 4x step localizes the plateau before spending cycles elsewhere.
