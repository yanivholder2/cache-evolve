# H03

Shrink v003's protected-tier cap from four-fifths to two-thirds of capacity to enlarge the probationary buffer and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the protected-tier sizing from `let protected_cap =
(capacity * 4) / 5;` to `let protected_cap = (capacity * 2) / 3;`, leaving the
subsequent `.min(capacity.saturating_sub(1))` clamp and every other line
untouched. This rebalances the SLRU split toward a larger probationary segment,
giving the admission gate more probationary residents to screen before a key is
forced into the protected tier and reducing premature protected-head demotion
churn. Preserve all other behaviour: promotion/demotion logic in `promote()`,
the single-victim eviction at `prob_head`, sketch width, and all linked-list
bookkeeping stay exactly as in v003. This is an isolated single-constant tuning
probe so its effect can be read cleanly against the parent.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004's open question asks directly whether the SLRU lever is marginal due
to *tuning* (smaller protected fraction) versus a workload ceiling; this is the
cheapest clean test of the smaller-protected-fraction hypothesis.
