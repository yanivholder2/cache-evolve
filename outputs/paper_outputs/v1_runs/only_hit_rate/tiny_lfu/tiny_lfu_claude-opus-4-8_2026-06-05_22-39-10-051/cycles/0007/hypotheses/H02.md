# H02

Admitting a proven-hot newcomer directly into the protected tier (instead of always landing in probation) raises hit-rate by rescuing repeatedly-requested-then-evicted keys before they are churned out again.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v002

## Changes

This probes an SLRU placement point not yet touched: *where* an admitted key
lands. Today every admitted key enters the probationary tail and must earn a
later hit to reach protected. A key that keeps being requested and evicted
accumulates a high sketch estimate yet may be evicted from probation again
before it ever records a hit — the recurring "head hides a hotter key" case,
attacked from the admission side rather than the eviction side.

Modify only the full-cache admission branch of `put()` (lines 115–130). After
the existing gate `if incoming_freq > victim_freq` removes the probationary
victim and frees `victim_idx`, decide placement for the incoming key:

- If `self.protected_len < self.protected_cap` (spare protected room) AND
  `incoming_freq` strictly exceeds the protected LRU head's estimate
  (`self.prot_head.map(|h| self.sketch.estimate(&self.nodes[h].key))`, treating
  an empty protected segment as estimate 0), then reuse `victim_idx`, set its
  key/value, and place it via `push_back_prot(victim_idx)` with
  `self.protected_len += 1` — admitting it directly to protected.
- Otherwise fall back to the current behaviour exactly: `push_back_prob(victim_idx)`.

Guard the direct-to-protected path with the `protected_len < protected_cap`
spare-room check so no protected node is demoted here — this keeps the change
self-contained, avoids a second eviction, and preserves the invariant that the
probationary segment always retains a victim (the freed slot is the only one
that moved). Do not alter the admission gate itself, `get()`, `promote()`, or
the sketch.

## Rationale (optional)

Every prior structural lever fired on the eviction/demotion side; admission
*placement* is the one untested steering point. A newcomer hotter than the
protected head is exactly the key SLRU is meant to shield, so seating it in
protected immediately — only when there is free room — is directionally safe
and targets the churn case without trampling the protected set.
