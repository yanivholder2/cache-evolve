## Changes

Modify the admission gate logic in the `put()` method (currently line 118: `if incoming_freq > victim_freq`) to use a hybrid score instead of frequency alone. Compute `incoming_age_factor = max(1, current_time - RECENCY_HORIZON)` (penalize stale items) and `gate_score = incoming_freq + (current_time - 0) / RECENCY_SCALE`, where `RECENCY_HORIZON` bounds how far back to measure recency and `RECENCY_SCALE` normalizes age to the frequency scale. Replace the single comparison `incoming_freq > victim_freq` with `gate_score > (victim_freq * GATE_THRESHOLD_MULTIPLIER)`. This allows the gate to admit items with moderate frequency if they are being accessed recently, while still filtering stale, low-frequency items. The hypothesis mirrors the success of frequency-age hybrids in victim selection: just as the eviction layer benefits from combining orthogonal signals, the admission layer may benefit from similar hybridization to better distinguish candidates.
