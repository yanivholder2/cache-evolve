# H03

Relax the admission gate to accept tied frequencies and retain more hot items at capacity boundaries.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the comparison operator in the admission contest within the `put()` method (currently around line 93).

Change the condition from:
```
if incoming_freq > victim_freq {
```

to:
```
if incoming_freq >= victim_freq {
```

This is the only change. All other logic remains intact: frequency increment, victim selection (LRU head), eviction/slot reuse, and map updates all proceed as before.

## Rationale

The baseline's strict `>` gate rejects items with frequency equal to the victim. When two items have the same frequency estimate (either due to sketch collision or because they were equally popular), the resident victim is kept and the newcomer is dropped. By accepting ties with `>=`, incoming items with matching victim frequency will be admitted and promoted to MRU. This increases the flow of newly-arrived items into the cache even when frequency estimates are uncertain (low-frequency range or high collision). The change trades perfect frequency-order for broader admission, potentially increasing hit rate on workloads with many similarly-hot items or where recency can break frequency ties.
