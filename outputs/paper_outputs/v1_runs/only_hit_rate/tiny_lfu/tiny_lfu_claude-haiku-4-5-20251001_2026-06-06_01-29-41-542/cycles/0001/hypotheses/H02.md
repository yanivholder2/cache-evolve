# H02

Implement periodic sketch decay to prevent frequency inflation from early-accessed items and improve responsiveness to recent access patterns.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Add a `decay_counter` field to `TinyLfuCache<K, V>` initialized to 0, and modify the `put()` method to trigger decay.

After admitting or rejecting an item in `put()` (at the end of the function), check if `decay_counter >= capacity`. If true, iterate through all rows and columns of `sketch.counts` and divide each counter by 2 (bitshift right by 1), then reset `decay_counter` to 0.

On every call to `put()`, increment `decay_counter` by 1 before the decay check. This means decay triggers approximately once every `capacity` puts.

Preserve all existing increment/estimate logic. The CountMinSketch methods remain unchanged; decay operates only on the counts array in-place. Ensure the decay loop maintains the depth and width bounds of the sketch (iterate `0..self.depth` and `0..self.width`).

## Rationale

The baseline sketch accumulates counters monotonically until saturation (u32::MAX). Items accessed early in a workload retain high frequencies indefinitely, even if they become cold later. This causes the admission gate to unfairly favor stale items over newly-arrived hot ones. Periodic decay (halving every capacity puts) resets the frequency scale, making estimates reflect recent behavior. The half-life approach is conservative: items maintain relative ordering while giving newer accesses proportional influence.
