# H01

Extend the width-optimized sketch with adaptive counter recycling when saturation occurs to preserve frequency discrimination at high occupancy and increase hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

Modify the Count-Min sketch's counter update logic in the increment operation: instead of saturating counters at u32::MAX when they overflow, divide all sketch counters by 2 (right-shift by 1 bit) when any counter reaches saturation. This allows frequency information to continue accumulating dynamically while preserving relative ordering of hot vs. cold items. The recycling preserves the sketch's ability to discriminate between items during admission decisions even after long runs, which currently loses precision when counters max out. Keep the strict admission gate logic and 16x width from v001 intact; only change the counter saturation strategy.
