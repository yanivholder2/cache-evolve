# H03

Combine width and depth improvements by setting SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR to 8 and SKETCH_DEPTH to 8 to maximize frequency estimate precision and hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify two constants in src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs: increase SKETCH_WIDTH_FACTOR from 4 to 8 (matching v001's winning change) and increase SKETCH_DEPTH from 4 to 8 (adding stronger hash function independence). The resulting sketch will have 8 rows of counters, each with width = (capacity * 8).max(16).next_power_of_two(). This creates a much larger frequency estimation surface with both broader width and deeper independence. All other behavior—admission threshold, increment capping, LRU ordering, and eviction logic—remains unchanged. The change is purely to the sketch's dimensions.

## Rationale (optional)

H01 and H02 test width and depth independently. This hypothesis tests whether the benefits compound when both are applied together. On Zipfian traces where hot items benefit from precise frequency estimates, simultaneous improvements in width (horizontal scalability) and depth (independence) could yield multiplicative gains in hit rate.
