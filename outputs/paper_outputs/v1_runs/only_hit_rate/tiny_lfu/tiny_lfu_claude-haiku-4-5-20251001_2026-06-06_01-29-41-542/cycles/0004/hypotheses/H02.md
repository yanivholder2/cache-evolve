# H02

Strengthen frequency weighting in eviction victim selection with non-linear scaling to better protect hot items and improve hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Implement frequency-recency eviction victim scoring with a non-linear frequency damping term. Score candidates as (current_time - last_access_time) / (sqrt(frequency) + 1) instead of the linear (frequency + 1) form tested in cycle/0003/v002. The square root dampens the protective effect of high frequencies (making highly-hot items only slightly more protected than moderately-hot items), while keeping the age term linear. This may better discriminate between the coldest items in the cache by reducing the dominance of frequency differences and allowing age to participate more equally in victim selection. Keep baseline sketch, sketch parameters, depth, width factor, admission gate logic, and related infrastructure intact.
