# H03

Normalize eviction victim selection by relative frequency to better discriminate items much colder than the working set and improve hit-rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Implement frequency-recency eviction victim scoring using relative frequency: score = (current_time - last_access_time) / (1 + frequency / max_frequency), where frequency is the sketch estimate, max_frequency is the highest frequency among all cached items, and age is (current_time - last_access_time). Iterate through all cached items, compute this score for each, and evict the item with the maximum score. This normalizes frequency relative to the hottest item in the cache, helping identify items that are much colder than the working set peak. Items barely above zero frequency will have high scores even if young, while items that are warm relative to the working set get protected. Keep baseline sketch parameters, depth, width factor, admission gate logic, and related infrastructure intact.
