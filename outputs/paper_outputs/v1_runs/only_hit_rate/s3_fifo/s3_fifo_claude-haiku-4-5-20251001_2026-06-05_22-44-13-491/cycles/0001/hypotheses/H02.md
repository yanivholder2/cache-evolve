# H02

Re-admit items from the ghost queue with initial frequency 2 to reflect their prior survival, giving ghost hits better protection in main queue and improving hit rate.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the `put()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to assign a higher initial frequency to items re-admitted from the ghost queue. In the branch where `in_ghost` is true (lines 85-91), change the push to main queue from `self.main.push_front(Entry::new(key, value));` to first create an entry and set its frequency: `let mut entry = Entry::new(key, value); entry.freq = 2; self.main.push_front(entry);`. This signals that ghost hits represent proven reusable items (they survived eviction once) and deserve more protection than cold new items entering small queue. Ghost-hit items will then require 2 eviction passes in main before permanent eviction, vs. 3-4 for items with freq=0 that survive promotion.
