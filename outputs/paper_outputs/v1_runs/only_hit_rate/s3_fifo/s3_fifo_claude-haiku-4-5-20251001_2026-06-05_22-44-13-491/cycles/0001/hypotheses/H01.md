# H01

Increase the frequency depth in main-queue eviction from 3 to 4 to give hot items more second chances before permanent eviction, improving hit rate on sustained reuse patterns.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant at the top of `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 4;`. This extends the maximum frequency counter, allowing items to survive up to 4 eviction rounds in the main queue before being permanently discarded. Items in the main queue that survive an eviction pass (freq > 0) are decremented and re-inserted; with MAX_FREQ=4, they get one additional pass compared to the baseline before reaching freq=0 and eviction. This protects moderately hot items longer and should improve hit rate on workloads exhibiting moderate-to-high reuse.
