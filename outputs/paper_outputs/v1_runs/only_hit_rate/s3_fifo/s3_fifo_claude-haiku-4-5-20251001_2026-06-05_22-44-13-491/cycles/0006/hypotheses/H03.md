# H03

Jump the small-queue admission ratio to 1/12 to aggressively probe the utilization saturation boundary and rapidly locate the selectivity cliff.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small queue capacity. Change the line containing `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 12).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This reduces the small queue from the baseline 1/4 to 1/12 of total capacity, enforcing dramatically tighter frequency-based filtering at the admission gate. Keep MAX_FREQ=3 and all other logic intact: frequency increment on access, freq > 1 threshold for promotion, main-queue eviction unchanged. This skips intermediate ratios to quickly test whether saturation has been crossed or whether the workload continues to reward even more aggressive selectivity.

## Rationale

Six consecutive cycles (baseline through cycle 0005) have shown consistent improvements from 1/4 down to 1/10 with no degradation. The cycle-0005 insight raises a critical question: "Where is the true saturation boundary?" and notes that "the absence of a cliff at 1/10 is surprising and warrants aggressive boundary search." This hypothesis takes the aggressive path by jumping two steps to 1/12, quickly determining whether saturation exists above or below 1/11. If 1/12 still improves, the boundary remains further out; if it degrades, saturation lies between 1/10 and 1/12, informing how tightly constrained the optimal ratio actually is.
