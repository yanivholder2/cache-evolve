# H02

Pair the proven 1/10 small-queue selectivity with MAX_FREQ=4 to test whether frequency-depth scaling compounds optimally at this high-selectivity boundary.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

Modify the frequency constant at the top of `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`. Change `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 3;` to `const MAX_FREQ: u8 = 4;`. Keep the small-queue ratio at 1/10 (do not change the `(capacity / 10)` line). This extends the maximum frequency counter by one level, allowing items in the main queue to survive one additional eviction round before permanent discarding. Items with freq > 0 are decremented and re-inserted; with MAX_FREQ=4, they persist longer. All other logic remains unchanged: frequency increments on access, freq > 1 for small-to-main promotion, main-queue eviction strategy identical. This isolates the frequency-depth dimension to test compounding effects at the 1/10 admission boundary.

## Rationale

The cross-cycle insights note that "frequency-depth and structural selectivity dimensions are provably independent" and that "the two-axis optimization space remains incompletely explored at high selectivity ratios (1/8+) paired with higher MAX_FREQ values." Cycle 0005 tested 1/8+MAX_FREQ=5 (v003, hit-rate 0.7247) but that lost to pure 1/10 (v002, hit-rate 0.7248). However, 1/10+MAX_FREQ=4 has not been tested. This hypothesis bridges the gap by adding modest frequency depth to the proven 1/10 selectivity, testing whether the two levers compound at the boundary where v002 currently dominates.
