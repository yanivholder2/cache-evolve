# H03

Reduce small queue size to 1/5 of capacity to increase frequency selectivity in the admission gate, improving hit rate through tighter frequency-based filtering.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the `try_new()` method in `/src/s3_fifo/mod.rs` to reduce the small queue admission size. Change the line `(capacity / 4).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` to `(capacity / 5).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` when computing `small_size`. This reduces the small queue from 1/4 to 1/5 of total capacity, narrowing the admission path and requiring items to demonstrate higher reuse frequency before earning space in the main queue. A smaller small queue creates tighter frequency-selectivity constraints, where only items with stronger frequency evidence can promote to main, potentially refining the frequency balance that v001 succeeded in tuning. This approach complements v001's frequency-depth work by adjusting the structural parameter that gates frequency accumulation.
