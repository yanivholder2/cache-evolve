# H02

Lowering the small-queue promotion threshold from `freq > 1` to `freq > 0` on top of cycle/0002/v003's ghost-precision base will improve hit rate by admitting single-access items into main, where the tighter ghost filter compensates for any quality loss.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

In `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, inside the `evict_from_small()` function (or the inline eviction block that drains the small queue), locate the condition that decides whether a candidate item graduates to main or is demoted to ghost. Change:

```rust
if tail.freq > 1 {
```

to:

```rust
if tail.freq > 0 {
```

All other logic from the v003 overlay stays unchanged:
- `ghost_size = (main_size / 2).max(1)` and the `ghost_size` struct field
- `small_size = (capacity / 10).max(1).min(capacity - 1)` (10% of capacity)
- `MAX_FREQ = 3`
- Ghost re-insertion path (ghost-hit items still bypass small and enter main with `freq: 0`)
- All existing tests from the v003 overlay are preserved

## Rationale

Cycle 0003's v002 showed `freq > 0` promotion is operationally safe but produced no measurable gain — v002 paired it with an unshrunken ghost (full `main_size`), diluting ghost signal with stale evictions and leaving the workload no better off. v003 demonstrated that ghost precision at `main_size/2` is the decisive discriminator. Combining ghost precision with `freq > 0` admission is the natural completion of the two experiments: items accessed even once in small are admitted to main rather than ghosted, and when they are eventually displaced from main the tighter ghost cap ensures only the most recently evicted keys are fast-tracked for re-admission. The prediction is that the two mechanisms reinforce each other in a way that neither achieved alone.
