1. **Deeper ghost queue**: added a `ghost_size` field set to `2 * main_size` (saturating, clamped `>= 1`), used both for the ghost `VecDeque` capacity and as the eviction cap in `evict_small()` — decoupling ghost history from `main_size`.

Front-half hits (`pos < ghost.len() / 2`, most recently evicted = "fresh") keep the original direct-to-main re-admission; back-half ("stale") hits are removed from ghost and re-admitted as a normal new `small`-queue entry (`evict_small()` if full, `push_front` with `freq = 0`).

Capacity bounds are preserved — the stale path mirrors the normal admission path, and ghost entries are non-resident — so the structural invariants (no duplicate keys, no overflow) hold.
