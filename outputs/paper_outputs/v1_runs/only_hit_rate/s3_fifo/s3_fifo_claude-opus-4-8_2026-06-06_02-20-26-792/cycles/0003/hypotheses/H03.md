# H03

Tightening the small-to-main promotion gate from two accesses (`freq > 1`) to three (`freq > 2`) will keep more two-hit-wonders out of the now-large main queue and raise hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v001

## Changes

This is an orthogonal probe of the promotion gate itself, in the
direction opposite to the one that already regressed. Cycle 0001 H01
*loosened* the gate (`freq > 0`) and lost hit-rate, showing the strict
filter is load-bearing; the untested direction is to make it stricter.

In `evict_small()` in `src/s3_fifo/mod.rs`, change the promotion
condition from `if tail.freq > 1` to `if tail.freq > 2`, so a small-queue
entry must have been accessed enough to reach `freq == 3` (the `MAX_FREQ`
ceiling) before it is promoted into `main`; everything below that is sent
to the ghost queue as today. Leave `MAX_FREQ`, the `get()` increment, the
`evict_main()` second-chance decrement, the partition sizing, and the
ghost re-admission path unchanged. The claim is that with v001's already
large main, demanding stronger reuse evidence before admission filters out
borderline two-hit keys that currently displace hotter residents. If
hit-rate instead falls, it confirms genuine two-hit keys are valuable and
`freq > 1` is the true optimum for this gate.
