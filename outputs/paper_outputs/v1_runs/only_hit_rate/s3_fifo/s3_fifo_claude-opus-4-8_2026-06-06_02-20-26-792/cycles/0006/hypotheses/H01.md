# H01

Promote a small-queue key to main eagerly the moment a get() bump raises its freq to the promotion threshold, instead of waiting for FIFO eviction, to raise hit-rate by protecting multi-hit keys before the thin admission queue drops them.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Target the small-queue hit branch in `get()` (the `self.small.iter().position(...)`
block). Today a small hit only bumps `freq` (clamped to `MAX_FREQ`) and leaves the
entry in the FIFO small queue; promotion to main happens lazily and only at
`evict_small()` time when `tail.freq > 1`. Change this so promotion timing is
eager: after the `freq` bump, if the entry's `freq` now reaches the same
promotion threshold used in `evict_small` (i.e. `freq > 1`, meaning `freq >= 2`),
immediately `remove` it from `small`, call `evict_main()` if `self.main.len() >=
self.main_size`, and `push_front` it onto `main` (mirroring the promotion path in
`evict_small`). Return the value from its new position in `main`.

Rationale: the small queue is only ~4% of capacity and pure FIFO, so a key that is
reaccessed while resident can still be evicted to ghost before its accumulated
`freq` is ever read at eviction time. Eager promotion converts an in-residence
reuse signal into protection immediately and frees a small slot for genuine
one-hit-wonders.

Invariants to preserve: do not change the `evict_small` promotion threshold or the
direct-to-main ghost re-admission path; keep `MAX_FREQ` clamping; keep main's
move-to-front behaviour for main-resident hits; the total resident count
(`small.len() + main.len()`) must stay within capacity (removing from small before
pushing to main, evicting main first when full, keeps the bound).
