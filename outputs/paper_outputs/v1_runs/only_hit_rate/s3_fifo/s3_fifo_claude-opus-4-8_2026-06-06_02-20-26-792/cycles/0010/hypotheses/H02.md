# H02

Make the small admission queue recency-aware by moving an entry to the front on a small-queue hit, so the FIFO sheds the genuinely coldest admission candidate and raises hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v001

## Changes

In `S3Fifo::get`, modify the small-queue hit branch. It currently does an
in-place `freq` bump and returns `&self.small[pos].value`, leaving small in pure
insertion order. Change it to mirror main's proven move-to-front discipline:
`remove(pos)` the entry, apply the saturating `freq` bump (clamped to
`MAX_FREQ`), `self.small.push_front(entry)`, and return
`&self.small.front().expect(...).value`. This makes `evict_small`'s tail pop
(`pop_back`) shed the least-recently-accessed admission candidate instead of the
oldest-inserted one.

Invariants to preserve: do not change the `freq > 1` promotion test in
`evict_small`, the ghost tagging (`from_main: false` for weak small evictions),
the adaptive-split controller, or any main-side logic. Resident counts and
`small_size + main_size == capacity` are unaffected — only intra-small ordering
changes.

## Rationale (optional)

The cross-cycle record shows explicit move-to-front recency was load-bearing for
main ("ordering discipline ... beat position-blind FIFO"), but that win was only
ever applied to the main queue; the small admission filter remains strict FIFO.
On a LIRS-style recency trace, a small key that was just re-accessed is a better
retention bet than one inserted earlier and untouched — reordering small lets
quick-demotion fall on the truly cold tail. This is the one queue the ordering
lever has never been applied to.
