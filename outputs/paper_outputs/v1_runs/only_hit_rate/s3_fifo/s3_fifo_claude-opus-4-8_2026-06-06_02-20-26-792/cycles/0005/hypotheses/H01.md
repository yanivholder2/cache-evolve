# H01

Make main second-chance eviction recency-preserving by decrementing survivors in place instead of rotating them to the front, raising hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In `evict_main()`, the current loop does `pop_back()`, and on a survivor
(`freq > 0`) decrements `freq` and `push_front`s it. That `push_front`
scrambles the most-recently-used ordering that v003's move-to-front
`get()` path carefully builds: a cold-but-not-yet-zero tail entry jumps
ahead of genuinely hot keys, polluting the MRU front.

Rewrite `evict_main()` so the second-chance pass walks the queue from the
tail toward the head **without reordering survivors**. Concretely: scan
indices from `main.len() - 1` downward; for each entry with `freq > 0`,
decrement its `freq` in place and continue toward the head; evict (remove)
the first entry encountered whose `freq == 0`. If the entire pass finds no
`freq == 0` entry (all were decremented), evict the original tail
(`pop_back()`) to guarantee forward progress and an unchanged eviction
count. Do not call `push_front` on any survivor. This keeps the head-is-hot
ordering from `get()` intact so the eviction scan always reaches the
genuinely coldest residents.

Preserve all invariants: exactly one entry is removed per `evict_main()`
call, capacity is never exceeded, and the `freq` decrement semantics
(saturating at 0, second-chance protection for hot keys) are unchanged.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 showed ordering discipline is the only live lever but paid only
a sliver; the leak may be that eviction itself corrupts the recency order
get() establishes. Fixing that compounds the move-to-front win without
adding protection to weak-signal keys.
