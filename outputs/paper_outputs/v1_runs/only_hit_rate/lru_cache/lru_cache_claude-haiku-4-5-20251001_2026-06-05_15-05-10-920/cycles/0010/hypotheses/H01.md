# H01

Pure LRU eviction without frequency tracking will degrade hit-rate below 0.70, confirming frequency discrimination is essential to the cache's performance.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

Remove frequency tracking entirely from the eviction decision. Revert to pure LRU by eliminating the `freq_bucket` field from the Node struct and all frequency-based logic. In the eviction path, evict only the head (oldest) item in the linked list without consulting any frequency counter. On access (get/put), still promote items to the tail to maintain LRU ordering, but do not increment or track any frequency metric. This test confirms whether frequency discrimination is the critical factor driving the incumbent's 0.7334 hit-rate or if pure recency suffices on this workload.

## Rationale

The incumbent's 0.7334 hit-rate vs. baseline's 0.665 stems from protecting hot items via frequency counters with periodic decay. This hypothesis directly tests the necessity of frequency as an eviction signal: if pure LRU degrades significantly (< 0.70), it confirms frequency is essential; if it remains competitive, it suggests the 0.75× decay mechanism may be overhead without commensurate gain. This is a saturation boundary test.
