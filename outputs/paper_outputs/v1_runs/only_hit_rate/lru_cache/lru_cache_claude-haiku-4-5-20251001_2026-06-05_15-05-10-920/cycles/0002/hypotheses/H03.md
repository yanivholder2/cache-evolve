# H03

Improve hit rate on shifting workloads by implementing periodic frequency counter decay to allow new access patterns to gradually replace old hotspots.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Add a `frequency_reset_counter: u64` field to `LruCache`, initialized to 0. This counter increments on each operation (get or put).

Modify `get()` and `put()` to increment `frequency_reset_counter` after each operation. Define a reset threshold as `capacity * 16` (or `capacity * 10` if 16 is too conservative).

Every time `frequency_reset_counter` reaches a multiple of this threshold, execute a frequency reset: iterate through all nodes in the arena and perform a right-shift on each `freq` value: `freq >>= 1` (divide by 2, rounding down). This halves all frequency counters while preserving their relative ordering.

After the reset, reset `frequency_reset_counter` to 0 and continue.

Preserve all other aspects of v001: the frequency-based eviction logic (minimum-frequency victim selection), MRU promotion, and capacity bounds remain unchanged.

## Rationale

The open question about saturation behavior suggests that ancient hotspots may monopolize cache weight indefinitely. Periodic decay allows the cache to adapt to workload shifts: items that were hot in the past gradually lose frequency advantage, allowing newly-popular items to gain relative weight. This is especially relevant for long-running workloads or traces that exhibit phase changes, where the most-accessed items early in the trace may not remain the most-accessed later.
