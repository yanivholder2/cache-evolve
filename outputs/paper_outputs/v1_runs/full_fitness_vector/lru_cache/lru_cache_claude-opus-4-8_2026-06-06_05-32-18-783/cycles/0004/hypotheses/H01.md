# H01

Narrow the SLRU champion's recency links from `Option<usize>` to `u32` sentinels to cut metadata-size from 56 bytes toward the 24-32-byte class while holding its 0.724 hit-rate.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Take the SLRU-plus-fast-hasher champion (`cycle/0002/v002`) and re-encode
the two recency links in `Node<K,V>` as bare `u32` indices with a
`const NIL: u32 = u32::MAX` sentinel for "no link", replacing the two
`Option<usize>` fields. A no-niche `Option<usize>` costs 16 bytes each, so
the two links dominate the node footprint; narrowing both to 4-byte words
drops `Node<u64,u64>` from 56 bytes into the 32-byte class.

Centralise the conversions in two `#[inline]` helpers `fn idx(link: u32)
-> Option<usize>` (mapping `NIL` to `None`, else zero-extend) and
`fn link(index: usize) -> u32`, and update every head/tail field
(`prot_head`, `prot_tail`, `prob_head`, `prob_tail`) plus the
`set_head`/`set_tail`/`unlink`/`push_back` helpers to store `u32`. Read
each link once into a local at the top of a routine and write it back once
per structural mutation so traversal stays a free zero-extend on the
64-bit target (avoid v002's per-access cast penalty — do NOT sprinkle
`as usize` through the bodies).

Stretch goal to reach the 24-byte class: pack the `protected` flag into
the high bit of the `prev` link via masking helpers, since arena indices
never approach `2^31` at the harness's capacities; if that proves fiddly,
keeping `protected` as a separate `bool` (32-byte node) is still an
acceptable, large metadata win. Preserve every SLRU invariant exactly:
the two-segment probation/protected geometry, the `prot_cap` soft bound,
victim selection (probation LRU first, protected LRU fallback), the
demote-on-overflow path, and the segment-MRU fast path on get/put-update.
Hit-rate and the eviction policy must be byte-for-byte unchanged; only the
node representation shrinks.
