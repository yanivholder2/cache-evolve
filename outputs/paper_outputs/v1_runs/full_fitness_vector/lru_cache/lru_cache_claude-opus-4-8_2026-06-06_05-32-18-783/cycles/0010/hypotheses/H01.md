# H01

Stepping the protected soft cap from 7/8 to a gentle 8/9 raises hit-rate by retaining slightly more promoted entries without crossing the known starvation cliff.

## Parent

cycle/0009/v002

## Changes

In `try_new`, change the protected sizing fraction from `capacity * 7 / 8`
to `capacity * 8 / 9` when computing `prot_cap` (keep the existing
`.max(1).min(capacity.saturating_sub(1))` clamps and the `as u32` store
into the packed control block). Touch nothing else: the `get`/`put` fast
paths, `demote_protected_lru`, victim selection, and the 24-byte node
layout all stay byte-for-byte identical.

Motivation: Cycle 0006 found free hit-rate at 7/8 and Cycle 0007 showed
15/16 (a +128-slot jump at capacity 2048) slams into a starvation cliff
that wrecks latency. 8/9 is a far gentler step (~+28 slots at 2048),
sitting just above 7/8 and well below the cliff. The cross-cycle finding
that "small geometry nudges can buy hit-rate for free even though large
ones don't — effects are magnitude-dependent" predicts this finer step may
capture retention headroom the coarse 15/16 jump overshot.

Confirm: hit-rate rises above the parent's 0.7291 while get/put p50 hold
at/near the parent (no fast-path starvation). Refute: latency jumps like
the 15/16 probe, indicating the cliff begins below 8/9.

## Rationale (optional)

Builds on the joint-best hit-rate incumbent (cycle/0009/v002, 0.7291) and
probes the only unexplored slice of the geometry axis — the gap between
the proven-good 7/8 and the proven-bad 15/16 — from the bottom.
