# H02

Organize nodes into frequency-level buckets to enable O(1) eviction-candidate discovery, eliminating linear scans entirely.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v002

## Changes

Add a `Vec<Option<usize>>` structure indexed by frequency level (0, 1, 2, ..., up to a reasonable max like 16 or 32) to maintain bucket heads for each frequency tier. When a node's frequency increments (via `get()` or `put()`), unlink it from its current frequency bucket and link it to the next-higher bucket. When evicting, directly index into the lowest non-empty frequency bucket and evict a node from that bucket in O(1) time. Update the decay mechanism to shift nodes to lower buckets instead of in-place division. This eliminates the O(N) scan bottleneck in `find_evictable_node()`, preserving V002's hit-rate gains while achieving latency-predictable eviction regardless of frequency distribution.
