# H01

Enlarging the protected quota on the link-array baseline (prob_cap = capacity/8) raises hit-rate without per-op-time regression, resolving whether v003's apparent latency cost was real or measurement noise.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v002

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the probationary capacity assignment from:

```rust
let prob_cap = (capacity / 4).max(1);
```

to:

```rust
let prob_cap = (capacity / 8).max(1);
```

No other changes. The protected queue quota rises from 75% to 87.5% of total capacity (256 additional protected slots at capacity=2048), which reduces demotion pressure and keeps moderately-frequent Zipfian items resident longer. The parent (v002) has a non-zero p50 measurement floor (100 ns at p50 for put), unlike v003's parent which had an anomalously-zero floor that masked whether the cost was real. This retest isolates the quota policy change against a cleaner measurement baseline and determines whether the per-op-time signal observed in cycle/0005/v003 was genuine or artefact.

## Rationale

cycle/0005/v003 showed hit-rate=0.7291 (a real improvement over baseline) but per-op-time=100 (suspected noise from the parent's zero p50 floor). cycle/0005/v002 now provides a better parent: same structural change, non-zero p50. A single constant change carries negligible implementation risk and directly answers the open question from cycle 0005.
