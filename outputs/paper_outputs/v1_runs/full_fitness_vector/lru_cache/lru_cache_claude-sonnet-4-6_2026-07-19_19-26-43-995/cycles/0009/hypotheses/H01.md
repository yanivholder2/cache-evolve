# H01

Reducing `prob_cap` from `capacity/8` to `capacity/12` will raise hit-rate above cycle/0006/v001's 0.7291 while preserving per-op-time at 0 on workload-a.

## Parent

cycle/0006/v001

## Changes

In `try_new()`, change the single line:

```rust
let prob_cap = (capacity / 8).max(1);
```

to:

```rust
let prob_cap = (capacity / 12).max(1);
```

No other changes. This moves the protected quota from 87.5% to ~91.67%, placing it at an intermediate point between the confirmed cost-free setting (capacity/8 = 87.5%) and the confirmed latency-regressing setting (capacity/16 = 93.75%). The demotion rate scales with how far the protected queue is pushed beyond the point where it overflows; at capacity/12, overflows should be rarer than at capacity/16, potentially keeping p50 demotion frequency below the measurement floor while recovering part of the 0.7316 hit-rate peak seen at 93.75%.

## Rationale

Cycle 0008's `cycle-insight.md` explicitly recommends probing capacity/12 or capacity/24 as the next step. `cycle/0006/v001` is the cleanest parent for this test because it is the confirmed per-op-time=0 baseline at 87.5%; adding one variable (quota level) keeps the experiment interpretable.
