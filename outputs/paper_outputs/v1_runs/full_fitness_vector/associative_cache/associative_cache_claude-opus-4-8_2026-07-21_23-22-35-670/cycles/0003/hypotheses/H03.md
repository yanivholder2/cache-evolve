# H03

Insert cache-miss items at the bucket front (probationary position) so one-hit-wonder scans are evicted before previously-hot items, raising hit-rate at the 16-byte metadata floor without any per-item flag.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v003

## Changes

This is a positional, stateless alternative to H01's bucket-side reuse bit:
scan-resistance from insertion geometry alone, so it composes with v003's
16-byte `Item` (key + value, no counter, no flag).

Baseline/v003 `ClassicLRU` inserts miss items at the back (MRU) and evicts
the front (index 0), so a burst of never-reused scan items marches from MRU
toward the front and can flush genuinely hot items that have not been
touched recently. Invert the admission position instead:

- In `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs`, override the
  concrete bucket's `put` so that a **new (miss) item is inserted at the
  front, index 0** — the immediate eviction position — rather than pushed to
  the back. Update-in-place of an existing key keeps its current
  ClassicLRU behaviour (move-to-back on hit).
- Keep `ClassicLRU` eviction at the front (index 0) and keep its
  `get_from_position` move-to-back on hit. Net effect: a freshly inserted
  item sits on probation at the front; if it is hit before the next
  conflicting insert it is promoted to the back (protected region); if it is
  never hit (a scan) it is the first evicted, shielding previously-hot items
  clustered near the back. This is the 2Q/S3-FIFO probation idea expressed
  purely through position.

Scope the change to the concrete `AssociativeCacheBucket` used by the eval
(ClassicLRU) so the shared trait default `put` and its FIFO/LIFO unit tests
are untouched; adjust any local `src/associative_cache/mod.rs` test that
assumes back-insertion, but do not edit any file under `tests/`,
`test_utils/`, or the other gate-integrity paths. Preserve capacity
conservation and get-returns-put.

Success = hit-rate above v003's 0.6611 (ideally approaching v001's 0.7267)
with metadata-size held at 16.

## Rationale (optional)

Gives the cohort a second, independent path to the same prize as H01
(hit-rate lift at the metadata floor) via a completely different mechanism —
insertion geometry vs. an explicit reuse bit — so the two do not collapse
into one bet.
