# H01

Apply early-exit scanning and cache-line alignment to ClassicLRU on baseline geometry (128×16) to improve latency without widening buckets.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Modify the AssociativeCacheBucket struct to align to 64-byte cache-line boundaries by adding `#[repr(align(64))]`. Implement early-exit optimization in the `get_position()` method: instead of scanning the entire bucket, return immediately upon locating the key without examining remaining items. Keep the baseline geometry (128 buckets × 16 items per bucket) and ClassicLRU eviction policy unchanged.

The optimization targets the observation from v004/v003 that cache-line alignment and early-exit reduce p99 latency spikes in ClassicLRU's linear bucket scanning. This hypothesis tests whether those micro-optimizations, which succeeded on 32×64 geometry, generalize backward to the baseline's narrower bucket configuration, improving latency without geometry risk.
