# H02

Reorganize bucket items by access recency to improve CPU prefetch effectiveness and reduce p99 tail latencies while preserving 32×64 geometry and micro-optimizations.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Extend the `AssociativeCacheBucket` to track `last_access_epoch` for each item (same field as H01, but here we use it for structural optimization, not eviction policy).

Every 16 eviction operations, trigger a reorganization phase in the bucket's `evict_one()` method: iterate through the Vec items, identify the items accessed most recently (highest `last_access_epoch`), and reorder them so that hot items appear at the beginning of the Vec.

Use a simple in-place swap approach: partition the Vec so that items accessed within the last N operations come first (where N is a tunable threshold, e.g., 32 operations). This reordering is O(n) per reorganization but happens infrequently (every 16 evictions).

Preserve the existing `#[repr(align(64))]` cache-line alignment, the pre-reserved capacity, and the early-exit logic in `get_position()`. The reorganization happens between evictions, not during get/put hot path.

## Rationale

Cycle 0004's v003 succeeded by optimizing the hit path (early-exit) on the 32×64 geometry. Cycle 0005 showed that this optimization doesn't generalize, but also showed the hypothesis was sound for its target geometry. This variant seeks to strengthen v003's prefetch locality by explicitly clustering hot items at the vector's head, enabling CPU hardware prefetch to pull multiple items into cache on a single load. This addresses a potential gap in v003's implementation: while early-exit reduces iterations, it doesn't optimize which items the CPU prefetches from memory. By grouping recently-used items, we improve spatial locality without changing the eviction policy or geometry, remaining within the scope of micro-optimization but targeting a different dimension (prefetch coherency vs. early-exit).
