# H01

Correctly implement FIFO eviction policy on 64×32 geometry to decouple per-hit overhead from bucket width and reduce latency.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Modify the eviction policy from ClassicLRU to FIFO (first-in-first-out). In the eviction selection logic, replace recency-based victim ranking with timestamp-based selection: evict the item with the oldest insertion timestamp within the target bucket. Remove all per-hit bookkeeping operations from the `get()` and `put()` hit paths—items must not be repositioned, promoted, or updated on access. Keep the bucket geometry exactly as cycle/0002/v002: 64 buckets with 32 items per bucket on average.

Cycle 0003's H02 attempted this change but failed to execute; this retry focuses on ensuring the FIFO policy is correctly applied without vestigial ClassicLRU bookkeeping overhead.
