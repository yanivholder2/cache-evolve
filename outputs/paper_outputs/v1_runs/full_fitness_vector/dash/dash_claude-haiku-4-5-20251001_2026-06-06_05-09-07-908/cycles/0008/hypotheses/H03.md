Cycle 0007 then tested divisor 2 (threshold=12) on baseline and found it regressed hit-rate to 0.6709, the worst among cycle winners. This suggests the permissiveness frontier is bounded: divisor 2 is TOO conservative (threshold too high), while divisor 4 may represent the sweet spot on baseline geometry. The cycle 0007 open question asks: "Has the permissiveness frontier been reached (divisor 2 on baseline), or could divisor 3, 2.5, or lower yield further improvements?" This hypothesis answers the opposite direction: rather than pushing permissiveness lower (more restrictive), it tests the hypothesis that divisor 4's more permissive gate (threshold=6) actually outperforms divisor 2 by allowing genuinely hot items to promote without cascading over-promotion. If divisor 4 recovers hit-rate toward or beyond baseline, it repositions baseline geometry as a viable candidate paired with adaptive threshold tuning, potentially competing with v002's 80/20 geometry.
