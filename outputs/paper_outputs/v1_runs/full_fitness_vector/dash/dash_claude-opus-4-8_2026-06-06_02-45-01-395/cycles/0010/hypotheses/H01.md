# H01

Dropping the deepest cascade victim instead of re-inserting it into the stash breaks the stash coupling and lowers put-p99 and get-p50 together (per-op-time), at a small hit-rate cost.

## Parent

cycle/0007/v001

## Changes

Modify `demote_from_normal_bucket` in `src/dash/segments/dash_segment.rs`. In
the `else` branch (the `true_target != source_idx` foreign-resident retry,
currently lines ~155-167), keep the one-more-chance retry that re-homes the
foreign resident into its true target via `core.put_at(Slot::Target, ...)`,
but when that retry itself returns a `cascade` victim, **drop it** — delete
the follow-up `core.put_at(Slot::Stash, insert_pos, cascade)` so the
second-order conflict victim leaves the cache entirely instead of terminating
in the stash. Leave the `true_target == source_idx` branch (the direct
straight-to-stash case) exactly as is, and leave the `promote_to_target` /
`promote_to_probing` call sites unchanged.

This is the cycle-0009 continuation's requested "conflict-resolution path that
does not terminate in the stash": the cascade no longer grows stash occupancy,
so the put path sheds its second `put_at` (the documented put-p99 bound) AND
the stash that the median get scans stays shorter (the get-p50 the stash
coupling inflates). The trade is the handful of rescued hits the dropped
victim would have kept resident, so score this on hit-rate AND both op
latencies at once, never on the put tail in isolation. Preserve every other
invariant: slot legality, the heat side table, and the `insert_pos` plumbing
through the retained inserts.

## Rationale (optional)

Every prior cascade move (0008/v003 shallow, 0009/v001 reroute, 0009/v003
deepen) ended in the stash and paid there, sliding along the latency↔hit-rate
trade. Dropping the terminal victim is the one move that removes a stash
insert outright rather than relocating it, so it is the cleanest test of
whether decoupling the two paths beats the residency it concedes.
