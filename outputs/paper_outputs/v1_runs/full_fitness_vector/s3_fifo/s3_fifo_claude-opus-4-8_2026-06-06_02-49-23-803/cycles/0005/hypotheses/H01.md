# H01

Capping the ghost queue at 3/8 of main (instead of 1/2) pins a finer point in the 25–50% re-admission band and raises hit-rate over the incumbent.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v001

## Changes

In `evict_small()`, change the ghost trim threshold from
`(self.main_size / 2).max(1)` to `(self.main_size * 3 / 8).max(1)`. This
shortens the ghost window to ~37.5% of main, so only the most-recent
three-eighths of demotions remain eligible for fast re-admission into
main. Leave everything else identical to the parent: keep `small_size =
(capacity / 32).max(1).min(capacity - 1)`, keep `MAX_FREQ = 3`, keep the
`freq > 1` graduation threshold and the `freq == 0` demotion rule, and
do not touch `evict_main()`. The only behavioural change is the
re-admission selectivity / ghost-scan length.

## Rationale (optional)

Cycle 0004 found a hit-rate optimum at 50% (100% readmitted stale far
keys, 25% over-trimmed near reuse). The optimum within the 25–50% band
has not been pinned; 3/8 probes whether the peak sits below 50% and, if
so, also shortens the put membership scan.
