# H01

Halving sketch depth from 4 to 2 rows cuts per-op-time on every get and put while holding hit-rate, because sample depth is already accuracy-saturated on the wide table.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v001

## Changes

In `src/tiny_lfu/mod.rs`, change the constant `SKETCH_DEPTH` from `4` to
`2`. This is the only edit; `CountMinSketch::new` already reads
`SKETCH_DEPTH` for its `depth` field, so `increment`, `estimate`, and
`heap_bytes` automatically iterate two rows instead of four. The
double-hashing `position_for` already produces a distinct column per
`row` index, so two rows remain well-separated — do not change the
hashing or the `(h1 + row*(h2|1)) & (width-1)` formula.

Rationale: `increment` runs on the get and put hot path and `estimate`
runs five times per full-cache eviction; both are linear in `SKETCH_DEPTH`.
The cross-cycle log records depth as accuracy-saturated (0007) and the
wide (factor-16) table already suppresses the aliasing extra rows would
correct, so dropping to 2 rows should halve the sketch's per-op work and
its heap footprint (65536→32768 bytes) with hit-rate essentially
unchanged. The experiment confirms the claim if hit-rate stays within
noise of 0.7425 while get p50 drops below the incumbent 44/60 ns.
