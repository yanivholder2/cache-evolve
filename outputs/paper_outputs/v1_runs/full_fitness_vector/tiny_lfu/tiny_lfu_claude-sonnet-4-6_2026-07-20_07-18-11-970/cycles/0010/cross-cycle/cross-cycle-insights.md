## Recurring wins

- **Eliminating serial hash dependencies pays off reliably.** Bit-slicing a single hash into non-overlapping row windows (c0003), two-hash bit-slicing (c0004), and a bijective multiply-xorshift remix (c0005) all won by removing intra-iteration data dependencies; ILP recovery on the sketch put path is a durable gain.
- **Amortising expensive operations at 1-in-8 preserves latency neutrality while capturing real signal.** Eviction-probe sampling (c0004 v002), the amortised strict admission gate (c0006 v002), and amortised gate layered on protected segment (c0009) all won cleanly at this rate; the gain survives coarse sampling, confirming the underlying signals are strong.
- **Second-chance semantics improve hit rate when implemented with low per-op bookkeeping.** Protected segment (c0008 v002, c0009), and especially CLOCK (c0010 v003), both deliver reprieve for hot residents. CLOCK is the Pareto-dominant form: it abolishes dual-chain overhead and matches or exceeds SLRU hit quality.
- **Idiomatic Rust abstractions outperform explicit arithmetic optimisations.** `Option<NonZeroU32>` match arms (c0002), and standard hash invocations over hand-rolled serial chains (c0001, c0006), confirm the compiler's codegen for standard patterns beats hand-tuned alternatives on this workload.
- **Structural policy changes are an orthogonal hit-rate lever from sketch tuning.** Segment-based protection (c0008) and CLOCK (c0010) each raised hit rate through admission policy rather than sketch accuracy, confirming these two axes are independent and combinable.

## Recurring failures

- **Sketch decay in any form degrades this workload.** Both the aggressive `width/2` interval (c0001) and the moderate `capacity×10` interval (c0003) hurt hit rate and latency; the LIRS trace's stable working set makes accumulated frequency a reliable admission signal that blanket half-decay destroys. This axis is definitively closed.
- **Sketch structural simplification cannot beat the baseline layout.** Flat allocation (c0002), depth halving (c0002), and remix chains as hash replacements (c0006) all regressed. The four-row multi-allocation design is well-balanced; collision suppression is load-bearing and structural shortcuts compound cache-set pressure or correlation noise.
- **Counter narrowing (u16) does not translate to latency gains.** Both c0007 v002 and c0008 compound u16+8× confirmed that per-access zero-extension overhead cancels L2 footprint savings at current sketch sizes. Closed unless the cast overhead is eliminated.
- **Strict admission gating on every eviction doubles p99 through residency churn.** Firing `incoming_freq > victim_freq + threshold` unconditionally (c0005 v003, c0006 v001) consistently disrupts hot-node residency in a way that the 1-in-8 amortisation rate cannot cancel. Any tighter threshold must stay below the per-eviction churn rate.
- **Best-of-2 victim selection at the LRU cold end adds no value.** Both the LRU head and its immediate neighbour are equally cold relative to the working set; the admission gate comparison against the incoming key is the operative filter. Closed definitively in c0007.
- **Sketch width beyond 8× exceeds the L2 ceiling at this capacity.** 16× width (c0010 v002) crossed 256 KB and converted sporadic tail cost into systematic per-operation slowdown. The 4-row 8× u32 configuration is the width ceiling; do not revisit.
- **Proportional frequency amplification does not shift admission orderings.** Double-incrementing on hits (c0008 v003) fails because cold residents accumulate the boosted signal at the same relative rate; the comparison is structurally unaffected and the overhead falls on the majority (≈74%) hit path.

## Open questions

- **Window-admission buffer upstream of the main CLOCK pool.** A small recency-first segment feeding the frequency-filtered zone could improve cold-start and scan-heavy patterns the sketch currently misclassifies. Untouched across all ten cycles; highest-priority unexplored structural thread.
- **Adaptive CLOCK scan bound.** The current ≈20% capacity scan limit was chosen heuristically. Scaling it with recent eviction hit rate or estimated working-set churn could improve eviction quality under non-Zipfian traces without increasing average scan cost.
- **CLOCK + amortised gate interaction.** Whether the two mechanisms operate on orthogonal paths — meaning each can be tuned independently and stacked — or share enough state that combining them yields diminishing returns has not yet been tested on the CLOCK baseline.
- **Softer admission gate on the amortised path only.** A fractional threshold applied exclusively on the 1-in-8 probe path (rather than globally) may capture a portion of the hit-rate benefit while limiting residency churn to a rate the cache can absorb.

## Per-policy notes

- **LRU (original baseline, c0001–c0003):** Stable platform that resisted both sketch and layout perturbations; the hot-path codegen for standard list operations proved hard to beat. Node compaction with `Option<NonZeroU32>` is the correct encoding; no further layout gains remain.
- **SLRU / protected-segment (c0008–c0009):** Segment split delivers a real hit-rate gain but dual-list bookkeeping imposes a measurable put-path latency tax (cross-chain promotion on every full-cache write). The 20% protected fraction is near-optimal for Zipfian traces; wider fractions fill protected slots with lukewarm items and hurt both dimensions. Bitset compaction shrinks off-struct heap 8× but the saving is invisible to current fitness metrics.
- **CLOCK (c0010, current baseline):** Pareto-dominant over SLRU: single reference bit per node provides equivalent second-chance semantics without maintaining two chain head/tail pointers or a live protected-node count. Bounded scan at ≈20% of capacity keeps per-eviction cost predictable. Struct size is smaller than SLRU and the hot-path is cleaner. All future variants should build on this as the parent.
- **CountMinSketch (all cycles):** Four-row 8× u32 sketch is the established optimal configuration. Row independence from two seeded hashes is genuine and cannot be replaced by remix chains rooted at a single seed (correlation raises collision noise). Decay is closed; sketch width is at L2 ceiling; counter narrowing is closed. The one remaining sketch thread is whether a cheaper bijective transform than a second full SipHash can preserve the independence advantage.
