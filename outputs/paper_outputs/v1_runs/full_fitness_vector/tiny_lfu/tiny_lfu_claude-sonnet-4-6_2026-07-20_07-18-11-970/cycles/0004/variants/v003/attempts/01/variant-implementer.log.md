**What this does:**  
The new condition requires an incoming item's estimated frequency to exceed the LRU victim's by at least **2** before it is admitted. Previously, a strictly-greater-by-1 margin was enough. Using `saturating_add(1)` ensures correctness at `u32::MAX` (no overflow), though in practice sketch counters never reach saturation on this workload.

**Why it should help:**  
Count-Min Sketch estimates carry ±1 noise from hash collisions. When two keys have near-identical true frequencies, their CMS min-estimates can differ by 1 in either direction — meaning the admission decision at `incoming_freq == victim_freq + 1` is essentially a coin flip. Requiring a margin of 2 gives incumbents a one-count advantage, stabilising the resident set against noise-driven churn and preventing unnecessary evictions of hot items in the LIRS steady state.
