# H01

Delay LRU promotion until an item's second hit so one-hit-wonders cannot displace hot items, improving hit rate on scan-mixed traces.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

In `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs`, replace the body of
`impl<K, V> EvictionPolicy<K, V> for ClassicLRU`'s `get_from_position` so
that an access does not unconditionally move the item to the back of the
bucket. Instead, treat `Item.lfu_counter` as a small "seen" counter:

1. Increment `items[position].lfu_counter` by 1.
2. If `items[position].lfu_counter == 1` after the bump, leave the item
   in place and return `position` unchanged (the first hit only marks
   the item as "seen").
3. If `items[position].lfu_counter >= 2`, do the classic move-to-back
   (`items.remove(position)` followed by `items.push(item)`), reset
   `lfu_counter` to `1` on the moved item, and return `items.len() - 1`.

Keep `eviction_candidate_index` returning `0` (front) and
`promotion_candidate_index` returning `items.len() - 1` (back), so the
bucket trait's `evict_item` and `is_full` paths are untouched and the
P1/P2/P3 invariants continue to hold. Do not touch
`AssociativeCacheBucket` or any other policy impl. Insertion of a brand
new key keeps `Item::new`'s default `lfu_counter = 0`, so freshly
inserted items naturally land at the front of the bucket (oldest
position) and remain there until a second access, which is the source
of the scan-resistance effect.

## Rationale (optional)

ClassicLRU's blanket move-to-back on every hit lets a single sequential
scan rotate genuinely hot items down to the eviction front. Requiring a
second hit before promotion costs one extra `lfu_counter` field write
per access (the field already exists on `Item`, so no metadata growth)
and is expected to lift hit rate on the workload-a trace without
regressing per-op time meaningfully.
