# H01

Narrowing the probationary victim-scan from the LRU half to the LRU quarter will retain the frequency-selection benefit introduced in cycle 4 while exposing whether the half-list scan was wider than necessary.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

In the probationary eviction path inside `put()`, change the scan-length computation from

```rust
let scan_len = (self.prob_count / 2).max(1);
```

to

```rust
let scan_len = (self.prob_count / 4).max(1);
```

No other logic changes. The minimum-frequency victim selection loop, the ghost recording step (`add_to_ghost`), and all other eviction paths remain exactly as in v003.

## Rationale

Cycle 4 established that the LRU quarter is the efficient scan window for the protected-segment demotion path: widening to 1/3 gave only marginal improvement, and the 1/4 fraction already captures most frequency variation near the LRU end. If the same property holds in probationary — i.e., the coldest entries cluster near the LRU head — then a 1/4 scan should identify nearly the same minimum-frequency victim as the 1/2 scan. A positive result (hit rate maintained) would confirm that the probationary scan can be halved with no cost; a regression would establish that probationary frequency variation is more spread out and the wider scan is genuinely necessary.
