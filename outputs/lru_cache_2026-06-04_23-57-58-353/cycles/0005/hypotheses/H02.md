# H02

Capping the per-node frequency counter at a small ceiling (8) will improve hit rate by preventing long-resident protected entries from accumulating disproportionately high scores that make them effectively immortal against newer hot items.

## Parent

cycle/0004/v003

## Changes

Add a module-level constant near the top of `mod.rs`:

```rust
const MAX_FREQ: u32 = 8;
```

In `get()`, replace the unconditional saturating-add on both the Protected and Probationary arms:

```rust
self.nodes[idx].freq = self.nodes[idx].freq.saturating_add(1);
```

with:

```rust
self.nodes[idx].freq = (self.nodes[idx].freq + 1).min(MAX_FREQ);
```

No other changes. The demotion path (`demote_prot_head`) halves the counter as before; with the cap at 8 a maximally-warm entry arrives in probationary with freq=4, and reaches freq=0 after two more demotions — giving exactly four generations of protection before it competes on equal terms with cold entries.

## Rationale

v003's frequency counter is a `u32` with `saturating_add(1)` and no ceiling. An entry that was heavily accessed months of trace-time ago can accumulate a freq value of thousands, making it dominate every frequency comparison even if it has not been touched recently. The demotion halving slows the decay but never resets it, so very long-lived entries in protected are essentially locked in. Capping at 8 converts the counter from a cumulative lifetime score into a small-range recency score: entries that have not been accessed in the last few demotion cycles converge toward zero and become fair victims. The cap value of 8 was chosen so that three halvings (demotion events) reduce the maximum to 1, giving warm entries a modest but bounded residency advantage over cold newcomers.
