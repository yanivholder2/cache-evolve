# H01

Widening the frequency-aware demotion scan from the LRU quarter to the LRU third of the protected segment gives the selector more candidates and raises hit rate by finding better demotion victims.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v002

## Changes

In `demote_prot_head()`, change the scan length calculation from
`(self.prot_count / 4).max(1)` to `(self.prot_count / 3).max(1)`.
No other logic changes. The scan still walks from `prot_head` forward for
exactly `scan_len` nodes, selects the minimum-freq node (LRU-biased
tie-breaking), unlinks it, moves it to probationary MRU, and halves its
freq counter. The only difference is that roughly 33 % of the segment is
inspected instead of 25 %, giving the frequency selector a larger pool of
candidates at the cost of a modest increase in scan work per demotion
event. All other invariants (probationary overflow, ghost recording,
promotion path) remain identical to cycle/0003/v002.

## Rationale

The cycle-3 insight noted that the 1/4 scan fraction is the first value
that "worked" and explicitly flagged fraction tuning as an open question.
A wider window keeps the O(n) cost proportional to segment size — the
structural property that made the v002 redesign succeed — while potentially
finding a lower-frequency victim than the narrower scan would have missed.
The change is minimal, so any measured difference cleanly isolates the
effect of the fraction alone.
