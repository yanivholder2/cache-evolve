# H01

Promote items to the MRU (Back) end of the next tier instead of the LRU (Front) end to raise hit rate on recency-skewed traces.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

The baseline `PromotionColdSegment::get` (in
`src/dash/segments/promotion_cold_segment.rs`) calls
`promotion_get(&mut self.core, key, InsertPosition::Front)`. Change the threaded
`InsertPosition` from `Front` to `Back` — i.e. turn the cold-promotion segment
into a hot-promotion segment.

This single value threads through `promotion_logic.rs`: `promote_to_target`,
`promote_to_probing`, and both stash/target inserts inside
`demote_from_normal_bucket` all forward `insert_pos` to `SegmentCore::put_at`. With
`Back`, a stash hit lands at the **target MRU end**, a hottest target item promotes
to the **probing MRU end**, and demoted items re-enter at MRU.

Rationale grounded in the policy: under `ClassicLRU`,
`eviction_candidate_index = 0` (Front) and `promotion_candidate_index = len-1`
(Back). The baseline (`Front`) therefore promotes an item straight onto the
eviction-candidate end, so a freshly promoted hot key is the very next victim
unless it earns MRU again. Inserting at `Back` instead protects a just-promoted
item and means one further access promotes it to the next tier — biasing retention
toward recently hot keys.

Preserve everything else in `promotion_get`: the hottest check via
`promotion_candidate_index()`, the three-slot search order, the demotion cascade
probing→target→stash, and the two `unreachable!` post-conditions. Do **not** change
`put` (new keys still enter stash at Back). Slot derivation and
single-eviction-per-put are untouched, so invariants P1–P5 continue to hold.

Confirm by comparing the hit-rate summary against the baseline 0.94725 (per-config
dash@16=0.9655, dash@32=0.929, assoc@16=0.8775, assoc@32=0.9055).
