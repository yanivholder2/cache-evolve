# H01

Replace the recency "is hottest" trigger for target to probing promotion with a max-access-frequency trigger, accumulating a per-item hit counter, to raise hit rate on the scan/loop LIRS trace.

## Parent

baseline

## Changes

Edit `promotion_get` in `src/dash/segments/promotion_logic.rs`. Today the
target-hit branch decides promotion by recency:

```
let is_hottest = Some(item_idx) == core.buckets[target_idx].promotion_candidate_index();
```

Under `ClassicLRU` this is purely positional (MRU end), and the cycle-1 result
showed recency is the wrong signal on this scan/loop trace. Change the promotion
decision to be frequency-driven instead:

1. On every hit handled by `promotion_get` (stash, target, and probing
   branches), increment the resident item's `lfu_counter` by 1 *before* acting
   on it. Do this explicitly here rather than relying on the bucket's eviction
   policy, because the eval path runs `ClassicLRU`, whose `get_from_position`
   never touches `lfu_counter`. Access the field via
   `core.buckets[idx].get_items_mut()[item_idx].lfu_counter += 1` (and the
   stash equivalent).
2. In the target-hit branch, replace the `is_hottest` recency test with a
   frequency test: promote to probing only when the hit item's `lfu_counter` is
   the strict maximum among the items currently in `core.buckets[target_idx]`
   (ties do not promote). Otherwise fall through to the existing
   touch-in-place path (`get_from_position`), exactly as the `else` arm does
   today.

Keep everything else intact: stash to target promotion stays immediate and at
`InsertPosition::Front` (cold geometry), `promote_to_probing` /
`promote_to_target` / `demote_from_normal_bucket` are unchanged, the two
`unreachable!` post-conditions stay, and the eviction *victim* selection remains
the bucket policy's default (this hypothesis changes only the promotion trigger,
not who gets evicted — a deliberate scope boundary).

This preserves invariants P1–P5: each promotion still removes exactly one item
and re-inserts it into a slot derived from its key, so single-eviction-per-put,
slot legality, and capacity bounds are untouched. Keep the crate compiling and
update any in-crate `#[cfg(test)]` cases in `promotion_logic.rs` /
`promotion_cold_segment.rs` that assert the old recency-based promotion timing
(e.g. `target_hit_hottest_promotes_to_probing`) to reflect the new
frequency-based trigger.

## Rationale (optional)

On a LIRS scan/loop workload a key that is merely most-recently-used is often a
one-shot scan key; gating the climb into the protected probing tier on
accumulated access frequency keeps soon-cold keys out of the hot tier while
letting recurring loop keys earn their way up. This is the "frequency retention"
direction the cycle-1 insight explicitly recommended and which no variant has
exercised yet.
