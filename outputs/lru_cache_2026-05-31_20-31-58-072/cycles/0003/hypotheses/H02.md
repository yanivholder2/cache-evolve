# H02

Making the Hot tag sticky — requiring two ref-clear sweeps before a Hot slot demotes to Cold — raises hit rate by protecting promoted working-set keys through a single cold-scan storm.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Modify `evict_one()` in src/lru_cache/mod.rs to make Hot demotion
two-step instead of one-step.

Today, v002's pass 2 demotes a Hot ref-set slot to Cold in a single
visit by setting both `entry.referenced = false` and `entry.state =
SlotState::Cold`. Change this so a Hot slot survives one extra pass:

1. Extend the per-slot `Entry` with a second referenced-bit-like field
   — call it `hot_credit: u8` (range 0..=1) — or repurpose
   `referenced` plus a new `hot_age` bool; pick whichever is cleaner.
   The promotion path in `get()` sets `hot_credit = 1` whenever it
   promotes Cold→Hot or hits an already-Hot slot.

2. In `evict_one()` pass 2:
   - `(Hot, referenced=true)`: clear `referenced`, leave state Hot,
     leave `hot_credit` untouched. Advance hand.
   - `(Hot, referenced=false, hot_credit>0)`: decrement `hot_credit`,
     leave state Hot. Advance hand. (This is the new "second-chance"
     step — instead of demoting on the first ref-clear visit, we
     consume a credit.)
   - `(Hot, referenced=false, hot_credit==0)`: demote to Cold (state =
     Cold), keep ref-clear, advance hand. The slot is now a Cold
     ref-clear victim that pass 1 of the *next* eviction call will
     pick up. For this call, if no Hot ref-clear-and-credit-0 slot
     was found, fall through to the existing fallback (evict the slot
     at `hand`, treat as Cold for ghost purposes).
   - `(Cold, _)`: unchanged — skip, advance hand.

3. Pass 1's Cold branch is unchanged. Promotion in `get()` always
   refreshes `hot_credit = 1`, so a get-hit during a sweep restores
   full protection.

Preserve every other invariant: ghost ring mechanics, Cold-only ghost
insertion, the `was_cold` flag at `evict_at`, the capacity bookkeeping,
and the `len`/`map`/`slots` triple. The fallback eviction at the end
of `evict_one()` stays exactly as today (evict slot-at-hand, was_cold
= true) — under the new policy that path is reached only when every
Hot slot is sitting at credit ≥ 1 AND ref-set, which is rarer than in
v002 but still possible and must terminate.

## Rationale

Cycle-2 insight calls out "alternative Hot-demotion aggressiveness" as
the second tuning axis on the champion. v002's pass 2 demotes the
first ref-clear Hot it finds, which means one bursty cold scan can
strip the Hot tag off a slot whose key was genuinely re-referenced
just one sweep ago. A single-bit credit costs almost nothing per slot
but converts demotion from "one ref-clear visit" to "two consecutive
ref-clear visits", which is the smallest possible increase in Hot
stickiness without bringing back the frequency-counter trap that sank
cycle-2 v001.
