# H03

Routing Hot evictions into the ghost ring (alongside Cold evictions) and re-admitting their ghost-hits as Hot-with-ref-set raises hit rate by reclaiming working-set keys that were squeezed out under pressure.

## Parent

cycle/0002/v002

## Changes

Today v002 only pushes Cold-eviction keys into the ghost ring (the
`was_cold` flag in `evict_at()` gates ghost insertion). A ghost-hit on
re-insertion admits the new key as `Hot` with `referenced = false`.
Hot evictions skip the ghost entirely, so working-set keys evicted via
pass 2 are forgotten the moment they leave the ring.

Make two coupled changes in src/lru_cache/mod.rs:

1. **Hot evictions enter the ghost too.** In `evict_one()`, the pass-2
   `(SlotState::Hot, false)` branch currently calls
   `self.evict_at(idx, /*was_cold=*/ false)`. Change it to pass
   `was_cold = true` so the Hot key is pushed into the ghost ring.
   This is the minimal way to give Hot evictions the same recall
   privilege Cold evictions already enjoy.

   Equivalently — and slightly cleaner — drop the `was_cold` parameter
   on `evict_at` altogether and unconditionally `push_ghost(entry.key)`.
   The two formulations are equivalent under this hypothesis; pick
   whichever produces less diff.

2. **Mark ghost-hit re-admissions as already-warm.** In `put()`, where
   `from_ghost` is true and the new entry is currently constructed
   with `state: SlotState::Hot, referenced: false`, change `referenced:
   false` to `referenced: true`. Reasoning: a Cold-ghost or Hot-ghost
   hit is, by construction, a re-reference event. Setting the
   reference bit means the next sweep treats the slot as freshly
   touched and skips it on pass 1 instead of clearing-then-evicting it
   on the pass after. The slot is also Hot, so pass 1 already skips
   it — but the ref bit affects pass 2 behavior and, under H02-style
   stickier demotion variants, would compound.

Leave the rest of the policy intact: pass-1 Cold-only scope, pass-2
Hot demotion rule, the ghost ring size (`capacity/4`), `ghost_remove`
on ghost-hit, and the capacity bookkeeping. Preserve all four gates
(build / contract / universal-invariant / invariant); the contract
file `src/lru_cache/lru_cache_jni.rs` is not touched.

## Rationale

The cycle-2 insight invites a "recency-anchored admission filter" as
the third extension on the champion. Treating ghost membership as a
strong recency signal — and recording Hot evictions, which by
definition were promoted because the key was re-referenced — is the
admission-side analogue. The change is small and orthogonal to the
ring-size knob (H01) and the demotion-stickiness knob (H02), so the
three hypotheses occupy three distinct points in the champion's design
space.
