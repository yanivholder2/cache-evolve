# H02

Selecting the least-recently-touched Cold ref-clear slot as victim — instead of the first one the hand encounters — recovers recency information CLOCK throws away and raises hit-rate on lirs:multi1@512.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Replace pass 1's hand-position victim selection in
`src/lru_cache/mod.rs::evict_one` with a least-recently-touched choice
over the Cold pool:

- Add `tick: u64` to `LruCache` (monotonic counter) and `last_touch: u64`
  to `Entry`. On every `get` hit and every `put` (insert and resident
  update) set `entry.last_touch = self.tick` and then increment
  `self.tick`. New inserts also receive a `last_touch` of the current
  tick.
- Rewrite pass 1 of `evict_one` so it performs a full ring sweep in
  one traversal that does both ref-bit clearing *and* tracking the
  ref-clear-Cold candidate with the minimum `last_touch`. Concretely:
  walk all `capacity` slots from `hand`; for each Cold ref-set slot
  clear its ref bit and continue; for each Cold ref-clear slot, if
  `last_touch` is the smallest seen so far, remember its index. After
  the sweep, if any Cold ref-clear candidate was found, evict the one
  with the smallest `last_touch`, advance `hand` to `(idx + 1) %
  capacity`, and return.
- If no Cold ref-clear candidate exists, fall through to the existing
  pass 2 (Hot demotion) and fallback paths unchanged.
- Ghost ring behaviour, Cold→Hot second-touch promotion, the ghost-hit
  Hot admission path, and `lru_cache_jni.rs` must remain unchanged.

Preserve invariants: every Cold ref-set slot still has its bit
cleared on each pass-1 sweep (so CLOCK aging still progresses);
`tick` overflow is not a concern at u64 width.

## Rationale (optional)

Pass 1 currently evicts whichever Cold ref-clear slot happens to lie
nearest the hand — a coarse stand-in for recency. CLOCK-Pro-lite keeps
no real recency among Cold slots, so this is the precise place
recency information is being discarded. Adding a per-slot tick lets
pass 1 evict the *least-recently-touched* Cold ref-clear slot, which
is exactly LRU semantics restricted to the Cold pool, while preserving
v001's ghost-ring and promotion mechanisms.
