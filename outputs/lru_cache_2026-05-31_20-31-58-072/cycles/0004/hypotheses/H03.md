# H03

Requiring two Cold-side touches (via an intermediate Warm sub-state) before promoting to Hot, and evicting Warm only after fresh Cold is drained, raises hit-rate on lirs:multi1@512 by deferring the Hot pool to genuinely re-referenced keys.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

Refine the Cold→Hot promotion ladder in `src/lru_cache/mod.rs` so
promotion is gated on two touches rather than one, by inserting a
`Warm` sub-state between `Cold` and `Hot`:

- Extend `enum SlotState` with a `Warm` variant: now `Cold`, `Warm`,
  `Hot`. Update `Entry` and all match arms accordingly.
- Change `get`: a hit on `Cold` promotes the slot to `Warm` and sets
  `referenced = true`; a hit on `Warm` promotes to `Hot` and sets
  `referenced = true`; a hit on `Hot` only sets `referenced = true`
  (unchanged).
- Change `put` for brand-new keys: ghost-hit promotions still admit
  directly as `Hot` (an evicted-then-re-referenced key has already
  proved itself); non-ghost brand-new keys still admit as `Cold` with
  `referenced = false`.
- Change `evict_one`'s pass 1 so victim eligibility expands across two
  cycles of the hand: on the first revolution it considers only
  `Cold` ref-clear slots as victims (Warm and Hot are skipped, Cold
  ref-set is cleared as before); only if that revolution returns no
  victim does it allow `Warm` ref-clear slots as victims on a second
  revolution (Warm ref-set slots are cleared and demoted back to
  `Cold` on the second sweep; Hot is still skipped). Pass 2 (Hot
  demotion) and the fallback remain as in v001.
- Ghost ring behaviour and sizing, the `lru_cache_jni.rs` ABI, and the
  `framework::Cache` impl must remain untouched.

Preserve invariants: total resident count, ref-bit clearing progress
per sweep, and ghost-push behaviour on Cold/Warm eviction (Warm
evictions push to ghost like Cold; only Hot evictions skip the
ghost).

## Rationale (optional)

The v001 champion promotes on the first re-hit, making Hot relatively
easy to enter; once Hot, a slot is shielded from pass 1 entirely.
Inserting a Warm step requires two Cold-side touches before Hot
admission, which keeps one-touch wanderers from cluttering the Hot
pool while still protecting truly re-referenced keys. Unlike a
saturating frequency counter (which regressed by shielding stale
keys), Warm slots are still drained by pass 1's second revolution, so
the recency bias of the trace is preserved.
