# H03

Retry S4LRU with both fixes the cycle-5 cycle-insight named — an asymmetric top-heavy split AND a ghost ring on the top level — to test whether the segmented-LRU skeleton can match the CLOCK-Pro-lite plateau when its two structural weaknesses are addressed together.

## Parent

cycle/0005/v003

## Changes

The parent S4LRU lost (0.4753 vs 0.4946) because (a) every overflowing
promotion was paired with a demotion back, so each level's effective
capacity was smaller than its declared `capacity/4`, and (b) the top
level was the same size as the bottom three, so most warm keys were
evicted from L0's tail before climbing the three-hop ladder. The
cycle-5 cycle-insight prescribed exactly two complementary fixes — top
heavy split and ghost-ring graft — and explicitly told future cohorts
not to retry unaugmented S4LRU. This variant applies both together so
the two-fix prescription is tested in one slot rather than two.

Concrete changes to cycle/0005/v003's `mod.rs`:

1. **Asymmetric split.** Replace the four equal `capacity/4` segments
   with a top-heavy partition: `L3_cap = capacity / 2`, and the
   remaining three levels (`L0`, `L1`, `L2`) each get
   `(capacity - L3_cap) / 3` slots, with any remainder going to `L0`
   (the bottom). With `capacity = 512` this gives `L3 = 256`,
   `L2 = L1 = 85`, `L0 = 86`. The protected top quarter expands to
   half the cache, so a key reaching L3 has materially more retention
   room than the parent's symmetric split.
2. **Ghost ring on L3.** Add a ghost ring of length `L3_cap`
   tracking keys evicted *from L3 directly* (not from L0/L1/L2). On
   a `put` whose key is in this ghost ring, install the key directly
   into L3 (at its MRU end), bypassing the L0→L1→L2→L3 ladder and
   evicting from L3's LRU end (which becomes a ghost entry). Keep a
   `HashSet` mirror of the ghost ring for O(1) membership lookup,
   in lockstep with the ring slots exactly the way the champion's
   `ghost`/`ghost_set` pair works.
3. **Conservation invariant unchanged** for the L0/L1/L2 sub-stack:
   every overflowing promotion still pairs with a demotion back to
   the source level. Only L3 differs — overflow from L3 either
   demotes to L2 (the existing rule) for in-ladder promotions, or
   evicts to ghost (the new rule) for ghost-hit direct admissions.
4. **Skeleton otherwise unchanged.** Keep the slot-array
   representation, the per-key level tag, the per-level intra-level
   LRU ordering, the public API (`get` returns Option<&V>, `put`
   inserts/updates), and the four universal invariants (capacity
   bound, ghost-set/ring lockstep where applicable, no duplicate
   keys across levels, total residents ≤ capacity).

Net effect: a key earned into L3 has 2× the retention room of the
parent's L3, and the ghost ring lets a once-promoted key skip the
three-hop climb on re-reference. Both of the structural deficits the
cycle-5 insight identified are addressed simultaneously.

## Rationale (optional)

This is the *one* allowed retry of S4LRU. Splitting it across two
cohorts would burn two slots on incomplete tests (each fix alone
might still trail the plateau, leaving us no signal about whether the
combination works). One slot, both fixes, decisive answer to the
cross-cycle open question: "Can any non-CLOCK-Pro-lite skeleton keep
up on this trace?"
