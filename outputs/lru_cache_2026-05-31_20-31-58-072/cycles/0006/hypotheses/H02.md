# H02

Cap the Hot pool at half of capacity and force-demote the longest-resident Hot slot when a Cold→Hot promotion would exceed it, raising hit-rate on lirs:multi1@512 by leaving more room for Cold-side recency churn.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v001

## Changes

The champion has no upper bound on the Hot pool: every Cold slot that
gets a second touch flips to Hot and stays Hot until pass 2 fires.
Pass 2 barely fires at cap=512 on this trace (cycle 3's insight noted
this directly), so Hot can grow without bound until it crowds out the
Cold-side recency window. v002's cycle-5 takeaway named this as the
next lever: "target the Hot-pool budget or the Cold→Hot rule itself."

Concrete changes to cycle/0003/v001's `mod.rs`:

1. Add a field `hot_cap: usize` to `LruCache`, computed in `try_new`
   as `capacity / 2` with a floor of 1 (so a cap=1 cache still
   functions — Hot can be 1 at most).
2. Add a field `hot_len: usize`, initialised to 0, maintained by
   every state transition: incremented on Cold→Hot and on a Hot
   insert (ghost-hit), decremented on Hot→Cold (pass 2 demote AND
   the new forced demote) and on every Hot eviction.
3. After any operation that would push `hot_len` above `hot_cap` —
   the `get()` Cold→Hot promotion at line ~100, or a ghost-hit `put`
   that installs as Hot — call a new helper `force_demote_one_hot()`
   *before* returning. The helper walks the slot ring starting at
   `(self.hand + capacity - 1) % capacity` (i.e. opposite the CLOCK
   hand, where the oldest Hot slot is most likely) and demotes the
   first slot whose `state == Hot` to `Cold` *with* `referenced =
   false` (so it becomes immediately evictable on the next pass-1
   sweep). Walk at most `capacity` slots; if every slot is already
   Hot=false there is nothing to do.
4. The two-pass `evict_one` stays untouched: pass 1 still prefers
   Cold-ref-clear, pass 2 still demotes Hot ref-set and evicts the
   first Hot ref-clear, fallback unchanged.
5. Preserve every invariant of the parent: `len <= capacity`,
   `ghost.len() == capacity`, ghost-set/ring lockstep, single
   occupancy per key. Add a new internal invariant
   `hot_len <= hot_cap` enforced after every operation, and
   `hot_len` equals the count of `state == Hot` slots (a
   `debug_assert!` in tests).

Net effect: the Hot pool can never exceed `capacity/2`, leaving at
least half of `capacity` for Cold-side circulation regardless of how
many second-touches the trace produces. The Cold pool — which carries
the recency window on lirs:multi1 — is protected from being squeezed
out by long-lived Hot residents that pass 2 rarely reaches.

## Rationale (optional)

v002's insight pointed straight here ("future gains should target the
Hot-pool budget"). Cycle 3's insight observed pass 2 barely fires at
cap=512, which is exactly the failure mode this hypothesis attacks:
without an explicit cap, Hot grows monotonically and pass 2 is too
weak to push back.
