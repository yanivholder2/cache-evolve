# H01

Adding a per-bucket second-chance stash on top of v001's frequency-aware ClassicLRU will raise hit rate above either mechanism alone by recovering items that LFU eviction still fails to protect during cold-burst displacement.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Carry forward v001's overlay of `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` unchanged — the LFU-weighted `eviction_candidate_index` (min by `(lfu_counter, position)`) and the `lfu_counter` increment in `get_from_position` remain intact.

In addition, add an overlay for `src/associative_cache/associative_cache_bucket.rs` that introduces the stash mechanism from v003 on top of v001's eviction policy:

1. Add two fields to `AssociativeCacheBucket`: `stash: Vec<Item<K, V>>` and `stash_max_size: usize`. Initialise `stash_max_size` to `(settings.bucket_size / 4).max(1)` in `new()`.

2. Add a private `stash_push` helper that enforces the cap by removing `stash[0]` (FIFO overflow) when `stash.len() >= stash_max_size` before appending.

3. Override `put` in the `Bucket<K, V>` impl block:
   - If the key already exists in `items`, update in place and call `get_from_position` as before.
   - If the key is found in `stash`, remove it from the stash, increment its `lfu_counter` by 1 to credit the access, evict one primary item into the stash (if primary is full), then push the promoted item to the back of `items`.
   - For a new key: evict one primary item into the stash (if full), push the new item.

4. Override `get` in the `Bucket<K, V>` impl block:
   - Primary hit: call `get_from_position` as before (this already increments `lfu_counter`).
   - Stash hit: remove the item from the stash, increment its `lfu_counter` by 1, evict one primary item into the stash (if primary is full), push the stash item to the back of `items`, and return a reference to it.
   - Miss: return `None`.

The LFU-weighted `eviction_candidate_index` from v001 applies on every eviction from primary — items evicted into the stash are the globally least-frequent in their bucket. The stash then provides a second chance specifically for those items, which tend to be medium-frequency items displaced by a cold burst rather than genuinely cold items.
