# H02

Capping `lfu_counter` at a maximum of 16 in ClassicLRU will raise hit rate by preventing stale-high-frequency items from permanently blocking rising-frequency items when the workload's hot set shifts.

## Parent

cycle/0001/v001

## Changes

Modify `src/shared/eviction_policy.rs` starting from v001's overlay. The only change relative to v001 is in `ClassicLRU::get_from_position`: replace the unbounded increment

```rust
items[back].lfu_counter += 1;
```

with a saturating increment capped at 16:

```rust
items[back].lfu_counter = items[back].lfu_counter.saturating_add(1).min(16);
```

All other logic in v001's `ClassicLRU` stays identical: the move-to-back access discipline in `get_from_position`, the `min_by_key(|(i, item)| (item.lfu_counter, *i))` eviction criterion in `eviction_candidate_index`, and the `items.len() - 1` promotion candidate.

The cap of 16 is chosen to preserve discrimination among truly cold items (counters 0–3) and moderately hot items (counters 4–15) while ensuring that an item accessed thousands of times does not accumulate a counter so large that a newly-rewarming item — whose counter just crossed 1 — can never compete with it for survival. Items genuinely in the Zipfian hot set will saturate quickly and still dominate the eviction order; the benefit accrues when a formerly-hot item's access rate drops and another item is rising, which the uncapped version handles poorly.
