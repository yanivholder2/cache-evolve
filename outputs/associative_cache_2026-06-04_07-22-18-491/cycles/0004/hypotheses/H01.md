# H01

Raising the stash-to-primary promotion credit from +2 to +3 will increase hit rate by giving promoted items one additional eviction cycle of protection against the next cold-burst displacement.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

In `associative_cache_bucket.rs`, change the promotion credit in both stash-hit code paths from `+= 2` to `+= 3`:

1. In `put`, Case 2 (key found in stash):
   ```rust
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 3;
   ```
   replacing the current `stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;`

2. In `get`, stash-hit branch:
   ```rust
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 3;
   ```
   replacing the current `stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;`

No other changes. The stash capacity, FIFO ordering, and all other bucket logic remain exactly as in cycle/0003/v003. This is a minimal single-constant probe to determine whether the credit lever's gain is monotone beyond +2 or whether +2 is already near the optimum.

## Rationale

The cycle-3 insight found that +2 beat +1 with a real but small improvement, leaving open whether the gain continues at +3. Testing +3 is the most direct way to characterise the lever's range before exploring more structural changes.
