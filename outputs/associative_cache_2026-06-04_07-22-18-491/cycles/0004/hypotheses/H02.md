# H02

Scaling the stash promotion credit by the item's pre-eviction frequency — giving more credit to items that were demonstrably popular before displacement — will raise hit rate by differentiating valuable second-chance candidates from incidental ones.

## Parent

cycle/0003/v003

## Changes

In `associative_cache_bucket.rs`, replace the fixed `+= 2` promotion credit in both stash-hit paths with a frequency-proportional credit. The extra credit is derived from the item's `lfu_counter` value at promotion time (which equals the value it carried when it was evicted to the stash, since the stash does not modify counters).

Formula: `extra = (stash_item.lfu_counter / 2).min(2)`, giving a total credit of `2 + extra`.
- `lfu_counter == 0`: total credit +2 (same as v003 baseline)
- `lfu_counter` in 2..=3: total credit +3
- `lfu_counter >= 4`: total credit +4 (capped)

Apply this in both locations:

1. In `put`, Case 2 (key found in stash):
   ```rust
   let extra = (stash_item.lfu_counter / 2).min(2);
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 2 + extra;
   ```
   replacing `stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;`

2. In `get`, stash-hit branch:
   ```rust
   let extra = (stash_item.lfu_counter / 2).min(2);
   stash_item.lfu_counter += 2 + extra;
   ```
   replacing `stash_item.lfu_counter += 2;`

No new fields or data structures needed. Stash capacity, FIFO eviction order, and all other logic remain unchanged from cycle/0003/v003.

## Rationale

The fixed +2 credit treats every promoted item identically regardless of its history. Items that arrived in the stash with a higher counter were the LFU minimum in primary despite having genuine hit history — they were displaced by a burst of newer items competing at zero frequency. Rewarding these more valuable candidates with a larger head-start credit addresses the cross-cycle open question of whether a dynamic promotion credit outperforms a fixed one.
